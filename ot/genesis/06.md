## Clear & Faithful

**1** When man began to multiply on the face of the land and daughters were born to them,
**2** the sons of God saw that the daughters of man were attractive. And they took as their wives any they chose.
**3** Then the LORD said, "My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, for he is flesh: his days shall be 120 years."
**4** The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God came in to the daughters of man and they bore children to them. These were the mighty men who were of old, the men of renown.
**5** The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.
**6** And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart.
**7** So the LORD said, "I will blot out man whom I have created from the face of the land, man and animals and creeping things and birds of the heavens, for I am sorry that I have made them."
**8** But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.
**9** These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation. Noah walked with God.
**10** And Noah had three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
**11** Now the earth was corrupt in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence.
**12** And God saw the earth, and behold, it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted their way on the earth.
**13** And God said to Noah, "I have determined to make an end of all flesh, for the earth is filled with violence through them. Behold, I will destroy them with the earth.
**14** Make yourself an ark of gopher wood. Make rooms in the ark, and cover it inside and out with pitch.
**15** This is how you are to make it: the length of the ark 300 cubits, its breadth 50 cubits, and its height 30 cubits.
**16** Make a roof for the ark, and finish it to a cubit above, and set the door of the ark in its side. Make it with lower, second, and third decks.
**17** For behold, I will bring a flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life under heaven. Everything that is on the earth shall die.
**18** But I will establish my covenant with you, and you shall come into the ark, you, your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives with you.
**19** And of every living thing of all flesh, you shall bring two of every sort into the ark to keep them alive with you. They shall be male and female.
**20** Of the birds according to their kinds, and of the animals according to their kinds, of every creeping thing of the ground, according to its kind, two of every sort shall come in to you to keep them alive.
**21** Also take with you every sort of food that is eaten, and store it up. It shall serve as food for you and for them."
**22** Noah did this; he did all that God commanded him.

## Everyday English

**1** As people began to spread across the earth and daughters were born to them,
**2** the sons of God noticed how beautiful human women were, and they married whichever ones they wanted.
**3** Then the LORD said, "My Spirit won't put up with humanity forever—they are mortal flesh. Their lifespan will be 120 years."
**4** The Nephilim (giants) were on the earth in those days—and afterward too—when the sons of God slept with human women and had children by them. Those were the famous heroes and warriors of ancient times.
**5** The LORD saw how widespread human evil had become on the earth, and that every single thought and desire of the human heart was constantly bent toward evil.
**6** The LORD was deeply sorry he had made humanity on the earth—it broke his heart.
**7** So the LORD said, "I'm going to wipe humanity—the people I created—off the face of the earth, along with the animals, crawling creatures, and birds, because I'm grieved that I made them."
**8** But Noah found favor with the LORD.
**9** Here is the story of Noah's family: Noah was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time, and he walked closely with God.
**10** Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
**11** Now the earth was ruined in God's eyes—it was overflowing with violence.
**12** God looked at the earth and saw how corrupt it was, because everyone had corrupted their way of life.
**13** So God told Noah, "I've decided to put an end to all living beings, because the earth is full of their violence. I'm going to destroy them along with the earth.
**14** Build yourself an ark out of gopher wood, divide it into rooms, and seal it inside and out with pitch.
**15** Make it 450 feet long, 75 feet wide, and 45 feet high.
**16** Put a roof on it, leaving an 18-inch gap under the roofline, and put a door in the side. Build it with three decks—lower, middle, and upper.
**17** I am about to bring a flood of water over the earth to destroy every living creature under the sky that breathes. Everything on earth will die.
**18** But I will make a solemn covenant with you: you will go into the ark—you, your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives with you.
**19** Bring a pair of every living creature into the ark—one male and one female—to keep them alive with you:
**20** birds, livestock, and every creature that crawls on the ground, two of each kind will come to you to survive.
**21** Also gather every kind of food that can be eaten and store it up for yourself and for them."
**22** Noah did everything just as God commanded him.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `בְּנֵי־הָאֱלֹהִים` (*bene ha-Elohim*):** "Sons of God" (heavenly beings/angels, or godly line of Seth, or ancient rulers).
- **v. 5 `יֵצֶר` (*yetser*):** "Inclination," "framing," "intention" (like a potter's shaping).
- **v. 6 `יִנָּחֶם` (*yinachem*):** "Relented," "grieved," "regretted"—same root as Noah's name (*nacham*).
- **v. 14 `תֵּבָה` (*tevah*):** "Ark," "chest," "basket" (used only here and for Moses' papyrus basket in Exod 2:3).
- **v. 14 `כֹּפֶר` (*kofer*):** "Pitch/tar," sharing the root of *kippur* ("covering/atonement").
