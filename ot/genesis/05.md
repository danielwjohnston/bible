## Clear & Faithful

**1** This is the book of the generations of Adam. When God created man, he made him in the likeness of God.
**2** Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and named them Man when they were created.
**3** When Adam had lived 130 years, he fathered a son in his own likeness, after his image, and named him Seth.
**4** The days of Adam after he fathered Seth were 800 years; and he had other sons and daughters.
**5** Thus all the days that Adam lived were 930 years, and he died.
**6** When Seth had lived 105 years, he fathered Enosh.
**7** Seth lived after he fathered Enosh 807 years and had other sons and daughters.
**8** Thus all the days of Seth were 912 years, and he died.
**9** When Enosh had lived 90 years, he fathered Kenan.
**10** Enosh lived after he fathered Kenan 815 years and had other sons and daughters.
**11** Thus all the days of Enosh were 905 years, and he died.
**12** When Kenan had lived 70 years, he fathered Mahalalel.
**13** Kenan lived after he fathered Mahalalel 840 years and had other sons and daughters.
**14** Thus all the days of Kenan were 910 years, and he died.
**15** When Mahalalel had lived 65 years, he fathered Jared.
**16** Mahalalel lived after he fathered Jared 830 years and had other sons and daughters.
**17** Thus all the days of Mahalalel were 895 years, and he died.
**18** When Jared had lived 162 years, he fathered Enoch.
**19** Jared lived after he fathered Enoch 800 years and had other sons and daughters.
**20** Thus all the days of Jared were 962 years, and he died.
**21** When Enoch had lived 65 years, he fathered Methuselah.
**22** Enoch walked with God after he fathered Methuselah 300 years and had other sons and daughters.
**23** Thus all the days of Enoch were 365 years.
**24** Enoch walked with God, and he was not, for God took him.
**25** When Methuselah had lived 187 years, he fathered Lamech.
**26** Methuselah lived after he fathered Lamech 782 years and had other sons and daughters.
**27** Thus all the days of Methuselah were 969 years, and he died.
**28** When Lamech had lived 182 years, he fathered a son
**29** and called his name Noah, saying, "Out of the ground that the LORD has cursed, this one shall bring us relief from our work and from the painful toil of our hands."
**30** Lamech lived after he fathered Noah 595 years and had other sons and daughters.
**31** Thus all the days of Lamech were 777 years, and he died.
**32** After Noah was 500 years old, Noah fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Everyday English

**1** Here is the family record of Adam's line. When God created humanity, he made them to be like God.
**2** He created them male and female, blessed them, and called them "Human" on the day they were created.
**3** When Adam was 130 years old, he had a son who looked just like him—in his own image and likeness—and named him Seth.
**4** Adam lived another 800 years after Seth was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**5** Adam lived 930 years in total, and then he died.
**6** When Seth was 105, he had a son named Enosh.
**7** Seth lived another 807 years after Enosh was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**8** Seth lived 912 years in total, and then he died.
**9** When Enosh was 90, he had a son named Kenan.
**10** Enosh lived another 815 years after Kenan was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**11** Enosh lived 905 years in total, and then he died.
**12** When Kenan was 70, he had a son named Mahalalel.
**13** Kenan lived another 840 years after Mahalalel was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**14** Kenan lived 910 years in total, and then he died.
**15** When Mahalalel was 65, he had a son named Jared.
**16** Mahalalel lived another 830 years after Jared was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**17** Mahalalel lived 895 years in total, and then he died.
**18** When Jared was 162, he had a son named Enoch.
**19** Jared lived another 800 years after Enoch was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**20** Jared lived 962 years in total, and then he died.
**21** When Enoch was 65, he had a son named Methuselah.
**22** After Methuselah was born, Enoch walked closely with God for 300 years, and had more sons and daughters.
**23** Enoch lived 365 years in total.
**24** Enoch walked with God, and then one day he was simply gone, because God took him.
**25** When Methuselah was 187, he had a son named Lamech.
**26** Methuselah lived another 782 years after Lamech was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**27** Methuselah lived 969 years in total, and then he died.
**28** When Lamech was 182, he had a son
**29** and named him Noah ("Rest/Comfort"), saying, "He's the one who will bring us relief from our exhausting work on the ground that the LORD cursed."
**30** Lamech lived another 595 years after Noah was born, and had more sons and daughters.
**31** Lamech lived 777 years in total, and then he died.
**32** By the time Noah was 500 years old, he had become the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `תּוֹלְדֹת` (*toledot*):** "Generations," "family history," "account."
- **v. 24 `וְאֵינֶנּוּ` (*ve-enennu*):** Literally "and he was not" (i.e., no longer present on earth).
- **v. 29 `נֹחַ` (*Noach*, Noah) & `יְנַחֲמֵנוּ` (*yenachamenu*, "will comfort us"):** Soundplay linking Noah's name to *nacham* (comfort/relief) and *nuach* (rest).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Hebrews 11:5** (cites Gen 5:24): *"By faith Enoch was taken up so that he should not see death, and he was not found, because God had taken him."*
- **Jude 1:14–15** (cites Enoch, seventh from Adam): *"It was also about these that Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied..."*
- **Luke 3:36–38** (genealogy of Jesus): Traces the line from Jesus back through Noah, Enoch, Seth, and Adam, "the son of God."
