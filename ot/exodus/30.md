## Clear & Faithful

**1** You shall make an altar for burning incense; make it of acacia wood.
**2** It shall be a cubit long and a cubit wide—square—and two cubits high, with its horns of one piece with it.
**3** Overlay it with pure gold—its top, its sides all around, and its horns—and craft a gold molding around it.
**4** Make two gold rings for it beneath its molding on two opposite ribs; place them on its two sides to serve as holders for poles with which to carry it.
**5** Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.
**6** Position it in front of the veil that hangs before the ark of the testimony, before the atonement cover that rests over the testimony, where I will meet with you.
**7** Aaron shall burn fragrant incense upon it every morning; when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it,
**8** and when Aaron lights the lamps at twilight, he shall burn it as a continual incense offering before the LORD throughout your generations.
**9** Do not offer unauthorized incense on it, nor a burnt offering, nor a grain offering, and do not pour a drink offering upon it.
**10** Once a year Aaron shall make atonement upon its horns with the blood of the sin offering of atonement; once each year he shall make atonement upon it throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD.
**11** The LORD also spoke to Moses, saying:
**12** "When you take a census of the people of Israel according to their registered number, each person shall give a ransom for his life to the LORD when they are counted, so that no plague may strike them during the census.
**13** This is what everyone who passes into the count shall pay: half a shekel according to the sanctuary shekel (twenty gerahs to the shekel), half a shekel as a sacred contribution to the LORD.
**14** Everyone included in the count from twenty years old and upward shall give the LORD's contribution.
**15** The wealthy shall not pay more and the poor shall not pay less than the half-shekel when giving the LORD's contribution to make atonement for your lives.
**16** Receive the atonement silver from the Israelites and assign it to the service of the tent of meeting, so that it may serve as a memorial for the people of Israel before the LORD to make atonement for your lives."
**17** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:
**18** "You shall also make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing, and set it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it.
**19** Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it.
**20** Whenever they enter the tent of meeting they must wash with water so that they do not die, or whenever they approach the altar to minister by sending up an offering by fire to the LORD.
**21** They shall wash their hands and their feet so that they do not die; this shall be a perpetual statute for them, for Aaron and his descendants throughout their generations."
**22** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:
**23** "Take the finest spices: 500 shekels of liquid myrrh, half as much—250 shekels—of fragrant cinnamon, 250 shekels of aromatic cane,
**24** and 500 shekels of cassia, measured by the sanctuary shekel, along with a hin of olive oil.
**25** Blend these into a sacred anointing oil, a skilled perfumer's compound; it shall be a sacred anointing oil.
**26** With it you shall anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony,
**27** the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, the altar of incense,
**28** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand.
**29** Consecrate them so that they become most holy; whatever touches them will become holy.
**30** Also anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them to serve me as priests.
**31** Speak to the people of Israel, saying, 'This shall be my sacred anointing oil throughout your generations.
**32** It shall not be poured upon any ordinary human body, nor shall you duplicate anything like its formula; it is holy, and it shall be holy to you.
**33** Whoever compounds anything like it or applies any of it to an unauthorized person shall be cut off from his people.'"
**34** The LORD said to Moses, "Take fragrant spices—stacte, onycha, and galbanum—together with pure frankincense, equal amounts of each,
**35** and blend them into incense, a skilled perfumer's compound, seasoned with salt, pure and holy.
**36** Grind part of it into fine powder and place some of it before the testimony in the tent of meeting where I will meet with you; it shall be most holy to you.
**37** As for the incense that you make, you shall not make any for yourselves by its formula; it shall be sacred to you for the LORD.
**38** Whoever makes any like it to enjoy its fragrance shall be cut off from his people."

## Everyday English

**1** "Build a small altar out of acacia wood for burning incense.
**2** Make its top a square—18 inches wide and 18 inches deep—and build it 3 feet tall, with corner horns carved right out of the same frame.
**3** Plate the entire altar in pure gold—top, all four sides, and horns—and add a decorative gold trim around the upper rim.
**4** Mount two gold rings under the trim on opposite sides to hold carrying poles.
**5** Carve the poles from acacia wood and plate them with gold as well.
**6** Place this incense altar right in front of the inner curtain shielding the covenant chest—directly opposite the gold lid of atonement where I'll meet with you.
**7** Every morning when Aaron trims and tends the lamps, he must burn fragrant incense on this altar,
**8** and again at dusk when he lights the lamps, keeping incense rising continually before the LORD generation after generation.
**9** Never burn unauthorized incense on it, and never use it for burnt offerings, grain offerings, or poured-out drink offerings.
**10** Once a year Aaron must smear blood from the annual purification sacrifice on its horns to make atonement for it. Do this every year for all generations—this altar is completely sacred to the LORD."
**11** Then the LORD told Moses:
**12** "Whenever you take a census of the Israelites, every man who is counted must pay a life-ransom to the LORD so that no deadly plague strikes them during the count.
**13** Each person enrolled must pay one-fifth of an ounce of silver—that's half a sanctuary shekel, where a full shekel equals twenty gerahs—as a dedicated gift to the LORD.
**14** Every man aged twenty and older who is registered must pay this contribution to the LORD.
**15** Rich people must not pay extra and poor people must not pay less than that one-fifth of an ounce of silver when giving the LORD's offering to ransom their lives.
**16** Collect this atonement silver from the Israelites and put it toward maintaining the meeting tent. It will serve as a constant reminder of the Israelites before the LORD, ransoming their lives."
**17** Next, the LORD told Moses:
**18** "Cast a large bronze washbasin with a bronze stand for washing, and set it halfway between the meeting tent and the burnt-offering altar; fill it with water.
**19** Aaron and his sons must wash their hands and feet there.
**20** Whenever they are about to step into the meeting tent, or walk up to the altar to serve and burn an offering to the LORD, they must wash with water so they won't die.
**21** Washing their hands and feet keeps them from dying; this rule stays permanently in effect for Aaron and his descendants for all generations."
**22** Then the LORD instructed Moses:
**23** "Gather top-grade spices: 12.5 pounds of liquid myrrh, half that much (6.25 pounds) of sweet cinnamon, 6.25 pounds of fragrant cane,
**24** and 12.5 pounds of cassia—all weighed by the sanctuary standard—plus 1 gallon of olive oil.
**25** Have an expert perfumer blend these into a sacred anointing oil.
**26** Use it to anoint the meeting tent, the covenant chest,
**27** the bread table and all its dishes, the lampstand and its tools, the incense altar,
**28** the burnt-offering altar and all its utensils, and the bronze washbasin with its stand.
**29** Dedicate all of them so they become completely sacred; anything that touches them will become holy too.
**30** Anoint Aaron and his sons as well, setting them apart to serve me as priests.
**31** Tell the Israelites, 'This oil is permanently set apart as my sacred anointing oil across every generation.
**32** Never pour it on ordinary people, and never mix up another batch using this recipe; it's sacred, so treat it as sacred.
**33** Anyone who copies this formula or puts any of it on an unauthorized person must be cut off from the community.'"
**34** Finally, the LORD told Moses: "Take equal parts of fragrant spices—gum resin, shell-spice, and galbanum—along with pure frankincense,
**35** and have a master perfumer blend them with salt into a pure, sacred incense.
**36** Crush part of it into a fine powder and set it in front of the covenant chest inside the meeting tent, where I'll meet you; treat it as completely sacred.
**37** Never copy this incense recipe for personal use; keep it strictly dedicated to the LORD.
**38** Anyone who mixes a batch just to enjoy its scent must be cut off from the community."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `מִזְבֵּחַ מִקְטַר קְטֹרֶת` (*mizbeach miqtar qetoret*):** "Altar of a place of burning incense"—compact inside the Holy Place directly before the veil (v. 6), contrasting with the large outdoor bronze altar.
- **v. 12 `בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל` (*bene-Yisra'el*):** WLC retains the Codex Leningradensis pointing and accent mark on `יִשְׂרָאֵל` (flagged by OSIS note `n="1"` in `Exod.xml`).
- **v. 12 `כֹּפֶר נַפְשׁוֹ` (*kofer nafsho*):** "Ransom of his life/soul"—shares the *k-p-r* root with `כִּפֶּר` (*kipper*, make atonement, vv. 10, 15, 16) and `כַּפֹּרֶת` (*kapporet*, atonement cover, v. 6), protecting those enrolled in a military/tribal census from plague (`נֶגֶף`).
- **v. 13 `מַחֲצִית הַשֶּׁקֶל` (*machatsit ha-sheqel*):** Half a sanctuary shekel (10 gerahs, roughly 5.7 grams or 0.2 ounces of silver); flat-rate equality between rich and poor (v. 15) symbolizes equal standing and worth before God.
- **v. 29 `קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים` (*qodesh qodashim*):** Indefinite superlative ("most holy / completely sacred"), applied to the anointed altar, furnishings, and sacred incense (vv. 10, 29, 36), distinct from the definite spatial room `קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים` ("the Most Holy Place").
- **v. 35 `מְמֻלָּח` (*memullach*):** "Salted / seasoned with salt"—either literal salt added to promote even combustion and symbolize covenant permanence (Lev 2:13) or thoroughly blended.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **2 Samuel 24:1–15 & 1 Chronicles 21:1–14** (census without ransom): *David's unauthorized census triggers a devastating plague across Israel, illustrating the peril warned against in Exodus 30:12.*
- **Matthew 17:24–27** (temple half-shekel tax): *The two-drachma temple tax questioned in Capernaum draws its lineage from Exodus 30:13–16's half-shekel sanctuary contribution.*
- **Luke 1:9–11 & Revelation 8:3–4** (incense and prayer): *Zechariah's ministry at the golden altar of incense and the heavenly golden altar in Revelation link rising smoke from Exodus 30:7–8 with Israel's prayers.*
- **Hebrews 10:22** (washing with water): *Draws on priestly washing at the bronze basin (Exod 30:18–21) as imagery for believers approaching God's presence with washed bodies and pure hearts.*
