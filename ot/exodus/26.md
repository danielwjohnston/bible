## Clear & Faithful

**1** "Moreover, you shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twined linen and blue, purple, and crimson yarn; you shall make them with cherubim skillfully woven into them.
**2** The length of each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; all the curtains shall have the same measurement.
**3** Five of the curtains shall be joined to one another, and the other five curtains shall be joined to one another.
**4** Make loops of blue yarn on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, and likewise make them on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.
**5** Make fifty loops on the one curtain, and make fifty loops on the edge of the curtain that is in the second set; the loops shall correspond opposite one another.
**6** Make fifty clasps of gold and join the curtains one to another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle becomes a single whole.
**7** You shall also make curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; eleven such curtains you shall make.
**8** The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; the eleven curtains shall all have the same measurement.
**9** Join five curtains by themselves and six curtains by themselves, and fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent.
**10** Make fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in one set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.
**11** Make fifty bronze clasps, put the clasps into the loops, and join the tent together so that it becomes a single whole.
**12** As for the overlapping excess of the tent curtains, the half curtain that remains over shall hang down over the back of the tabernacle.
**13** And the extra cubit on one side and the extra cubit on the other side in the length of the tent curtains shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle on both sides to cover it.
**14** Also make a covering for the tent of tanned ram skins, and a covering of fine leather above that.
**15** For the tabernacle you shall make upright frames of acacia wood.
**16** Ten cubits shall be the length of a frame, and a cubit and a half the width of each frame.
**17** Each frame shall have two tenons paired side by side; so you shall do for all the frames of the tabernacle.
**18** Make the frames for the tabernacle: twenty frames for the south side, facing southward,
**19** and make forty silver bases under the twenty frames—two bases under one frame for its two tenons, and two bases under the next frame for its two tenons.
**20** And for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, twenty frames
**21** and their forty silver bases—two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.
**22** For the rear of the tabernacle, facing west, make six frames.
**23** And make two frames for the rear corners of the tabernacle;
**24** they shall be coupled together at the bottom and joined tightly together at the top into a single ring; so it shall be for both of them—they shall form the two corners.
**25** Thus there shall be eight frames with their silver bases, sixteen bases in all—two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.
**26** Make bars of acacia wood: five for the frames of one side of the tabernacle,
**27** five bars for the frames of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the frames of the rear side of the tabernacle facing west.
**28** The middle bar, halfway up the frames, shall run all the way from end to end.
**29** Overlay the frames with gold, make their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and overlay the bars with gold.
**30** Then you shall erect the tabernacle according to the plan for it that you were shown on the mountain.
**31** You shall make a veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine twined linen; it shall be made with cherubim skillfully woven into it.
**32** Hang it upon four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, with gold hooks, set on four silver bases.
**33** Hang the veil beneath the clasps, and bring the ark of the testimony inside behind the veil; the veil shall separate for you between the Holy Place and the Most Holy Place.
**34** Place the atonement cover upon the ark of the testimony in the Most Holy Place.
**35** Set the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle; and place the table on the north side.
**36** For the entrance of the tent, make a screen of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine twined linen, embroidered with needlework.
**37** Make five pillars of acacia for the screen and overlay them with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, and you shall cast five bronze bases for them."

## Everyday English

**1** "Construct the sacred tent itself out of ten long woven panels of finely twisted linen blended with blue, purple, and bright red yarn, featuring winged guardian figures woven right into the fabric by a master artist.
**2** Cut every panel to the exact same size: forty-two feet long by six feet wide.
**3** Sew five panels edge-to-edge into one wide sheet, and sew the remaining five panels into a second matching sheet.
**4** Stitch blue fabric loops along the outer border of the first large sheet, and do the same along the matching border of the second sheet.
**5** Attach fifty loops along each sheet so that the loops line up cleanly across from one another.
**6** Then craft fifty golden hooks to link the two large sheets together, pulling the whole sacred tent roof into a single seamless canopy.
**7** Next, weave eleven rugged cloth strips out of goat hair to form an outer protective tent over the sacred dwelling.
**8** Make every strip identical in size: forty-five feet long by six feet wide.
**9** Stitch five of those goat-hair strips into one sheet and six into another, folding half of the sixth strip double at the front entrance of the tent.
**10** Sew fifty loops along the outer edge of the first goat-hair sheet and fifty more along the edge of the second sheet.
**11** Cast fifty bronze hooks, slip them through the matching loops, and fasten the entire outer tent together as one piece.
**12** Let the extra half-panel from the goat-hair tent drape down over the back wall of the sacred tent,
**13** and let the extra eighteen inches on either end of those longer strips hang down on both flanks to shelter the structure.
**14** Top off the outer tent with two weatherproof covers: one made of ram skins dyed red, and a top layer of durable fine leather.
**15** Build standing wall boards for the sacred tent out of solid acacia timber.
**16** Cut each upright board fifteen feet tall and twenty-seven inches wide,
**17** with two wooden pegs carved at the bottom of every board, spaced identically across all the tent walls.
**18** Line up twenty upright boards along the south wall,
**19** anchored into forty silver mounting sockets underneath—two sockets beneath every board to seat its twin pegs.
**20** Set up twenty matching boards along the north wall opposite it,
**21** resting in another forty silver sockets—two under each board.
**22** Build the back west wall out of six upright boards,
**23** plus two special corner posts reinforcing the rear corners.
**24** Join those corner posts together from the bottom all the way up into a single top ring so both rear corners lock securely together.
**25** That completes eight rear boards seated in sixteen silver sockets—two sockets beneath every board.
**26** Carve horizontal crossbars out of acacia wood: five crossbars for the north wall boards,
**27** five for the south wall boards, and five more for the back wall facing west.
**28** Slide the center crossbar halfway up the wall right through the boards from one end all the way to the other.
**29** Coat every wall board with gold, attach gold rings to hold the crossbars, and plate the crossbars themselves with gold.
**30** Assemble the entire sacred tent strictly following the architectural design shown to you up on the mountain.
**31** Weave an inner dividing curtain from blue, purple, and bright red yarn and twisted linen, with winged guardian figures skillfully worked into the fabric.
**32** Suspend it with gold hooks on four gold-plated acacia posts resting in four silver sockets.
**33** Hang that dividing curtain directly underneath the canopy hooks, then carry the covenant chest inside behind it. This curtain will separate the Holy Room from the Innermost Holy Room.
**34** Set the gold lid of atonement on top of the covenant chest inside the Innermost Holy Room.
**35** Outside the dividing curtain, place the bread table along the north side of the tent and set the lampstand directly across from it along the south wall.
**36** Finally, craft an embroidered entrance drape for the front doorway of the tent using blue, purple, and red yarn and finely twisted linen.
**37** Mount that entrance drape on gold hooks attached to five gold-plated acacia posts seated in five cast bronze sockets."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב` (*ma'aseh choshev*):** "Work of a skillful weaver/designer" (pattern woven directly into the cloth with `כְּרֻבִים` cherubim, used for the inner tabernacle curtains and veil, vv. 1, 31), contrasted with `מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵם` (*ma'aseh roqem*, "needlework/embroiderer's work" on the outer screen, v. 36).
- **v. 3 `אִשָּׁה אֶל־אֲחֹתָהּ` (*ishah el-achotah*):** Literally "a woman to her sister"—standard Hebrew idiom for pairing identical architectural parts "one to another" (vv. 3, 5, 6, 17).
- **v. 7 `אֹהֶל` (*ohel*) vs. `מִשְׁכָּן` (*mishkan*):** Distinguishes the outer protective goat-hair `אֹהֶל` ("tent," vv. 7–14) from the inner embroidered linen `מִשְׁכָּן` ("dwelling-place/tabernacle," vv. 1–6).
- **v. 15 `קְרָשִׁים` (*qerashim*):** "Upright planks/boards/frames" of acacia wood (`אַמָּה וַחֲצִי` = 1.5 cubits wide, ~27 inches, by 10 cubits tall, ~15 feet), seated in silver `אֲדָנִים` (*adanim*, "socket-bases").
- **v. 30 `כְּמִשְׁפָּטוֹ` (*ke-mishpato*):** "According to its plan/specification"—showing `מִשְׁפָּט` (*mishpat*, normally "judgment/legal ruling") used architecturally for prescribed design.
- **v. 31 `פָּרֹכֶת` (*parokhet*):** The inner "veil/curtain" separating `הַקֹּדֶשׁ` (*ha-qodesh*, "the Holy Place") from `קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים` (*qodesh ha-qodashim*, "the Holy of Holies / Most Holy Place," vv. 33–34)—as opposed to the outer entrance `מָסָךְ` (*masakh*, "screen," v. 36).
