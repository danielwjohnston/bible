## Clear & Faithful

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
**2** "Speak to the Israelites so that they may take a contribution for me; from every person whose heart moves him willingly you shall receive my contribution.
**3** This is the contribution that you shall receive from them: gold, silver, and bronze,
**4** blue, purple, and crimson yarn, fine twined linen, and goat hair,
**5** tanned ram skins, fine leather, and acacia wood,
**6** oil for the lamp, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,
**7** and onyx stones and stones for setting in the ephod and in the breastpiece.
**8** And let them make a sanctuary for me, so that I may dwell in their midst.
**9** According to all that I show you—the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings—so you shall make it.
**10** They shall make an ark of acacia wood: two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high.
**11** You shall overlay it with pure gold; inside and outside you shall overlay it, and you shall make a gold molding around it.
**12** Cast four rings of gold for it and attach them to its four feet, two rings on one side of it and two rings on the other side.
**13** Make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.
**14** Insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark by them.
**15** The poles shall remain in the rings of the ark; they shall not be removed from it.
**16** And into the ark you shall place the testimony that I will give you.
**17** You shall make an atonement cover of pure gold: two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.
**18** Make two cherubim of gold; of hammered work you shall make them at the two ends of the atonement cover.
**19** Make one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end; of one piece with the atonement cover you shall make the cherubim at its two ends.
**20** The cherubim shall spread out their wings above, overshadowing the atonement cover with their wings, with their faces toward one another; toward the atonement cover the faces of the cherubim shall be turned.
**21** Set the atonement cover on top of the ark, and inside the ark put the testimony that I will give you.
**22** There I will meet with you, and from above the atonement cover, from between the two cherubim that are upon the ark of the testimony, I will speak with you concerning everything that I command you for the Israelites.
**23** You shall make a table of acacia wood: two cubits long, one cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high.
**24** Overlay it with pure gold and make a gold molding around it.
**25** Make for it a rim a handbreadth wide all around, and make a gold molding around its rim.
**26** Make four gold rings for it and fasten the rings to the four corners at its four legs.
**27** The rings shall be close to the rim as holders for the poles to carry the table.
**28** Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, so that the table may be carried with them.
**29** Make its plates and dishes for incense, and its flagons and bowls with which to pour drink offerings; of pure gold you shall make them.
**30** And you shall set the bread of the Presence upon the table before me continually.
**31** You shall make a lampstand of pure gold. The lampstand shall be made of hammered work; its base, its shaft, its cups, its calyxes, and its blossoms shall be of one piece with it.
**32** Six branches shall extend from its sides, three branches of the lampstand from one side and three branches of the lampstand from the other side:
**33** three cups shaped like almond blossoms on one branch, each with calyx and flower, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the other branch, each with calyx and flower—so for all six branches extending from the lampstand.
**34** On the lampstand itself there shall be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with their calyxes and flowers:
**35** one calyx under the first pair of branches from it, one calyx under the next pair of branches from it, and one calyx under the third pair of branches from it, for all six branches extending from the lampstand.
**36** Their calyxes and their branches shall be of one piece with it, all of it a single hammered work of pure gold.
**37** Make seven lamps for it; the lamps shall be mounted so as to give light upon the space in front of it.
**38** Its tongs and its trays shall be of pure gold.
**39** Of a talent of pure gold it shall be made, together with all these utensils.
**40** And see that you make them after the pattern for them that is being shown you on the mountain."

## Everyday English

**1** The LORD told Moses,
**2** "Instruct the Israelites to bring me a voluntary gift—accept an offering from everyone whose heart generously prompts them to give.
**3** Collect these materials from them: gold, silver, and bronze;
**4** blue, purple, and bright red yarn; fine spun linen and goat hair;
**5** ram skins dyed red, durable fine leather, and acacia wood;
**6** olive oil for lighting the lamps, spices for the anointing oil and fragrant incense;
**7** plus onyx stones and other gems to set into the sacred vest and the decision pouch.
**8** Have them construct a holy sanctuary for me so I can live right in the middle of them.
**9** Build the sacred tent and every piece of its furniture strictly following the exact blueprint I'm showing you now.
**10** Build a sacred chest out of acacia wood—about three feet nine inches long, twenty-seven inches wide, and twenty-seven inches high.
**11** Coat the entire chest with pure gold, inside and out, and wrap a decorative gold trim around the top edge.
**12** Cast four gold rings and mount them onto its four lower corners—two rings along one side and two along the opposite side.
**13** Carve carrying poles out of acacia wood and plate them with gold,
**14** then slide the poles through the side rings so the chest can be carried.
**15** Leave those poles permanently inside the rings; never pull them out.
**16** Place inside the chest the covenant tablets I am going to give you.
**17** Next, craft a gold lid of atonement out of pure gold—matching the chest at about three feet nine inches long and twenty-seven inches wide.
**18** Hammer two winged guardian creatures out of gold to stand at opposite ends of that lid.
**19** Form one winged guardian on the left end and the other on the right end, hammered out as a single solid piece with the gold lid itself.
**20** Shape their wings spreading upward to shelter the lid, with the two figures facing each other while looking down toward the atonement lid.
**21** Place that lid directly on top of the sacred chest, right above the covenant tablets you've placed inside.
**22** Right there I will meet with you—speaking to you from above the gold lid of atonement, between the two winged guardians on top of the covenant chest—to give you every command for the Israelites.
**23** Also build a table out of acacia wood—three feet long, eighteen inches wide, and twenty-seven inches high.
**24** Plate it with pure gold and run a gold trim around the outer edge.
**25** Attach a border frame about three inches wide around the tabletop, edged with its own gold trim.
**26** Cast four gold rings and mount them at the four corners right by the legs,
**27** positioned just beneath the border frame to hold the carrying poles for moving the table.
**28** Make those carrying poles from acacia wood plated in gold so the table can be transported easily.
**29** Craft pure gold platters, incense spoons, pitchers, and bowls for pouring out drink offerings.
**30** Keep sacred display loaves resting on that table in my presence at all times.
**31** Hammer a golden lampstand out of solid pure gold—its base, central stem, oil cups, ornamental bulbs, and open blossoms must all be shaped from one continuous piece.
**32** Six side arms must curve out from the main stem—three arms branching from one side and three from the other.
**33** Each arm must feature three cups sculpted like almond blossoms, complete with bulb and petals—identical across all six side arms.
**34** The central shaft itself must have four almond-blossom cups, each with bulb and petals:
**35** position one ornamental bulb directly underneath each of the three pairs of side arms branching from the shaft.
**36** Every bulb and branch must belong to the same single piece—one continuous sculpture hammered out of pure gold.
**37** Fashion seven oil lamps for the top and arrange them so their light shines forward onto the space in front of the stand.
**38** Its wick trimmers and ash trays must also be pure gold.
**39** Use roughly seventy-five pounds of pure gold to craft the lampstand and all its accessories.
**40** Be sure you craft everything strictly according to the model shown to you here on the mountain."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `תְּרוּמָה` (*terumah*):** From `רוּם` (*rum*, "lift up/elevate")—a "lifted/set-apart contribution" given freely (`יִדְּבֶנּוּ לִבּוֹ`, "whose heart prompts him willingly").
- **v. 5 `תְּחָשִׁים` (*techashim*):** Uncertain animal or leather term—variously understood as sea-cow/dugong hides, dolphin leather, or beaded/dyed fine leather.
- **v. 8–9 `מִקְדָּשׁ` (*miqdash*) & `מִשְׁכָּן` (*mishkan*):** "Sanctuary" (holy place) and "tabernacle/dwelling-place" (from `שָׁכַן` *shakhan*, "dwell/settle," linked explicitly to `וְשָׁכַנְתִּי בְּתוֹכָם`, "that I may dwell in their midst").
- **v. 10 `אָרוֹן` (*aron*):** Literally a wooden chest or box (cf. Gen 50:26 for Joseph's coffin), designated `אֲרֹן הָעֵדֻת` (*aron ha-edut*, "ark of the testimony," v. 22) because it housed the covenant tablets (`הָעֵדֻת`).
- **v. 17 `כַּפֹּרֶת` (*kapporet*):** "Atonement cover" (sharing the *k-p-r* root of `כִּפֶּר` "make atonement" and `כֹּפֶר` "ransom" in 21:30)—the pure gold lid above the Ark where YHWH meets Israel (`v. 22`).
- **v. 18 `כְּרֻבִים` (*keruvim*):** "Cherubim"—winged heavenly guardians (cf. Gen 3:24) forming YHWH's earthly throne-chariot seat above the Ark.
- **v. 30 `לֶחֶם פָּנִים` (*lechem panim*):** Literally "bread of faces/presence," twelve loaves set continually before YHWH's face (Lev 24:5–9).
- **v. 39 `כִּכָּר` (*kikkar*):** "Talent" (~34 kg / ~75 pounds), used here for the total weight of pure gold in the seven-branched `מְנֹרָה` (*menorah*).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Hebrews 8:5** (cites Exod 25:40): *"Quotes God's command to Moses to make everything according to the pattern shown on the mountain, presenting the earthly sanctuary as a copy and shadow of heavenly realities."*
- **Hebrews 9:2–5** (describes Exod 25:10–40): *"Recalls the lampstand, the table with the bread of the Presence, and—behind the second curtain—the gold-covered ark of the covenant overshadowed by the cherubim of glory above the atonement cover."*
- **Romans 3:25** (echoes Exod 25:17): *"Uses the Greek term hilasterion (matching the Septuagint rendering of kapporet) to present Christ as the place of atonement through his blood."*
- **2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3** (echoes Exod 25:8): *"Takes up the promise 'I will dwell in their midst' as fulfilled in God's indwelling presence among his redeemed people."*
