## Clear & Faithful

**1** From the blue, purple, and crimson yarn they made woven service garments for ministering in the holy place, and they made the sacred garments for Aaron, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**2** He made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen.
**3** They hammered out thin sheets of gold and cut them into threads to work them into the blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, the work of a master weaver.
**4** They made joined shoulder straps for it; it was fastened together at its two upper ends.
**5** The finely woven waistband on it was of one piece with it and of the same workmanship: gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**6** They crafted the onyx stones enclosed in gold filigree settings, engraved like signet seals with the names of the sons of Israel,
**7** and mounted them on the shoulder straps of the ephod as stones of memorial for the Israelites, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**8** He made the breastpiece, the work of a master weaver, matching the workmanship of the ephod: of gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen.
**9** It was square; they made the breastpiece folded double, a span long and a span wide when doubled.
**10** They mounted four rows of stones on it: in the first row, carnelian, topaz, and emerald;
**11** in the second row, turquoise, lapis lazuli, and moonstone;
**12** in the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst;
**13** and in the fourth row, beryl, onyx, and jasper, each enclosed in gold filigree settings in their mountings.
**14** There were twelve stones corresponding to the names of the sons of Israel, engraved like signet seals, each stone with its own name for the twelve tribes.
**15** For the breastpiece they made braided chains of pure gold, twisted like cords.
**16** They made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and placed the two rings at the two upper corners of the breastpiece.
**17** They attached the two gold cords to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece,
**18** and fastened the other two ends of the two cords to the two settings, placing them on the shoulder straps of the ephod toward the front.
**19** They made two more gold rings and set them on the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on its inner edge facing the ephod.
**20** They also made two gold rings and attached them to the lower front of the two shoulder straps of the ephod, near its seam, just above the woven waistband of the ephod.
**21** Then they bound the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so that it rested above the woven waistband of the ephod and would not shift loose from the ephod, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**22** He made the robe of the ephod, the work of a weaver, entirely of blue.
**23** The neck opening of the robe in its center was like the opening of a coat of mail, with a bound collar around its opening so that it would not tear.
**24** Around the lower hem of the robe they made pomegranates of blue, purple, and crimson twisted yarn.
**25** They also made bells of pure gold and placed the bells among the pomegranates around the entire hem of the robe, alternating among the pomegranates:
**26** a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe for ministering, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**27** They wove the tunics of fine linen for Aaron and his sons,
**28** the turban of fine linen, the ornate headbands of fine linen, the linen undergarments of finely twisted linen,
**29** and the sash of finely twisted linen and blue, purple, and crimson yarn, the work of an embroiderer, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**30** They fashioned the medallion of the holy diadem out of pure gold and inscribed upon it in signet-seal engraving: "Holiness to the LORD."
**31** They fastened a blue cord to it to attach it high upon the turban, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**32** Thus all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was completed; the Israelites carried out everything exactly as the LORD had commanded Moses—that is how they did it.
**33** Then they brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings—its clasps, its frames, its crossbars, its pillars, and its socket-bases;
**34** the covering of tanned rams' skins, the covering of fine leather skins, and the screening veil;
**35** the ark of the testimony with its poles and the atonement cover;
**36** the table with all its utensils and the bread of the Presence;
**37** the pure lampstand with its lamps—the lamps arranged in order—and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;
**38** the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the entrance screen for the tent;
**39** the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils, the basin and its stand;
**40** the courtyard hangings with its pillars and socket-bases, the screen for the courtyard gate, its cords and tent pegs, and all the equipment for the service of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting;
**41** and the woven service garments for ministering in the holy place—the sacred vestments for Aaron the priest and the vestments for his sons to serve as priests.
**42** According to everything that the LORD had commanded Moses, so the people of Israel performed all the work.
**43** Moses inspected all the work, and behold, they had done it exactly as the LORD had commanded—that is how they had made it. And Moses blessed them.

## Everyday English

**1** Using the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the craftspeople wove the special service cloths for sanctuary duties and tailored the sacred outfits for Aaron, following every detail the LORD had given Moses.
**2** Bezalel crafted the priestly vest out of gold thread, blue, purple, and bright red yarn, and fine twisted linen.
**3** They hammered pure gold into wafer-thin sheets and sliced them into fine threads to weave right alongside the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen in artistic patterns.
**4** They sewed on two shoulder straps, attaching them securely at both top edges of the vest.
**5** The woven belt attached to it was crafted in one piece from the same rich blend—gold thread, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen—matching what the LORD had ordered Moses.
**6** They mounted the two onyx stones in gold bezels, engraving the names of Israel's twelve sons on them like an official stamp,
**7** and fastened them onto the vest's shoulder straps as reminder stones for the Israelites, just as the LORD had instructed Moses.
**8** Then he wove the decision pouch with the same master craftsmanship as the priestly vest, using gold thread, blue, purple, and bright red yarn, and fine twisted linen.
**9** Folded in half to form a neat square, the pouch measured 9 inches long and 9 inches wide.
**10** Across the front they mounted four rows of precious gems: row one held carnelian, topaz, and emerald;
**11** row two held turquoise, lapis lazuli, and moonstone;
**12** row three held jacinth, agate, and amethyst;
**13** and row four held beryl, onyx, and jasper, each stone firmly framed in gold filigree.
**14** Those twelve gemstones matched the twelve tribes of Israel, each carved like a royal seal with one tribe's name.
**15** They braided chains of pure gold, twisted like cord, for the decision pouch,
**16** crafted two gold bezels and two gold rings, and sewed the two rings onto the top corners of the pouch.
**17** They threaded the two gold chains through those upper corner rings,
**18** and hooked the opposite ends into the two gold bezels mounted on the front of the vest's shoulder straps.
**19** They forged two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom corners of the pouch, on the inside facing the vest,
**20** plus two more gold rings low on the front of the vest's shoulder straps, right above the seam where the woven belt joined.

**22** Next, he wove the long priestly robe completely out of solid blue fabric.
**23** Its central neck hole was reinforced all around like the collar of leather armor so it wouldn't rip.
**24** Around the bottom hem they stitched yarn pomegranates made from twisted blue, purple, and scarlet yarn,
**25** and crafted tiny bells of pure gold, placing them right between the pomegranates all the way around the hem—
**26** alternating gold bell, yarn pomegranate, gold bell, yarn pomegranate around the entire robe for priestly service, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**27** They also wove fine linen tunics for Aaron and his sons,
**28** along with a fine linen turban, ornate linen caps, and undergarments of fine twisted linen,
**29** plus an embroidered sash of fine twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn—all following what the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31** They tied a blue ribbon onto it to secure it across the upper front of the turban, just as the LORD had ordered Moses.
**32** At last, every single piece of work for the sacred meeting tent was finished! The Israelites had built everything down to the last detail just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**33** Then they carried all the components of the sacred tent to Moses: the tent canopy and all its hardware—its fasteners, wall frames, crossbars, posts, and bases;
**34** the cover of red-dyed ram hides, the weatherproof cover of fine-grained hides, and the inner divider curtain;
**35** the covenant chest with its carrying poles and the gold lid of atonement;
**36** the bread table with all its dishes and the sacred bread of presence;
**37** the pure-gold lampstand with its seven lamps lined up in place, all its tools, and the lamp oil;
**38** the golden incense altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the doorway curtain for the tent entrance;
**39** the bronze burnt-offering altar with its bronze grate, carrying poles, and all its tools, plus the washbasin and its pedestal;
**40** the courtyard curtains with their posts and bases, the curtain for the courtyard gate, the ropes and tent stakes, and every piece of service gear for the sacred meeting tent;
**41** as well as the woven service cloths for sanctuary work, the sacred outfits for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons' priestly service.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `פַּחֵי הַזָּהָב וְקִצֵּץ פְּתִילִם` (*pache ha-zahav ve-qitsets petilim*):** Explains the ancient gold-thread technique omitted in ch. 28: artisans beat gold into thin foil sheets and sliced narrow strips to spin and weave directly alongside dyed wool and linen.
- **v. 4 `קְצוֹתָיו` (*qetsotav*, Qere) / `קצוותו` (Ketiv):** `Exod.xml` records unpointed Ketiv `קצוותו` while the Masoretic Qere reads `קְצוֹתָיו` ("its two ends/edges"), identical to the Ketiv/Qere pair at 37:8.
- **v. 30 `צִיץ נֵזֶר־הַקֹּדֶשׁ` (*tsits nezer-ha-qodesh*):** Combines both terms from earlier chapters—`צִיץ` (blossom/plate, 28:36) and `נֵזֶר` (consecrated diadem/crown, 29:6)—for the pure gold forehead emblem inscribed `קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה`.
- **v. 33 `בְּרִיחָיו` (*berichav*, Qere) / `בריחו` (Ketiv):** `Exod.xml` prints unpointed Ketiv `בריחו` (singular-looking "its crossbar") while the Masoretic Qere reads plural `בְּרִיחָיו` ("its crossbars"), contrasting with 35:11 where Leningrad prints `בְּרִיחָו` without Ketiv/Qere.
- **v. 43 `וַיְבָרֶךְ אֹתָם מֹשֶׁה` (*vayvarekh otam Mosheh*):** "And Moses blessed them"—echoing God's inspection and blessing at the completion of creation in Genesis 1:31–2:3, underscored by the sevenfold refrain "just as YHWH had commanded Moses" across vv. 1–43.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 28:1–43 & 31:6–11** (commanded vestments and furnishings): *Chapter 39 documents the exact completion of the priestly garments and the comprehensive delivery of every sanctuary component for inspection.*
- **Genesis 1:31–2:3** (creation inspection and blessing): *Moses seeing all the finished work (`וַיַּרְא מֹשֶׁה אֶת־כָּל־הַמְּלָאכָה`) and blessing the workers directly mirrors God seeing all that he had made and blessing the seventh day.*
- **Leviticus 8:7–9** (vesting Aaron): *Records Aaron actually being clothed in the tunic, sash, blue robe, ephod, breastpiece, Urim and Thummim, turban, and gold diadem crafted here.*
- **Ephesians 6:14–17 & Revelation 1:13** (priestly and spiritual armor imagery): *Draw on motifs of the breastpiece of righteousness and the golden sash worn by the exalted priest-king.*
