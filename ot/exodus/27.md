## Clear & Faithful

**1** You shall make the altar out of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar shall be square, and three cubits high.
**2** Make horns for it on its four corners; its horns shall be of one piece with it, and overlay it with bronze.
**3** Make pots to clear away its ashes, along with its shovels, tossing basins, meat forks, and firepans; make all of its utensils out of bronze.
**4** Make a grating for it, a bronze mesh network, and attach four bronze rings on the grating at its four corners.
**5** Set it underneath the altar's ledge from below so that the mesh reaches halfway up the altar.
**6** Make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.
**7** Its poles are to be inserted into the rings so that the poles lie along two sides of the altar when it is carried.
**8** Make it hollow out of planks; just as you were shown on the mountain, so shall they construct it.
**9** You shall make the courtyard of the tabernacle: on the south side facing the Negev, the courtyard shall have hangings of finely twisted linen, a hundred cubits long for that one side,
**10** with twenty pillars and twenty bronze socket-bases, while the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be silver.
**11** Likewise along the length of the north side there shall be hangings a hundred cubits long, with twenty pillars and twenty bronze socket-bases, and silver hooks and bands for the pillars.
**12** Across the width of the courtyard on the west side there shall be hangings of fifty cubits, with ten pillars and ten socket-bases.
**13** The width of the courtyard on the east side toward the sunrise shall be fifty cubits.
**14** There shall be fifteen cubits of hangings for the one wing beside the entrance, with three pillars and three socket-bases,
**15** and fifteen cubits of hangings for the second wing, with three pillars and three socket-bases.
**16** For the courtyard gate there shall be a screen twenty cubits wide, of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer, with four pillars and four socket-bases.
**17** All the pillars surrounding the courtyard shall be banded with silver, with silver hooks and bronze socket-bases.
**18** The length of the courtyard shall be a hundred cubits, the width fifty cubits throughout, and the height five cubits, of finely twisted linen, with bronze socket-bases.
**19** All the equipment of the tabernacle for every task of its service, along with all its tent pegs and all the pegs of the courtyard, shall be bronze.
**20** You yourself shall command the people of Israel to bring you pure beaten olive oil for the lampstand, so that a lamp may be kept burning continually.
**21** In the tent of meeting, outside the veil that hangs before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall tend it from evening until morning before the LORD; this is a lasting statute throughout their generations on behalf of the Israelites.

## Everyday English

**1** Build the altar out of acacia wood. Make it a square—7.5 feet long and 7.5 feet wide—and 4.5 feet tall.
**2** Carve a horn-shaped projection out of each of the four corners as part of the frame itself, then cover the whole altar with bronze.
**3** Craft all its tools out of bronze as well: buckets for scooping out ashes, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and coal pans.
**4** Fashion a woven bronze grate for it, with a bronze ring at each of its four corners.
**5** Slide the grate under the rim of the altar so it sits halfway up inside the frame.
**6** Carve carrying poles from acacia wood and plate them with bronze.
**7** Slip the poles through the rings on both sides of the altar whenever it needs to be carried.
**8** Build the altar as a hollow wooden box, following the exact design God showed you up on the mountain.
**9** Next, set up a fenced courtyard around the sacred tent. Along the south side, hang fine twisted linen curtains stretching 150 feet long.
**10** Support them on 20 posts seated in 20 bronze bases, using silver hooks and silver trim bands on the posts.
**11** Do the same along the north side: 150 feet of linen curtains hung on 20 posts in 20 bronze bases, fitted with silver hooks and bands.
**12** Across the western end of the courtyard, hang 75 feet of curtains on 10 posts set in 10 bronze bases.
**13** The eastern front, facing sunrise, will also measure 75 feet wide.
**14** Put 22.5 feet of curtains on one side of the entrance, held up by 3 posts in 3 bases,
**15** and another 22.5 feet of curtains on the other side, also on 3 posts in 3 bases.
**16** Across the 30-foot entrance itself, hang an embroidered curtain woven from blue, purple, and bright red yarn with fine twisted linen, supported by 4 posts in 4 bases.
**17** Every post circling the courtyard must have silver bands, silver hooks, and bronze bases.
**18** So the entire courtyard will measure 150 feet long by 75 feet wide, enclosed by 7.5-foot-high curtains of fine twisted linen anchored in bronze bases.
**19** Every tool used in the sacred tent, along with every tent stake for both the tent and the courtyard, must be made of bronze.
**20** Order the Israelites to keep supplying you with pure, cold-pressed olive oil for the lampstand, so the light keeps burning day after day.
**21** Inside the meeting tent, just outside the curtain that shields the covenant chest, Aaron and his sons must tend the lamps from dusk till dawn in the LORD's presence. This rule holds permanently for Israel across every generation.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `מִכְבָּר מַעֲשֵׂה רֶשֶׁת נְחֹשֶׁת` (*mikbar ma'aseh reshet nechoshet*):** A bronze latticework grating (*mikbar*) suspended halfway up inside the hollow altar box to hold the sacrificial fire and allow ashes to drop underneath.
- **v. 8 `נְבוּב לֻחֹת` (*nevuv luchot*):** "Hollow of planks/boards"—a light, portable acacia-wood frame plated in bronze rather than solid stone or solid metal.
- **v. 11 `וְעַמּוּדָיו` (*ve-ammudav*, Qere) / `ועמדו` (Ketiv):** In WLC `Exod.xml`, the unpointed Ketiv consonant string `ועמדו` appears singular-looking (*ve-ammudo*), while the Masoretic Qere reads plural `וְעַמּוּדָיו` ("and its pillars"), matching the plural verb/count ("twenty") and the parallel in v. 10.
- **v. 15 `חְמֵשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה` (*chemesh esreh*):** WLC preserves the unusual initial vocal shewa under *chet* (`חְמֵשׁ` instead of standard `חֲמֵשׁ`), flagged by a Leningrad Codex preservation note in the critical apparatus.
- **v. 20 `שֶׁמֶן זַיִת זָךְ כָּתִית` (*shemen zayit zakh katit*):** "Pure beaten olive oil"—oil obtained by gently pounding olives in a mortar rather than crushing them in a heavy press, yielding clean, low-smoke fuel for the sanctuary lamps.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **1 Kings 8:64** (altar capacity): *Solomon later consecrated the courtyard floor when the bronze altar became too small to hold all the burnt and grain offerings.*
- **2 Chronicles 4:1** (enlarged temple altar): *The bronze altar built for Solomon's temple expanded the proportions of this portable wilderness altar fourfold in length and width.*
- **Leviticus 24:2–4** (lamp oil statute): *Reiterates the command to supply pure pressed olive oil so Aaron keeps the lamps burning continually before the LORD from evening to morning.*
- **Revelation 1:12–20** (lampstands in sanctuary imagery): *John's vision of seven golden lampstands echoes the Tabernacle's perpetual light tended in God's presence.*
