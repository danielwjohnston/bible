## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then God spoke all these words, saying:
**2** "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.
**3** You shall have no other gods before me.
**4** You shall not make for yourself an idol or any likeness of what is in heaven above, or on the earth beneath, or in the waters under the earth.
**5** You shall not bow down to them or serve them; for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of fathers upon children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me,
**6** yet showing steadfast love to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments.
**7** You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not leave unpunished anyone who takes his name in vain.
**8** Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy.
**9** Six days you shall labor and do all your work,
**10** but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God; on it you shall do no work—neither you, nor your son, nor your daughter, nor your male servant, nor your female servant, nor your livestock, nor the sojourner within your gates.
**11** For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested on the seventh day; therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and consecrated it.
**12** Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be prolonged upon the land that the LORD your God is giving you.
**13** You shall not murder.
**14** You shall not commit adultery.
**15** You shall not steal.
**16** You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.
**17** You shall not covet your neighbor's house; you shall not covet your neighbor's wife, or his male servant, or his female servant, or his ox, or his donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor."
**18** Now all the people witnessed the thunder and the flashes of lightning, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; when the people saw it, they trembled and stood at a distance.
**19** They said to Moses, "You speak with us, and we will listen; but do not let God speak with us, lest we die."
**20** Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid, for God has come to test you, so that the fear of him may be before you, that you may not sin."
**21** So the people stood at a distance while Moses approached the thick darkness where God was.
**22** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the Israelites: 'You yourselves have seen that I have spoken with you from heaven.
**23** You shall not make gods of silver alongside me, nor shall you make gods of gold for yourselves.
**24** An altar of earth you shall make for me, and sacrifice upon it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your cattle; in every place where I cause my name to be remembered I will come to you and bless you.
**25** And if you make an altar of stones for me, you shall not build it of dressed stone; for if you wield your chisel upon it, you will profane it.
**26** Nor shall you go up by steps to my altar, so that your nakedness may not be exposed upon it.'"

## Everyday English

**1** God spoke every word of this directly to them:
**2** "I am the LORD your God, the one who rescued you from Egypt—out of the land where you were enslaved.
**3** Never worship any other gods besides me.
**4** Don't carve any statue or craft any image modeled after anything in the sky above, on the earth below, or in the waters beneath the earth.
**5** Never bow down to them or worship them, because I, the LORD your God, demand total loyalty. When people hate me, I bring the consequences of parents' wrongdoing down on their children to the third and fourth generations,
**6** yet I pour out faithful love on thousands of generations of people who love me and obey my commands.
**7** Never misuse the name of the LORD your God, because the LORD won't let anyone off the hook who treats his name lightly.
**8** Make sure you remember the Sabbath day and treat it as sacred.
**9** Spend six days working and getting all your labor done,
**10** but the seventh day belongs to the LORD your God as a Sabbath rest. On that day nobody in your household may do any work—not you, your son, your daughter, your male or female workers, your farm animals, or any foreigner living in your town.
**11** After all, the LORD created the skies, the earth, the sea, and everything in them in six days, and rested on the seventh day. That's why the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and set it apart as holy.
**12** Respect and care for your father and mother, so you'll live a long, full life in the land the LORD your God is giving you.
**13** Do not murder.
**14** Do not commit adultery.
**15** Do not steal.
**16** Do not tell lies about your neighbor in court.
**17** Don't greedily desire your neighbor's home, and don't crave your neighbor's spouse, male or female worker, ox, donkey, or anything else your neighbor owns."
**18** Watching the thunderclaps, blazing lightning, deafening horn blasts, and the smoking mountain, all the people shook with terror and backed far away.
**19** They begged Moses, "You talk to us, and we'll listen! Just don't let God speak to us directly, or we'll die!"
**20** "Don't be terrified," Moses reassured them. "God has come here to test you and put a healthy awe of him in front of you, so you'll steer clear of sin."
**21** So the crowd stayed far back while Moses walked straight into the thick dark cloud where God was.
**22** Then the LORD instructed Moses, "Tell the Israelites this: 'You saw for yourselves that I spoke to you from heaven.
**23** Don't craft silver gods to place alongside me, and don't make gold gods for yourselves.
**24** Build me an altar out of plain earth, and offer your burnt sacrifices and fellowship offerings on it—your sheep, goats, and cattle. Wherever I designate a place to honor my name, I will meet you there and bless you.
**25** If you build me an altar out of stones, don't shape them with iron tools; swinging a chisel over stone defiles it.
**26** And don't build steps leading up to my altar, so you won't expose yourself inappropriately while climbing it.'"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `עֲבָדִים` (*avadim*):** "Slaves/bondservants"—framing the Decalogue not as abstract law but as liberation ("from the house of slaves") by a rescuing covenant Lord.
- **v. 2–17 `טַעַם עֶלְיוֹן` (*ta'am elyon* vs. *ta'am tachton*):** WLC preserves **dual cantillation** across `20:2–10, 13–15`: lower accents (*ta'am tachton*) divide by standard verses, while upper accents (*ta'am elyon*) group entire commandments for public liturgical chanting (joining vv. 2–6 and vv. 8–11 into single prosodic units, and linking vv. 13–16).
- **v. 2–17 `פָּרָשָׁה` (*parashah* divisions & numbering):** Masoretic *setumah* (`ס`) and *petuchah* (`פ`) paragraph breaks appear after `20:1` (`ס`), `20:6` (`ס`), `20:7` (`פ`), `20:11` (`ס`), `20:12` (`ס`), `20:13` (`ס`), `20:14` (`ס`), `20:15` (`ס`), `20:16` (`ס`), and `20:17` (`פ`); because there is no break between v. 2 and v. 3, Jewish tradition counts v. 2 as the First Word and vv. 3–6 as the Second, whereas Reformed/Eastern Christian traditions count v. 3 as First and vv. 4–6 as Second, and Augustinian/Roman Catholic/Lutheran traditions unite vv. 2–6 as First and divide v. 17 into Ninth and Tenth.
- **v. 5 `אֵל קַנָּא` (*El qanna*):** "A jealous/zealous God"—covenant fidelity that tolerates no rival allegiance, paired with generational justice (to the third/fourth generation) outweighed by `חֶסֶד` (*chesed*, "steadfast love") to thousands.
- **v. 7 `לַשָּׁוְא` (*lashshav*):** "For emptiness/falsehood/futility"—forbidding false oaths, magical manipulation, or empty invocation of `יְהוָה`.
- **v. 13 `תִּרְצָח` (*tirtsach*):** From `רָצַח` (*ratsach*), unlawful killing/murder (distinct from judicial execution or warfare `הָרַג` / `מוּת`).
- **v. 25 `גָּזִית` (*gazit*):** "Cut/dressed stone"; `חַרְבְּךָ` (*charbecha*, "your sword/chisel") symbolizes human violence/artifice intruding upon an unadorned altar of natural earth or unhewn fieldstones.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Deuteronomy 5:6–21** (parallel Decalogue): *"Reiterates the Ten Words on the plains of Moab, grounding Sabbath observance in redemption from Egyptian slavery rather than creation alone."*
- **Matthew 5:21–30; 19:18–19** (expands Exod 20:12–16): *"Jesus deepens the prohibitions against murder and adultery to address anger and lust of the heart, and affirms honoring parents and speaking truth."*
- **Romans 13:8–10** (cites Exod 20:13–17): *"Summarizes the commandments against adultery, murder, theft, and coveting under the single command to love one's neighbor."*
- **1 Corinthians 8:4–6; 10:14** (echoes Exod 20:3–5): *"Urges believers to flee idolatry and confess one God, the Father, and one Lord, Jesus Christ."*
