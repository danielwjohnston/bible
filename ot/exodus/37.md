## Clear & Faithful

**1** Bezalel made the ark out of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high.
**2** He overlaid it with pure gold inside and out, and crafted a gold molding all around it.
**3** He cast four gold rings for it at its four feet—two rings on one side and two rings on its other side.
**4** He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold,
**5** and inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark.
**6** He fashioned an atonement cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.
**7** He made two cherubim of gold, hammering them out of one piece from the two ends of the atonement cover:
**8** one cherub on the first end and one cherub on the other end; out of the atonement cover itself he shaped the cherubim at its two ends.
**9** The cherubim spread their wings upward, overshadowing the atonement cover with their wings while facing one another; toward the atonement cover the faces of the cherubim were turned.
**10** He made the table out of acacia wood, two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high.
**11** He overlaid it with pure gold and crafted a gold molding all around it.
**12** He made a rim of a handbreadth all around it, and crafted a gold molding around its rim.
**13** He cast four gold rings for it and fastened the rings to the four corners at its four legs.
**14** Close beside the rim were the rings, serving as holders for the poles to carry the table.
**15** He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold to carry the table.
**16** Then he made the utensils that go on the table—its plates, its ladles, its bowls, and its flagons for pouring drink offerings—out of pure gold.
**17** He made the lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work he fashioned the lampstand—its base, its central shaft, its cups, its buds, and its blossoms were all of one piece with it.
**18** Six branches extended from its sides: three lampstand branches from one side and three lampstand branches from the other side.
**19** There were three almond-blossom cups on one branch, each with bud and petal, and three almond-blossom cups on the next branch, each with bud and petal—so for all six branches extending from the lampstand.
**20** On the central lampstand itself were four almond-blossom cups, with their buds and blossoms;
**21** and a bud beneath the first pair of branches out of it, a bud beneath the next pair of branches out of it, and a bud beneath the third pair of branches out of it—for the six branches extending from it.
**22** Their buds and their branches were of one piece with it, all of it one single hammered piece of pure gold.
**23** He made its seven lamps, its wick-trimmers, and its trays out of pure gold.
**24** Out of one talent of pure gold he made it together with all its utensils.
**25** He made the altar of incense out of acacia wood, one cubit long and one cubit wide—square—and two cubits high, with its horns of one piece with it.
**26** He overlaid it with pure gold—its top, its walls all around, and its horns—and crafted a gold molding all around it.
**27** He made two gold rings for it beneath its molding on two opposite ribs, on its two sides, to hold the poles for carrying it.
**28** He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.
**29** He also compounded the sacred anointing oil and the pure fragrant incense, the work of a skilled perfumer.

## Everyday English

**1** Bezalel built the sacred chest out of acacia wood, measuring 3 feet 9 inches long, 2 feet 3 inches wide, and 2 feet 3 inches tall.
**2** He plated it inside and out with pure gold and trimmed the top edge with a gold molding all around.
**3** He cast four gold rings and mounted them on its four bottom corners—two rings along one side and two along the opposite side.
**4** He carved carrying poles from acacia wood, covered them with gold,
**5** and slid them through the side rings so the chest could be carried.
**6** Next, he hammered out a gold lid of atonement from pure gold, measuring 3 feet 9 inches long by 2 feet 3 inches wide.
**7** He sculpted two winged guardian creatures out of solid gold, hammering them straight out from both ends of the lid—
**8** one winged guardian on the left end and one on the right end, seamlessly formed from the lid itself at its two ends.
**9** The winged guardians stretched their wings upward to shelter the lid, facing each other while looking down toward the atonement lid.
**10** Then Bezalel crafted the bread table out of acacia wood, measuring 3 feet long, 18 inches wide, and 2 feet 3 inches high.
**11** He plated it with pure gold and added a decorative gold trim around it.
**12** He fitted a 3-inch-wide border around the tabletop and trimmed that border with gold molding as well.
**13** He cast four gold rings and attached them to the four corners right above its four legs.
**14** Placed just under the border, those rings served as brackets for the carrying poles.
**15** He shaped the carrying poles from acacia wood and plated them with gold for moving the table.
**16** Out of pure gold he also fashioned every dish used on the table: the platters, incense dishes, bowls, and pitchers for pouring drink offerings.
**17** Next, he hammered the lampstand out of pure gold—shaping its pedestal, central stem, cups, buds, and flower petals out of a single piece.
**18** Six curved arms branched out from the sides of the stem—three arms on one side and three on the other.
**19** Each arm held three cups shaped like almond blossoms, complete with bud and open flower—three blossom cups on every one of the six branches.
**20** Along the central stem itself were four more almond-blossom cups with buds and petals,
**21** with a single bud positioned directly beneath each of the three pairs of arms branching out from the stem.
**22** Both the buds and the six branches formed a single continuous piece with the stem, all hammered from solid pure gold.
**23** He also crafted seven oil lamps, along with snuffers and ash trays, out of pure gold.
**24** In total, he used about 75 pounds of pure gold to build the lampstand and all its accessories.
**25** Then he built the incense altar from acacia wood—18 inches square and 3 feet tall—carving its corner horns out of the same piece of wood.
**26** He plated its top, all four sides, and its horns in pure gold, and added a gold trim around the rim.
**27** Below the trim on two opposite sides, he mounted two gold rings to hold the carrying poles,
**28** and carved the poles from acacia wood plated in gold.
**29** Finally, following the recipe of a master perfumer, he blended the sacred anointing oil and the pure sweet incense.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 6 `כַּפֹּרֶת` (*kapporet*):** "Atonement cover"—from the *k-p-r* root ("cover/atone"), functioning as the pure gold lid of the covenant chest and the earthly footstool/throne beneath the cherubim where blood was sprinkled on the Day of Atonement (Lev 16:14–15).
- **v. 8 `קְצוֹתָיו` (*qetsotav*, Qere) / `קצוותו` (Ketiv):** `Exod.xml` records unpointed Ketiv `קצוותו` (from *qatsvah*), while the Masoretic Qere points `קְצוֹתָיו` (from *qatseh*, "its two ends"), matching the plural "two ends" (`מִשְּׁנֵי`) of the atonement cover.
- **v. 12 `טֹפַח` (*tofach*):** "Handbreadth" (the width of four fingers, roughly 3 inches / 7.5 cm), forming a raised lip around the table to keep the bread and vessels secure.
- **v. 19 `מְשֻׁקָּדִים` (*meshuqqadim*):** "Almond-shaped" (from *shaqed*, "almond tree"), evoking Aaron's budding almond rod (Num 17:8[23]) and the almond's early spring bloom ("watcher" wordplay in Jer 1:11–12).
- **v. 24 `כִּכָּר זָהָב טָהוֹר` (*kikkar zahav tahor*):** One talent (3,000 sanctuary shekels, roughly 75 pounds / 34 kilograms) of pure gold hammered as a single seamless piece (`מִקְשָׁה אַחַת`, v. 22) for the seven-branched menorah and its tools.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 25:10–40 & 30:1–5, 22–38** (blueprint counterparts): *Chapter 37 narrates Bezalel's exact construction of the ark, atonement cover, table, lampstand, incense altar, oil, and incense previously prescribed on Sinai.*
- **Leviticus 16:14–16** (Day of Atonement): *Describes the high priest sprinkling sacrificial blood on the eastern front of the atonement cover (kapporet) fashioned in Exodus 37:6–9.*
- **Romans 3:25** (place of atonement): *Paul uses hilasterion (the Septuagint word for the kapporet in Exod 25:17/37:6) to describe Christ presented by God as the place of atonement through his blood.*
- **Zechariah 4:2–14 & Revelation 1:12–20** (golden lampstand vision): *Both prophetic and apocalyptic visions draw upon the seven-lamped pure gold menorah crafted in Exodus 37:17–24.*
