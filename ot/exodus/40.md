## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying:
**2** "On the first day of the first month you shall erect the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.
**3** Place the ark of the testimony there, and screen the ark with the veil.
**4** Bring in the table and arrange its setting, and bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.
**5** Set the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony, and hang the screen at the entrance of the tabernacle.
**6** Place the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.
**7** Set the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it.
**8** Erect the courtyard all around and hang the screen at the courtyard gate.
**9** Then take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything in it, consecrating it and all its furnishings, so that it becomes holy.
**10** Anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and consecrate the altar, so that the altar becomes most holy.
**11** Anoint the basin and its stand and consecrate it.
**12** Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.
**13** Clothe Aaron in the sacred vestments, anoint him, and consecrate him so that he may serve me as priest.
**14** Bring his sons forward and clothe them in tunics,
**15** and anoint them just as you anointed their father, so that they may serve me as priests; their anointing shall establish for them a perpetual priesthood throughout their generations."
**16** Moses did everything exactly as the LORD had commanded him—so he did.
**17** Thus in the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the tabernacle was erected.
**18** Moses set up the tabernacle: he laid its socket-bases, positioned its frames, inserted its crossbars, and raised its pillars.
**19** He spread the tent over the tabernacle and placed the covering of the tent above it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**20** He took the testimony and placed it inside the ark, attached the poles to the ark, and set the atonement cover atop the ark.
**21** He brought the ark into the tabernacle, hung the screening veil, and screened off the ark of the testimony, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**22** He placed the table inside the tent of meeting on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil,
**23** and arranged the loaves of bread in order upon it before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**24** He positioned the lampstand inside the tent of meeting opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle,
**25** and set up its lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**26** He placed the gold altar inside the tent of meeting in front of the veil,
**27** and burned fragrant incense upon it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**28** Then he hung the screen for the entrance of the tabernacle.
**29** At the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting he set the altar of burnt offering, and offered upon it the burnt offering and the grain offering, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**30** He placed the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it for washing.
**31** From it Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and their feet;
**32** whenever they entered the tent of meeting or approached the altar, they would wash, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**33** Finally, he erected the courtyard surrounding the tabernacle and the altar, and hung the screen for the courtyard gate. So Moses finished the work.
**34** Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.
**35** Moses was unable to enter the tent of meeting because the cloud had settled upon it, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.
**36** Throughout all their journeys, whenever the cloud lifted up from above the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out;
**37** but if the cloud did not lift up, they would not set out until the day it lifted.
**38** For the cloud of the LORD rested over the tabernacle by day, and fire appeared in it by night, before the eyes of the whole house of Israel throughout all their journeys.

## Everyday English

**1** Then the LORD instructed Moses:
**2** "On New Year's Day—the first day of the first month—set up the sacred tent, the meeting tent.
**3** Place the covenant chest inside and hang the inner divider curtain in front of it.
**4** Carry in the bread table and set its loaves and dishes in order, then bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.
**5** Position the golden incense altar right in front of the divider shielding the covenant chest, and hang the entrance curtain over the sacred tent's doorway.
**6** Set the burnt-offering altar outside the front doorway of the meeting tent,
**7** and place the washbasin between the meeting tent and the altar, filling it with water.
**8** Put up the courtyard fence all around and hang the gate curtain at the courtyard entrance.
**9** Then take the anointing oil and anoint the sacred tent and everything inside it; dedicate it and all its gear so it becomes holy.
**10** Anoint the burnt-offering altar and all its tools, consecrating the altar so it becomes completely sacred.
**11** Anoint the washbasin and its pedestal to dedicate it too.
**12** Next, bring Aaron and his sons to the doorway of the meeting tent and wash them with water.
**13** Dress Aaron in his holy garments, pour anointing oil on him, and dedicate him to serve as my priest.
**14** Bring his sons forward, dress them in their tunics,
**15** and anoint them just as you anointed their father so they can serve as my priests. Their anointing will establish an enduring priesthood for all future generations."
**16** Moses carried out every single detail exactly as the LORD had ordered him.
**17** On the first day of the first month in the second year after leaving Egypt, the sacred tent was raised up.
**18** Moses put the tent together: he laid down its silver bases, stood up the wall frames, slid in the crossbars, and raised the posts.
**19** He stretched the goat-hair tent over the sacred tent and fastened the outer leather covers over the top, following everything the LORD had commanded Moses.
**20** He placed the stone covenant tablets inside the chest, slipped the poles into the chest's rings, and fitted the gold lid of atonement on top.
**21** Then he carried the chest inside the sacred tent and hung the divider curtain to shield the covenant chest, just as the LORD had ordered Moses.
**22** He set the bread table inside the meeting tent along the north wall, outside the inner curtain,
**23** and laid out the rows of sacred bread on it in the LORD's presence, following the LORD's orders to Moses.
**24** Directly across from the table along the south wall inside the meeting tent, he placed the golden lampstand,
**25** and lit its lamps in the LORD's presence, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**26** He set the golden incense altar inside the meeting tent right in front of the inner curtain,
**27** and burned sweet incense on it, exactly as the LORD had instructed Moses.
**28** Next, he hung the entrance curtain at the doorway of the sacred tent.
**29** Outside the doorway of the meeting tent, he positioned the burnt-offering altar and sacrificed the burnt offering and grain offering on it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
**30** He set the bronze washbasin between the meeting tent and the altar, and poured water into it for washing.
**31** Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons all washed their hands and feet there;
**32** every time they entered the meeting tent or stepped up to the altar, they washed first, obeying what the LORD had ordered Moses.
**33** Last of all, Moses put up the courtyard curtains circling the sacred tent and the altar, and hung the curtain across the courtyard gate. At last Moses finished the entire project!
**34** Immediately, the cloud covered the meeting tent, and the radiant glory of the LORD filled the sacred tent completely.
**35** Moses couldn't even step inside the meeting tent, because the cloud had settled over it and the LORD's radiant glory filled the dwelling.
**36** From then on, throughout all their travels, whenever the cloud rose up from above the sacred tent, the Israelites packed up and marched forward.
**37** Whenever the cloud stayed put, they stayed camped and didn't move until the day it lifted.
**38** Day after day, the cloud of the LORD hovered over the sacred tent, and night after night a glow of fire shone inside it—visible to every household of Israel throughout every stage of their journey.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `בְּיוֹם־הַחֹדֶשׁ הָרִאשׁוֹן בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ` (*be-yom ha-chodesh ha-rishon be-echad la-chodesh*):** "On the first day of the first month" (Abib/Nisan 1, v. 17)—nearly one full year after the Exodus Passover (Exod 12:2) and nine months after arriving at Sinai (19:1), establishing a new liturgical calendar anchored in God's indwelling presence.
- **v. 22 `עַל־יֶרֶךְ הַמִּשְׁכָּן` (*al-yerekh ha-mishkan*):** Literally "upon the thigh/flank of the tabernacle"—an anatomical spatial idiom for the long northern and southern inner sides of the Holy Place (vv. 22, 24).
- **v. 30 `לְרָחְצָה` (*lerachtsah*):** "For washing"—infinitive construct with feminine ending, emphasizing the ongoing ritual prerequisite for approaching YHWH's dwelling (vv. 31–32).
- **v. 34 `כְּבוֹד יְהוָה מָלֵא אֶת־הַמִּשְׁכָּן` (*kevod YHWH male et-ha-mishkan*):** "The glory (*kavod*) of YHWH filled the dwelling-place (*mishkan*)." The verb `שָׁכַן` (*shakhan*, "settled/dwelt", v. 35) shares the exact root of `מִשְׁכָּן` (*mishkan*), uniting Sinai's cloud-theophany (24:16) with Israel's portable sanctuary.
- **v. 35 `וְלֹא־יָכֹל מֹשֶׁה לָבוֹא` (*ve-lo-yakhol Mosheh lavo*):** "Moses was unable to enter"—creating the dramatic narrative bridge directly into Leviticus 1:1, where YHWH calls to Moses from inside the tent and institutes the sacrificial liturgy enabling human approach.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 24:15–18 & 25:8** (Sinai glory and indwelling promise): *The cloud and kavod that rested on Mount Sinai now relocate into the completed Tabernacle so YHWH dwells among Israel.*
- **Numbers 9:15–23** (guidance by cloud and fire): *Expands Exodus 40:36–38's account of Israel's camp marching and halting solely at the lifting or settling of the LORD's cloud.*
- **1 Kings 8:10–11 & 2 Chronicles 7:1–2** (glory filling Solomon's temple): *When Solomon dedicates the temple, the cloud and glory of the LORD fill the house so the priests cannot stand to minister—directly paralleling Exodus 40:34–35.*
- **John 1:14 & Revelation 21:3, 22** (the Word pitching his tent): *John's declaration that the Word "tabernacled" (eskenosen, echoing mishkan/shakhan) among us so that we beheld his glory (doxa / kavod) fulfills the closing climax of Exodus.*
