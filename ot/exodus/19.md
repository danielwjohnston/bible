## Clear & Faithful

**1** In the third month after the Israelites had gone out from the land of Egypt, on that very day they entered the wilderness of Sinai.
**2** Having set out from Rephidim, they came to the wilderness of Sinai and camped in the wilderness; Israel camped there directly facing the mountain.
**3** Then Moses went up to God, and the LORD called to him from the mountain, saying, "This is what you shall say to the house of Jacob and declare to the Israelites:
**4** 'You yourselves have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I carried you on eagles' wings and brought you to myself.
**5** Now therefore, if you will indeed listen to my voice and keep my covenant, you shall be my treasured possession out of all the peoples, for the whole earth is mine;
**6** and you shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.' These are the words that you shall speak to the Israelites."
**7** So Moses came back, summoned the elders of the people, and laid before them all these words that the LORD had commanded him.
**8** All the people answered together and said, "Everything that the LORD has spoken we will do." And Moses brought the people's words back to the LORD.
**9** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Behold, I am coming to you in a dense cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you and may also trust you forever." When Moses reported the people's words to the LORD,
**10** the LORD told Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow, and have them wash their garments.
**11** Let them be prepared for the third day, for on the third day the LORD will come down in the sight of all the people upon Mount Sinai.
**12** Set boundaries for the people all around, saying, 'Be careful not to go up on the mountain or touch its border; whoever touches the mountain shall surely be put to death.
**13** No hand shall touch him, but he must surely be stoned or shot with arrows; whether beast or human, he shall not live.' Only when the ram's horn sounds a long blast may they come up on the mountain."
**14** So Moses came down from the mountain to the people, consecrated the people, and they washed their garments.
**15** He said to the people, "Be ready for the third day; do not go near a woman."
**16** On the morning of the third day, there were peals of thunder and flashes of lightning, a thick cloud over the mountain, and an exceedingly loud blast of a trumpet, so that all the people in the camp trembled.
**17** Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they took their stand at the foot of the mountain.
**18** Now Mount Sinai was completely wrapped in smoke, because the LORD had descended upon it in fire; its smoke billowed up like the smoke of a furnace, and the entire mountain shook violently.
**19** As the sound of the trumpet grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him in thunder.
**20** The LORD descended upon Mount Sinai, to the summit of the mountain, and the LORD summoned Moses to the summit of the mountain, and Moses went up.
**21** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go down and solemnly warn the people, lest they break through to gaze at the LORD and many of them perish.
**22** Even the priests who approach the LORD must consecrate themselves, lest the LORD break out against them."
**23** Moses replied to the LORD, "The people cannot come up to Mount Sinai, for you yourself solemnly warned us, saying, 'Set boundaries around the mountain and consecrate it.'"
**24** But the LORD said to him, "Go down, and then come back up with Aaron alongside you; yet do not let the priests and the people break through to come up to the LORD, lest he break out against them."
**25** So Moses went down to the people and told them.

## Everyday English

**1** Exactly two months to the day after leaving Egypt, the Israelites entered the Desert of Sinai.
**2** They had marched from Rephidim into the Sinai desert and pitched their tents right across from the mountain.
**3** Moses climbed up to meet God, and the LORD called out to him from the mountain: "Deliver this message to Jacob's descendants—tell the people of Israel:
**4** 'You saw with your own eyes what I did to Egypt, and how I carried you like an eagle bearing its young on its wings and brought you safely here to me.
**5** Now, if you truly obey me and stay faithful to my covenant agreement, you'll be my most prized treasure among all the nations—even though the entire world belongs to me!
**6** You will serve me as a royal kingdom of priests and a nation set apart as holy.' Pass these exact words along to the Israelites."
**7** So Moses returned, gathered the community elders, and laid out everything the LORD had instructed him to say.
**8** Everyone responded with one voice: "We will do everything the LORD has said!" Then Moses carried the people's answer back to the LORD.
**9** The LORD told Moses, "I'm going to come to you inside a thick, dark cloud so the people can listen while I talk with you—and trust your leadership from now on." When Moses reported the people's response back to the LORD,
**10** the LORD instructed him, "Go back to the people and set them apart as holy today and tomorrow. Have everyone wash their clothes
**11** and get ready for the third day, because on the third day the LORD will come down onto Mount Sinai where all the people can see.
**12** Mark off strict boundary lines around the mountain and warn them: 'Stay back! Don't climb the mountain or even touch its edge. Anyone who touches the mountain will be executed.
**13** Nobody may lay a hand on the offender—instead, stone them or shoot them down with arrows from a distance. Whether animal or human, they must not survive.' Only when the ram's horn blows a long, steady blast may they approach the mountain."
**14** Moses walked down the mountain to the people, led them through the purification rites, and had them wash their clothes.
**15** He told everyone, "Prepare yourselves for the third day, and abstain from sexual relations."
**16** Early on the third morning, thunder boomed and lightning flashed across a heavy cloud resting on the mountain, accompanied by a piercing ram's horn blast. Everyone inside the camp shook with fear.
**17** Moses led the people out of camp to meet God, and they lined up at the base of the mountain.
**18** All of Mount Sinai was billowing with smoke because the LORD had come down onto it in blazing fire. Smoke poured upward like a blast furnace, and the whole mountain trembled violently.
**19** The horn's blast grew louder and louder. Moses spoke, and God answered him in a booming voice.
**20** The LORD came down to the peak of Mount Sinai and called Moses up to the summit, so Moses climbed up.
**21** There the LORD told Moses, "Head back down and strictly warn the people not to push past the barriers to get a look at the LORD, or many of them will drop dead.
**22** Even the priests who regularly approach the LORD have to purify themselves, or the LORD will strike them down too."
**23** "The people won't dare climb Mount Sinai," Moses answered the LORD, "because you already warned us to mark off the mountain and keep it strictly off-limits."
**24** Still, the LORD insisted, "Go down anyway, and bring Aaron back up with you. Make sure neither the priests nor the crowd push through to climb up to the LORD, or he will strike them down."
**25** So Moses went back down and relayed the warning to the people.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `כַּנְפֵי נְשָׁרִים` (*kanfe nesharim*):** "Wings of eagles" (or griffon vultures), an image of swift, protective rescue echoed in Deut 32:11.
- **v. 5 `סְגֻלָּה` (*segullah*):** "Treasured possession"—a royal technical term in the ancient Near East for a monarch's private, prized wealth (cf. Mal 3:17; 1 Pet 2:9).
- **v. 6 `מַמְלֶכֶת כֹּהֲנִים` (*mamlekhet kohanim*):** "A kingdom of priests" alongside `גּוֹי קָדוֹשׁ` (*goy qadosh*, "a holy nation")—defining Israel's corporate vocation to mediate between God and the nations.
- **v. 13 `הַיֹּבֵל` (*ha-yovel*):** "The ram's horn" (related to the Jubilee horn in Lev 25), distinct from `שֹׁפָר` (*shofar*, v. 16), though both signify a ritual horn trumpet.
- **v. 16 `כָּבֵד` (*kaved*):** "Heavy/dense" cloud—linking back to `כְּבוֹד יְהוָה` ("glory/weightiness of YHWH") and contrasting with Pharaoh's "heavy" heart.
- **v. 22 `הַכֹּהֲנִים` (*ha-kohanim*):** "The priests" functioning prior to Aaron's formal consecration in Exod 28–29 (likely firstborn or clan ritual leaders, cf. 24:5).
