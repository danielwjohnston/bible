## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD assured Moses, "Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh: compelled by a mighty hand, he will release them; indeed, by a mighty hand he will drive them out of his land."
**2** God spoke further to Moses and declared to him, "I am the LORD.
**3** I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as God Almighty, yet by my name 'the LORD' I did not fully disclose myself to them.
**4** I also established my covenant with them to grant them the land of Canaan, the land in which they lived as resident aliens.
**5** Furthermore, I have heard the groaning of the Israelites whom the Egyptians are enslaving, and I have remembered my covenant.
**6** Therefore announce to the Israelites: 'I am the LORD. I will bring you out from beneath Egypt's oppressive burdens, rescue you from their bondage, and redeem you with an outstretched arm and mighty acts of judgment.
**7** I will take you to myself as a people, and I will be your God; then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who brings you out from beneath the burdens of Egypt.
**8** And I will bring you into the land that I swore with an uplifted hand to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and I will grant it to you as a lasting heritage—I am the LORD.'"
**9** Moses relayed this to the Israelites, yet they could not listen to Moses because of brokenness of spirit and brutal bondage.
**10** Then the LORD instructed Moses:
**11** "Go in and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to release the Israelites from his country."
**12** But Moses protested before the LORD, "Look, even the Israelites have not listened to me! How then will Pharaoh listen to me, since I am faltering of lips?"
**13** Still, the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and commissioned them to both the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt, to lead the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.
**14** These are the heads of their ancestral households: The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these formed the clans of Reuben.
**15** The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman; these formed the clans of Simeon.
**16** These are the names of the sons of Levi according to their lineages: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari; Levi lived 137 years.
**17** The sons of Gershon, listed by their clans, were Libni and Shimei.
**18** The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel; Kohath lived 133 years.
**19** The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi; these formed the Levite clans according to their lineages.
**20** Amram married his father's sister Jochebed, who bore him Aaron and Moses; Amram lived 137 years.
**21** The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.
**22** The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.
**23** Aaron married Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
**24** The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these formed the Korahite clans.
**25** Eleazar son of Aaron married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These were the ancestral heads of the Levites by their clans.
**26** It was this very Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, "Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt, company by company."

## Everyday English

**1** Then the LORD told Moses, "Watch closely what I'm about to do to Pharaoh! Once I unleash my power, he won't just let them leave—he'll force them out of his country!"
**2** God spoke to Moses again: "I am the LORD.
**3** I revealed myself to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty, but I didn't make the full meaning of my name, the LORD, known to them.
**4** I also made a binding covenant with them, promising to hand over the land of Canaan—the very land where they used to live as outsiders.
**5** Now I've heard the groans of the Israelites under Egyptian slavery, and I haven't forgotten my covenant.
**6** So tell the Israelites: 'I am the LORD! I'll pull you out from under Egypt's crushing labor, free you from being their slaves, and rescue you with unmistakable power and decisive acts of judgment.
**7** I'll claim you as my very own people and be your God. Then you'll truly realize that I am the LORD your God, the one lifting Egypt's heavy load off your backs.
**8** I'll bring you right into the land I raised my hand and swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. It will belong to you as your permanent inheritance. I am the LORD!'"
**9** Moses passed this promise on to the Israelites, but they were too crushed in spirit and worn down by brutal slavery to even hear him out.
**10** Then the LORD told Moses,
**11** "Go back inside and demand that Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, let the Israelites leave his country."
**12** Moses argued right back to the LORD: "Even my own people won't listen to me! Why on earth would Pharaoh listen—especially when I struggle so badly just to speak clearly?"
**13** Even so, the LORD gave Moses and Aaron their official orders for both the Israelites and Egypt's king: get the Israelites out of Egypt.
**14** Here is the record of Israel's family leaders: Reuben, the oldest son of Israel, fathered Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi—the ancestors of Reuben's clans.
**15** Next came Simeon's descendants: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul (whose mother was Canaanite)—these became Simeon's clans.
**16** Moving to Levi's line, his sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari; Levi reached the age of 137.
**17** Gershon's clan founders were Libni and Shimei.
**18** Kohath fathered Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, living to be 133.
**19** Merari fathered Mahli and Mushi, completing the Levite family lines.
**20** Amram married his aunt Jochebed, and together they had Aaron and Moses; Amram lived 137 years.
**21** Izhar's children were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.
**22** Uzziel's children were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.
**23** Aaron married Elisheba—Amminadab's daughter and Nahshon's sister—and they had four sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
**24** Korah's sons were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph, forming the Korahite family line.
**25** Aaron's son Eleazar married one of Putiel's daughters, and they had a son named Phinehas. That completes the roster of Levite ancestral heads.
**26** Those two—Aaron and Moses—are the exact brothers whom the LORD ordered, "Lead the Israelites out of Egypt organized in full companies."
**27** They are the very ones who stood up to Pharaoh, Egypt's king, to bring Israel out of Egypt.
**28** Resuming the story from the day the LORD addressed Moses in Egypt:
**29** The LORD declared to Moses, "I am the LORD. Repeat to Pharaoh, Egypt's king, every single word I tell you."
**30** Once more Moses protested to the LORD, "Look, I can barely get my words out! Why would Pharaoh ever listen to me?"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `בְּאֵל שַׁדָּי` (*be-El Shaddai*) & `יְהוָה` (*YHWH*):** Since *YHWH* already appears in Genesis, "did not make myself known" points not to total ignorance of the syllables, but to the full experiential unfolding of *YHWH*'s covenant-keeping character now about to be revealed through redemption (vv. 6–8).
- **v. 6 `וְגָאַלְתִּי` (*vega'alti*):** From *ga'al* ("redeem/act as kinsman-redeemer"), casting God as the nearest relative who pays the price and steps in to rescue enslaved family members.
- **v. 6 `וּבִשְׁפָטִים` (*u-vishfatim*):** Plural noun *shefatim* ("acts of judgment/vindication"), used specifically for God's decisive plague-judgments against Egypt and its deities (cf. 7:4; 12:12), distinct from *mishpat* ("legal ordinance").
- **v. 9 `מִקֹּצֶר רוּחַ` (*miqqotser ruach*):** Literally "from shortness of breath/spirit," portraying people whose emotional and spiritual breathing room has been suffocated by relentless toil.
- **v. 12 `עֲרַל שְׂפָתָיִם` (*aral sefatayim*):** Literally "uncircumcised of lips" (repeated in v. 30), an idiom meaning "blocked, unskillful, or hesitant in speech," paralleling "heavy of mouth" in 4:10.
- **v. 26 `עַל־צִבְאֹתָם` (*al-tsiv'otam*):** From *tsava* ("army/organized host"), anticipating Israel departing not as scattered fugitives, but as the ordered hosts of YHWH (12:41, 51).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 15:18; 17:1–8** (Abrahamic covenant): *God's reminder of his self-revelation as El Shaddai and his sworn gift of Canaan directly recalls the covenant scenes with Abraham.*
- **Romans 2:28–29; Jeremiah 6:10** ("uncircumcised" metaphor): *Hebrew and New Testament writers extend the image of uncircumcision from lips (Exod 6:12) and ears (Jer 6:10) to the human heart to describe inward dullness or incapacity.*
- **1 Corinthians 6:20; Galatians 3:13** (redemption language): *The New Testament's vocabulary of buying back and liberating captives builds on Exodus's foundational portrait of God as Israel's redeemer (Exod 6:6).*
