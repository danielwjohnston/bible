## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
**2** "Tell the Israelites to turn back and camp in front of Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, facing Baal-zephon; you shall camp right opposite it by the sea.
**3** Pharaoh will say of the Israelites, 'They are wandering confused in the land; the desert has closed them in.'
**4** I will harden Pharaoh's heart so that he pursues them, and I will gain glory through Pharaoh and all his army; then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD." And the Israelites did so.
**5** When the king of Egypt was told that the people had fled, the mind of Pharaoh and his officials changed toward the people, and they said, "What is this we have done, releasing Israel from serving us?"
**6** So he had his chariot readied and took his troops with him.
**7** He took six hundred select chariots along with all the chariots of Egypt, with commanders over every one of them.
**8** The LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursued the Israelites as the Israelites marched out defiantly.
**9** The Egyptians chased after them—all of Pharaoh's chariot horses, his horsemen, and his army—and overtook them where they were encamped by the sea near Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon.
**10** As Pharaoh approached, the Israelites lifted up their eyes and saw the Egyptians advancing behind them. Terrified, the Israelites cried out to the LORD.
**11** They said to Moses, "Was it because there were no graves in Egypt that you brought us out to die in the wilderness? What have you done to us by leading us out of Egypt?
**12** Is this not the very thing we told you in Egypt: 'Leave us alone and let us serve the Egyptians'? It would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in the wilderness."
**13** Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid! Stand firm and watch the deliverance the LORD will accomplish for you today. For the Egyptians whom you see today you will never see again forever.
**14** The LORD will fight for you; you need only to keep still."
**15** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Why are you crying out to me? Tell the Israelites to move forward.
**16** As for you, raise your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea and divide it, so that the Israelites may pass through the middle of the sea on dry ground.
**17** And I myself am about to harden the hearts of the Egyptians so that they follow them in, and I will win glory over Pharaoh and his entire army, his chariots, and his horsemen.
**18** Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD when I gain glory through Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen."
**19** Then the angel of God, who had been going ahead of Israel's camp, shifted and moved behind them, and the pillar of cloud shifted from in front of them and stood behind them.
**20** It came between the Egyptian camp and the Israelite camp; the cloud brought darkness on one side yet lit up the night on the other, so neither came near the other all night long.
**21** Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the LORD drove the sea back with a fierce east wind throughout the night, turning the sea into dry land; the waters were divided.
**22** So the Israelites walked into the middle of the sea on dry ground, with the waters forming a wall for them on their right and on their left.
**23** The Egyptians pursued and went in after them—all of Pharaoh's horses, his chariots, and his horsemen—into the midst of the sea.
**24** During the morning watch, the LORD looked down on the Egyptian camp from the pillar of fire and cloud, and threw the Egyptian camp into confusion.
**25** He clogged their chariot wheels so that they drove with difficulty. Then the Egyptians cried, "Let us flee from Israel, for the LORD is fighting for them against Egypt!"
**26** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea so that the waters may flow back over the Egyptians, over their chariots and their horsemen."
**27** So Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and as dawn broke, the sea returned to its normal depth while the Egyptians were fleeing straight toward it; thus the LORD swept the Egyptians into the midst of the sea.
**28** The returning waters covered the chariots and the horsemen, the entire army of Pharaoh that had entered the sea behind them; not even a single one of them survived.
**29** Yet the Israelites had walked on dry ground right through the middle of the sea, with the waters standing like a wall on their right and on their left.
**30** On that day the LORD rescued Israel from the hand of Egypt, and Israel saw the Egyptians lying dead upon the seashore.
**31** When Israel witnessed the mighty power that the LORD displayed against the Egyptians, the people revered the LORD, and they put their trust in the LORD and in his servant Moses.

## Everyday English

**1** The LORD told Moses,
**2** "Instruct the Israelites to turn back and set up camp near Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, right across from Baal-zephon. Camp there by the water.
**3** Pharaoh will assume the Israelites are lost in the countryside and trapped by the desert.
**4** I'll make Pharaoh stubborn again so he chases after them. Then I'll show my majesty by defeating Pharaoh and his whole army, and Egypt will know beyond a doubt that I am the LORD." So the Israelites followed those orders.
**5** When word reached Egypt's king that the people had escaped, Pharaoh and his advisors had a change of heart. "What have we done?" they asked. "Why did we let Israel go and lose our workforce?"
**6** So Pharaoh got his chariot ready and rallied his troops.
**7** He took six hundred of Egypt's finest chariots, along with every other chariot in the country, each manned by top officers.
**8** Because the LORD made King Pharaoh stubborn, he chased after the Israelites—even though they had marched out boldly and openly.
**9** Egypt's entire army—every chariot horse, rider, and soldier—pursued them and caught up as they camped along the shore near Pi-hahiroth, across from Baal-zephon.
**10** When Pharaoh got close, the Israelites looked up and spotted the Egyptian army closing in behind them. Panic-stricken, they cried out to the LORD for help.
**11** They turned on Moses and complained, "Were there no graves in Egypt? Is that why you dragged us out here to die in the desert? Why did you bring us out of Egypt at all?
**12** Didn't we warn you back in Egypt, 'Leave us alone and let us work for the Egyptians'? Better to be slaves in Egypt than corpses in the desert!"
**13** Moses answered the people, "Don't panic! Stand your ground and watch how the LORD will rescue you today. These Egyptians you're staring at right now—you'll never lay eyes on them again!
**14** The LORD himself will fight for you. All you have to do is stay calm."
**15** Then the LORD asked Moses, "Why are you crying out to me? Tell the Israelites to break camp and march!
**16** Raise your walking stick, hold your hand out over the sea, and split it apart so the Israelites can walk right through on dry ground.
**17** Meanwhile, I'm going to make the Egyptians stubborn enough to chase them in. I'll display my glory at the expense of Pharaoh and his entire force—chariots and riders alike.
**18** Then Egypt will learn that I am the LORD when I win honor over Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen."
**19** At that, God's messenger, who had been traveling ahead of Israel's camp, moved behind them, and the pillar of cloud shifted from the front to the rear.
**20** It settled right between Egypt's camp and Israel's camp. On the Egyptian side the cloud cast pitch darkness, while on Israel's side it lit up the night, keeping the two camps apart until morning.
**21** Moses stretched his hand out over the sea, and all night long the LORD pushed the water back with a roaring east wind. He turned the seabed into dry land, and the water split open.
**22** The Israelites marched straight into the sea on dry ground, with a towering wall of water on their right and another on their left.
**23** The Egyptians charged in right behind them—every last chariot, horse, and rider of Pharaoh's army—straight into the middle of the sea.
**24** Just before dawn, the LORD looked down from the pillar of fire and cloud at the Egyptian forces and threw their ranks into total chaos.
**25** He jammed their chariot wheels so they could barely roll forward. The Egyptians shouted, "Run away from Israel! The LORD is fighting on their side against Egypt!"
**26** Then the LORD told Moses, "Hold your hand out over the sea again so the water crashes back down on the Egyptians, their chariots, and their riders."
**27** Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and just as morning broke, the water surged back to full depth while the Egyptians fled straight into it. The LORD swept them right into the churning sea.
**28** The waves came crashing back and swallowed up every chariot, rider, and soldier in Pharaoh's army who had followed Israel into the sea—not a single survivor was left.
**29** Meanwhile, the Israelites had walked across on dry ground right down the middle of the sea, with walls of water flanking them on both sides.
**30** That day the LORD saved Israel from Egypt's grip, and the Israelites saw Egyptian bodies washed up dead along the shore.
**31** When Israel saw the awesome power the LORD had unleashed against Egypt, the people stood in awe of the LORD and placed their trust in him and in his servant Moses.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `וְחִזַּקְתִּי` (*ve-chizzaqti*):** Piel of `חָזַק` (*chazaq*), "make firm/strong/unyielding"—one of three verbs Exodus uses for heart-hardening (alongside `כָּבֵד` *kaved*, "make heavy," and `קָשָׁה` *qashah*, "make stiff/hard").
- **v. 4 `וְאִכָּבְדָה` (*ve-ikkavdah*):** Niphal cohortative of `כָּבֵד` (*kavad*), "get weight/honor/glory for myself," echoing `כָּבוֹד` (*kavod*, glory) and contrasting ironically with Pharaoh's "heavy" heart.
- **v. 8 `בְּיָד רָמָה` (*be-yad ramah*):** Literally "with an uplifted/high hand," an idiom for open, unashamed boldness or defiance.
- **v. 13 `יְשׁוּעַת יְהוָה` (*yeshu'at YHWH*):** "Deliverance/salvation of YHWH," echoed immediately in the Song of the Sea (15:2).
- **v. 21 `רוּחַ קָדִים עַזָּה` (*ruach qadim azzah*):** "A fierce east wind"; `רוּחַ` (*ruach*) spans "wind," "breath," and "Spirit," recalling the wind/Spirit over the primordial waters in Gen 1:2 and 8:1.
- **v. 25 `וַיָּסַר` (*vayyasar*) & `בִּכְבֵדֻת` (*bi-khvedut*):** "He removed/jammed" the chariot wheels and drove them "with heaviness" (from *kaved*, "heavy"), another ironic play on Pharaoh's heavy-hearted stubbornness.
- **v. 27 `לְאֵיתָנוֹ` (*le-etano*):** "To its constant/perennial flow" or full strength—returning from dry land to deep channel.

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
**2** “Speak to the children of Israel, that they turn back and encamp before Pihahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, before Baal Zephon. You shall encamp opposite it by the sea.
**3** Pharaoh will say of the children of Israel, ‘They are entangled in the land. The wilderness has shut them in.’
**4** I will harden Pharaoh’s heart, and he will follow after them; and I will get honor over Pharaoh, and over all his armies; and the Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD.” They did so.
**5** The king of Egypt was told that the people had fled; and the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants was changed toward the people, and they said, “What is this we have done, that we have let Israel go from serving us?”
**6** He prepared his chariot, and took his army with him;
**7** and he took six hundred chosen chariots, and all the chariots of Egypt, with captains over all of them.
**8** The LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursued the children of Israel; for the children of Israel went out with a high hand.
**9** The Egyptians pursued them. All the horses and chariots of Pharaoh, his horsemen, and his army overtook them encamping by the sea, beside Pihahiroth, before Baal Zephon.
**10** When Pharaoh came near, the children of Israel lifted up their eyes, and behold, the Egyptians were marching after them; and they were very afraid. The children of Israel cried out to the LORD.
**11** They said to Moses, “Because there were no graves in Egypt, have you taken us away to die in the wilderness? Why have you treated us this way, to bring us out of Egypt?
**12** Isn’t this the word that we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, ‘Leave us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians’? For it would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in the wilderness.”
**13** Moses said to the people, “Don’t be afraid. Stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD, which he will work for you today; for you will never again see the Egyptians whom you have seen today.
**14** The LORD will fight for you, and you shall be still.”
**15** The LORD said to Moses, “Why do you cry to me? Speak to the children of Israel, that they go forward.
**16** Lift up your rod, and stretch out your hand over the sea and divide it. Then the children of Israel shall go into the middle of the sea on dry ground.
**17** Behold, I myself will harden the hearts of the Egyptians, and they will go in after them. I will get myself honor over Pharaoh, and over all his armies, over his chariots, and over his horsemen.
**18** The Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD when I have gotten myself honor over Pharaoh, over his chariots, and over his horsemen.”
**19** The angel of God, who went before the camp of Israel, moved and went behind them; and the pillar of cloud moved from before them, and stood behind them.
**20** It came between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel. There was the cloud and the darkness, yet it gave light by night. One didn’t come near the other all night.
**21** Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the LORD caused the sea to go back by a strong east wind all night, and made the sea dry land, and the waters were divided.
**22** The children of Israel went into the middle of the sea on the dry ground; and the waters were a wall to them on their right hand and on their left.
**23** The Egyptians pursued, and went in after them into the middle of the sea: all of Pharaoh’s horses, his chariots, and his horsemen.
**24** In the morning watch, the LORD looked out on the Egyptian army through the pillar of fire and of cloud, and confused the Egyptian army.
**25** He took off their chariot wheels, and they drove them heavily; so that the Egyptians said, “Let’s flee from the face of Israel, for the LORD fights for them against the Egyptians!”
**26** The LORD said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the waters may come again on the Egyptians, on their chariots, and on their horsemen.”
**27** Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the sea returned to its strength when the morning appeared; and the Egyptians fled against it. The LORD overthrew the Egyptians in the middle of the sea.
**28** The waters returned, and covered the chariots and the horsemen, even all Pharaoh’s army that went in after them into the sea. There remained not so much as one of them.
**29** But the children of Israel walked on dry land in the middle of the sea, and the waters were a wall to them on their right hand and on their left.
**30** Thus the LORD saved Israel that day out of the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the seashore.
**31** Israel saw the great work which the LORD did to the Egyptians, and the people feared the LORD; and they believed in the LORD and in his servant Moses.
