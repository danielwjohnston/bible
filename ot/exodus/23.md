## Clear & Faithful

**1** "You shall not spread a false report. Do not join hands with a wicked person to be a malicious witness.
**2** You shall not follow a crowd to do evil, nor shall you testify in a lawsuit so as to side with the majority and pervert justice.
**3** Neither shall you show partiality to a poor person in his lawsuit.
**4** When you come upon your enemy's ox or donkey wandering astray, you shall surely bring it back to him.
**5** When you see the donkey of someone who hates you collapsed under its load, you shall not walk past and leave it to him; you must surely help him lift it up.
**6** You shall not pervert the justice due to the needy among you in their lawsuit.
**7** Keep far away from any false charge, and do not put the innocent and righteous to death, for I will never acquit the guilty.
**8** You shall take no bribe, for a bribe blinds the clear-sighted and twists the words of the righteous.
**9** You shall not oppress a sojourner; you yourselves know the heart of a sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.
**10** For six years you shall sow your land and gather its harvest,
**11** but in the seventh year you shall let it rest and lie fallow, so that the needy of your people may eat, and what they leave behind the wild animals may eat. You shall do the same with your vineyard and your olive grove.
**12** Six days you shall do your work, but on the seventh day you shall cease from labor, so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your female servant and the sojourner may be refreshed.
**13** Pay careful attention to everything I have told you. Make no mention of the names of other gods; do not let them be heard on your lips.
**14** Three times a year you shall celebrate a festival to me.
**15** You shall keep the Festival of Unleavened Bread: for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of Abib, for in it you came out of Egypt. None shall appear before me empty-handed.
**16** You shall also keep the Festival of Harvest with the firstfruits of your labor that you sow in the field, and the Festival of Ingathering at the end of the year, when you gather in the fruit of your labor from the field.
**17** Three times a year all your males shall appear before the Lord GOD.
**18** You shall not offer the blood of my sacrifice with anything leavened, nor shall the fat of my festival sacrifice remain overnight until morning.
**19** You shall bring the very best of the firstfruits of your soil into the house of the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.
**20** Behold, I am sending an angel before you to guard you along the way and to bring you into the place that I have prepared.
**21** Be attentive to him and obey his voice; do not rebel against him, for he will not pardon your transgression, since my name is in him.
**22** But if you faithfully obey his voice and do everything that I speak, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and an adversary to your adversaries.
**23** When my angel goes ahead of you and brings you into the land of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I blot them out,
**24** you shall not bow down to their gods or serve them, nor follow their practices; rather, you shall utterly demolish them and smash their sacred pillars to pieces.
**25** Serve the LORD your God, and he will bless your bread and your water; and I will take sickness away from your midst.
**26** No woman will miscarry or be barren in your land; I will fulfill the full number of your days.
**27** I will send my terror ahead of you and throw into confusion all the peoples among whom you come, and I will make all your enemies turn their backs to you in flight.
**28** I will send the hornet ahead of you to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites from before you.
**29** I will not drive them out before you in a single year, lest the land become desolate and the wild beasts multiply against you.
**30** Little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you increase and take possession of the land.
**31** I will set your borders from the Sea of Reeds to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the Euphrates River; for I will hand over to you the inhabitants of the land, and you shall drive them out before you.
**32** You shall make no covenant with them or with their gods.
**33** They shall not live in your land, lest they cause you to sin against me; for if you serve their gods, it will surely become a snare to you."

## Everyday English

**1** "Never spread malicious rumors, and don't team up with a crooked person to give dishonest testimony.
**2** Don't go along with the crowd to do wrong. When you testify in court, don't bend the truth just to side with popular opinion.
**3** And don't favor someone in a lawsuit simply because they're poor either!
**4** If you run across your enemy's stray ox or donkey wandering loose, make sure you return it to him.
**5** Even if you spot your worst enemy's donkey buckled under its load, don't walk away and leave him stuck—pitch in and help him unload it!
**6** Never cheat poor people out of fair justice when they bring a case to court.
**7** Steer completely clear of false accusations. Never execute an innocent, law-abiding person, because I won't let the guilty off the hook.
**8** Refuse every bribe—bribes blind even sharp-eyed judges and twist the truth of honest people.
**9** Don't mistreat foreigners living among you. You know firsthand what it feels like to be an outsider, since you lived as foreigners in Egypt!
**10** Plant and harvest your fields for six years straight,
**11** then let the land rest unplanted every seventh year so poor people among you can eat whatever grows wild—and let wild animals eat whatever is left. Do the exact same thing with your vineyards and olive groves.
**12** Work six days a week, and take a complete break on the seventh day so your ox and donkey can rest, and your hired workers' children and resident foreigners can catch their breath.
**13** Follow every single instruction I've given you carefully. Never invoke the names of other gods—don't even let their names cross your lips!
**14** Celebrate three pilgrimage festivals in my honor every year:
**15** First, celebrate the Festival of Unleavened Bread—eat flatbread without yeast for seven days, just as I ordered, during the set season in the month of Abib, because that's the month you marched out of Egypt. Nobody should show up before me empty-handed!
**16** Second, celebrate the Harvest Festival with the earliest crops from the fields you planted. Third, celebrate the Festival of Final Gathering at the close of the agricultural year, once you've brought in all your crops from the fields.
**17** Three times a year every Israelite man must appear in person before the Sovereign LORD.
**18** Never offer the blood of my sacrifice alongside anything made with yeast, and don't leave the fat from my festival offering sitting out until morning.
**19** Bring the very finest firstfruits of your soil to the house of the LORD your God. And never boil a young goat in its mother's milk.
**20** Look, I'm sending my messenger ahead of you to protect you along the road and bring you safely to the home I've prepared.
**21** Pay close attention to him and obey every word he says. Don't defy him, because he won't excuse your rebellion—my own authority and name reside in him!
**22** But if you listen carefully to him and do everything I command, I'll stand against your enemies and oppose everyone who fights you.
**23** My messenger will march ahead of you and lead you into the territory of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will wipe them out.
**24** Never bow down to their gods, worship them, or imitate their practices. Instead, smash their shrines completely and shatter their sacred stone pillars!
**25** Worship only the LORD your God, and he'll bless your food and water and drive sickness far away from you.
**26** Not a single woman in your land will suffer miscarriage or infertility, and I'll grant you full, long lives.
**27** I'll send panic out ahead of you to throw every nation you meet into chaos, and I'll force all your enemies to turn tail and flee.
**28** I'll send terror like a swarm of hornets ahead of you to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites before you arrive.
**29** I won't clear them all out in a single year, though, or the countryside would sit empty and wild beasts would overrun you.
**30** Instead, I'll push them out bit by bit until your population grows large enough to fill and inherit the land.
**31** I'll establish your borders from the Sea of Reeds to the Mediterranean Sea, and from the southern desert up to the Euphrates River. I'll hand the people living there over to you, and you will drive them out ahead of you.
**32** Never sign a treaty with them or with their gods.
**33** Don't let them stay inside your country, or they'll lure you into sinning against me—worshiping their gods would trap you in ruin!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `וְדָל לֹא תֶהְדַּר` (*ve-dal lo tehdar*):** "And a poor person you shall not favor/defer to"—balancing v. 6 (`אֶבְיֹנְךָ` "your needy") so justice neither skews toward the wealthy nor tilts artificially out of pity.
- **v. 5 `עָזֹב תַּעֲזֹב` (*azov ta'azov*):** Uses two distinct roots/senses of `עָזַב` (*azav*): first "leave/abandon" (`מֵעֲזֹב`), then "assist/release/unbind" (`עָזֹב תַּעֲזֹב עִמּוֹ`, "help him untie/lift it").
- **v. 11 `תִּשְׁמְטֶנָּה` (*tishmetennah*):** From `שָׁמַט` (*shamat*, "release/let drop"), technical term for the seventh-year *shemittah* fallow release (cf. Deut 15:1–3).
- **v. 12 `וְיִנָּפֵשׁ` (*veyinnafesh*):** Niphal of `נָפַשׁ` (*nafash*, linked to `נֶפֶשׁ` *nefesh*, "soul/breath")—"catch one's breath / be refreshed," the very verb used of God resting on the seventh day in Exod 31:17.
- **v. 14 `רְגָלִים` (*regalim*):** Literally "feet / times," giving the three annual "pilgrimage festivals" (`חָג` *chag* = festival procession/gathering) in vv. 15–16: Unleavened Bread (`מַצּוֹת`), Harvest (`קָצִיר`), and Ingathering (`אָסִף`).
- **v. 17 `הָאָדֹן יְהוָה` (*ha-Adon YHWH*):** "The Lord/Sovereign YHWH," emphasizing covenant overlordship at the three annual convocations.
- **v. 21 `שְׁמִי בְּקִרְבּוֹ` (*shemi beqirbo*):** "My name is within him"—identifying this `מַלְאָךְ` (*mal'akh*, messenger/angel) as bearing YHWH's own authority and presence (cf. 14:19; 33:14–15).
- **v. 28 `הַצִּרְעָה` (*ha-tsir'ah*):** "The hornet" (literal stinging swarms or a poetic metaphor for divine panic/Egyptian royal emblem, cf. Deut 7:20; Josh 24:12).
