## Clear & Faithful

**1** Now the LORD declared to Moses, "I am about to bring one final plague upon Pharaoh and Egypt. Afterward he will release you from here—indeed, when he lets you go, he will drive you completely out of this place.
**2** Instruct the people so that every man asks his neighbor and every woman asks her neighbor for articles of silver and gold."
**3** And the LORD granted the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians; furthermore, the man Moses himself was held in tremendous esteem throughout Egypt by Pharaoh's officials and the general populace alike.
**4** So Moses announced, "Thus says the LORD: 'Around midnight I will go forth through the midst of Egypt,
**5** and every firstborn in the land of Egypt shall die—from the firstborn of Pharaoh seated on his throne down to the firstborn of the female slave behind the millstones, along with every firstborn among the livestock.
**6** Then an agonizing cry will rise up across the whole land of Egypt, unlike anything that has ever happened or ever will happen again.
**7** Yet among all the Israelites not even a dog will bare its teeth against person or beast, so that you may recognize how decisively the LORD distinguishes between Egypt and Israel.'
**8** Then all these officials of yours will come bowing down to me and plead, 'Leave—both you and all the people following behind you!' Only after that will I depart." Burning with indignation, Moses walked out from Pharaoh's presence.
**9** Earlier the LORD had told Moses, "Pharaoh will refuse to heed you, so that my wonders may multiply throughout the land of Egypt."
**10** Moses and Aaron performed all these wonders before Pharaoh, yet the LORD stiffened Pharaoh's heart, and he would not release the Israelites from his country.

## Everyday English

**1** Then the LORD privately told Moses, "I'm hitting Pharaoh and Egypt with one last devastating blow. Once it lands, he won't merely let you leave—he'll shove every single one of you right out of the country!
**2** Tell the Israelites ahead of time that each man and woman should ask their Egyptian neighbors for silver and gold jewelry."
**3** And the LORD made the Egyptians look kindly on the Israelites. On top of that, Moses himself had earned enormous respect across Egypt from Pharaoh's court officials and ordinary citizens alike.
**4** Still standing in Pharaoh's court, Moses delivered this final warning: "Here is what the LORD declares: 'At midnight I myself am walking straight through Egypt!
**5** Every oldest son in the nation will die—from the crown prince heir to Pharaoh's throne all the way down to the son of the lowest slave girl grinding grain at the handmill—plus every firstborn animal.
**6** A scream of grief will erupt across all of Egypt, worse than anything history has ever heard or will ever hear again.
**7** Meanwhile, over on the Israelite side, not a single dog will even bark at a person or an animal! That way everyone will see for sure that the LORD treats Israel differently from Egypt.'
**8** And mark my words: every one of these royal advisors of yours will come groveling at my feet, begging, 'Get out of here—you and all your followers!' Only then will I march out!" Furious, Moses stormed out of Pharaoh's palace.
**9** Now the LORD had already explained to Moses, "Pharaoh won't listen to a word you say, so that my astonishing miracles keep piling up across Egypt."
**10** Moses and Aaron carried out every one of those wonders right in front of Pharaoh, yet the LORD kept Pharaoh's heart unyielding, and he wouldn't let the Israelites leave his nation.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `נֶגַע` (*nega*) & `גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ` (*garesh yegaresh*):** *Nega* means "blow/stroke/plague" (anticipating `לִנְגֹּף` in 12:23); the emphatic infinitive absolute *garesh yegaresh* ("driving out he will drive out") underscores that Egypt will actively expel Israel rather than reluctantly grant leave.
- **v. 4 `כַּחֲצֹת הַלַּיְלָה` (*kachatsot ha-laylah*):** Literally "about the dividing/midpoint of the night"; here YHWH announces his own personal march through Egypt, contrasting with the *mashchit* ("destroyer") mentioned in 12:23.
- **v. 7 `לֹא יֶחֱרַץ־כֶּלֶב לְשֹׁנוֹ` (*lo yecherats-kelev leshono*):** Idiom literally meaning "not a dog will sharpen/move its tongue" (cf. Josh 10:21), conveying absolute peace and zero threat amid Egypt's terror.
- **v. 8 `אֲשֶׁר־בְּרַגְלֶיךָ` (*asher-beraglekha*):** Literally "who are at your feet," an idiom for dependents or followers marching in a leader's train (cf. Judg 4:10).
- **v. 10 `וַיְחַזֵּק` (*vayyechazzeq*):** Summary note using `חָזַק` (*chazaq*, "made strong/firm/rigid") to close the nine-plague cycle before the Passover night of Exodus 12.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Joshua 10:21** (same silence idiom): *Joshua uses the identical phrase—no one moved a tongue against the Israelites—to describe total protection during victory.*
- **Exodus 3:21–22; Psalm 105:37** (leaving with silver and gold): *The Israelites' request for silver and gold fulfills the promise at the burning bush that they would depart not empty-handed, but laden with Egypt's wealth.*
- **1 Thessalonians 5:2; Matthew 25:6** (midnight arrival): *The motif of the Lord arriving suddenly at midnight echoes the decisive hour announced in Exod 11:4.*

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** The LORD said to Moses, “I will bring yet one more plague on Pharaoh, and on Egypt; afterwards he will let you go. When he lets you go, he will surely thrust you out altogether.
**2** Speak now in the ears of the people, and let every man ask of his neighbor, and every woman of her neighbor, jewels of silver, and jewels of gold.”
**3** The LORD gave the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians. Moreover, the man Moses was very great in the land of Egypt, in the sight of Pharaoh’s servants, and in the sight of the people.
**4** Moses said, “This is what the LORD says: ‘About midnight I will go out into the middle of Egypt,
**5** and all the firstborn in the land of Egypt shall die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sits on his throne, even to the firstborn of the female servant who is behind the mill, and all the firstborn of livestock.
**6** There will be a great cry throughout all the land of Egypt, such as there has not been, nor will be any more.
**7** But against any of the children of Israel a dog won’t even bark or move its tongue, against man or animal, that you may know that the LORD makes a distinction between the Egyptians and Israel.
**8** All these servants of yours will come down to me, and bow down themselves to me, saying, “Get out, with all the people who follow you;” and after that I will go out.’” He went out from Pharaoh in hot anger.
**9** The LORD said to Moses, “Pharaoh won’t listen to you, that my wonders may be multiplied in the land of Egypt.”
**10** Moses and Aaron did all these wonders before Pharaoh, but the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he didn’t let the children of Israel go out of his land.
