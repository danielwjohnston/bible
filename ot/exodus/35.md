## Clear & Faithful

**1** Moses assembled the whole congregation of the Israelites and said to them, "These are the things that the LORD has commanded you to carry out:
**2** For six days work may be done, but on the seventh day you shall have a holy day, a Sabbath of solemn rest to the LORD; whoever performs any work on it shall be put to death.
**3** You shall not kindle a fire in any of your dwellings on the Sabbath day."
**4** Then Moses spoke to the whole congregation of the Israelites, saying, "This is the word that the LORD has commanded:
**5** 'Take from among yourselves a sacred contribution for the LORD; let everyone whose heart is willing bring the LORD's contribution: gold, silver, and bronze;
**6** blue, purple, and crimson yarn, fine linen, and goats' hair;
**7** tanned rams' skins, fine leather skins, and acacia wood;
**8** oil for the lampstand, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;
**9** and onyx stones and gemstones for setting in the ephod and the breastpiece.
**10** Let every wise-hearted artisan among you come and make everything that the LORD has commanded:
**11** the tabernacle, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its frames, its crossbars, its pillars, and its socket-bases;
**12** the ark and its poles, the atonement cover, and the screening veil;
**13** the table and its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;
**14** the lampstand for the light, its utensils and its lamps, and the oil for the light;
**15** the altar of incense and its poles, the anointing oil and the fragrant incense, and the entrance screen for the doorway of the tabernacle;
**16** the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils, along with the basin and its stand;
**17** the courtyard hangings, its pillars and its socket-bases, and the screen for the courtyard gate;
**18** the tent pegs for the tabernacle, the tent pegs for the courtyard, and their cords;
**19** and the woven service garments for ministering in the holy place—the sacred vestments for Aaron the priest and the vestments for his sons to serve as priests.'"
**20** Then the entire congregation of the Israelites withdrew from Moses' presence.
**21** And everyone whose heart stirred him and everyone whose spirit moved him came and brought the LORD's contribution for the work on the tent of meeting, for all its service, and for the sacred garments.
**22** Men and women alike came—everyone with a willing heart—bringing brooches, earrings, signet rings, and ornaments, all kinds of gold articles, each one waving an offering of gold to the LORD.
**23** Everyone who possessed blue, purple, or crimson yarn, fine linen, goats' hair, tanned rams' skins, or fine leather skins brought them.
**24** Everyone who offered a contribution of silver or bronze brought the LORD's contribution, and everyone who possessed acacia wood for any part of the service brought it.
**25** Every skilled woman spun with her own hands and brought what she had spun: blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and fine linen.
**26** And all the women whose hearts moved them in skill spun the goats' hair.
**27** The leaders brought onyx stones and stones for setting in the ephod and the breastpiece,
**28** as well as the spice and the oil for the light, for the anointing oil, and for the fragrant incense.
**29** Every Israelite man and woman whose heart moved them to contribute toward all the work that the LORD had commanded through Moses brought a freewill offering to the LORD.
**30** Then Moses told the people of Israel, "See, the LORD has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.
**31** He has filled him with the Spirit of God—with wisdom, discernment, knowledge, and every kind of craftsmanship—
**32** to devise artistic designs, to work in gold, silver, and bronze,
**33** in cutting stones for mounting and in carving wood, to perform every kind of ingenious craft.
**34** Moreover, he has placed in his heart the ability to teach—both him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.
**35** He has filled them with skill of heart to carry out every craft of an engraver, a master designer, an embroiderer in blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, and a weaver—artisans of every craft and creators of intricate designs."

## Everyday English

**1** Moses called the entire Israelite community together and told them, "Here are the instructions the LORD ordered you to follow:
**2** You may do your work for six days, but the seventh day must be sacred to you—a complete Sabbath of total rest honoring the LORD. Anyone who works on that day must be executed.
**3** Don't even light a cooking or heating fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day."
**4** Next, Moses passed along another message to the whole Israelite assembly: "Here is what the LORD commanded:
**5** 'Collect a special offering for the LORD from what you own. Everyone who feels moved to give generously should bring a gift to the LORD: gold, silver, and bronze;
**6** blue, purple, and bright red yarn, fine linen, and goat hair;
**7** red-dyed ram hides, durable fine-grained hides, and acacia lumber;
**8** olive oil for the lamps, plus spices for the anointing oil and fragrant incense;
**9** and onyx gems along with other precious stones to mount on the priestly vest and the decision pouch.
**10** Every talented craftsperson among you should step up and build everything the LORD has ordered:
**11** the sacred tent along with its outer tent cover and weatherproof tarp, its fasteners, wall frames, crossbars, posts, and bases;
**12** the covenant chest with its carrying poles, the gold lid of atonement, and the inner divider curtain;
**13** the table with its carrying poles, all its dishes, and the sacred bread of presence;
**14** the lampstand for giving light, with its tools, lamps, and lamp oil;
**15** the incense altar with its poles, the anointing oil, the sweet incense, and the entrance curtain for the sacred tent doorway;
**16** the burnt-offering altar with its bronze grate, carrying poles, and all its tools, plus the washbasin and its pedestal;
**17** the courtyard curtains, posts, and bases, along with the curtain for the courtyard gate;
**18** the tent stakes and ropes for both the sacred tent and the courtyard;
**19** and the finely woven cloths for sanctuary service, plus the sacred clothes for Aaron the priest and the outfits for his sons to serve as priests.'"
**20** So the entire Israelite community headed back to their tents from Moses' presence.
**21** Soon, everyone whose heart felt stirred and whose spirit moved them generously returned, bringing their gifts to the LORD for building the meeting tent, for all its worship gear, and for the holy garments.
**22** Men and women streamed in together—anyone with a willing heart—handing over brooches, earrings, signet rings, necklaces, and every sort of gold jewelry, each waving their gold gift in dedication to the LORD.
**23** Those who owned blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, fine linen, goat hair, red-dyed ram hides, or tough fine hides donated them.
**24** Whoever had silver or bronze brought it as a gift to the LORD, and anyone who had acacia timber suitable for the construction work donated that too.
**25** Talented women spun yarn by hand and brought in what they had spun—blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, along with fine linen.
**26** Other gifted women who felt inspired spun the goat hair into thread.
**27** The tribal leaders contributed the onyx gems and the other gemstones to set on the priestly vest and decision pouch,
**28** as well as the spices and olive oil needed for the lamps, the anointing oil, and the fragrant incense.
**29** Every Israelite man and woman who wanted to give willingly brought a freewill gift to the LORD for all the projects the LORD had ordered through Moses.
**30** Then Moses announced to the Israelites, "Look! The LORD has personally chosen Bezalel—son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from Judah's tribe—
**31** and filled him with God's Spirit, giving him extraordinary wisdom, insight, know-how, and skill in every kind of craft:
**32** designing artwork, working in gold, silver, and bronze,
**33** cutting and setting gemstones, carving wood, and mastering every type of fine design.
**34** God has also given both Bezalel and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, the ability to teach others.
**35** He has filled them with brilliant skill to handle every craft—gem engraving, artistic designing, embroidering with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn on fine linen, and weaving—true masters of every trade and creative design."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן` (*shabbat shabbaton*):** "Sabbath of complete rest"—placed immediately ahead of the tabernacle construction appeal (vv. 4–29) to establish that even sacred building work halts on the seventh day, alongside the explicit ban on kindling fire (v. 3).
- **v. 5 `נְדִיב לִבּוֹ` (*nediv libbo*):** "Everyone willing of heart" (paired with `נְדָבָה` *nedavah* "freewill offering" in v. 29 and `נְשָׂאוֹ לִבּוֹ` "whose heart lifted him" in v. 21)—contrasting sharply with the coerced gold collection for the golden calf (32:2–3).
- **v. 7 `תְּחָשִׁים` (*techashim*):** WLC preserves the Leningrad spelling `תְּחָשִׁים` (flagged by OSIS note `n="1"` in `Exod.xml`), denoting a durable outer hide or fine-grained leather (variously identified with dugong/sea-cow skin, badger/dolphin hide, or Egyptian *tchs* beaded/dyed leather).
- **v. 11 `בְּרִיחָו` (*berichav*):** WLC follows Codex Leningradensis in printing defective `בְּרִיחָו` without adding a Ketiv/Qere apparatus (marked with OSIS note `n="q"` in `Exod.xml`), contrasting with `39:33` where `בריחו` / `בְּרִיחָיו` carries an explicit Ketiv/Qere variant.
- **v. 13 `לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים` (*lechem ha-panim*):** Literally "bread of the faces/presence"—the twelve loaves arranged continuously on the golden table inside the Holy Place before the face of YHWH.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 25:1–9 & 31:1–17** (command and Sabbath frame): *Moses relays to the congregation the materials list and Sabbath mandate previously given to him on Mount Sinai, now reversing the order so Sabbath precedes construction.*
- **1 Chronicles 29:1–9** (freewill generosity for the temple): *David and Israel's leaders bring voluntary gold, silver, bronze, and precious stones with willing hearts, echoing Exodus 35:21–29.*
- **2 Corinthians 8:12 & 9:7** (cheerful, willing giving): *Paul commends giving cheerfully from what one has with a willing heart rather than under compulsion, reflecting the ethos of Exodus 35:5, 21, 29.*
- **Numbers 15:32–36** (Sabbath firewood case): *Illustrates the enforcement of the Sabbath work and kindling prohibition in Exodus 35:2–3.*
