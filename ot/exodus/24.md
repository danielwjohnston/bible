## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Come up to the LORD—you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel—and worship from a distance.
**2** Moses alone shall approach the LORD, but the others shall not come near, nor shall the people come up with him."
**3** Moses came and told the people all the words of the LORD and all the ordinances; and all the people answered with one voice and said, "All the words that the LORD has spoken we will do."
**4** So Moses wrote down all the words of the LORD. Rising early in the morning, he built an altar at the foot of the mountain and set up twelve stone pillars corresponding to the twelve tribes of Israel.
**5** He sent young men of the Israelites, who offered burnt offerings and sacrificed bulls as peace offerings to the LORD.
**6** Moses took half of the blood and put it in basins, and the other half of the blood he splashed against the altar.
**7** Then he took the Book of the Covenant and read it in the hearing of the people; and they said, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do, and we will obey."
**8** Moses took the blood and splashed it on the people, saying, "Behold the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you in accordance with all these words."
**9** Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up,
**10** and they saw the God of Israel. Beneath his feet there was something like a pavement of sapphire stone, as clear as the very heavens.
**11** Yet he did not lay his hand against the nobles of the Israelites; they beheld God, and they ate and drank.
**12** The LORD said to Moses, "Come up to me on the mountain and stay there, and I will give you the tablets of stone, along with the law and the commandment that I have written to instruct them."
**13** So Moses set out with his assistant Joshua, and Moses went up onto the mountain of God.
**14** To the elders he said, "Wait here for us until we return to you. Look, Aaron and Hur are with you; whoever has a dispute may bring it to them."
**15** When Moses went up on the mountain, the cloud covered the mountain.
**16** The glory of the LORD settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days; on the seventh day he called to Moses from the midst of the cloud.
**17** To the eyes of the Israelites, the appearance of the glory of the LORD was like a devouring fire on the top of the mountain.
**18** Moses entered into the midst of the cloud as he went up the mountain, and Moses remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Everyday English

**1** Next, the LORD told Moses, "Climb up the mountain to meet me—bring Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel's elders along—and bow in worship from a distance.
**2** Only Moses may step close to the LORD; the rest must stay back, and none of the ordinary people may climb up with him."
**3** Moses went down and reported to the people every word and legal rule the LORD had given him. Everyone answered together with one voice, "We will carry out everything the LORD has commanded!"
**4** Moses wrote down all the LORD's words. Early the next morning, he built an altar at the base of the mountain and set up twelve standing stones, one for each of Israel's twelve tribes.
**5** Then he sent young Israelite men to sacrifice young bulls as burnt offerings and fellowship sacrifices to the LORD.
**6** Moses poured half of the blood into bowls and splashed the other half onto the altar.
**7** After that, he picked up the Scroll of the Covenant and read it aloud to the crowd. Once again they answered, "We will obey and put into practice everything the LORD has said!"
**8** Then Moses sprinkled the remaining blood over the people and declared, "Look—this is the blood that seals the covenant the LORD has made with you, based on all these terms!"
**9** Afterwards, Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and the seventy Israelite elders climbed up the mountain,
**10** and there they saw the God of Israel! Under his feet lay what looked like a gleaming floor of deep-blue sapphire, as pure and bright as the open sky.
**11** Even though these Israelite leaders looked directly at God, he didn't strike them down—they gazed on God and shared a celebratory meal, eating and drinking together.
**12** Later the LORD told Moses, "Climb higher up the mountain to me and wait there, and I will hand you the stone tablets containing the teachings and commands I have written down to instruct the people."
**13** So Moses got up with his young aide Joshua and climbed up God's mountain.
**14** Before leaving, he instructed the elders, "Wait right here for us until we get back. Aaron and Hur are staying with you—anyone with a legal dispute can take it to them."
**15** As Moses climbed up the mountain, the cloud wrapped all around it.
**16** The radiant glory of the LORD rested on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered the peak for six days. On the seventh day, God called out to Moses from inside the cloud.
**17** To the Israelites watching from below, the LORD's glory looked like a blazing, all-consuming fire on the mountaintop.
**18** Moses walked straight into the cloud as he climbed higher up the mountain, and he stayed up on the mountain for forty days and forty nights.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `מַצֵּבָה` (*matsevah*):** Uncarved memorial stone pillar representing the twelve tribes in covenant ratification—permitted here as a witness marker, in contrast to Canaanite cultic pillars (`23:24`) which had to be shattered.
- **v. 7 `סֵפֶר הַבְּרִית` (*sefer ha-berit*):** "The scroll/book of the covenant" (likely Exod 20:2–23:33), answered by the classic pledge `נַעֲשֶׂה וְנִשְׁמָע` (*na'aseh venishma*, "we will do and we will hear/obey").
- **v. 8 `דַּם־הַבְּרִית` (*dam ha-berit*):** "The blood of the covenant" (`כָּרַת` *karat*, literally "cut" a covenant), binding altar (YHWH's side) and people together through shared sacrificial blood.
- **v. 10 `לִבְנַת הַסַּפִּיר` (*livnat ha-sappir*):** "Pavement/brickwork of sapphire" (ancient lapis lazuli—deep azure speckled with gold pyrites, evoking the starry sky).
- **v. 11 `אֲצִילֵי` (*atsile*):** Rare plural noun meaning "nobles/leaders/distinguished representatives" of Israel, sharing a covenant meal (`וַיֹּאכְלוּ וַיִּשְׁתּוּ`) in God's presence without harm.
- **v. 16 `וַיִּשְׁכֹּן כְּבוֹד־יְהוָה` (*vayyishkon kevod-YHWH*):** "The glory of YHWH dwelt/settled"—using `שָׁכַן` (*shakhan*), the very verb root behind `מִשְׁכָּן` (*mishkan*, "tabernacle/dwelling," `25:9`).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions
