## Clear & Faithful

**1** Meanwhile Moses was tending the flock of his father-in-law Jethro, the priest of Midian. Leading the flock to the far side of the wilderness, he came to Horeb, the mountain of God.
**2** There the angel of the LORD appeared to him in a blazing fire from the heart of a bush. Moses looked, and although the bush was engulfed in flames, it was not burning up.
**3** "I must turn aside," Moses said to himself, "and examine this extraordinary sight—why does the bush not burn away?"
**4** When the LORD saw that he had turned aside to look, God called to him from inside the bush: "Moses! Moses!" "Here I am," he replied.
**5** God said, "Do not come any closer. Take your sandals off your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy ground."
**6** Then he said, "I am the God of your father—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." At that, Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look at God.
**7** The LORD continued, "I have surely seen the misery of my people in Egypt, and I have heard them crying out under their slave drivers; indeed, I know their sufferings.
**8** So I have come down to rescue them from the power of Egypt and bring them up out of that land into a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey—the territory of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.
**9** Now the cry of the Israelites has reached me, and I have also seen how harshly the Egyptians are oppressing them.
**10** Go now—I am sending you to Pharaoh to bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt."
**11** But Moses asked God, "Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh and lead the Israelites out of Egypt?"
**12** God answered, "Certainly I will be with you! And this shall be the sign for you that I myself have sent you: once you have brought the people out of Egypt, you will worship God on this very mountain."
**13** Moses said to God, "Suppose I go to the Israelites and tell them, 'The God of your ancestors has sent me to you,' and they ask me, 'What is his name?' What should I tell them?"
**14** God said to Moses, "I AM WHO I AM." And he added, "Say this to the Israelites: 'I AM has sent me to you.'"
**15** God spoke further to Moses: "Tell the Israelites, 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob—has sent me to you.' This is my name forever, and this is how I am to be remembered across every generation.
**16** Go, assemble the elders of Israel, and say to them, 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—has appeared to me and said: I have carefully watched over you and observed everything done to you in Egypt.
**17** And I have declared that I will bring you up from your affliction in Egypt to the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, a land flowing with milk and honey.'
**18** They will heed your voice; then you and the elders of Israel shall go to the king of Egypt and tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us. Please let us travel three days into the wilderness to offer sacrifices to the LORD our God.'
**19** Yet I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go, not unless compelled by a mighty hand.
**20** Therefore I will stretch out my hand and strike Egypt with all the wonders I will perform within it; after that, he will release you.
**21** Moreover, I will grant this people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians so that when you leave, you will not depart empty-handed.
**22** Each woman shall ask her neighbor and any woman staying in her house for silver and gold articles and fine clothing; you will put them on your sons and daughters, and thus you will strip Egypt of its wealth."

## Everyday English

**1** One day, Moses was out herding sheep and goats for his father-in-law Jethro, the priest of Midian. He guided the flock all the way past the edge of the desert until he reached Horeb, God's mountain.
**2** Suddenly, the messenger of the LORD appeared to him as a bright flame blazing right out of the middle of a thornbush. Moses stared—the bush was completely on fire, yet it wasn't burning up!
**3** "Let me walk over and check this out," Moses thought. "Why isn't that bush turning to ash?"
**4** When the LORD saw him step over for a closer look, God called out from inside the bush, "Moses! Moses!" "I'm here!" Moses answered.
**5** "Don't come any closer," God told him. "Slip off your sandals, because the ground you're standing on is sacred."
**6** Then God said, "I am the God of your ancestors—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob." Immediately Moses covered his face, too frightened to look directly at God.
**7** The LORD went on, "I've watched my people suffer in Egypt, and I've heard their cries under those cruel slave drivers. I feel every bit of their pain.
**8** So I've come down to pull them free from Egypt's grip and bring them up into a rich, wide-open land—a land overflowing with milk and honey, where the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites currently live.
**9** The Israelites' cries for help have reached me, and I've seen firsthand how relentlessly the Egyptians crush them.
**10** So get ready—I'm sending you straight to Pharaoh to bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt."
**11** "Who am I to march into Pharaoh's court and lead an entire nation out of Egypt?" Moses protested.
**12** God replied, "I'll be right there with you! And here's the proof that I'm the one sending you: once you bring the people out of Egypt, all of you will worship me right here on this mountain."
**13** Still hesitant, Moses asked, "Suppose I go to the Israelites and tell them, 'The God of your ancestors sent me to you,' and they ask, 'What's his name?' What am I supposed to say?"
**14** God told Moses, "I AM WHO I AM." Then he added, "Tell the Israelites, 'The One Who Is—I AM—has sent me to you.'"
**15** God also said to Moses, "Give the Israelites this message: 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors—Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—has sent me to you.' That is my name for all time, the name by which every future generation will know and remember me.
**16** Now go, gather Israel's leaders together, and tell them, 'The LORD, the God of your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, appeared to me and said: I have been watching closely and seen everything done to you in Egypt.
**17** I promise I'm going to rescue you out of Egypt's misery and bring you to the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—a rich land overflowing with milk and honey.'
**18** They'll listen to you. Then you and the Israelite leaders must go before the king of Egypt and tell him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us. Please let us take a three-day trip into the desert so we can offer sacrifices to the LORD our God.'
**19** I already know the king of Egypt won't let you leave unless a mighty force makes him do it.
**20** So I'll raise my hand and hit Egypt with every kind of astonishing miracle inside their borders—and after that, he'll finally let you go.
**21** Even better, I'll make the Egyptians feel generous toward you, so you won't walk away empty-handed.
**22** Every Israelite woman will ask her Egyptian neighbor—and any guest in her home—for silver and gold jewelry and fine clothes. You'll dress your sons and daughters in them and carry Egypt's wealth away with you."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה` (*mal'akh YHWH*):** "Messenger/angel of YHWH"; notice how v. 2 speaks of the *mal'akh*, while v. 4 identifies the speaker directly as *YHWH* and *Elohim*, showing God's own manifest presence in the flame.
- **v. 5 `אַדְמַת־קֹדֶשׁ` (*admat-qodesh*):** "Ground of holiness," meaning soil consecrated by God's presence rather than inherently sacred land.
- **v. 12 `תַּעַבְדוּן` (*ta'avdun*):** From *avad* ("serve/work/worship"), deliberately reversing Pharaoh's forced *avodah* (1:14) into joyful covenantal worship at Sinai/Horeb.
- **v. 14 `אֶהְיֶה אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה` (*Ehyeh asher Ehyeh*):** Uses the imperfect verb *hayah* ("to be/become/be actively present"), allowing both "I AM WHO I AM" and "I WILL BE WHO I WILL BE"; it functions as an etymological wordplay explaining the covenant name `יְהוָה` (*YHWH*, third-person "He Is / He Causes to Be") revealed in v. 15.
- **v. 16 `פָּקֹד פָּקַדְתִּי` (*paqod paqadti*):** Infinitive absolute plus finite verb ("visiting I have visited"), expressing attentive intervention and echoing Joseph's dying promise in Gen 50:24–25.
- **v. 22 `וְנִצַּלְתֶּם` (*venitsaltem*):** Piel of *natsal* ("strip/deliver/plunder"), portraying the gifts freely given (v. 21) as the rightful wages of generations of unpaid slavery.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 15:14** (exodus with great possessions): *God's pledge that Abraham's enslaved offspring would leave Egypt with abundant wealth is echoed in Exod 3:21–22.*
- **Mark 12:26; Luke 20:37** (God of the living): *Jesus cites the burning-bush declaration ("I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob") to argue that the patriarchs live before God.*
- **John 8:58** (echo of Exod 3:14): *Jesus declares before Abraham existed, "I am," resonating with the self-existent divine declaration at the burning bush.*
- **Acts 7:30–35** (Stephen's retelling): *Stephen recalls the fiery bush in the wilderness near Sinai and God commissioning Moses as ruler and deliverer.*
