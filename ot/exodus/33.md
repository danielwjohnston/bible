## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Depart and go up from here, you and the people whom you brought up out of the land of Egypt, to the land of which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'To your offspring I will give it.'
**2** I will send an angel ahead of you, and I will drive out the Canaanite, the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite,
**3** leading you to a land flowing with milk and honey. Yet I will not go up in your midst, because you are a stiff-necked people, lest I consume you along the way."
**4** When the people heard this distressing word, they went into mourning, and no one put on his ornaments.
**5** For the LORD had said to Moses, "Say to the Israelites, 'You are a stiff-necked people; if I were to go up among you for a single moment, I would consume you. Now therefore take off your ornaments, that I may decide what to do with you.'"
**6** So the people of Israel stripped themselves of their ornaments from Mount Horeb onward.
**7** Now Moses used to take the tent and pitch it for himself outside the camp, at a distance from the camp, and he called it the tent of meeting. Everyone who sought the LORD would go out to the tent of meeting, which stood outside the camp.
**8** Whenever Moses went out to the tent, all the people would rise and stand, each at the entrance of his own tent, watching Moses until he had entered the tent.
**9** As soon as Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance of the tent, and the LORD would speak with Moses.
**10** When all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, all the people would rise and bow down, each at the entrance of his tent.
**11** Thus the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, just as a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, while his young attendant Joshua son of Nun did not depart from inside the tent.
**12** Moses said to the LORD, "Look, you tell me, 'Lead this people up,' yet you have not let me know whom you will send with me. Still, you have said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in my sight.'
**13** Now therefore, if I have truly found favor in your sight, please teach me your ways so that I may know you and continue to find favor in your sight. Remember, too, that this nation is your own people."
**14** And he answered, "My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."
**15** Moses said to him, "If your presence does not go with us, do not bring us up from here.
**16** How else can it be known that I have found favor in your sight—I and your people—unless you go with us? That is what sets us apart, me and your people, from every other people on the face of the earth."
**17** The LORD replied to Moses, "I will do this very thing that you have asked, for you have found favor in my sight and I know you by name."
**18** Then Moses said, "Please show me your glory."
**19** He answered, "I will cause all my goodness to pass before you, and I will proclaim the name of the LORD before you. I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will show mercy on whom I will show mercy."
**20** But he added, "You cannot see my face, for no human being may see me and live."
**21** Then the LORD said, "See, there is a place beside me; station yourself upon the rock.
**22** And while my glory passes by, I will place you in a cleft of the rock and cover you with my hand until I have passed by.
**23** Then I will take away my hand, and you shall see my back; but my face shall not be seen."

## Everyday English

**1** Then the LORD told Moses, "Pack up and head out from here—you and the people you led out of Egypt—toward the land I promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob when I swore, 'I will give it to your descendants.'
**2** I'll send an angel ahead of you to clear out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites,
**3** bringing you into a land rich with milk and honey. But I won't travel right among you myself, because you're such a stubborn people that I might wipe you out along the road."
**4** When the Israelites heard that grim announcement, they started mourning, and nobody put on their jewelry.
**5** The LORD had told Moses to pass this message to Israel: "You are a stubborn people. If I traveled in your midst for even one instant, I'd destroy you. So take off your jewelry right now while I decide what to do with you."
**6** From Mount Horeb onward, the Israelites stripped off all their jewelry.
**7** Meanwhile, Moses used to take a tent and set it up well outside the camp; he called it the Meeting Tent. Anyone who wanted to inquire of the LORD would walk out to that Meeting Tent beyond the camp boundary.
**8** Every time Moses headed out to the tent, all the Israelites got up, stood in their own tent doorways, and watched him until he stepped inside.
**9** Once Moses entered, the pillar of cloud always came down and hovered at the tent doorway while God talked with Moses.
**10** Whenever everyone saw that pillar of cloud standing at the tent doorway, they all stood up and bowed in worship right at the entrance of their own tents.
**11** Inside, the LORD talked with Moses face to face, the way a person talks with a close friend. Afterward Moses would walk back to camp, while his young assistant, Joshua son of Nun, stayed right inside the tent.
**12** One day Moses told the LORD, "Look, you keep telling me, 'Lead these people forward,' yet you still haven't told me whom you're sending along with me. You've said, 'I know you personally by name, and you have my favor.'
**13** So if I really have your favor, please show me your plans so I can know you better and keep enjoying your favor. And don't forget—this nation belongs to you!"
**14** The LORD answered, "My own presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."
**15** Moses replied, "If your presence isn't coming with us, don't even make us leave this place!
**16** After all, how could anyone tell that you favor me and your people unless you walk with us? Your presence with us is the very thing that makes us different from every other nation on earth."
**17** The LORD told Moses, "I'll grant even this request you just made, because you have my favor and I know you personally by name."
**18** Then Moses asked, "Please let me see your radiant glory!"
**19** God replied, "I will make all my goodness pass right in front of you, and I will announce my name—the LORD—before you. I freely show grace to anyone I choose to favor, and I show compassion to anyone I choose to pity.
**20** However," he said, "you can't look directly at my face, because no mortal can see my face and survive."
**21** Then the LORD added, "Look, here's a spot right beside me. Stand on this rock.
**22** As my radiant glory passes by, I'll tuck you into a crevice in the rock and shield you with my hand until I've gone past.
**23** Then I'll lift my hand away, and you'll catch sight of my back—but my face will remain unseen."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 7 `אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד` (*Ohel Mo'ed*):** "Tent of Meeting"—a provisional sanctuary pitched outside the camp after the golden calf rupture, prior to the construction of the permanent Tabernacle (chs. 35–40).
- **v. 10 `וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּ` (*ve-hishtachavu*):** WLC preserves the Leningrad Codex geminated-*waw* spelling `וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּ` (flagged with OSIS note `n="1"` in `Exod.xml`), from *shachah* ("bow down low in reverence").
- **v. 14 `פָּנַי יֵלֵכוּ` (*panai yelekhu*):** Literally "My faces shall go"—idiom for God's personal, intimate presence (`פָּנִים` *panim*, plural in form), contrasting with sending a mere delegate angel (vv. 2–3).
- **v. 16 `וְנִפְלֵינוּ` (*ve-niflenu*):** "And we shall be distinguished/set apart" (Niphal of *p-l-h*/*p-l-'*, flagged by OSIS note `n="1"` for Leningrad's unusual orthography), echoing the plagues' distinction between Israel and Egypt (Exod 8:18[22]; 9:4; 11:7).
- **v. 18 `כְּבֹדֶךָ` (*kevodekha*):** "Your glory (*kavod*)"—paired in vv. 19–22 with YHWH's "goodness" (`טוּבִי`) and proclaimed name (`יְהוָה`), revealing that God's deepest glory is moral character rather than unmediated visual brilliance (which mortals cannot survive, v. 20).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **1 Kings 19:8–13** (cleft/cave at Horeb): *Elijah returns to Horeb and experiences YHWH passing by while sheltered at the entrance of a mountain cave, echoing Moses in the cleft of the rock.*
- **Romans 9:15** (citation of Exodus 33:19): *Paul quotes God's sovereign declaration to Moses about showing mercy and compassion to whom he wills.*
- **John 1:14, 17–18** (glory, grace, and truth): *Presents Jesus as tabernacling among humanity full of grace and truth—echoing God's goodness and unseen face in Exodus 33:18–20.*
- **1 Timothy 6:16** (unapproachable light): *Affirms that God dwells in unapproachable light whom no human being has seen or can see.*
