## Clear & Faithful

**1** "Now these are the ordinances that you shall set before them:
**2** When you buy a Hebrew male servant, he shall serve for six years, and in the seventh he shall go out free without paying anything.
**3** If he came in single, he shall go out single; if he was a married man, then his wife shall go out with him.
**4** If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and her children shall belong to her master, and he shall go out alone.
**5** But if the servant plainly declares, 'I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free,'
**6** then his master shall bring him before God, and he shall bring him to the door or the doorpost; and his master shall pierce his ear with an awl, and he shall serve him for life.
**7** When a man sells his daughter as a female servant, she shall not go out as the male servants do.
**8** If she is displeasing in the eyes of her master, who designated her for himself, then he shall let her be redeemed; he shall have no right to sell her to a foreign people, since he has broken faith with her.
**9** And if he designates her for his son, he shall deal with her according to the customary right of daughters.
**10** If he takes another wife for himself, he shall not diminish her food, her clothing, or her marital rights.
**11** And if he does not provide these three things for her, she shall go out free without any payment of silver.
**12** Whoever strikes a person so that he dies shall surely be put to death.
**13** Yet if he did not lie in wait, but God allowed it to fall into his hand, then I will appoint for you a place to which he may flee.
**14** But if a man schemes willfully against his neighbor to kill him by treachery, you shall take him even from my altar to die.
**15** Whoever strikes his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.
**16** Whoever kidnaps a person and sells him—or if the victim is still found in his possession—shall surely be put to death.
**17** Whoever curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.
**18** When men quarrel and one strikes the other with a stone or with his fist, and the victim does not die yet takes to his bed,
**19** then if he gets up again and walks outdoors with his staff, the one who struck him shall be acquitted; he shall only compensate him for his lost time and ensure his complete recovery.
**20** When a man strikes his male servant or his female servant with a rod and the victim dies under his hand, he shall surely be punished.
**21** However, if the servant survives a day or two, he shall not be punished, for the servant is his property.
**22** When men fight and strike a pregnant woman so that her children come out prematurely, yet there is no serious injury, the offender shall surely be fined whatever the woman's husband demands, paying as the judges determine.
**23** But if serious injury occurs, then you shall give life for life,
**24** eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,
**25** burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.
**26** When a man strikes the eye of his male servant or the eye of his female servant and destroys it, he shall let the servant go free on account of his eye.
**27** And if he knocks out the tooth of his male servant or his female servant, he shall let the servant go free on account of his tooth.
**28** When an ox gores a man or a woman to death, the ox shall surely be stoned, and its flesh shall not be eaten; yet the owner of the ox shall be acquitted.
**29** But if the ox was previously in the habit of goring and its owner had been warned, yet did not keep it penned in, and it kills a man or a woman, the ox shall be stoned and its owner also shall be put to death.
**30** If a ransom is imposed on him, then he shall pay for the redemption of his life whatever amount is imposed on him.
**31** Whether it gores a son or gores a daughter, according to this same rule it shall be done to him.
**32** If the ox gores a male servant or a female servant, the owner shall give thirty shekels of silver to that servant's master, and the ox shall be stoned.
**33** When a man opens a pit, or when a man digs a pit and fails to cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,
**34** the owner of the pit shall make restitution; he shall repay silver to its owner, and the dead animal shall belong to him.
**35** When one man's ox injures another's ox so that it dies, they shall sell the live ox and divide its proceeds, and they shall also divide the dead animal.
**36** Or if it was known that the ox had been in the habit of goring previously, yet its owner did not keep it penned in, he shall surely pay ox for ox, and the dead animal shall belong to him."

## Everyday English

**1** "Here are the legal rules you must present to the Israelites:
**2** Whenever you purchase a Hebrew bondservant, he will work for six years, then walk free in the seventh year owing nothing.
**3** If he arrived single, he leaves single; if he already had a wife when he came, his wife leaves with him.
**4** If his master provided him with a wife and she gave birth to sons or daughters, that woman and her children still belong to her master, and the man leaves by himself.
**5** But if the bondservant insists, 'I love my master, my wife, and my kids—I don't want to walk free,'
**6** then his master must bring him before God's judges, stand him beside the door or doorframe, and pierce his ear with an awl. After that, he stays in his master's service for life.
**7** When a man sells his daughter into household service, she isn't released the same way male bondservants are.
**8** If she displeases the master who chose her for himself, he has to let her family buy her back. He has no right to sell her off to foreigners, because he broke his commitment to her.
**9** If he chose her to marry his son, he must treat her with all the legal rights of a daughter.
**10** And if he marries another woman as well, he must not cut back on the first woman's food, clothing, or conjugal rights.
**11** If he refuses to give her those three essentials, she walks out completely free without owing a penny.
**12** Anyone who hits someone and kills them must be executed.
**13** However, if it wasn't premeditated—if it happened purely by accident—I'll designate a safe place where that person can run for shelter.
**14** But if anyone deliberately plots to murder a neighbor through treachery, drag that killer straight away from my altar and put them to death.
**15** Anyone who physically attacks their father or mother must be executed.
**16** Anyone who kidnaps another human being—whether they've already sold them or are still holding them—must be executed.
**17** Anyone who curses or reviles their father or mother must be executed.
**18** Suppose two people get into a brawl and one hits the other with a rock or a fist—not fatally, though the victim ends up bedridden.
**19** If the injured person recovers enough to walk outside leaning on a cane, the attacker won't face criminal charges, but must pay for the lost work time and cover full medical care until healing is complete.
**20** If an owner beats a male or female enslaved worker with a club and the worker dies on the spot, the owner must be punished.
**21** If the worker survives a day or two, the owner isn't executed, since the worker represented his financial investment.
**22** If men are brawling and accidentally bump a pregnant woman so she gives birth early, yet no lasting harm comes to mother or child, the guilty man must pay whatever fine the woman's husband asks and the judges approve.
**23** But if serious harm occurs, the penalty matches the damage: life for life,
**24** an eye to pay for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, and a foot for a foot,
**25** a burn to pay for a burn, a cut for a cut, and a bruise for a bruise.
**26** If an owner strikes the eye of a male or female enslaved worker and destroys their sight, he must set that worker free to compensate for the lost eye.
**27** And if he knocks out an enslaved man's or woman's tooth, he must set that worker free to compensate for the tooth.
**28** If an ox gores a man or a woman to death, the ox must be stoned, and nobody may eat its meat, though the owner isn't held criminally liable.
**29** Yet if that ox already had a habit of goring and the owner had been warned—but still failed to keep it fenced in—and it kills a man or a woman, then both the ox must be stoned and the owner must be put to death.
**30** If the victim's family accepts a settlement instead, the owner can pay whatever life-ransom price is set to spare his own life.
**31** The exact same rule applies if the ox gores a boy or a girl.
**32** If the ox gores an enslaved man or woman, the owner must pay about twelve ounces of silver to the worker's master, and the ox must still be stoned.
**33** If someone uncovers a cistern or digs a new pit and leaves it uncovered, and an ox or a donkey tumbles into it,
**34** the person responsible for the pit must pay full silver compensation to the animal's owner, and the dead animal becomes theirs.
**35** If one person's ox attacks and kills a neighbor's ox, they must sell the living ox, split the cash down the middle, and also split the dead carcass evenly.
**36** However, if everyone already knew that the ox had a habit of goring and its owner failed to pen it up, that owner must pay back a live ox to replace the dead one, and keep the carcass."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `הַמִּשְׁפָּטִים` (*ha-mishpatim*):** "The legal rulings / case-laws"—opening the Covenant Code (21:1–23:19), moving from apodictic Decalogue commands to casuistic ("if/when...") jurisprudence.
- **v. 6 `אֶל־הָאֱלֹהִים` (*el ha-Elohim*):** Literally "to God"—either a local sanctuary ritual before God or judicial magistrates acting with divine authority (cf. 22:8–9).
- **v. 7 `אָמָה` (*amah*):** "Female servant/bondwoman," a homograph in unpointed script with `אַמָּה` (*ammah*, "cubit," used in Exod 25–27).
- **v. 8 `לֹא` / `לוֹ` (*lo'* / *lo*):** Crucial *Ketiv/Qere* pair in WLC: the written consonantal *Ketiv* is `לא` ("who did *not* designate her"), whereas the Masoretic *Qere* reads `ל֥וֹ` ("who designated her *for himself*," matching v. 9's parallel "for his son").
- **v. 10 `עֹנָתָהּ` (*onatah*):** Rare legal noun meaning "her conjugal rights / marital intimacy" (or "dwelling/shelter").
- **v. 22 `אָסוֹן` (*ason*):** "Serious/fatal harm"—defining the threshold between a civil fine assessed by `פְּלִלִים` (*pelilim*, "judges/mediators") and proportional *talion* in vv. 23–25.
- **v. 30 `כֹּפֶר` (*kofer*):** "Ransom-payment / redemption sum," paired with `פִּדְיֹן נַפְשׁוֹ` (*pidyon nafsho*, "redemption of his life")—sharing the *k-p-r* root with `כַּפֹּרֶת` (*kapporet*, 25:17).
- **v. 36 `שַׁלֵּם יְשַׁלֵּם` (*shallem yeshallem*):** "Repaying he shall repay" (Piel infinitive absolute + imperfect); WLC continues chapter 21 with one more verse (`21:37`, theft of an ox/sheep), which English versification numbers as `22:1`.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Deuteronomy 15:12–18** (expands Exod 21:2–6): *"Extends seventh-year release equally to Hebrew women and commands generous parting provisions along with the awl-ear ceremony."*
- **Matthew 5:38–39** (cites Exod 21:24): *"Jesus quotes the courtroom rule of 'eye for eye and tooth for tooth' and calls his disciples to renounce personal retaliation."*
- **1 Timothy 1:10** (echoes Exod 21:16): *"Lists kidnappers/enslavers alongside murderers and parricides among lawbreakers condemned by sound teaching."*
- **Numbers 35:11–31** (expands Exod 21:12–14): *"Establishes six cities of refuge for accidental killers while forbidding any ransom payment for deliberate murder."*

**9** If he marries her to his son, he shall deal with her as a daughter.
**10** If he takes another wife to himself, he shall not diminish her food, her clothing, and her marital rights.
**11** If he doesn’t do these three things for her, she may go free without paying any money.
**12** “One who strikes a man so that he dies shall surely be put to death,
**13** but not if it is unintentional, but God allows it to happen; then I will appoint you a place where he shall flee.
