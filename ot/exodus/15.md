## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to the LORD: "I will sing to the LORD, for he has triumphed gloriously; horse and rider he has hurled into the sea!
**2** The LORD is my strength and my song, and he has become my salvation; this is my God, and I will praise him, the God of my father, and I will exalt him.
**3** The LORD is a warrior; the LORD is his name.
**4** Pharaoh's chariots and his army he has cast into the sea, and his finest officers have sunk in the Sea of Reeds.
**5** The deep waters covered them; they sank down into the depths like a stone.
**6** Your right hand, O LORD, is glorious in power; your right hand, O LORD, shatters the enemy.
**7** In the greatness of your majesty you overthrow your adversaries; you unleash your burning anger, and it consumes them like stubble.
**8** By the blast of your nostrils the waters piled up; the surging currents stood upright like a wall; the deep waters congealed in the heart of the sea.
**9** The enemy boasted, 'I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the spoil; my appetite shall be gorged on them; I will draw my sword, my hand shall destroy them.'
**10** You blew with your breath, and the sea covered them; they sank like lead in the mighty waters.
**11** Who is like you among the gods, O LORD? Who is like you, majestic in holiness, awesome in glorious deeds, working wonders?
**12** You stretched out your right hand; the earth swallowed them.
**13** In your steadfast love you have led the people whom you redeemed; in your strength you have guided them to your holy dwelling.
**14** The nations have heard and tremble; anguish has gripped the inhabitants of Philistia.
**15** Now the chiefs of Edom are dismayed; trembling seizes the leaders of Moab; all the inhabitants of Canaan melt away.
**16** Terror and dread fall upon them; by the greatness of your arm they stand silent as stone until your people pass by, O LORD, until the people whom you purchased pass by.
**17** You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain of your inheritance—the place, O LORD, you have made for your dwelling, the sanctuary, O Lord, that your hands have established.
**18** The LORD will reign forever and ever!"
**19** For when Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and horsemen went into the sea, the LORD brought the waters of the sea back over them, while the Israelites walked on dry ground through the middle of the sea.
**20** Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took a tambourine in her hand, and all the women followed her with tambourines and dancing.
**21** And Miriam sang back to them: "Sing to the LORD, for he has triumphed gloriously; horse and rider he has hurled into the sea!"
**22** Then Moses led Israel onward from the Sea of Reeds, and they entered the wilderness of Shur; they traveled three days in the wilderness without finding water.
**23** When they arrived at Marah, they could not drink the water of Marah because it was bitter; that is why it was named Marah.
**24** So the people grumbled against Moses, saying, "What are we to drink?"
**25** He cried out to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a piece of wood; he threw it into the water, and the water turned sweet. There the LORD established a statute and an ordinance for them, and there he tested them,
**26** saying, "If you earnestly heed the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is right in his sight, and pay attention to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will bring upon you none of the diseases that I brought upon the Egyptians; for I am the LORD who heals you."
**27** Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there beside the water.

## Everyday English

**1** At that, Moses and the people of Israel broke into this song for the LORD: "I'll sing to the LORD, because he won a towering victory—he threw both horse and rider straight into the sea!
**2** The LORD is my strength and my music; he's the one who saved me. He is my God, and I'll honor him; my ancestor's God, and I'll lift him high!
**3** The LORD is a mighty warrior; the LORD is his very name.
**4** He flung Pharaoh's chariots and army into the sea, and Egypt's best commanders drowned in the Sea of Reeds.
**5** Deep waves swallowed them up; they dropped like stones to the ocean floor.
**6** Your strong right hand, LORD, shines with awesome power; your right hand crushes every foe!
**7** With unmatched greatness you topple everyone who opposes you; your blazing anger burns them up like dry straw.
**8** One blast of your breath stacked the sea up high—rushing waves froze into solid walls, and the ocean's deep heart hardened like ice.
**9** The enemy bragged, 'I'll hunt them down, catch them, and split up the loot! I'll satisfy my fury on them, draw my sword, and wipe them out!'
**10** But you blew once, and the sea buried them; they sank like lead weights into churning water.
**11** Who compares to you among all the gods, LORD? Nobody! Who equals your dazzling holiness, your awe-inspiring fame, your breathtaking miracles?
**12** You reached out your right hand, and the earth swallowed them whole.
**13** With faithful love you guided the people you rescued; with your mighty power you led them toward your sacred home.
**14** Foreign nations hear the news and shake in fear; panic grips the people living in Philistia.
**15** Edom's rulers are stunned with terror, Moab's champions tremble uncontrollably, and everyone in Canaan loses heart.
**16** Sheer dread washes over them; your unstoppable power freezes them still as stone until your people cross safely past, LORD—until the nation you claimed as your own marches through.
**17** You'll carry them home and plant them on your very own mountain—the home you built for yourself, LORD, the holy shrine your own hands shaped, O Lord!
**18** The LORD will rule as king forever and ever!"
**19** Indeed, when Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and riders marched into the sea, the LORD brought the waves crashing back down on them, while the Israelites walked safely across the dry seabed.
**20** Then Aaron's sister, the prophet Miriam, picked up a tambourine, and all the women followed behind her playing tambourines and dancing.
**21** Miriam led them in the refrain: "Sing to the LORD, for he won a towering victory—he threw both horse and rider straight into the sea!"
**22** After leaving the Sea of Reeds, Moses guided Israel into the Desert of Shur. They hiked through the desert for three days without finding a drop of water.
**23** When they finally reached Marah, the water there was too bitter to drink—which is why the place was called Marah ("Bitter").
**24** The people started complaining to Moses, "What are we supposed to drink?"
**25** Moses called out to the LORD, and the LORD pointed out a tree to him. When Moses tossed the wood into the pool, the water turned fresh and sweet. Right there the LORD gave Israel a binding rule and legal standard, and put them to the test.
**26** He told them, "If you truly listen to the voice of the LORD your God, do what pleases him, pay close attention to his commands, and obey every one of his rules, I won't strike you with any of the diseases I sent on Egypt—because I am the LORD, your healer."
**27** Next they reached Elim, an oasis with twelve fresh water springs and seventy date palms, and they set up camp right by the water.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `יָהּ` (*Yah*):** Shortened poetic form of the divine name `יְהוָה` (*YHWH*), paired with `זִמְרָת` (*zimrat*, "song" or "strength/defense").
- **v. 2 `וְאַנְוֵהוּ` (*ve-anvehu*):** From *navah* ("praise/beautify") or *naveh* ("dwelling," cf. v. 13 "holy pasture/dwelling")—meaning either "I will praise/glorify him" or "I will prepare a dwelling for him."
- **v. 4 `יַם־סוּף` (*Yam-Suph*):** Literally "Sea of Reeds" (*suph* = reeds/rushes, as in Exod 2:3), traditionally rendered "Red Sea" via the Septuagint *erythra thalassa*.
- **v. 5 `יְכַסְיֻמוּ` (*yekhasyumu*):** Archaic poetic 3mp object suffix `-מוֹ` (*-mo*, "them"), recurring in vv. 7, 9, 10, 12, 15, 17 as a hallmark of early Hebrew poetry.
- **v. 13 `זוּ` (*zu*):** Archaic poetic relative pronoun ("whom/that"), repeated in v. 16 alongside `גָּאַל` (*ga'al*, "redeem as kinsman") and `קָנָה` (*qanah*, "acquire/purchase/create").
- **v. 17 `אֲדֹנָי` (*Adonai*):** Distinct title "Lord" used alongside the personal covenant name `יְהוָה` (*YHWH*) in the same verse.
- **v. 23 `מָרָה` (*Marah*):** Wordplay on `מָרִים` (*marim*, "bitter"), explaining the place-name etiology.
- **v. 26 `יְהוָה רֹפְאֶךָ` (*YHWH Rophecha*):** "YHWH your healer/physician"—the first of the compound covenant titles in Exodus (cf. `יְהוָה נִסִּי` in 17:15).
