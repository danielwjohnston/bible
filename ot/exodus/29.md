## Clear & Faithful

**1** Now this is the rite you shall perform for them to consecrate them as my priests: take one young bull and two unblemished rams,
**2** together with unleavened bread, unleavened loaves kneaded with oil, and unleavened wafers brushed with oil; make them from fine wheat flour.
**3** Place them in a single basket and bring them forward in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.
**4** Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.
**5** Take the vestments and dress Aaron in the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod itself, and the breastpiece, and fasten the woven waistband of the ephod around him.
**6** Set the turban on his head and place the sacred diadem upon the turban.
**7** Then take the anointing oil, pour it upon his head, and anoint him.
**8** Bring his sons forward and clothe them in tunics,
**9** fasten sashes around Aaron and his sons, and wrap headbands upon them; the priesthood shall belong to them as a perpetual statute. In this way you shall ordain Aaron and his sons.
**10** Next, bring the bull before the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the bull.
**11** Slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
**12** Take some of the bull's blood and apply it to the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour all the rest of the blood out at the base of the altar.
**13** Collect all the fat covering the entrails, the appendage on the liver, and both kidneys with the fat upon them, and send them up in smoke on the altar.
**14** As for the bull's meat, its hide, and its offal, burn them in fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.
**15** Then take the first ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.
**16** Slaughter the ram, take its blood, and splash it all around against the sides of the altar.
**17** Cut the ram into its pieces, wash its entrails and legs, and place them with its pieces and its head.
**18** Burn the entire ram in smoke upon the altar; it is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.
**19** Take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.
**20** Slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the lobes of his sons' right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet; then splash the remaining blood against the altar all around.
**21** Take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it upon Aaron and his garments, and upon his sons and his sons' garments with him; thus he and his garments, and his sons and their garments with him, shall be consecrated.
**22** Take from the ram the fat, the fat tail, the fat covering the entrails, the appendage of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat upon them, and the right thigh—for it is a ram of ordination—
**23** along with one loaf of bread, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that stands before the LORD.
**24** Place all of these into the palms of Aaron and the palms of his sons, and present them as a wave offering before the LORD.
**25** Then receive them back from their hands and burn them on the altar atop the burnt offering as a pleasing aroma before the LORD; it is an offering by fire to the LORD.
**26** Take the breast from Aaron's ram of ordination and present it as a wave offering before the LORD, and it shall become your share.
**27** Consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the sacred contribution—what was waved and what was raised up from the ram of ordination, both what belongs to Aaron and what belongs to his sons.
**28** It shall belong to Aaron and his sons as a perpetual due from the people of Israel, because it is a sacred contribution; it shall be a contribution from the Israelites out of their peace sacrifices, their sacred contribution to the LORD.
**29** Aaron's sacred garments shall pass to his sons after him, to be worn when they are anointed and ordained.
**30** For seven days the son who succeeds him as priest—the one who enters the tent of meeting to minister in the holy place—shall put them on.
**31** Take the ram of ordination and boil its meat in a sacred place.
**32** Aaron and his sons shall eat the meat of the ram and the bread in the basket at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
**33** They shall eat those things by which atonement was made to ordain and consecrate them; no outsider may eat of them, because they are holy.
**34** Should any of the ordination meat or any of the bread remain until morning, you must burn the remainder in the fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is holy.
**35** Carry out all these instructions for Aaron and his sons exactly as I have commanded you; over seven days you shall ordain them.
**36** Offer a bull each day as a sin offering for atonement; purify the altar by making atonement upon it, and anoint it to consecrate it.
**37** For seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it; then the altar shall be most holy, and whatever touches the altar will become holy.
**38** Now this is what you shall offer upon the altar regularly every day: two yearling lambs.
**39** Offer the first lamb in the morning, and offer the second lamb at twilight.
**40** With the first lamb offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mingled with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, along with a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of wine.
**41** Offer the second lamb at twilight, preparing it with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.
**42** This shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak with you there.
**43** There I will meet with the people of Israel, and that place shall be consecrated by my glory.
**44** I will consecrate the tent of meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve me as priests.
**45** I will dwell in the midst of the people of Israel, and I will be their God.
**46** Then they shall know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I might dwell among them; I am the LORD their God.

**1** "Here's the ceremony you must follow to dedicate Aaron and his sons so they can serve me as priests: Choose one healthy young bull and two unblemished rams.
**2** Bake three kinds of yeast-free bread using fine wheat flour: plain loaves, thick cakes mixed with olive oil, and thin wafers brushed with oil.
**3** Pack all the breads into a single basket and bring them along with the bull and both rams.
**4** Have Aaron and his sons step up to the doorway of the meeting tent, and wash them with water.
**5** Dress Aaron in the priestly tunic, the blue robe, the priestly vest, and the decision pouch, securing everything with the vest's woven belt.
**6** Wrap the turban on his head and tie the sacred gold medallion onto the front of it.
**7** Pour anointing oil over Aaron's head to anoint him.
**8** Next, bring his sons forward and put their linen tunics on them,
**9** tie sashes around Aaron and his sons, and fit caps on their heads. The priesthood belongs to them by permanent law. That's how you will install Aaron and his sons into office.
**10** Lead the bull in front of the meeting tent, and have Aaron and his sons press their hands onto its head.
**11** Slaughter the bull in the LORD's presence right outside the meeting tent entrance.
**12** Dip your finger in the bull's blood, smear some on the four horns of the altar, and pour the rest out around the bottom of the altar.
**13** Gather all the fat covering the inner organs, the fatty lobe of the liver, and both kidneys with their fat, and burn them completely on the altar.
**14** Haul the bull's carcass, skin, and waste outside the camp and burn them there; this is a purification offering for sin.
**15** Next, bring up the first ram, and have Aaron and his sons press their hands on its head.
**16** Slaughter it, catch its blood, and splash the blood against all four sides of the altar.
**17** Cut the ram into pieces, rinse its inner organs and lower legs, and stack them on the altar alongside the rest of the cuts and the head.
**18** Burn the whole ram on the altar as a completely burned offering to the LORD—a fragrant gift consumed by fire in his honor.
**19** Then bring up the second ram, and again have Aaron and his sons press their hands on its head.
**20** Slaughter this ram too, dab a drop of its blood on Aaron's right earlobe and on his sons' right earlobes, on their right thumbs, and on their right big toes; splash the remaining blood around the altar sides.
**21** Mix some blood from the altar with anointing oil and sprinkle it over Aaron, his sons, and all their vestments. That will set apart both the men and their clothes as sacred.
**22** From this second ram—the ordination ram—take all its fat, the fatty tail, the fat covering the organs, the liver lobe, both kidneys with their fat, and the right hind leg,
**23** plus one loaf of plain bread, one oil-mixed cake, and one wafer from the basket of yeast-free bread before the LORD.
**24** Place all of these into the open hands of Aaron and his sons so they can lift and present them in a wave-offering gesture before the LORD.
**25** Take the items back from their hands and burn them on the altar over the burnt offering as a fragrant gift to the LORD.
**26** Take the breast meat from Aaron's ordination ram and lift it in a wave offering before the LORD; that cut will be your own share, Moses.
**27** Set apart as holy both the waved breast and the lifted thigh from the ordination ram—the portions belonging to Aaron and his sons.
**28** From now on, whenever the Israelites present peace offerings, that breast and thigh belong permanently to Aaron and his descendants as Israel's dedicated contribution to the LORD.
**29** When Aaron dies, pass his sacred high-priestly clothes down to whichever son succeeds him, to be worn during his seven-day anointing and ordination.
**30** That successor must wear those garments for seven straight days as he enters the meeting tent to serve in the sanctuary.
**31** Cook the meat from the ordination ram by boiling it in a sacred area.
**32** Aaron and his sons are to eat that ram's meat together with the bread in the basket right at the doorway of the meeting tent.
**33** Only they may eat the sacrificial food used in the atonement ceremony that installed and consecrated them; no outsider is allowed to taste it, because it's holy.
**34** If any of the ordination meat or bread is left over by sunrise, burn it up in the fire—don't eat it, because it's sacred.
**35** Carry out this whole ordination ritual for Aaron and his sons just as I've instructed you, repeating it for seven days.
**36** Every day for those seven days, sacrifice a young bull as a purification offering to make atonement, cleansing and anointing the altar to dedicate it.
**37** After seven days of atonement and consecration, the altar itself becomes intensely holy—anything that touches it is claimed as holy.
**38** Every single day from then on, offer two year-old lambs on the altar without fail.
**39** Sacrifice one lamb at daybreak and the second lamb at dusk.
**40** With the morning lamb, bring two quarts of fine flour blended with one quart of cold-pressed olive oil, plus one quart of wine to pour out as a drink offering.
**41** Offer the evening lamb at dusk with the exact same flour-and-oil gift and wine offering, rising as a fragrant gift consumed by fire for the LORD.
**42** Keep this daily burnt offering going generation after generation at the entrance to the meeting tent in the LORD's presence—the very place where I'll meet you and speak with you.
**43** There I will meet with the people of Israel, and my radiant presence will make the place holy.
**44** I'll consecrate the meeting tent and the altar, and I'll set apart Aaron and his sons to serve as my priests.
**45** I will live right among the Israelites and be their God.
**46** Then they'll know firsthand that I am the LORD their God, who rescued them from Egypt precisely so I could live among them. I am the LORD their God."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 9 `וּמִלֵּאתָ יַד־אַהֲרֹן` (*u-milleta yad-Aharon*):** Literally "and you shall fill the hand of Aaron"—the Hebrew technical idiom for priestly ordination (`מִלֻּאִים` *millu'im*, "ordinations/installations" in vv. 22, 26, 31, 34), evoking placing sacrificial portions into the newly commissioned priest's hands (v. 24).
- **v. 18 `רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ` (*reyach nichoach*):** Literally "aroma of soothing/rest" (from *nuach*, "to rest/be appeased"), expressing divine delight and acceptance of a wholehearted offering.
- **v. 33 `אֲשֶׁר כֻּפַּר בָּהֶם` (*asher kuppar bahem*):** Passive Pual of `כִּפֶּר` (*kipper*, "make atonement/purge/ransom"); the sacrificial portions effect ritual cleansing and reconciliation during the seven-day installation (vv. 36–37).
- **v. 40 `עִשָּׂרוֹן ... הִין` (*issaron ... hin*):** An *issaron* is one-tenth of an ephah (roughly 2 quarts / 2.2 liters of dry flour); a quarter-*hin* is roughly 1 quart / 0.95 liters of oil or wine.
- **v. 43 `וְנִקְדַּשׁ בִּכְבֹדִי` (*ve-niqdash bikhvodi*):** "And it shall be consecrated by my glory (*kavod*)"—ultimately it is YHWH's manifest fiery/cloud-veiled presence, not human ceremony alone, that sanctifies the tent (`מִשְׁכָּן` from *shakhan*, "dwell", v. 45).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Leviticus 8:1–36** (fulfillment of ordination): *Records Moses carrying out the seven-day washing, clothing, anointing, blood application, and ordination offerings commanded here.*
- **Numbers 28:3–8** (daily burnt offering): *Repeats and expands the law of the continual morning and twilight lambs with their flour, oil, and wine accompaniments.*
- **Hebrews 7:27 & 10:11–14** (daily vs. once-for-all sacrifice): *Contrasts the daily repeated priestly sacrifices on the altar with Christ's single self-offering.*
- **John 1:14 & Revelation 21:3** (God dwelling among his people): *Echoes Exodus 29:45–46's promise that God will pitch his dwelling in the midst of his people.*

**28** It shall be for Aaron and his sons as their portion forever from the children of Israel; for it is a wave offering. It shall be a wave offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifices of their peace offerings, even their wave offering to the LORD.
**29** “The holy garments of Aaron shall be for his sons after him, to be anointed in them, and to be consecrated in them.
**30** Seven days shall the son who is priest in his place put them on, when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to minister in the holy place.
**31** “You shall take the ram of consecration and boil its meat in a holy place.
**32** Aaron and his sons shall eat the meat of the ram, and the bread that is in the basket, at the door of the Tent of Meeting.
**33** They shall eat those things with which atonement was made, to consecrate and sanctify them; but a stranger shall not eat of it, because they are holy.
**34** If anything of the meat of the consecration, or of the bread, remains to the morning, then you shall burn the remainder with fire. It shall not be eaten, because it is holy.
**35** “You shall do so to Aaron and to his sons, according to all that I have commanded you. You shall consecrate them seven days.
**36** Every day you shall offer the bull of sin offering for atonement. You shall cleanse the altar when you make atonement for it. You shall anoint it, to sanctify it.
**37** Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar, and sanctify it; and the altar shall be most holy. Whatever touches the altar shall be holy.
**38** “Now this is that which you shall offer on the altar: two lambs a year old day by day continually.
**39** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning; and the other lamb you shall offer at evening;
**40** and with the one lamb a tenth part of an ephah of fine flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin of beaten oil, and the fourth part of a hin of wine for a drink offering.
**41** The other lamb you shall offer at evening, and shall do to it according to the meal offering of the morning and according to its drink offering, for a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.
**42** It shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the door of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you, to speak there to you.
**43** There I will meet with the children of Israel; and the place shall be sanctified by my glory.
**44** I will sanctify the Tent of Meeting and the altar. I will also sanctify Aaron and his sons to minister to me in the priest’s office.
**45** I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God.
**46** They shall know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, that I might dwell among them: I am the LORD their God.
