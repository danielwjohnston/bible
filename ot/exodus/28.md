## Clear & Faithful

**1** Bring near to yourself Aaron your brother, along with his sons from among the Israelites, to serve me as priests: Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's sons.
**2** Make sacred garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for splendor.
**3** Speak to every skilled artisan whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, so that they may craft Aaron's garments to consecrate him to serve me as priest.
**4** These are the garments they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a checkered tunic, a turban, and a sash. They shall craft sacred garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons to serve me as priests.
**5** They shall receive the gold, the blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and the fine linen.
**6** They shall fashion the ephod out of gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen, skillfully designed work.
**7** It shall have two shoulder straps joined to it at its two upper edges so that it may be fastened together.
**8** The finely woven waistband on it shall be made of the same workmanship and materials as the ephod: gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen.
**9** Take two onyx stones and engrave upon them the names of the sons of Israel—
**10** six of their names on the first stone, and the remaining six names on the second stone, in order of their birth.
**11** As a gem-cutter engraves a signet seal, engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel, and mount them in gold filigree settings.
**12** Place the two stones on the shoulder straps of the ephod as stones of memorial for the Israelites, so that Aaron may carry their names before the LORD upon his two shoulders as a memorial.
**13** Make gold filigree settings,
**14** and two chains of pure gold, braided like twisted cords, and attach the braided chains to the settings.
**15** Make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a master weaver; make it like the ephod, of gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and finely twisted linen.
**16** It shall be square when folded double, a span in length and a span in width.
**17** Set four rows of mounted gemstones on it: in the first row, carnelian, topaz, and emerald;
**18** in the second row, turquoise, lapis lazuli, and moonstone;
**19** in the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst;
**20** and in the fourth row, beryl, onyx, and jasper. Each stone shall be enclosed in gold filigree settings.
**21** There shall be twelve stones corresponding to the names of the sons of Israel, each engraved like a signet seal with one name for each of the twelve tribes.
**22** Make braided chains of pure gold, twisted like cords, for the breastpiece.
**23** Make two gold rings for the breastpiece and fasten the two rings to the two upper corners of the breastpiece.
**24** Attach the two gold cords to the two rings at the upper corners of the breastpiece,
**25** and fasten the other two ends of the two cords to the two filigree settings, attaching them to the front of the shoulder straps of the ephod.
**26** Make two more gold rings and set them on the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on its inner edge facing the ephod.
**27** Also make two gold rings and attach them to the lower front part of the two shoulder straps of the ephod, close to its seam, just above the woven waistband of the ephod.
**28** Then bind the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so that it rests above the woven waistband of the ephod and the breastpiece cannot shift out of place from the ephod.
**29** Thus Aaron shall carry the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of judgment over his heart whenever he enters the holy place, as a continual memorial before the LORD.
**30** Place the Urim and the Thummim inside the breastpiece of judgment, so that they rest over Aaron's heart when he goes in before the LORD; thus Aaron shall carry the judgment of the Israelites over his heart before the LORD continually.
**31** Make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue.
**32** It shall have an opening for the head in the center, with a woven binding all around its collar like the neck opening of a coat of mail, so that it will not tear.
**33** Around its lower hem make pomegranates of blue, purple, and crimson yarn, with gold bells alternating among them all around—
**34** a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, around the entire hem of the robe.
**35** Aaron shall wear it when he ministers, so that its sound is heard whenever he enters the holy place before the LORD and whenever he exits, lest he die.
**36** Make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it as on a signet seal: "Holiness to the LORD."
**37** Fasten it to a blue cord so that it sits on the front of the turban.
**38** It shall rest upon Aaron's forehead so that Aaron may bear any guilt connected with the sacred offerings that the Israelites consecrate as all their holy gifts; it shall remain on his forehead continually so that they may find acceptance before the LORD.
**39** Weave the checkered tunic of fine linen, craft a turban of fine linen, and make an embroidered sash.
**40** For Aaron's sons, make tunics, sashes, and headbands for glory and for splendor.
**41** Clothe your brother Aaron and his sons with them; then anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them so that they may serve me as priests.
**42** Make linen undergarments for them to cover their bare flesh, reaching from the waist to the thighs.
**43** Aaron and his sons must wear them whenever they enter the tent of meeting or approach the altar to minister in the holy place, so that they do not incur guilt and die. This is a perpetual statute for him and for his descendants after him.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `לְכָבוֹד וּלְתִפְאָרֶת` (*le-khavod u-le-tiferet*):** "For glory and for beauty/splendor"—the priestly vestments visually manifest the honor (*kavod*) of the sanctuary and its God, not merely personal status.
- **v. 4 `אֵפוֹד` (*ephod*):** A sleeveless outer liturgical apron or vestment woven with gold and dyed wool/linen, secured by shoulder straps and an integrated sash, supporting the high priest's breastpiece.
- **v. 15 `חֹשֶׁן מִשְׁפָּט` (*choshen mishpat*):** "Breastpiece of judgment/decision"—a folded double square pouch (v. 16, one *zeret*/span ≈ 9 inches on each side) bearing twelve tribal gemstones on the front and housing the sacred lots inside.
- **v. 28 `מִטַּבְּעֹתָיו` (*mi-tabbe'otav*, Qere) / `מטבעתו` (Ketiv):** `Exod.xml` records unpointed Ketiv `מטבעתו` (resembling singular "from its ring"), whereas the Masoretic Qere points plural `מִטַּבְּעֹתָיו` ("from its rings"), matching the two lower rings in v. 26.
- **v. 30 `הָאוּרִים וְהַתֻּמִּים` (*ha-Urim ve-ha-Thummim*):** Literally "the Lights and the Completions/Perfections"—sacred instruments kept inside the folded breastpiece by which the high priest sought divine verdicts (*mishpat*) for Israel.
- **v. 36 `צִיץ` (*tsits*):** A gleaming gold forehead plate or blossom-medallion inscribed `קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה` (*qodesh la-YHWH*, "Holiness to YHWH"), bearing any ritual impurity attached to Israel's consecrated offerings (v. 38).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **1 Samuel 23:9–12 & 30:7–8** (priestly inquiry via the ephod): *David calls for Abiathar the priest to bring the ephod in order to seek decisive yes-or-no guidance from the LORD.*
- **Ezra 2:63 & Nehemiah 7:65** (post-exilic absence of Urim and Thummim): *Returning exiles defer disputed priestly genealogies until a priest arises who can consult Urim and Thummim.*
- **Hebrews 7:26–28 & 9:7–12** (high-priestly mediation): *Contrasts the Aaronic high priest wearing garments to bear guilt before God with Christ's once-for-all priestly entry into the heavenly sanctuary.*
- **Revelation 21:19–21** (twelve foundation stones): *The New Jerusalem's twelve jeweled foundations echo the twelve engraved tribal stones set on the high priest's breastpiece.*
