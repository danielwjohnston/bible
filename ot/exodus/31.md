## Clear & Faithful

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:
**2** "See, I have called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.
**3** I have filled him with the Spirit of God—with wisdom, with discernment, with knowledge, and with every kind of craftsmanship—
**4** to conceive artistic designs, to work in gold, silver, and bronze,
**5** in stonecutting for settings, and in woodcarving, to execute every kind of skilled craft.
**6** Moreover, I myself have appointed with him Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and into the heart of every wise-hearted artisan I have placed wisdom, so that they may make everything I have commanded you:
**7** the tent of meeting, the ark of the testimony, the atonement cover upon it, and all the furnishings of the tent;
**8** the table and its utensils, the pure lampstand and all its utensils, and the altar of incense;
**9** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand;
**10** the woven service garments, the sacred vestments for Aaron the priest, and the vestments for his sons to serve as priests;
**11** as well as the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the sanctuary. Exactly as I have commanded you, they shall make them."
**12** Then the LORD said to Moses:
**13** "Speak to the people of Israel and say: 'Above all, you shall keep my Sabbaths, for this is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, so that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.
**14** Keep the Sabbath, therefore, because it is holy to you. Whoever profanes it shall surely be put to death; indeed, anyone who performs work on it, that person shall be cut off from among his people.
**15** For six days work may be done, but on the seventh day there is a Sabbath of complete rest, holy to the LORD; whoever does any work on the Sabbath day shall surely be put to death.
**16** Thus the Israelites shall keep the Sabbath, observing the Sabbath throughout their generations as a perpetual covenant.
**17** It is a lasting sign between me and the people of Israel forever, for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.'"
**18** When he had finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, he gave Moses the two tablets of the testimony, tablets of stone inscribed by the finger of God.

## Everyday English

**1** The LORD then told Moses:
**2** "Look, I've personally chosen Bezalel—son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah—
**3** and I've filled him with God's Spirit, giving him extraordinary skill, insight, expertise, and mastery across every craft:
**4** designing intricate patterns, working with gold, silver, and bronze,
**5** cutting and setting gemstones, carving timber, and handling every type of fine handiwork.
**6** I've also paired him with Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, and I've given creative skill to all the gifted craftspeople so they can build everything I ordered you to make:
**7** the meeting tent, the chest for the covenant tablets, the gold lid of atonement on top of it, and all the tent's gear;
**8** the bread table with its dishes, the pure-gold lampstand with its tools, and the incense altar;
**9** the altar for burnt offerings with all its tools, plus the washbasin and its stand;
**10** the special woven transport cloths, the sacred outfits for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons' priestly service;
**11** along with the anointing oil and the sweet incense for the holy sanctuary. They must craft it all strictly according to my instructions."
**12** The LORD also told Moses:
**13** "Tell the Israelites, 'Make sure you honor my Sabbaths above everything else, because the Sabbath is a sign between me and you across every generation, showing that I am the LORD who sets you apart as holy.
**14** Treat the Sabbath as sacred. Anyone who desecrates it must be put to death; whoever does work on that day will be cut off from their community.
**15** You have six days to get your work done, but the seventh day is a complete Sabbath of total rest, dedicated to the LORD. Anyone who works on the Sabbath day must be executed.
**16** The Israelites must guard the Sabbath and keep observing it generation after generation as a permanent covenant pledge.
**17** It stands forever as a sign between me and Israel, because in six days the LORD created the sky and the earth, and on the seventh day he stopped working and caught his breath.'"
**18** When the LORD finished speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, he handed him the two stone covenant tablets, written directly by the finger of God.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים בְּחָכְמָה וּבִתְבוּנָה וּבְדַעַת` (*ruach Elohim be-chokhmah u-vi-tevunah u-ve-da'at*):** "Spirit of God in wisdom, understanding, and knowledge"—the first time in Scripture a person is said to be filled with the Spirit of God, specifically for artistic craftsmanship (echoing the triad of Proverbs 3:19–20).
- **v. 10 `בִּגְדֵי הַשְּׂרָד` (*bigde ha-serad*):** "Woven/plaited service garments"—rendered either as finely woven priestly vestments or the protective woven cloths used to wrap sanctuary furnishings during transport (cf. Num 4:5–14).
- **v. 15 `שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן` (*shabbat shabbaton*):** Intensive cognate pairing, "a Sabbath of complete/solemn rest"—highlighting absolute cessation from tabernacle construction as well as daily labor.
- **v. 17 `שָׁבַת וַיִּנָּפַשׁ` (*shavat vayyinnapash*):** "He ceased and was refreshed"—*vayyinnapash* (Niphal of *n-p-sh*, linked to *nefesh* "breath/soul") anthropomorphically portrays the Creator "catching his breath" in delight over completed creation (cf. Exod 23:12).
- **v. 18 `בְּאֶצְבַּע אֱלֹהִים` (*be-etsba Elohim*):** "By the finger of God"—evokes the same divine power phrase used by Egypt's magicians in Exod 8:15[19] and later cited by Jesus in Luke 11:20.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 2:2–3** (creation Sabbath): *Exodus 31:17 explicitly grounds Israel's weekly Sabbath rest in God's seventh-day cessation after making heaven and earth.*
- **Proverbs 3:19–20 & Isaiah 11:2** (wisdom, understanding, knowledge): *Apply the same Spirit-given triad of wisdom, discernment, and knowledge from Bezalel's endowment to cosmic creation and royal leadership.*
- **Mark 2:27 & Hebrews 4:9–10** (Sabbath purpose and rest): *Reflect on the Sabbath as a gift and sign of God's own rest for his people.*
- **2 Corinthians 3:3** (stone tablets vs. heart): *Contrasts tablets of stone written with ink or by God's finger (Exod 31:18) with the Spirit writing on human hearts.*
