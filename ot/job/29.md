## Clear & Faithful

**1** Job again took up his discourse and said:
**2** "How I long to be back in the months of bygone years, in the days when God kept guard over me,
**3** when he made his lamp shine overhead, and guided by his radiance I walked right through the dark—
**4** just as I was in the days of my vigor, when God's intimate counsel rested over my tent,
**5** while the Almighty still lingered beside me, and my children gathered around my home,
**6** when my steps were bathed in cream, and the rock poured out streams of olive oil beside me!
**7** When I went out to the gate beside the town, and set my seat in the public square,
**8** the young men saw me and stepped back, and the elders rose and remained standing.
**9** Chieftains held back their words and pressed a palm against their lips;
**10** the voice of rulers fell silent, and their tongues stuck to the roof of their mouths.
**11** Whenever an ear heard of me, it blessed me, and whenever an eye saw me, it commended me,
**12** because I rescued the poor who cried out for aid, and the orphan who had no one to help.
**13** The blessing of the dying came upon me, and I made the widow's heart sing for joy.
**14** I clothed myself in righteousness, and it wore me like a garment; my justice was like a robe and a royal turban.
**15** I served as eyes for those who were blind, and as feet for those who walked lame.
**16** I was a father to the destitute, and I searched out the legal cause of strangers I did not know.
**17** I broke the jaws of the wicked oppressor and tore the prey from between his teeth.
**18** Then I thought, 'I shall die in my own nest, and multiply my days like grains of sand,
**19** with my roots spread open to the waters and dew resting all night on my branches,
**20** my dignity ever fresh within me, and my bow constantly renewed in my hand.'
**21** People listened to me with eager expectation and kept silent for my advice.
**22** After I spoke, they added nothing further; my words fell gently upon them like rain.
**23** They waited for me as parched earth waits for showers, opening their mouths wide as for the spring rain.
**24** When they lost heart, I smiled on them, and they never quenched the brightness of my countenance.
**25** I chose their course and presided as leader; I lived like a king among his troops, like one who brings comfort to those who mourn."

## Everyday English

**1** Job continued his speech:
**2** "I wish I could go back to the months that are gone—back to the days when God protected me!
**3** Back then, his lamp lit up the space over my head, and his glow guided me safely through the dark.
**4** Those were the prime years of my life, when God was a close friend inside my home,
**5** when the Almighty was right there beside me and my kids were gathered around me,
**6** when everything overflowed—my paths drenched in rich butter, and solid rock gushing streams of oil!
**7** Whenever I walked out to the city gate and took my seat in the town plaza,
**8** young guys would see me and step aside, and older men would rise to their feet in respect.
**9** Community leaders stopped talking and pressed their hands over their mouths.
**10** Even high-ranking officials lowered their voices until their tongues went dry.
**11** Anyone who heard about me spoke well of me, and anyone who watched me vouched for my character.
**12** Why? Because I stepped in to save helpless people begging for rescue, and orphans who had nobody in their corner.
**13** People on the brink of ruin blessed me, and I gave widows a reason to celebrate again.
**14** Doing right was what I wore every day—justice wrapped around me like a fine coat and crown.
**15** I served as eyes for people who couldn't see, and legs for those who couldn't walk.
**16** I acted like a father to the impoverished, and I investigated lawsuits even for complete strangers.
**17** I smashed the fangs of ruthless predators and ripped their victims straight from their jaws.
**18** I used to tell myself, 'I'll grow old and pass away peacefully at home, with days as countless as grains of sand.
**19** My roots reach deep into underground water, and morning dew settles on my leaves.
**20** My honor stays vibrant, and the bow in my grip never loses its strength.'
**21** People hung on my every word, waiting quietly to hear what I would suggest.
**22** Once I finished speaking, nobody felt the need to argue; my advice soaked into them slowly.
**23** They waited for my voice the way dry fields wait for a downpour, soaking up every word like spring rain.
**24** Whenever they felt discouraged, a smile from me lifted them up; their doubts never dimmed my confidence.
**25** I mapped out the direction they should go and sat at the head of the table—living like a commander among loyal soldiers, or like someone bringing real comfort to grieving friends."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `חָרְפִּי` (*chorpi*):** Literally "my autumn/harvest season" (often rendered "my prime" or "maturity"), evoking the productive fullness of life before winter adversity.
- **v. 4 `בְּסוֹד אֱלוֹהַּ` (*be-sod Eloah*):** *Sod* denotes an intimate council, confidential friendship, or protective presence (compare Ps 25:14), here hovering over Job's household.
- **v. 14 `צֶדֶק` (*tsedeq*) & `מִשְׁפָּט` (*mishpat*):** Righteousness and justice personified as royal garments—Job wears righteousness and righteousness in turn "clothes" him (`וַיִּלְבָּשֵׁנִי`).
- **v. 18 `וְכַחוֹל` (*ve-kha-chol*):** *Chol* normally means "sand" (symbolizing vast number, Gen 22:17), though early Jewish and rabbinic tradition also linked it to the mythical phoenix bird that renews its life from its nest (`קִנִּי`).
- **v. 22 `עָלֵימוֹ` (*aleymo*):** Archaic poetic preposition with the `-mo` 3mp suffix ("upon them"), characteristic of Job's elevated poetic style.
- **v. 24 `לֹא יַאֲמִינוּ` (*lo ya'aminu*):** Ambiguous syntax: either "they could hardly believe [my kindness]" or "when they lacked confidence / despaired, my smile reassured them."

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Isaiah 59:17** (imagery parallel): Both passages portray righteousness as clothing put on like a garment or armor in defense of justice.
- **Psalm 25:14** (thematic parallel on *sod*): Echoes the intimate friendship (*sod*) of God granted to those who revere him.
- **Genesis 22:17** (numerical simile): Uses sand (*chol*) as the emblem of uncountable abundance, mirrored here in Job's expectation of countless days.

**3** when his lamp shone on my head, and by his light I walked through darkness,
**4** as I was in my prime, when the friendship of God was in my tent,
**5** when the Almighty was yet with me, and my children were around me,
**6** when my steps were washed with butter, and the rock poured out streams of oil for me,
**7** when I went out to the city gate, when I prepared my seat in the street.
**8** The young men saw me and hid themselves. The aged rose up and stood.
**9** The princes refrained from talking, and laid their hand on their mouth.
**10** The voice of the nobles was hushed, and their tongue stuck to the roof of their mouth.
**11** For when the ear heard me, then it blessed me, and when the eye saw me, it commended me,
**12** because I delivered the poor who cried, and the fatherless also, who had no one to help him,
**13** the blessing of him who was ready to perish came on me, and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy.
**14** I put on righteousness, and it clothed me. My justice was as a robe and a diadem.
**15** I was eyes to the blind, and feet to the lame.
**16** I was a father to the needy. I researched the cause of him whom I didn’t know.
**17** I broke the jaws of the unrighteous and plucked the prey out of his teeth.
**18** Then I said, ‘I will die in my own house, I will count my days as the sand.
**19** My root is spread out to the waters. The dew lies all night on my branch.
**20** My glory is fresh in me. My bow is renewed in my hand.’
**21** “Men listened to me, waited, and kept silence for my counsel.
**22** After my words they didn’t speak again. My speech fell on them.
**23** They waited for me as for the rain. Their mouths drank as with the spring rain.
**24** I smiled on them when they had no confidence. They didn’t reject the light of my face.
**25** I chose out their way, and sat as chief. I lived as a king in the army, as one who comforts the mourners.
