## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite replied and said:
**2** "How long will you go on uttering such things, letting the words of your mouth blow like a gale?
**3** Would God ever twist justice, or would the Almighty bend what is right?
**4** If your own children sinned against him, he handed them over to the penalty of their rebellion.
**5** Yet if you yourself seek God earnestly and plead for mercy from the Almighty,
**6** if you truly are clean and upright, even now he will stir himself on your behalf and make your rightful home whole again.
**7** Though your former state seems small, your latter end will flourish beyond measure.
**8** Just ask the earlier generation and weigh what their ancestors searched out—
**9** for we were born only yesterday and know nothing; our days on earth pass like a fleeting shadow.
**10** Will they not instruct you and speak plainly to you, drawing out wisdom from deep within their hearts?
**11** Can papyrus shoot up where there is no marsh? Can reeds grow tall without water?
**12** Even while still green and uncut, they dry out sooner than any grass.
**13** Such is the destiny of everyone who forgets God; the hope of the godless withers away.
**14** What he leans on snaps like thread, and his security is no stronger than a spider's web.
**15** He presses against his house, yet it fails to hold; he clutches at it, yet it refuses to stand.
**16** Full of sap in the sunlight, his shoots spread across his garden;
**17** his roots wrap tightly around a pile of rocks, clinging fast among the stones.
**18** Yet once he is torn up from his spot, that very place disowns him: 'I never laid eyes on you!'
**19** Look—that is all the delight his career ever comes to, while strangers sprout from the very same soil.
**20** Truly, God never spurns the blameless, nor does he clasp the hand of evildoers.
**21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter and your lips with cries of triumph.
**22** Those who despise you will be clothed in disgrace, and the tent of the wicked will vanish."

## Everyday English

**1** Next, Bildad from Shuah spoke up:
**2** "How long are you going to keep talking like this? Your speech is just a loud blast of hot air!
**3** Does God ever bend the rules? Would the Almighty ever pervert true justice?
**4** Obviously, if your children sinned against him, he let their own wrongdoing catch up with them.
**5** But if you turn to God yourself and beg the Almighty for mercy—
**6** assuming you're actually innocent and honest—he'll step in for you right away and restore your good home!
**7** In fact, everything you started with will look tiny compared to how wildly you'll prosper in the end.
**8** Go ask the older generations; pay attention to what our ancestors learned from experience.
**9** Compared to them, we showed up yesterday and don't know a thing—our time on earth flickers by like a shadow.
**10** Won't they teach you and talk straight with you, sharing hard-earned lessons from the heart?
**11** Does papyrus grow tall without a swamp? Can marsh reeds thrive without water?
**12** Even while they're still lush and never cut down, they shrivel up faster than ordinary grass!
**13** That's exactly what happens to everybody who ignores God; a phony person's hope evaporates.
**14** Their confidence snaps easily, and what they trust in is fragile as a cobweb.
**15** Lean on that web-house and it caves in; grab onto it and it collapses under your weight.
**16** Sure, they look thriving and green in the sun, spreading vines all over their yard,
**17** wrapping roots around rock piles and anchoring between the stones—
**18** but the moment they're ripped out of the ground, their own home denies them: 'Never knew you!'
**19** So much for their happy life! Someone else springs right up out of the dirt in their place.
**20** Listen: God doesn't throw away people of integrity, and he doesn't prop up crooked people either.
**21** He can still fill your mouth with laughter and put shouts of joy back on your lips.
**22** Your enemies will end up wearing shame, and the homes of wicked people will disappear."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `וַיְשַׁלְּחֵם בְּיַד־פִּשְׁעָם` (*va-yeshallechem be-yad-pish'am*):** Literally "he sent them away into the hand [power/grip] of their rebellion." Bildad coldly applies strict retribution dogma directly to Job's dead children (contrast Job's priestly anxiety in 1:5).
- **v. 6 `וְשִׁלַּם נְוַת צִדְקֶךָ` (*ve-shillam nevat tsidqekha*):** *Shillam* ("make whole / restore / recompense") shares the root *sh-l-m* ("peace/wholeness") and anticipates Job's final restoration (`42:10–12`), though Bildad treats restoration as a neat transaction conditional on Job's purity.
- **v. 9 `תְמוֹל אֲנַחְנוּ` (*temol anachnu*):** Literally "yesterday [are] we"—a terse proverb mocking human short-sightedness relative to ancestral tradition (*dor rishon*, v. 8).
- **v. 14 `בֵּית עַכָּבִישׁ` (*beit akkavish*):** "A spider's house [web]"—Bildad's vivid metaphor for the illusory security of anyone who forgets God (*shokhechei El*).
- **v. 19 `מְשׂוֹשׂ דַּרְכּוֹ` (*mesos darko*):** "The joy of his way"—here tinged with biting irony ("that's all his boasted prosperity amounts to!"), as new shoots sprout from the dust in his place.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Psalm 1:3–4 & Jeremiah 17:5–8** (planted tree/reed imagery): Compare the flourishing vs. withering fates of those anchored in God against those cut off from living water.
- **Psalm 126:2** (laughter and joy): Echoes the promise of v. 21—"then our mouth was filled with laughter, and our tongue with shouts of joy"—when God reverses captivity and grief.
- **1 Chronicles 29:15 & Psalm 102:11 (MT 102:12)** (days like a shadow): Share Bildad's phrasing in v. 9 that mortal days pass over the earth like a fleeting shadow.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered,
**2** “How long will you speak these things? Shall the words of your mouth be a mighty wind?
**3** Does God pervert justice? Or does the Almighty pervert righteousness?
**4** If your children have sinned against him, he has delivered them into the hand of their disobedience.
**5** If you want to seek God diligently, make your supplication to the Almighty.
**6** If you were pure and upright, surely now he would awaken for you, and make the habitation of your righteousness prosperous.
**7** Though your beginning was small, yet your latter end would greatly increase.
**8** “Please inquire of past generations. Find out about the learning of their fathers.
**9** (For we are but of yesterday, and know nothing, because our days on earth are a shadow.)
**10** Shall they not teach you, tell you, and utter words out of their heart?
**11** “Can the papyrus grow up without mire? Can the rushes grow without water?
**12** While it is yet in its greenness, not cut down, it withers before any other reed.
**13** So are the paths of all who forget God. The hope of the godless man will perish,
**14** whose confidence will break apart, whose trust is a spider’s web.
**15** He will lean on his house, but it will not stand. He will cling to it, but it will not endure.
**16** He is green before the sun. His shoots go out along his garden.
**17** His roots are wrapped around the rock pile. He sees the place of stones.
**18** If he is destroyed from his place, then it will deny him, saying, ‘I have not seen you.’
**19** Behold, this is the joy of his way. Out of the earth, others will spring.
**20** “Behold, God will not cast away a blameless man, neither will he uphold the evildoers.
**21** He will still fill your mouth with laughter, your lips with shouting.
**22** Those who hate you will be clothed with shame. The tent of the wicked will be no more.”
