## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Job answered and said:
**2** "Even today my complaint remains defiant; heavy lies his hand upon my groaning.
**3** If only I knew where to find him, that I might reach his very court!
**4** I would lay my legal case out before him and fill my mouth with reasoned arguments.
**5** I would learn what words he uses to answer me and grasp whatever he says to me.
**6** Would he fight against me with brute force? No—surely he would hear me out!
**7** There an upright person could argue his case with him, and I would be acquitted once and for all by my Judge.
**8** Look: I walk eastward, and he is not there; westward, and I cannot perceive him;
**9** northward where he is at work, and I cannot lay hold of him; he turns southward, and I still cannot see him.
**10** Yet he knows the path I walk; when he has tested me, I will emerge like pure gold.
**11** My feet have stayed planted in his footprints; I have kept to his path without veering aside.
**12** I have not drifted from the command of his lips; more than my daily bread I have treasured the words of his mouth.
**13** Yet he remains unchangeable—who can dissuade him? Whatever his heart desires, that he carries out.
**14** Indeed, he will finish whatever he decreed for me, and plenty more like it lies ready in his store.
**15** That is why I tremble before him; when I think it over, I am gripped with dread of him.
**16** God has made my courage melt; the Almighty has thrown me into terror—
**17** yet I am not silenced by the darkness, even though thick gloom blankets my face."

## Everyday English

**1** Job replied:
**2** "My complaint is still bitter today—God's hand presses down hard even while I groan.
**3** I just wish I knew where to track him down! I'd walk straight into his courtroom,
**4** lay out my side of the story in front of him, and rattle off every piece of evidence I have.
**5** I'd finally hear what he has to say back and figure out what he's telling me.
**6** Would he just overpower me with raw strength? No—he'd actually pay attention to me!
**7** An honest person could get a fair hearing with him there, and my Judge would clear my name for good.
**8** Trouble is, I head east and he isn't there; I look west and can't spot him anywhere.
**9** I search north while he's busy working, and miss him; he slips south, and I still can't catch sight of him.
**10** Even so, he knows every step I've taken. Once he finishes testing me, I'll come out shining like gold!
**11** I've followed right in his footsteps, stayed on his road, and never wandered off.
**12** I've never walked away from what he told me; I've valued his words even more than my daily meals.
**13** Still, once God makes up his mind, nobody can change it—whatever he wants to do, he does.
**14** He's going to carry out his whole plan for me, and he has plenty more lined up just like it.
**15** No wonder I shake whenever I face him! The more I think about him, the more terrified I get.
**16** God has drained away my nerve; the Almighty has scared me stiff.
**17** Even so, this darkness hasn't shut me up—not even pitch-black night swallowing my face!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `מְרִי` (*meri*) & `יָדִי` (*yadi*):** *Meri* means "rebellious/defiant" (or by consonantal overlap with *mar*, "bitter"). Many interpreters read `יָדִי` ("my hand") as a scribal reverence-substitute or copyist slip for "his hand" (LXX), though "my hand" can mean "my power/restraint is weighed down despite my groaning."
- **v. 3 `תְּכוּנָתוֹ` (*tekhunato*):** "His fixed place / tribunal / seat" (from *kun*, "establish"). Job longs for an actual courtroom venue where God will grant a fair hearing.
- **v. 8–9 `קֶדֶם` / `אָחוֹר` / `שְׂמֹאול` / `יָמִין`:** Hebrew directional terms oriented eastward: *qedem* ("front" = east), *achor* ("back" = west), *semol* ("left" = north), *yamin* ("right" = south). Job searches all four compass points and finds God elusive.
- **v. 12 `מֵחֻקִּי` (*mechuqqi*):** *Choq* means "statute/decree" or "allotted portion of food" (cf. Prov 30:8)—parallel with v. 14 where *chuqqi* denotes God's fixed decree over Job's life.
- **v. 13 `וְהוּא בְאֶחָד` (*vehu ve'echad*):** Literally "and he is in one"—expressing God's singular, unyielding resolve ("of one mind / unchangeable").

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Psalm 17:3; 66:10; 1 Peter 1:7** (refining fire metaphor): Parallels Job's confidence in v. 10 that after God tests him in the crucible, he will emerge refined like pure gold.
- **Psalm 139:7–10** (searching the four compass directions): Contrasts strikingly with Job 23:8–9—where the psalmist cannot flee from God's inescapable presence in east or west, Job searches all four directions and cannot locate God to plead his case.
- **Jeremiah 15:16** (treasuring divine words): Echoes Job 23:12 in prizing the words of God's mouth above physical nourishment.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Job answered,
**2** “Even today my complaint is rebellious. His hand is heavy in spite of my groaning.
**3** Oh that I knew where I might find him! That I might come even to his seat!
**4** I would set my cause in order before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.
**5** I would know the words which he would answer me, and understand what he would tell me.
**6** Would he contend with me in the greatness of his power? No, but he would listen to me.
**7** There the upright might reason with him, so I should be delivered forever from my judge.
**8** “If I go east, he is not there. If I go west, I can’t find him.
**9** He works to the north, but I can’t see him. He turns south, but I can’t catch a glimpse of him.
**10** But he knows the way that I take. When he has tried me, I will come out like gold.
**11** My foot has held fast to his steps. I have kept his way, and not turned away.
**12** I haven’t gone back from the commandment of his lips. I have treasured up the words of his mouth more than my necessary food.
**13** But he stands alone, and who can oppose him? What his soul desires, even that he does.
**14** For he performs that which is appointed for me. Many such things are with him.
**15** Therefore I am terrified at his presence. When I consider, I am afraid of him.
**16** For God has made my heart faint. The Almighty has terrified me.
**17** Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither did he cover the thick darkness from my face.
