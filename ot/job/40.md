## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then the LORD answered Job and said:
**2** "Shall a faultfinder contend with the Almighty? Let the one who argues with God give an answer!"
**3** Job replied to the LORD with these words:
**4** "Behold, I am of small account—what can I reply to you? I lay my hand over my mouth.
**5** I have spoken once, and I will not answer; yes, twice, and I will say nothing more."
**6** Once more the LORD spoke to Job from within a tempest, saying:
**7** "Brace your loins like a warrior; I will question you, and you shall inform me!
**8** Would you really set aside my justice? Would you condemn me so that you might be proved right?
**9** Do you possess strength of arm to rival God, or can your voice roar in thunder like his?
**10** Deck yourself now in grandeur and loftiness; clothe yourself in splendor and majesty!
**11** Pour forth the overflow of your wrath; look upon every proud person and bring them low!
**12** Gaze on every arrogant one and humble them; trample the wicked right where they stand!
**13** Hide every one of them down in the soil together; shroud their faces in the secret gloom!
**14** Then I myself will acknowledge to you that your own right hand can deliver you!
**15** Look now at Behemoth, whom I made along with you: he feeds on grass just like an ox.
**16** See what strength is in his loins, and what power lies in the muscles of his belly!
**17** His tail sways rigid as a mountain cedar; the tendons across his thighs are braided tight.
**18** His limbs are cast like channels of bronze, and his frame like hammered rods of iron.
**19** He is the foremost of the works of God; only his Maker can draw near him with a sword.
**20** Indeed, the hills yield produce for him, where all the wild beasts of the field play.
**21** Beneath the lotus bushes he lies down, hidden among the reeds and the marsh.
**22** Spreading lotus branches canopy him in cool shadow, and streamside willows ring him round.
**23** Though a river rages violently, he does not panic; he stays unperturbed even when the Jordan surges against his mouth.
**24** Can anyone capture him while he watches, or pierce his nose with snares?"

## Everyday English

**1** Then the LORD put a direct question to Job:
**2** "Is a critic still going to argue with the Almighty? Let anyone who wants to correct God speak up now!"
**3** Job answered the LORD:
**4** "Look—I'm nobody special. What could I possibly say back to you? I'm clapping my hand over my mouth!
**5** I've said my piece once, and I won't reply; twice, and I'm completely done talking."
**6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the storm again:
**7** "Tighten your belt like a fighter—I've got more questions for you, and you're going to answer me!
**8** Are you really going to throw out my justice? Do you have to make me look guilty just so you can look innocent?
**9** Do you have muscles like God? Does your voice boom across the sky like his thunder?
**10** Go ahead—put on royal glory and towering majesty! Dress yourself in dazzling honor and splendor!
**11** Unleash waves of furious anger; spot every arrogant person and knock them down!
**12** Look straight at every proud bully and humble them; crush wicked people right where they stand!
**13** Hide all of them together in the dirt; wrap their faces in the dark grave!
**14** Do all that, and even I will praise you—because your own right hand could save you!
**15** Now take a close look at Behemoth, which I created right alongside you: he eats grass just like cattle.
**16** Look at the power packed into his hips, and the solid muscle across his belly!
**17** He swings his tail as stiff as a cedar trunk; the tendons in his thighs weave together like cables.
**18** His bones are solid pipes of bronze; his ribs are like heavy bars of iron.
**19** He's a masterpiece among God's creatures, and only the One who made him dares approach him with a sword.
**20** The mountains grow plenty of food for him, right where wild animals play nearby.
**21** He stretches out under shady lotus trees, tucked away in the reeds and swamp mud.
**22** Lotus branches cast cool shade over him, and riverside willows circle around him.
**23** If a wild river floods violently, he doesn't flinch—he stays completely calm even when the Jordan crashes straight into his mouth.
**24** Can anyone walk right up in front of him and catch him, or hook a ring through his nose with traps?"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `קַלֹּתִי` (*qalloti*):** From *qalal* ("to be light/small/insignificant"), contrasting with God's *kavod* ("weight/glory"); Job places his hand over his mouth (`לְמוֹ־פִי`, frozen poetic `לְמוֹ` with singular noun) in courtroom submission.
- **v. 6 `מִן סְעָרָה` (*min se'arah*, Qere) vs. `מנ סערה` (Ketiv):** As in 38:1, the Ketiv writes unpointed `מנ` without *nun* assimilation/vowel, while Qere reads `מִן סְעָרָה` ("out of a tempest," here indefinite without the article).
- **v. 15 `בְּהֵמוֹת` (*behemot*):** Intensive plural of *behemah* ("beast/cattle"), denoting a colossal beast—commonly linked both to the Nile hippopotamus (vv. 21–23) and to primordial chaos-fauna (v. 19: "first/chief of the ways of God," echoing `רֵאשִׁית` in Prov 8:22). Note WLC Job 40 ends at v. 24; vv. 25–32 in WLC correspond to English Job 41:1–8.
- **v. 17 `פַחֲדָיו` (*fachadav*, Qere) vs. `פחדו` (*fachdo*, Ketiv):** Plural Qere ("his thighs/sinews") vs. singular Ketiv ("his thigh").
- **v. 23 `יַעֲשֹׁק נָהָר` (*ya'ashoq nahar*):** *Ashaq* means "to press/oppress/rage violently"—even a raging torrent or surging Jordan (`יַרְדֵּן`) cannot rattle Behemoth.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Psalm 104:24–26** (creation wonders): Celebrates the sheer variety of God's creatures—large and small—fashioned in wisdom (vv. 15, 19).
- **Isaiah 45:21–24 & Psalm 93:1** (divine sovereignty): Mirrors YHWH's challenge in vv. 9–14 that majesty (`הוֹד וְהָדָר`), cosmic justice, and saving power belong uniquely to God alone.
- **Proverbs 8:22** (use of *reshit*): Uses `רֵאשִׁית דַּרְכּוֹ` ("beginning/foremost of his way"), echoed in Behemoth as `רֵאשִׁית דַּרְכֵי־אֵל` (v. 19).

**1** Moreover the LORD answered Job,
**2** “Shall he who argues contend with the Almighty? He who argues with God, let him answer it.”
**3** Then Job answered the LORD,
**4** “Behold, I am of small account. What will I answer you? I lay my hand on my mouth.
**5** I have spoken once, and I will not answer; Yes, twice, but I will proceed no further.”
**6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind:
**7** “Now brace yourself like a man. I will question you, and you will answer me.
**8** Will you even annul my judgment? Will you condemn me, that you may be justified?
**9** Or do you have an arm like God? Can you thunder with a voice like him?
**10** “Now deck yourself with excellency and dignity. Array yourself with honor and majesty.
**11** Pour out the fury of your anger. Look at everyone who is proud, and bring him low.
**12** Look at everyone who is proud, and humble him. Crush the wicked in their place.
**13** Hide them in the dust together. Bind their faces in the hidden place.
**14** Then I will also admit to you that your own right hand can save you.
**15** “See now behemoth, which I made as well as you. He eats grass as an ox.
**16** Look now, his strength is in his thighs. His force is in the muscles of his belly.
**17** He moves his tail like a cedar. The sinews of his thighs are knit together.
**18** His bones are like tubes of bronze. His limbs are like bars of iron.
**19** He is the chief of the ways of God. He who made him gives him his sword.
**20** Surely the mountains produce food for him, where all the animals of the field play.
**21** He lies under the lotus trees, in the covert of the reed, and the marsh.
**22** The lotuses cover him with their shade. The willows of the brook surround him.
**23** Behold, if a river overflows, he doesn’t tremble. He is confident, though the Jordan swells even to his mouth.
**24** Shall any take him when he is on the watch, or pierce through his nose with a snare?
