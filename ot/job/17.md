## Clear & Faithful

**1** "My spirit is shattered, my days are snuffed out, and only the grave awaits me.
**2** Mockers surround me on every side, and my eyes must dwell on their constant taunting.
**3** Put up a pledge for me yourself, O God—who else is willing to clasp my hand as guarantor?
**4** For you have closed their minds to discernment; therefore you will not let them triumph.
**5** Whoever betrays his friends for a share of profit will watch his own children's eyes grow dim.
**6** God has made me a cautionary proverb among the nations—I have become a target where people spit in my face.
**7** My eyes have grown cloudy with grief, and all my limbs have wasted to a shadow.
**8** Upright people are stunned at such a sight, and the innocent stir with indignation against the godless.
**9** Still the righteous person holds steady to his path, and the one with clean hands gathers fresh strength.
**10** As for all of you, come back and try again—yet I will not find a single wise person among you!
**11** My days have slipped away; my plans lie snapped apart, along with every cherished desire of my heart.
**12** These men try to pass off night as day; right in the teeth of darkness they claim, 'Light is close at hand!'
**13** If the only home I wait for is Sheol, if I have already spread my bed in the gloom,
**14** if I have called out to the Pit, 'You are my father,' and named the worm 'my mother' and 'my sister'—
**15** where then is my hope? As for my hope, who can spot any trace of it?
**16** It will sink down behind the barred gates of Sheol, when together we settle into the dust."

## Everyday English

**1** "I'm completely worn out; my life is flickering out like a dying candle, and the cemetery is waiting for me.
**2** All I see around me are sneering critics—I can't get away from their endless insults.
**3** God, please step up and back me yourself! Nobody else here is going to shake my hand and vouch for me.
**4** You've shut their minds off from real understanding, so don't let them come out on top.
**5** Anyone who sells out friends for personal gain will see his own kids go hungry.
**6** God has turned me into a public laughingstock; strangers feel free to spit right in my face.
**7** Crying has blurred my vision, and my whole body has shrunk away to nothing.
**8** Decent people look at what's happening to me in shock, and honest folks get angry at frauds who prosper.
**9** Even so, anyone who does right keeps walking straight ahead, and people with honest hands grow stronger every day.
**10** Look at you all—go ahead, take another shot at lecturing me! I still won't find one sensible person in the bunch.
**11** My time's up; everything I planned is ruined, every dream I held close is broken.
**12** Yet you keep insisting night is daylight, claiming dawn is right around the corner when pitch-black dark is all we have.
**13** The grave is the only home left for me to expect; I've already made my bed down in the shadows.
**14** I'm ready to call the tomb 'Dad,' and tell the worms, 'You're my mom and sister.'
**15** So tell me—what hope do I actually have left? Who can point to any future for me?
**16** Whatever hope I had is going straight down through death's gates, when we both lie down together in the dirt."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `עָרְבֵנִי` (*arveni*) & `לְיָדִי יִתָּקֵעַ` (*leyadi yittaqa*):** Legal commercial language—"stand surety/bail for me" and "strike [palms] into my hand" (the handshake sealing a surety bond, cf. Prov 6:1). Job asks God himself to post bail against God's own prosecution!
- **v. 6 `וְתֹפֶת לְפָנִים` (*vetofet lephanim*):** *Tofet* here derives from *tuf* ("spit") rather than the Topheth valley name: "a spitting in the face" (or "one before whom people spit").
- **v. 11 `מוֹרָשֵׁי לְבָבִי` (*morashei levavi*):** From *yarash* ("possess"), meaning "the cherished possessions/desires of my heart" (or "heartstrings").
- **v. 16 `בַּדֵּי שְׁאֹל` (*baddei She'ol*):** *Bad* denotes a bar, pole, or gate-bolt; here "the bars/gates of Sheol" enclosing the underworld (`תֵּרַדְנָה`, 3fp verb referring back to `תִּקְוָתִי` / plans, or read with LXX as "Will you go down with me?").

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Proverbs 6:1–2; 17:18** (shared legal idiom): Striking palms (*taqa kaf*) signifies assuming financial or legal liability as guarantor—the exact gesture Job appeals to in v. 3 when he asks God to vouch for him.
- **Psalm 88:10–12** (shared lament motif): Mirrors Job's despairing descent toward the Pit (*shachat*) and darkness in vv. 13–16, where human expectation seems extinguished in dust.
- **Isaiah 38:10** (parallel underworld imagery): Hezekiah similarly speaks of entering the "gates of Sheol" in mid-life crisis, paralleling the "bars of Sheol" in Job 17:16.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** “My spirit is consumed. My days are extinct and the grave is ready for me.
**2** Surely there are mockers with me. My eye dwells on their provocation.
**3** “Now give a pledge. Be collateral for me with yourself. Who is there who will strike hands with me?
**4** For you have hidden their heart from understanding, therefore you will not exalt them.
**5** He who denounces his friends for plunder, even the eyes of his children will fail.
**6** “But he has made me a byword of the people. They spit in my face.
**7** My eye also is dim by reason of sorrow. All my members are as a shadow.
**8** Upright men will be astonished at this. The innocent will stir himself up against the godless.
**9** Yet the righteous will hold to his way. He who has clean hands will grow stronger and stronger.
**10** But as for you all, come back. I will not find a wise man among you.
**11** My days are past. My plans are broken off, as are the thoughts of my heart.
**12** They change the night into day, saying ‘The light is near’ in the presence of darkness.
**13** If I look for Sheol as my house, if I have spread my couch in the darkness,
**14** if I have said to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ and ‘My sister,’
**15** where then is my hope? As for my hope, who will see it?
**16** Shall it go down with me to the gates of Sheol, or descend together into the dust?”
