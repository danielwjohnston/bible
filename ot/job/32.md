## Clear & Faithful

**1** At that point these three men quit replying to Job, since he remained convinced of his own innocence.
**2** Then the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the clan of Ram, burned hot. His anger burned against Job because he justified himself rather than God;
**3** and his anger also burned against Job's three friends because they had found no answer, yet had declared Job in the wrong.
**4** Elihu had held back from addressing Job until then, because all the others surpassed him in years.
**5** But when Elihu saw that there was no answer left in the mouths of those three men, his anger flared up.
**6** So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said: "I am young in years, while you are elderly; therefore I held back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you.
**7** I thought, 'Age ought to speak, and many years should teach wisdom.'
**8** Yet it is the spirit within a mortal, the breath of the Almighty, that gives them understanding.
**9** It is not the old alone who are wise, nor the aged who always discern what is just.
**10** Therefore I say, 'Listen to me; let me declare my knowledge as well.'
**11** Look, I waited patiently for your words; I listened closely to your arguments while you searched for what to say.
**12** I gave you my full attention, and behold, none of you refuted Job, none among you answered his claims.
**13** Do not say, 'We have discovered wisdom; let God vanquish him, not any human!'
**14** Job has not aimed his arguments at me, nor will I reply to him with your speeches.
**15** They are confounded and answer no more; words have deserted them.
**16** Shall I wait because they no longer speak, because they stand silent and offer no reply?
**17** I too will answer my part; I too will declare what I know.
**18** For I am bursting with words; the spirit within me compels me.
**19** Look, my belly is like unvented wine; like brand-new wineskins, it is ready to burst.
**20** Let me speak so that I may find relief; let me open my lips and answer!
**21** I will show partiality to no one, nor will I flatter any human being;
**22** for I do not know how to flatter—otherwise my Maker would swiftly sweep me away."

## Everyday English

**1** So the three friends gave up arguing with Job, since he insisted he was innocent.
**2** At that point, a younger man named Elihu (son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram) was furious. He was angry at Job for defending himself instead of defending God,
**3** and he was just as angry at Job's three friends because they couldn't come up with a solid rebuttal, yet still condemned Job anyway.
**4** Elihu had stayed quiet while the others talked to Job because they were all older than he was.
**5** But once he saw that the three men had run completely out of things to say, his temper boiled over.
**6** So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite spoke up: "I'm young, and all of you are elderly, so I kept my mouth shut and felt nervous about sharing my perspective with you.
**7** I told myself, 'Older people should do the talking; years of experience bring wisdom.'
**8** But actually, it's God's Spirit inside a person—the breath of the Almighty—that gives real insight!
**9** Being old doesn't automatically make someone wise, and advanced age doesn't guarantee sound judgment.
**10** That's why I'm asking you to hear me out: it's my turn to tell you what I know.
**11** Look, I sat here waiting for your arguments. I listened carefully to your reasoning while you hunted for the right words.
**12** I paid close attention to all of you, and honestly, not a single one of you proved Job wrong or answered what he actually said.
**13** Don't try to excuse yourselves by saying, 'We found true wisdom, so let God defeat him—humans can't!'
**14** Job didn't aim his complaints at me, and I'm certainly not going to answer him using your worn-out arguments.
**15** Look at them—stunned into silence, completely out of words!
**16** Am I supposed to keep waiting just because they've stopped talking and stand there speechless?
**17** No—I'll speak my piece too! I'll share what I've observed.
**18** I'm packed full of things to say; the spirit inside me is pushing me to speak.
**19** Inside, I feel like sealed wine fermenting—ready to pop right through brand-new wineskins!
**20** I need to talk just to breathe easy again; I'm going to open my mouth and respond.
**21** I won't play favorites with anybody, and I won't butter anyone up with sweet talk.
**22** Truth is, I don't even know how to flatter people—and if I tried, my Creator would wipe me out in no time!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `מֵאֱלֹהִים` (*me-Elohim*):** Ambiguous comparative/causal construction: either "justifying himself *rather than* God" (putting his own righteousness above God's) or "proving himself *more righteous than* God."
- **v. 8 `רוּחַ־הִיא בֶאֱנוֹשׁ` (*ruach-hi ve-enosh*):** Elihu attributes insight not to seniority (`רַבִּים`/`זְקֵנִים`) but to the divine breath/spirit (`רוּחַ`/`נְשָׁמָה`, echoing Gen 2:7 and Job 27:3; 33:4) active within human beings.
- **v. 11 `מִלִּין` (*millin*):** Aramaizing masculine plural ending (`-in` alongside Hebrew `-im` in vv. 15, 18), a recurring stylistic signature of Elihu's speeches and Job's poetry.
- **v. 19 `כְּאֹבוֹת חֲדָשִׁים` (*ke-ovot chadashim*):** New leather wineskins burst if fermenting wine is sealed without ventilation (`לֹא־יִפָּתֵחַ`); Elihu portrays his pent-up speech as irresistible internal pressure.
- **v. 21 `אֲכַנֶּה` (*akhanneh*):** From *kanah*, "to give an honorary title / flatter" (paired with `אֶשָּׂא פְנֵי־אִישׁ`, literally "lift up the face of a man," i.e., show partiality).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 2:7** (creation anthropology): The pairing of spirit/breath (`רוּחַ` / `נְשָׁמָה`) from the Almighty giving life and understanding (v. 8) recalls God breathing life into humanity at creation.
- **Matthew 9:17 / Mark 2:22 / Luke 5:37** (proverbial wineskin imagery): Echoes the ancient Near Eastern awareness of fermenting wine stretching and bursting wineskins (v. 19).
- **1 Timothy 4:12** (age-and-wisdom motif): Parallels Elihu's claim in vv. 6–9 that spiritual insight is not restricted to advanced years.

**1** So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.
**2** Then the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel, the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was kindled against Job. His wrath was kindled because he justified himself rather than God.
**3** Also his wrath was kindled against his three friends, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.
**4** Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job, because they were older than he.
**5** When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, his wrath was kindled.
**6** Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered, “I am young, and you are very old. Therefore I held back, and didn’t dare show you my opinion.
**7** I said, ‘Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.’
**8** But there is a spirit in man, and the Spirit of the Almighty gives them understanding.
**9** It is not the great who are wise, nor the aged who understand justice.
**10** Therefore I said, ‘Listen to me; I also will show my opinion.’
**11** “Behold, I waited for your words, and I listened for your reasoning, while you searched out what to say.
**12** Yes, I gave you my full attention, but there was no one who convinced Job, or who answered his words, among you.
**13** Beware lest you say, ‘We have found wisdom. God may refute him, not man;’
**14** for he has not directed his words against me; neither will I answer him with your speeches.
**15** “They are amazed. They answer no more. They don’t have a word to say.
**16** Shall I wait, because they don’t speak, because they stand still, and answer no more?
**17** I also will answer my part, and I also will show my opinion.
**18** For I am full of words. The spirit within me constrains me.
**19** Behold, my breast is as wine which has no vent; like new wineskins it is ready to burst.
**20** I will speak, that I may be refreshed. I will open my lips and answer.
**21** Please don’t let me respect any man’s person, neither will I give flattering titles to any man.
**22** For I don’t know how to give flattering titles, or else my Maker would soon take me away.
