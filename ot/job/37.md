## Clear & Faithful

**1** "At this too my heart pounds and leaps right out of its place!
**2** Listen closely to the roar of his voice, to the rumbling thunder that rolls from his mouth!
**3** He sends it forth beneath the entire sky, and his lightning flashes to the furthest edges of the earth.
**4** Behind it a voice bellows; he thunders with his majestic voice, and he does not hold back his lightnings when his voice resounds.
**5** God thunders marvelously with his voice; he performs great wonders beyond our understanding.
**6** For he commands the snow, 'Fall upon the earth!' and to the downpour, the torrential rains of his strength.
**7** He seals up the hand of every mortal, so that all people he has made may acknowledge his work.
**8** Then the wild beasts retreat to their lairs and remain inside their dens.
**9** Out of its inner chamber sweeps the tempest, and biting cold arrives with the scattering northern winds.
**10** By the breath of God ice is formed, and the broad expanse of waters freezes solid.
**11** He also loads the heavy cloud with moisture and scatters his lightning-laden clouds across the sky.
**12** Guided by his skillful direction, the clouds wheel round and round to accomplish whatever he commands them across the face of the habitable earth—
**13** whether he dispatches them as a rod of correction, or for his land, or as an act of steadfast mercy.
**14** Pay heed to these things, Job! Pause where you stand and ponder God's marvels.
**15** Do you know how God lays his command upon them, and makes the lightning flash from his storm cloud?
**16** Do you understand the balancing of the billowing clouds—those marvels of the one who is complete in knowledge—
**17** you whose clothes grow sweltering when the earth lies hushed beneath the south wind?
**18** Can you beat out the skies alongside him, hard and shining like a mirror of cast bronze?
**19** Teach us what we ought to say to him! We cannot draw up a case because of our darkness.
**20** Should he even be told that I wish to speak? Would anyone ask to be swallowed up?
**21** Right now people cannot look at the sunlight when it shines brilliantly in the skies after the wind has swept past and cleared them.
**22** From the northern horizon golden radiance advances; terrifying glory surrounds God.
**23** The Almighty—we cannot reach him; he is exalted in power, yet in justice and abundant righteousness he does not oppress.
**24** Therefore mortals revere him; he pays no regard to any who think themselves wise in heart."

## Everyday English

**1** "Honestly, just thinking about this makes my heart pound and jump in my chest!
**2** Listen to the thunderous boom of his voice—the deep rumble rolling straight from his mouth!
**3** He lets it rip across the whole sky and sends bolts of lightning to the ends of the earth.
**4** Right behind the flash comes a mighty roar; his majestic voice thunders, and once he speaks, he doesn't hold the storm back.
**5** God's thunder shakes the sky with wonders—he pulls off astonishing things we can't even begin to fathom.
**6** He gives the order to the snow, 'Drop down on the ground!' and tells heavy rainstorms, 'Pour down hard!'
**7** He puts a stop to everyone's outdoor work so that every person he created recognizes what he's doing.
**8** Wild animals slip back into their caves and stay curled up inside their dens.
**9** Fierce gales burst out of God's storm rooms, and icy blasts blow in with the freezing winds.
**10** One breath from God turns water into ice, freezing wide lakes and rivers rock-solid.
**11** He packs thick clouds full of water and spreads his bright lightning clouds across the horizon.
**12** Under his expert steering, the storms circle wherever he sends them, carrying out every single order across the entire globe—
**13** whether he sends rain as discipline, to water his earth, or to show loyal kindness.
**14** Pay attention to this, Job! Stop and take a long look at God's breathtaking power!
**15** Do you have any idea how God commands the weather or lights up a storm cloud with a flash of lightning?
**16** Can you explain how massive rainclouds stay suspended in midair—the handiwork of the One who knows everything—
**17** when you're already sweating in your own clothes the moment a hot south wind stills the land?
**18** Could you hammer out the sky canopy beside him until it's as solid as a polished bronze mirror?
**19** Tell us what we're even supposed to say to someone like that! In our ignorance, we wouldn't know where to start.
**20** Should anyone announce to God that I want to debate him? Nobody in their right mind begs to be wiped out!
**21** Look up: when the wind sweeps the clouds clean, the sun blazes so brightly in the clear sky that nobody can stare straight at it.
**22** Golden light pours down from the north—God is wrapped in terrifying glory!
**23** We can't possibly track down or comprehend the Almighty; his power has no equal, yet with total fairness and justice he never abuses anyone.
**24** That's why people stand in awe of him—and he isn't impressed by anyone who acts wise in their own eyes."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 6 `הֱוֵא` (*heve*):** An archaic/Aramaizing imperative of *hayah* ("Be!" / "Fall!"), commanding the snow just as in creation fiat.
- **v. 7 `יַחְתּוֹם` (*yachtom*):** "He seals up the hand of every mortal"—winter storms halt human outdoor labor, compelling all people to recognize divine governance.
- **v. 12 `בְּתַחְבּוּלֹתָיו` (*be-tachbulotav*, Qere) vs. `בְּתַחְבּוּלָתוֹ` (*be-tachbulato*, Ketiv):** Plural Qere ("by his skillful directions/counsels," a nautical steering metaphor from *chevel* "rope") vs. singular Ketiv ("by his guidance").
- **v. 18 `תַּרְקִיעַ` (*tarqi'a*):** Hiphil of *raqa* ("hammer out / spread flat," matching Gen 1:6's `רָקִיעַ` *raqia*), depicting the sky dome as hammered metal like a cast bronze mirror (`רְאִי מוּצָק`).
- **v. 22 `מִצָּפוֹן זָהָב יֶאֱתֶה` (*mitsafon zahav ye'eteh*):** "Out of the north comes golden splendor"—evoking both clearing northern storm light and the cosmic mountain of divine assembly (compare Isa 14:13; Ps 48:2), immediately anticipating YHWH's whirlwind arrival in 38:1.
- **v. 24 `לֹא־יִרְאֶה כָּל־חַכְמֵי־לֵב` (*lo-yir'eh kol-chakhmei-lev*):** Wordplay between `יְרֵאוּהוּ` (*yere'uhu*, "they fear/revere him," from *yare*) and `יִרְאֶה` (*yir'eh*, "he regards/looks upon," from *ra'ah*): God ignores the self-styled "wise of heart."

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 1:6–8** (firmament verb): Uses the root *raqa* (`תַּרְקִיעַ`, v. 18) for beating out the sky expanse.
- **Psalm 147:16–18** (winter weather motif): Echoes God scattering snow, freezing ice by his breath/cold, and commanding the wind to melt/clear the waters (vv. 6, 10, 21).
- **Psalm 48:2 & Isaiah 14:13** (northern cosmic mountain): Connects the radiant "north" (`צָפוֹן`, v. 22) with the dwelling of divine majesty.
- **Proverbs 3:7 & 26:12** (humility vs. self-conceit): Parallels v. 24's warning against those who regard themselves as "wise in their own heart."

**1** “Yes, at this my heart trembles, and is moved out of its place.
**2** Hear, oh, hear the noise of his voice, the sound that goes out of his mouth.
**3** He sends it out under the whole sky, and his lightning to the ends of the earth.
**4** After it a voice roars. He thunders with the voice of his majesty. He doesn’t hold back anything when his voice is heard.
**5** God thunders marvelously with his voice. He does great things, which we can’t comprehend.
**6** For he says to the snow, ‘Fall on the earth,’ likewise to the shower of rain, and to the showers of his mighty rain.
**7** He seals up the hand of every man, that all men whom he has made may know it.
**8** Then the animals take cover, and remain in their dens.
**9** Out of its room comes the storm, and cold out of the north.
**10** By the breath of God, ice is given, and the width of the waters is frozen.
**11** Yes, he loads the thick cloud with moisture. He spreads abroad the cloud of his lightning.
**12** It is turned around by his guidance, that they may do whatever he commands them on the surface of the habitable world,
**13** whether it is for correction, or for his land, or for loving kindness, that he causes it to come.
**14** “Listen to this, Job. Stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God.
**15** Do you know how God controls them, and causes the lightning of his cloud to shine?
**16** Do you know the workings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge?
**17** You whose clothing is warm when the earth is still by reason of the south wind?
**18** Can you, with him, spread out the sky, which is strong as a cast metal mirror?
**19** Teach us what we will tell him, for we can’t make our case by reason of darkness.
**20** Will it be told him that I would speak? Or should a man wish that he were swallowed up?
**21** Now men don’t see the light which is bright in the skies, but the wind passes, and clears them.
**22** Out of the north comes golden splendor. With God is awesome majesty.
**23** We can’t reach the Almighty. He is exalted in power. In justice and great righteousness, he will not oppress.
**24** Therefore men revere him. He doesn’t regard any who are wise of heart.”
