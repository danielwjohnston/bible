## Clear & Faithful

**1** Elihu continued speaking and said:
**2** "Bear with me a little longer and I will show you, for there is still more to be said on God's behalf.
**3** I will draw my insight from wide horizons and credit full justice to the One who formed me.
**4** For truly my words are no falsehood; one complete in knowledge stands before you.
**5** See how mighty God is, yet he looks down on no one; immense is his power of heart and mind.
**6** He does not keep the wicked alive, yet he grants justice to the afflicted.
**7** He never withdraws his eyes from the righteous; he sets them upon thrones with kings, seating them permanently so that they are exalted.
**8** Yet if they are bound in fetters and trapped by cords of affliction,
**9** then he exposes their deeds to them and their rebellions, showing how they acted arrogantly.
**10** He opens their ears to discipline and commands them to turn back from iniquity.
**11** Should they heed and obey him, they live out their days in flourishing well-being and their years in joy.
**12** But if they refuse to listen, they cross over by the sword and perish without understanding.
**13** Those who are godless in heart harbor resentment; they do not cry out for help even when he binds them.
**14** Their soul dies in youth, and their life ends among the depraved.
**15** Yet he rescues the afflicted through their affliction, and opens their ear by means of adversity.
**16** Indeed, he would draw you out from the jaws of distress into a spacious place free of constraint, where your table is set with rich abundance.
**17** Yet you are obsessed with the judgment of the wicked; judgment and justice have gripped you.
**18** Beware lest wrath entice you into scoffing, and do not let the greatness of the ransom turn you aside.
**19** Will your cry for wealth or all your strenuous efforts prevail without distress?
**20** Do not long for the night, when peoples vanish from their place!
**21** Be on guard—do not turn toward iniquity, for this you have preferred over affliction.
**22** Look—God stands unapproachably high in his might; what instructor can rival him?
**23** Who ever prescribed his way for him, or who dared say, 'You have done wrong'?
**24** Remember to extol his work, which mortals have celebrated in song.
**25** Every person has gazed upon it; humankind beholds it from afar.
**26** Behold, God is great beyond our knowing; the number of his years cannot be searched out.
**27** He gathers upward the droplets of water and refines them into rainfall out of his mist,
**28** which the skies pour down and shower abundantly upon humankind.
**29** Can anyone truly understand the spreading of the storm clouds, or the thunderings of his canopy?
**30** Look how he spreads his lightning around himself and covers the roots of the sea!
**31** For by these storms he governs peoples and supplies food in abundance.
**32** He fills both hands with lightning and directs it to strike its appointed mark.
**33** Its thunderclap announces his coming—even the cattle sense the rising storm."

## Everyday English

**1** Elihu kept on talking:
**2** "Hold on just a bit longer and hear me out—I still have more to say in defense of God!
**3** I'm going to pull insights from far and wide to prove that my Creator is completely fair.
**4** Believe me, I'm not making things up; the person talking to you knows his subject inside and out.
**5** Look, God is endlessly powerful, yet he doesn't look down on anyone; his strength of mind and heart is unmatched.
**6** He doesn't let wicked people thrive forever, and he makes sure mistreated people get justice.
**7** He never takes his eyes off those who do right—he lifts them up alongside kings on thrones and raises them to lasting honor.
**8** Now, if people end up locked in chains and tangled in painful trouble,
**9** God points out what they've done and shows them how their arrogance got out of hand.
**10** He opens their ears to correction and tells them plainly to walk away from sin.
**11** If they listen and follow him, they'll finish out their lives in peace and enjoy wonderful years.
**12** But if they tune him out, they walk straight into ruin and die without ever gaining wisdom.
**13** Meanwhile, people with fake, stubborn hearts nurse bitter anger; even when God puts them in handcuffs, they refuse to call on him for help.
**14** They burn out and die young, throwing their lives away in shameful company.
**15** Yet God uses hardship itself to rescue people who are hurting—he gets their attention right in the middle of their pain!
**16** In fact, he's trying to pull you out of this suffocating trap into wide-open freedom, where your table overflows with good things.
**17** Instead, you're consumed with demanding the kind of verdict wicked people get, and that obsession has trapped you!
**18** Watch out—don't let your anger push you into mocking God, and don't think a huge payoff lets you dodge his discipline.
**19** Can desperate yelling—or throwing all your wealth and effort at the problem—keep trouble away?
**20** Don't wish for the dark night of death, when whole populations get swept away in an instant!
**21** Be careful not to drift into rebellion just because you'd rather complain than endure this trial.
**22** Think about how towering God is in power! Is there any teacher on earth who compares with him?
**23** Who ever handed God his job description or told him, 'Hey, you got that wrong'?
**24** Remember to praise what he does—people have been singing songs about his works forever.
**25** Everyone on earth has seen his handiwork, even if we can only marvel at it from a distance.
**26** God is far greater than our minds can grasp; nobody can count how many years he's lived!
**27** He lifts tiny droplets of water into the air and turns his mist into steady rain;
**28** the clouds dump it down and drench the whole human race with generous showers.
**29** Can anyone explain how he spreads out massive storm clouds or sends thunder booming from his sky-tent?
**30** Watch him stretch sheets of lightning across the sky and blanket the deepest ocean floor!
**31** Through those very storms he shapes nations and grows overflowing harvests of food.
**32** He scoops lightning bolts into his palms and aims them right at their target.
**33** The rolling thunder warns that he's on his way—even cattle out in the field feel the storm building!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `תְּמִים דֵּעוֹת` (*temim de'ot*):** "Complete in knowledge"—Elihu claims integrity of insight (echoing Job's own claim of `תֻּמָּה` integrity, and ironized when YHWH uses the same phrase of himself in 37:16).
- **v. 14 `בַּקְּדֵשִׁים` (*ba-qedeshim*):** Literally "among the consecrated/cult-prostitutes"—pointing to premature, socially degraded ruin outside covenant blessing.
- **v. 16 `לֹא־מוּצָק` (*lo-mutsaq*):** Contrast between `צָר` (*tsar*, "narrow distress/jaws of straits") and `רַחַב` (*rachav*, "broad, unconstrained space").
- **v. 27 `לְאֵדוֹ` (*le-edo*):** *Ed* ("mist/vapor/stream") occurs in the OT only here and in Gen 2:6, linking Elihu's hydrological cycle directly to Genesis creation imagery.
- **v. 32 `בְּמַפְגִּיעַ` (*be-mafgi'a*):** From *paga* ("strike/meet/intercede"); can mean either "against a target/assailant" (lightning aimed at its mark) or "by command/intercession."
- **v. 33 `רֵעוֹ` (*re'o*):** Literally "his crash/shout" (or "his companion/thunder"); `עוֹלֶה` (*oleh*) can mean "rising storm" or "one who goes up [in pride]," making the closing line notoriously dense.

---

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 2:6** (hydrology allusion): Uses the rare noun `אֵד` (*ed*, "mist/stream"), echoed in v. 27 where God distills rain from his mist.
- **Psalm 18:11–14 & 104:3–14** (storm-theophany motif): Parallels God's cloud-canopy (`סֻכָּה`), lightning arrows, and life-giving rain feeding all peoples (vv. 27–32).
- **Isaiah 40:13–14** (rhetorical challenge): Echoes the question of who ever instructed God or dictated his path (vv. 22–23).

**12** But if they don’t listen, they will perish by the sword; they will die without knowledge.

**14** They die in youth. Their life perishes among the unclean.
**15** He delivers the afflicted by their affliction, and opens their ear in oppression.
**16** Yes, he would have allured you out of distress, into a wide place, where there is no restriction. That which is set on your table would be full of fatness.
**17** “But you are full of the judgment of the wicked. Judgment and justice take hold of you.
**18** Don’t let riches entice you to wrath, neither let the great size of a bribe turn you aside.
**19** Would your wealth sustain you in distress, or all the might of your strength?
**20** Don’t desire the night, when people are cut off in their place.
**21** Take heed, don’t regard iniquity; for you have chosen this rather than affliction.
**22** Behold, God is exalted in his power. Who is a teacher like him?
**23** Who has prescribed his way for him? Or who can say, ‘You have committed unrighteousness’?
**24** “Remember that you magnify his work, about which men have sung.
**25** All men have looked on it. Man sees it afar off.
**26** Behold, God is great, and we don’t know him. The number of his years is unsearchable.
**27** For he draws up the drops of water, which distill in rain from his vapor,
