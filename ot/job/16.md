## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Job answered and said:
**2** "I have heard plenty of speeches like these; miserable comforters are all of you!
**3** Will windy arguments ever come to an end? What stings you into answering back?
**4** I could speak just as you do if your soul stood in my soul's shoes—I could string speeches together against you and wag my head over you.
**5** I could brace you with encouraging words, and the soothing motion of my lips could hold back your sorrow.
**6** Yet if I speak, my pain is never eased; and if I stay silent, how much of it leaves me?
**7** Surely now God has worn me down; you have laid waste my entire household.
**8** You have shriveled me up—and that itself has become a witness against me; my wasted frame rises up to testify to my face.
**9** His fury has torn me apart and pursued me in hostility; he gnashes his teeth at me; my foe fixes his piercing eyes upon me.
**10** Men open their mouths wide against me; with insults they slap my cheeks; all together they crowd in around me.
**11** God hands me over to the ruthless and flings me straight into the grip of the wicked.
**12** I was living in peace when he shook me apart; he grabbed me by the back of the neck and smashed me, then set me up as his target.
**13** His archers encircle me; without pity he pierces my kidneys and spills my bile onto the ground.
**14** He batters me with breach after breach, charging down on me like a seasoned warrior.
**15** I have stitched sackcloth directly onto my skin and buried my dignity in the dust.
**16** My face burns crimson from weeping, and the shadow of death rests heavy on my eyelids,
**17** even though there is no violence on my hands and my prayer remains sincere.
**18** Earth, do not cover over my blood! Let my cry for justice find no resting place!
**19** Even now, look—my witness stands in heaven, and the one who testifies for me dwells in the heights.
**20** My own friends scoff at me, while my eyes pour out tears before God,
**21** longing for an arbiter to plead a mortal's case with God, just as someone pleads for a friend.
**22** For only a few numbered years remain before I walk the road from which I will never return."

## Everyday English

**1** Job replied:
**2** "I've heard this kind of lecture a hundred times! Every one of you brings misery instead of comfort.
**3** Don't you ever run out of hot air? What's bothering you so much that you have to keep firing back?
**4** Look, if we traded places, I could talk just like you do. I could pile up clever lines and shake my head at your troubles.
**5** I could give you big pep talks and pretend my sympathy would take your hurt away.
**6** But talking doesn't ease my pain, and keeping quiet doesn't make a bit of it go away.
**7** God has totally exhausted me; he's wiped out everyone around me.
**8** He's reduced me to skin and bones, and people treat my frail body as proof I'm guilty—my own gaunt face testifies against me.
**9** In his rage God tears at me and hunts me down; he grits his teeth and glares at me like an enemy.
**10** Crowds sneer and jeer at me, slap me in the face with insults, and gang up against me.
**11** God has handed me over to cruel people and tossed me straight into the hands of criminals.
**12** I was doing fine until he grabbed me by the neck, shook me senseless, shattered me, and used me for target practice.
**13** His bowmen surround me; he shows zero mercy as arrows rip into my vital organs and spill my insides in the dirt.
**14** He keeps smashing me blow after blow, rushing at me like an unstoppable soldier.
**15** So I've sewn coarse sackcloth straight over my skin and shoved my pride down into the dirt.
**16** My face is raw and swollen from crying, and dark circles like death hang over my eyelids—
**17** yet my hands are clean of any violence, and my prayers are honest.
**18** Earth, don't bury my blood! Don't let my cry for justice ever be silenced!
**19** Right now, I still have a witness up in heaven—someone in the highest place ready to speak up for me.
**20** While my friends mock me, my tears flow toward God,
**21** begging that someone would stand before God and argue a human being's case, the way a friend speaks up for a friend.
**22** In just a few short years, I'll take that one-way journey and never come back."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `מְנַחֲמֵי עָמָל` (*menachamei amal*):** Literally "comforters of toil/trouble"—an ironic reversal of `2:11`, where the friends originally arrived "to comfort him" (*lenachamo*).
- **v. 15 `קַרְנִי` (*qarni*):** "My horn"—a common Hebrew emblem of strength, honor, and royal or personal dignity, here thrust down into funerary dust.
- **v. 16 `חֳמַרְמְרוּ` (*chomarmaru*, Qere) vs. `חמרמרה` (Ketiv):** The verb comes from *chamar* ("to ferment, boil, flush red"). Ketiv preserves a 3rd-person feminine singular ending (`-ah`), while Qere reads 3rd-person plural (`-u`) to agree grammatically with the plural noun `פָּנַי` (*panay*, "my face").
- **v. 19 `עֵדִי` (*edi*) & `שָׂהֲדִי` (*sahadi*):** Parallel legal terms for "my witness" (Hebrew *ed* paired with the Aramaic-colored poetic synonym *sahed*). Job appeals to a heavenly witness/advocate even as he views God as his attacker in vv. 9–14, anticipating `19:25`.
- **v. 20 `מְלִיצַי רֵעָי` (*melitsay re'ay*):** *Melits* can mean "interpreter/spokesman" or "scoffer/mocker" (from *lits*). In context with Job's tears toward God, "my friends are my mockers" fits best, though "my friends act as interpreters" is a noted minority reading.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 4:10–11** (shared motif of unburied blood): Just as Abel's blood cries out from the ground against his killer, Job in v. 18 pleads that the earth not cover his innocent blood or stifle his outcry.
- **Isaiah 50:6** (shared suffering imagery): The servant endures cheeks struck and face exposed to insult and spitting, echoing the public humiliation Job describes in v. 10 (and 17:6).
- **Lamentations 3:12–13** (parallel archery metaphor): Lamentations uses the same striking image of God setting the sufferer as a target for his arrows, paralleling Job 16:12–13.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Job answered,
**2** “I have heard many such things. You are all miserable comforters!
**3** Shall vain words have an end? Or what provokes you that you answer?
**4** I also could speak as you do. If your soul were in my soul’s place, I could join words together against you, and shake my head at you,
**5** but I would strengthen you with my mouth. The solace of my lips would relieve you.
**6** “Though I speak, my grief is not subsided. Though I forbear, what am I eased?
**7** But now, God, you have surely worn me out. You have made all my company desolate.
**8** You have shriveled me up. This is a witness against me. My leanness rises up against me. It testifies to my face.
**9** He has torn me in his wrath and persecuted me. He has gnashed on me with his teeth. My adversary sharpens his eyes on me.
**10** They have gaped on me with their mouth. They have struck me on the cheek reproachfully. They gather themselves together against me.
**11** God delivers me to the ungodly, and casts me into the hands of the wicked.
**12** I was at ease, and he broke me apart. Yes, he has taken me by the neck, and dashed me to pieces. He has also set me up for his target.
**13** His archers surround me. He splits my kidneys apart, and does not spare. He pours out my bile on the ground.
**14** He breaks me with breach on breach. He runs at me like a giant.
**15** I have sewed sackcloth on my skin, and have thrust my horn in the dust.
**16** My face is red with weeping. Deep darkness is on my eyelids,
**17** although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure.
**18** “Earth, don’t cover my blood. Let my cry have no place to rest.
**19** Even now, behold, my witness is in heaven. He who vouches for me is on high.
**20** My friends scoff at me. My eyes pour out tears to God,
**21** that he would maintain the right of a man with God, of a son of man with his neighbor!
**22** For when a few years have come, I will go the way of no return.
