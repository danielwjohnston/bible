## Clear & Faithful

**1** "Can you drag Leviathan ashore on a hook, or pin his tongue down with a rope?
**2** Can you thread a reed-rope through his nose, or pierce his jaw with a hook?
**3** Will he make repeated pleas to you, or speak gentle words to you?
**4** Will he cut a covenant with you so that you take him as a lifelong servant?
**5** Will you play with him as with a bird, or leash him for your young girls?
**6** Will trading partners bargain over him, or divide him up among the merchants?
**7** Can you stud his hide with barbed spears, or pierce his head with fish harpoons?
**8** Lay your hand upon him—once you remember the battle, you will not try it again!
**9** Behold, any hope of subduing him proves false; the mere sight of him lays a person low!
**10** No one is fierce enough to rouse him—who then is able to stand before me?
**11** Who has given to me first, that I should repay him? Everything beneath the entire heavens belongs to me!
**12** I will not keep silent concerning his limbs, his mighty power, and his well-proportioned frame.
**13** Who has stripped off his outer coat? Who would dare reach inside his double armor?
**14** Who can pry open the doors of his face? Terror encircles his teeth!
**15** His back is made of rows of shields, locked together like a tight seal;
**16** one lies so close to the next that not even a breath of air can pass between them.
**17** Each is joined fast to its neighbor; they clasp one another and cannot be pried apart.
**18** His sneezes flash forth light, and his eyes glow like the eyelids of dawn.
**19** Blazing torches shoot from his mouth; sparks of fire leap out.
**20** Smoke pours out from his nostrils, like a boiling kettle over burning reeds.
**21** His breath sets coals ablaze, and flames dart out from his mouth.
**22** Strength resides in his neck, and dismay leaps ahead of him.
**23** The folds of his flesh are tightly fused; they are cast solid upon him and cannot be shaken.
**24** His heart is cast as hard as stone—as hard as a lower millstone.
**25** When he rises up, even the mighty are terrified; at his crashing surges they draw back in panic.
**26** Though a sword reaches him, it cannot hold, nor does spear, dart, or javelin avail.
**27** He regards iron as mere straw, and bronze as rotted wood.
**28** An arrow cannot drive him to flee; slingstones turn to chaff against him.
**29** A war club is counted as stubble, and he laughs at the whirring of a javelin.
**30** His underside is jagged like sharp potsherds; he drags a threshing sledge across the mud.
**31** He makes the deep waters boil like a cauldron; he churns the sea like a pot of simmering ointment.
**32** Behind him he leaves a glistening wake; one would think the deep had grown white hair.
**33** On earth there is nothing like him—a creature made without fear.
**34** He looks down upon everything that is lofty; he is king over all the proud beasts."

## Everyday English

**1** "Can you catch Leviathan on a fishing hook, or tie his tongue down with a piece of rope?
**2** Can you run a reed line through his snout, or punch a metal hook through his jaw?
**3** Do you think he'll beg you for mercy or sweet-talk you in a soft voice?
**4** Will he sign a peace treaty with you and agree to work as your permanent servant?
**5** Can you keep him as a pet like a songbird, or put him on a leash for your daughters to play with?
**6** Will fishermen haggle over his price or chop him up to sell at the market?
**7** Can you pepper his tough hide with harpoons or stab his skull with fishing spears?
**8** Go ahead and put your hand on him once—after remembering that fight, you'll never try it again!
**9** Honestly, anyone expecting to capture him is fooling themselves; just catching sight of him knocks people flat!
**10** Nobody is reckless enough to wake him up—so who thinks they can stand toe-to-toe with me?
**11** Who has ever put me in their debt so I owe them a payback? Everything under the entire sky already belongs to me!
**12** I'm not going to skip over his massive limbs, his raw strength, or his impressive build.
**13** Who has ever peeled back his outer armor, or dared reach inside his double row of jaws?
**14** Who could force open the doors of his mouth? Pure terror rings every one of his teeth!
**15** His back is plated with rows of shields, locked down tight like a royal seal;
**16** each scale fits so snugly against the next that not even a whisper of air gets through.
**17** They're welded side by side, clamped together so tightly nothing can pry them loose.
**18** When he snorts, flashes of light burst out; his eyes glow red like the first light of sunrise.
**19** Flaming torches shoot from his jaws, and fiery sparks scatter everywhere!
**20** Thick smoke billows out of his nostrils like steam pouring from a boiling pot over a reed fire.
**21** His breath lights up charcoal, and tongues of flame roar out of his mouth.
**22** Unstoppable strength sits in his neck, and sheer panic runs ahead wherever he goes.
**23** The folds of his body are packed rock-solid; nothing can dent or shift them.
**24** His heart is cast as hard as granite—tough as the heavy bottom stone of a grain mill.
**25** When he rises out of the water, even fearless warriors freeze in terror and lose their nerve at his thrashing.
**26** Hit him with a sword, and the blade bounces off; spears, darts, and lances do nothing!
**27** To him, iron is brittle straw, and bronze is crumbly rotten wood.
**28** Arrows can't make him run away, and slingstones bounce off him like dry chaff.
**29** A heavy club feels like a twig to him, and he just laughs at a whistling javelin.
**30** His belly is covered in sharp edges like broken pottery, dragging grooves through the mud like a threshing sled.
**31** He whips the ocean depths into a boiling kettle, churning the sea like a steaming cauldron of oil.
**32** He leaves a glowing white trail foaming behind him, making the deep sea look like it's covered in silver hair.
**33** Nothing on earth compares with him—a creature built completely without fear!
**34** He looks every towering rival straight in the eye; he reigns as king over every proud beast."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1–34 (WLC-to-English Versification Shift):** In the Westminster Leningrad Codex (MT), English **Job 41:1–8** corresponds to **WLC Job 40:25–32**, and English **Job 41:9–34** corresponds to **WLC Job 41:1–26** (`Job 40:25–32 -> 41:1–8` and `41:1–26 -> 41:9–34`). Verse numbers in this chapter follow standard English canon while translating the shifted WLC verses directly.
- **v. 1 `לִוְיָתָן` (*livyatan*):** Leviathan—both an awesome armored aquatic creature (suggesting the Nile crocodile) and an emblem of primordial cosmic chaos subdued only by YHWH (compare Job 3:8; Ps 74:14; Isa 27:1).
- **v. 11 `מִי הִקְדִּימַנִי וַאֲשַׁלֵּם` (*mi hiqdimani va-ashallem*):** "Who has come before me / given to me first, that I should repay?"—YHWH asserts absolute cosmic ownership, echoed by Paul in Rom 11:35.
- **v. 12 `לֹא־אַחֲרִישׁ` (*lo-acharish*, Ketiv) vs. `לוֹ` (*lo*, Qere):** WLC `41:4` (English `41:12`) writes Ketiv `לא` ("I will *not* keep silent [about] his limbs"), whereas Qere reads `לוֹ` ("for him," i.e., "Did I keep silent for him?"). Both preserve YHWH's transition into detailing Leviathan's armor.
- **v. 25 `אֵלִים` (*elim*):** Either "mighty heroes/warriors" or "gods/heavenly beings"—even divine or heroic powers cower when Leviathan surges (`מִשֵּׂתוֹ`).
- **v. 34 `בְּנֵי־שָׁחַץ` (*beney-shachats*):** Literally "sons of pride/stately majesty"—echoing `28:8` ("proud beasts"), placing Leviathan as monarch of untamed creation under God alone.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Romans 11:35** (theological echo): Mirrors v. 11's rhetorical challenge—no human has ever given to God first so as to put God in debt to repay.
- **Psalm 74:13–14 & 104:26** (Leviathan tradition): Portrays Leviathan both as the cosmic sea-monster crushed by YHWH and as a wondrous marine creature formed to sport in the sea.
- **Isaiah 27:1** (eschatological Leviathan): Identifies Leviathan as the twisting sea-serpent over whom only YHWH's great sword holds mastery.
