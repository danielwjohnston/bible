## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Zophar the Naamathite replied and said:
**2** "Should an avalanche of words go unanswered, or a smooth talker be declared in the right?
**3** Should your empty boasts silence everyone else, leaving you to scoff with nobody to put you to shame?
**4** For you have claimed, 'My doctrine is spotless, and I stand clean in your sight.'
**5** Yet if only God would speak up and unseal his lips against you,
**6** and reveal to you the hidden secrets of wisdom—for true insight has many sides! Recognize that God is actually overlooking part of what your guilt deserves.
**7** Can you fathom the deep mysteries of God, or reach the farthest boundary of the Almighty?
**8** Higher than the heavens—what can you accomplish? Deeper than Sheol—what can you comprehend?
**9** Its span stretches longer than the earth and wider than the sea.
**10** If he sweeps past, locks someone up, and convenes a court, who is able to stop him?
**11** For he recognizes deceitful mortals; he spots wickedness at a glance without needing to investigate.
**12** Yet an empty-headed man will gain wisdom only when a wild donkey gives birth to a human child!
**13** Still, if you prepare your heart uprightly and stretch out your palms toward him—
**14** if wrongdoing lingers in your hand, banish it far away, and allow no crookedness to camp inside your tents—
**15** then surely you will raise an unblemished face, standing firm as cast bronze without a trace of fear.
**16** You will forget all your misery, recalling it only like floodwaters that have already run past.
**17** Life will brighten beyond high noon, and thick gloom will turn into morning light.
**18** You will feel secure because hope remains; glancing around your camp, you will lie down in safety.
**19** You will stretch out at ease with nobody to startle you, and crowds will seek your favor.
**20** Yet the eyes of the wicked will dim and fail; every escape route vanishes from them, and their only hope is their final breath."

## Everyday English

**1** Next, Zophar from Naamah jumped in:
**2** "Are we supposed to let all that non-stop talking slide? Does running your mouth automatically make you innocent?
**3** Do you expect your bragging to shut everybody else up, so you can mock God with no one calling you out?
**4** You keep insisting, 'Everything I say is pure, and God can't find a single flaw in me.'
**5** Honestly, I wish God would just open his mouth and talk back to you himself!
**6** I wish he'd show you how deep real wisdom goes—it has way more layers than you see. Trust me, God is letting you off easy compared to what your sins actually earned!
**7** Do you think you can figure out God's deep mind or map out the limits of the Almighty?
**8** His wisdom towers higher than the sky—what could you possibly reach? It plunges deeper than the grave—what could you ever understand?
**9** It measures longer than the whole earth and wider than every ocean.
**10** If God sweeps in, arrests someone, and calls court into session, who's going to tell him no?
**11** He spots phony people instantly; he sees evil right away without even needing to double-check.
**12** A brainless fool will finally wise up about the same day a wild donkey gives birth to a human baby!
**13** Even so, if you set your heart straight and reach out your hands to pray to him—
**14** if you're holding onto any sin, throw it far away and kick injustice out of your home—
**15** then you'll hold your head high without shame, standing rock-solid and fearless!
**16** You'll put all this misery behind you, remembering it like spring runoff that's long gone downstream.
**17** Your future will shine brighter than noon, and your darkest nights will look like sunrise.
**18** You'll relax with real hope, check around your place, and sleep soundly in peace.
**19** You'll lie down with nobody sneaking up to scare you, and plenty of people will come asking for your help.
**20** As for wicked people, their eyes will go dim looking for rescue—they'll have nowhere left to run, and all they have to look forward to is their dying gasp."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `אִישׁ שְׂפָתַיִם` (*ish sefatayim*):** Literally "a man of lips," i.e., a glib talker or windbag, contrasting sharply with Zophar's blunt brevity (only 20 verses).
- **v. 6 `כִּפְלַיִם לְתוּשִׁיָּה` (*kiflayim le-tushiyyah*):** Literally "twofold/double [belongs] to sound wisdom," meaning either that divine wisdom is manifold and unfathomable or that God's discipline is lighter than Job's double debt of guilt (`יַשֶּׁה לְךָ אֱלוֹהַּ מֵעֲוֺנֶךָ`, "God remits/forgets for you part of your iniquity").
- **v. 12 `וְאִישׁ נָבוּב יִלָּבֵב` (*ve-ish navuv yillavev*):** A sharp Hebrew pun on hollow vs. heart: a "hollow/empty-headed (*navuv*) man" will get a "heart/mind (*lev*)" only when a wild donkey foal (*ayir pere*) is born a human—an ancient proverb of impossibility (though ironical, since Zophar unwittingly anticipates God's untamable wild donkey in 39:5!).
- **v. 15 `מֻצָּק` (*mutsaq*):** From *yatsaq* ("to pour/cast metal"), meaning "firm as cast bronze, steadfast, unshakable."
- **v. 20 `מַפַּח־נָפֶשׁ` (*mappach-nafesh*):** Literally "a breathing-out of the soul/life" (from *nafach*, "to blow/breathe"), portraying the wicked person's only remaining expectation as dying despair.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Romans 11:33–34** (unfathomable divine wisdom): Exclaims over the depth of God's riches, wisdom, and knowledge—echoing Zophar's reflection in vv. 7–9 that God's wisdom outstrips heaven, Sheol, earth, and sea.
- **Isaiah 58:8–10** (light out of gloom): Promises that when iniquity is put away, darkness will brighten like noonday (*tsohorayim*), paralleling Zophar's conditional promise in v. 17.
- **Psalm 139:7–12** (cosmic dimensions): Surveys height of heaven and depth of Sheol in contemplating the reach of God's presence and knowledge.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Zophar, the Naamathite, answered,
**2** “Shouldn’t the multitude of words be answered? Should a man full of talk be justified?
**3** Should your boastings make men hold their peace? When you mock, will no man make you ashamed?
**4** For you say, ‘My doctrine is pure. I am clean in your eyes.’
**5** But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against you,
**6** that he would show you the secrets of wisdom! For true wisdom has two sides. Know therefore that God exacts of you less than your iniquity deserves.
**7** “Can you fathom the mystery of God? Or can you probe the limits of the Almighty?
**8** They are high as heaven. What can you do? They are deeper than Sheol. What can you know?
**9** Its measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.
**10** If he passes by, or confines, or convenes a court, then who can oppose him?
**11** For he knows false men. He sees iniquity also, even though he doesn’t consider it.
**12** An empty-headed man becomes wise when a man is born as a wild donkey’s colt.
**13** “If you set your heart aright, stretch out your hands toward him.
**14** If iniquity is in your hand, put it far away. Don’t let unrighteousness dwell in your tents.
**15** Surely then you will lift up your face without spot. Yes, you will be steadfast, and will not fear,
**16** for you will forget your misery. You will remember it like waters that have passed away.
**17** Life will be clearer than the noonday. Though there is darkness, it will be as the morning.
**18** You will be secure, because there is hope. Yes, you will search, and will take your rest in safety.
**19** Also you will lie down, and no one will make you afraid. Yes, many will court your favor.
**20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail. They will have no way to flee. Their hope will be the giving up of the spirit.”
