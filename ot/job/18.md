## Clear & Faithful

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:
**2** "How long will you hunt for words and lay traps in speech? Come to your senses first, and then let us talk.
**3** Why do you treat us as dull cattle, looking down on us as senseless in your eyes?
**4** You who tear yourself apart in rage—should the earth be abandoned just for you, or the solid rock uprooted from its place?
**5** Yes, the light of the wicked goes out, and the flame of his fire stops glowing.
**6** Daylight darkens inside his tent, and the lamp hanging above him is snuffed out.
**7** His confident strides grow cramped and short, and his own schemes trip him up.
**8** His own feet walk him straight into a net; he steps right across a hidden pitfall.
**9** A trap clamps shut around his heel; a snare locks tight upon him.
**10** A cord lies concealed in the dirt for him, and a noose waits along his trail.
**11** Terrors ambush him from every direction and hound him step for step.
**12** Famine drains away his vigor, while disaster stands ready at his side.
**13** Disease consumes patches of his skin; Death's firstborn devours his limbs.
**14** He is ripped from the security of his tent and marched off to the king of terrors.
**15** Nothing belonging to him lingers in his tent; burning sulfur rains down upon his homestead.
**16** Down below, his roots shrivel dry; up above, his boughs wither away.
**17** Every memory of him vanishes from the land, and his name disappears across the countryside.
**18** People drive him out of the light into darkness and banish him from the living world.
**19** He leaves neither child nor grandchild among his people, and not a single survivor in the places he once lived.
**20** Those in the west are stunned at his doom, and those in the east shudder with horror.
**21** Such, without doubt, are the homes of the wicked, and such is the fate of anyone who refuses to know God."

**3** Why are you treating us like dumb animals, acting as if we're completely clueless?
**4** You're tearing yourself to shreds with your own anger! Do you expect the whole world to empty out for you, or cliffs to move out of your way?

**7** His big, confident steps get boxed in, and his own clever plans bring him crashing down.
**8** He walks straight into a trap with his own two feet, stepping right onto a hidden net.

**11** Nightmares jump out at him from every angle and chase right at his heels.
**12** Hunger wastes away his strength, and tragedy stays right beside him waiting for him to stumble.

**14** He's dragged out of the home he felt so safe in and marched straight to Death—the king of terrors.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `קִנְצֵי לְמִלִּין` (*qintsei lemillin*):** A rare phrase; *qintsei* may be a byform of *qets* ("ends/limits" to words, cf. 16:3) or a noun related to *qinets* ("hunt/snares" for words). Bildad also uses plural verbs (*tesimun*, *tavinu*), addressing Job and others who share his skepticism.
- **v. 13 `בְּכוֹר מָוֶת` (*bekhor mavet*):** Literally "firstborn of Death"—poetic personification of a lethal plague or wasting disease (paired with `מֶלֶךְ בַּלָּהוֹת` *melekh ballahot*, "king of terrors," in v. 14).
- **v. 15 `מִבְּלִי־לוֹ` (*mibbeli-lo*):** Literally "from-without-to-him"—an idiomatic phrase meaning "none of his own" (or fire/strangers occupying what is no longer his), followed by `גָפְרִית` (*gofrit*, "brimstone/sulfur," evocative of Sodom's judgment).
- **v. 20 `אַחֲרֹנִים` (*acharonim*) & `קַדְמֹנִים` (*qadmonim*):** Literally "hindmost/westerners" (or "later generations") and "foremost/easterners" (or "earlier generations"); facing east in Hebrew geography, *qedem* is east/front and *achor* is west/behind, encompassing the whole horizon.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Proverbs 13:9; 24:20** (shared wisdom motif): Proclaims that the lamp of the wicked will be extinguished, matching Bildad's opening image in Job 18:5–6.
- **Genesis 19:24** (judgment imagery): Brimstone/sulfur (*gofrit*) rained down on Sodom provides the background for the desolation of the wicked man's home in Job 18:15.
- **Psalm 109:13** (blotting out of memory): Prays that the wicked person's posterity be cut off and his name blotted out, echoing Job 18:17–19.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered,
**2** “How long will you hunt for words? Consider, and afterwards we will speak.
**3** Why are we counted as animals, which have become unclean in your sight?
**4** You who tear yourself in your anger, will the earth be forsaken for you? Or will the rock be removed out of its place?
**5** “Yes, the light of the wicked will be put out. The spark of his fire won’t shine.
**6** The light will be dark in his tent. His lamp above him will be put out.
**7** The steps of his strength will be shortened. His own counsel will cast him down.
**8** For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he wanders into its mesh.
**9** A snare will take him by the heel. A trap will catch him.
**10** A noose is hidden for him in the ground, a trap for him on the path.
**11** Terrors will make him afraid on every side, and will chase him at his heels.
**12** His strength will be famished. Calamity will be ready at his side.
**13** The members of his body will be devoured. The firstborn of death will devour his members.
**14** He will be rooted out of the security of his tent. He will be brought to the king of terrors.
**15** There will dwell in his tent that which is none of his. Sulfur will be scattered on his habitation.
**16** His roots will be dried up beneath. His branch will be cut off above.
**17** His memory will perish from the earth. He will have no name in the street.
**18** He will be driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world.
**19** He will have neither son nor grandson among his people, nor any remaining where he lived.
**20** Those who come after will be astonished at his day, as those who went before were frightened.
**21** Surely such are the dwellings of the unrighteous. This is the place of him who doesn’t know God.”
