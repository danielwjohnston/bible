## Clear & Faithful

**1** It was now two days before the Passover and the Festival of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were searching for a way to arrest Jesus by stealth and kill him;
**2** for they kept saying, "Not during the festival, or else there may be a riot among the people."
**3** While he was in Bethany, reclining at table in the house of Simon the leper, a woman arrived carrying an alabaster jar of costly perfume made from pure nard. She broke open the jar and poured the ointment over his head.
**4** Some present grew indignant among themselves: "Why has this ointment been wasted like this?
**5** This perfume could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and the money given to the poor!" And they scolded her harshly.
**6** Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why are you bothering her? She has performed a noble act for me.
**7** You always have the poor with you, and whenever you wish you can do good for them; yet you will not always have me.
**8** She did everything within her power; ahead of time she anointed my body for its burial.
**9** Truly I tell you, wherever the good news is proclaimed throughout the entire world, what this woman has done will also be told in memory of her."
**10** Then Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went off to the chief priests in order to betray Jesus to them.
**11** Hearing this, they were delighted and promised to pay him silver. So he began watching for an opportune moment to hand him over.
**12** On the first day of Unleavened Bread, when the Passover lamb was customarily sacrificed, his disciples asked him, "Where do you want us to go and prepare for you to eat the Passover?"
**13** So he dispatched two of his disciples with instructions: "Walk into the city, where a man carrying a clay water jar will cross your path. Follow behind him,
**14** and wherever he goes in, say to the owner of the house, 'The Teacher asks: Where is my guest room where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?'
**15** He will show you a large upstairs room, furnished and ready; make the preparations for us there."
**16** The disciples set out, entered the city, found everything exactly as he had told them, and prepared the Passover meal.
**17** When evening fell, he arrived with the Twelve.
**18** While they were reclining at table and eating, Jesus said, "Truly I tell you, one of you will betray me—one who is eating right here with me."
**19** Deeply grieved, they began asking him one after another, "Surely not I?"
**20** He told them, "It is one of the Twelve, the one dipping bread with me into the same bowl.
**21** For the Son of Man is going the way written concerning him; yet woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would have been better for that man never to have been born."
**22** While they were eating, he took a loaf of bread, spoke a blessing, broke it, gave it to them, and said, "Take it; this is my body."
**23** Then he took a cup, offered thanks, gave it to them, and all of them drank from it.
**24** And he said to them, "This is my blood of the covenant, poured out for the sake of many.
**25** Truly I tell you, I will certainly never again drink from the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God."
**26** After singing a hymn, they walked out toward the Mount of Olives.
**27** Jesus told them, "Every one of you will fall away, for it is written, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.'
**28** Yet after I have been raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee."
**29** Peter protested to him, "Even if everyone else falls away, I certainly will not!"
**30** Jesus replied to him, "Truly I tell you, today—this very night—before a rooster crows twice, you will disown me three times."
**31** Yet Peter insisted all the more vehemently, "Even if I must die alongside you, I will never disown you!" And all the others vowed the same thing.
**32** They came to a plot of land called Gethsemane, and he told his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."
**33** Taking Peter, James, and John along with him, he began to feel overwhelming distress and anguish,
**34** and he told them, "My soul is deeply grieved, even to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch."
**35** Going a short distance farther, he threw himself down on the ground and kept praying that, if possible, this hour might pass him by.
**36** He prayed, "Abba, Father, all things are possible for you; take this cup away from me. Yet not what I will, but what you will."
**37** Returning, he found them asleep, and said to Peter, "Simon, are you sleeping? Could you not stay awake for a single hour?
**38** Keep watch and pray so that you do not enter into testing; the spirit is willing, yet the flesh is weak."
**39** Once more he walked away and prayed, speaking the same words.
**40** Coming back again, he found them asleep, for their eyes were heavy with exhaustion, and they had no idea how to answer him.
**41** A third time he returned and told them, "Go ahead and sleep now and take your rest. That is enough! The hour has come; look, the Son of Man is being betrayed into the hands of sinners.
**42** Get up, let us go! Look, my betrayer is right here."
**43** At that very moment, while he was still speaking, Judas—one of the Twelve—arrived with a crowd armed with swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests, scribes, and elders.
**44** The betrayer had arranged a signal with them, saying, "The one I kiss is he; arrest him and lead him away under tight guard."
**45** Arriving, he walked straight up to Jesus, said, "Rabbi!" and kissed him warmly.
**46** Then the men laid hands on Jesus and seized him.
**47** One of the bystanders drew his sword, struck the high priest's servant, and sliced off his ear.
**48** Jesus responded to them, "Have you marched out as if against an armed rebel, with swords and clubs to capture me?
**49** Day after day I was right there with you teaching in the temple, and you never arrested me—yet this happens so that the Scriptures may be fulfilled."
**50** Then everyone deserted him and ran away.
**51** A certain young man followed along behind him wearing only a linen cloth wrapped over his bare body; when they grabbed hold of him,
**52** he slipped out of the linen cloth and fled naked.
**53** They led Jesus away to the high priest, and all the chief priests, elders, and scribes assembled together.
**54** Peter trailed him from a distance right inside into the high priest's courtyard, where he sat with the attendants warming himself beside the firelight.
**55** Meanwhile, the chief priests and the entire Sanhedrin kept searching for testimony against Jesus to put him to death, yet they could find none;
**56** for although many gave false testimony against him, their testimonies did not agree.
**57** Then several stood up and bore false witness against him, claiming,
**58** "We heard him say, 'I will destroy this temple made with human hands, and within three days I will build another not made with hands.'"
**59** Yet even on this point their testimony did not match.
**60** Standing up in the center of the assembly, the high priest questioned Jesus: "Have you no answer to make? What is this charge they are bringing against you?"
**61** Jesus remained silent and answered nothing. Again the high priest questioned him, asking, "Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?"
**62** Jesus answered, "I am; and you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and arriving with the clouds of heaven."
**63** Tearing his garments, the high priest cried out, "Why do we need any further witnesses?
**64** You have heard the blasphemy! What is your verdict?" All of them condemned him as deserving death.
**65** Then some began spitting on him, blindfolding his face, punching him, and taunting him, "Prophesy!" And the guards took him into custody with blows.
**66** While Peter was down below in the courtyard, one of the high priest's servant girls walked over.
**67** Seeing Peter warming himself, she stared at him and said, "You too were with that Nazarene, Jesus!"
**68** He denied it, saying, "I neither know nor understand what you are talking about." Then he walked outside into the entryway, and a rooster crowed.
**69** Noticing him again, the servant girl began telling the bystanders, "This man is one of them!"
**70** Once more he denied it. Shortly afterward, the bystanders told Peter again, "Surely you belong to them, for you are a Galilean too."
**71** Then he began calling down curses and swearing on oath, "I do not know this man you are talking about!"
**72** Instantly a rooster crowed a second time. And Peter remembered the exact word Jesus had spoken to him: "Before a rooster crows twice, you will disown me three times." Breaking down, he wept bitterly.

## Everyday English

**1** Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread were only two days away. Meanwhile, the chief priests and legal scholars were scheming how to trap Jesus secretly and have him executed.
**2** "Just don't do it during the festival," they warned each other, "or the crowds might start a riot."
**3** While Jesus was in Bethany eating dinner at the home of Simon (who used to have a skin disease), a woman walked in holding an alabaster bottle filled with very expensive pure nard perfume. She snapped off the neck of the bottle and poured the perfume over Jesus' head.
**4** A few guests got upset and muttered among themselves, "Why waste expensive perfume like that?
**5** That jar could've been sold for over a year's wages and the cash handed out to the poor!" And they snapped harshly at her.
**6** Jesus spoke up: "Leave her alone! Why are you giving her a hard time? What she just did for me is a beautiful act.
**7** You'll always have poor people around you, and you can help them whenever you choose, yet you won't always have me.
**8** She gave everything she had—she got my body ready for burial ahead of time.
**9** I promise you, wherever the good news is shared around the entire globe, what this woman did will be told so people always remember her."
**10** Just then Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, slipped away to the chief priests to arrange handing Jesus over to them.
**11** They were thrilled to hear his offer and promised to pay him silver. From that moment on, Judas watched for the right chance to betray him.
**12** On the opening day of Unleavened Bread, the day Passover lambs were sacrificed, Jesus' disciples asked him, "Where do you want us to go set up the Passover meal for you?"
**13** Jesus sent two of his followers with these instructions: "Walk into the city, and you'll bump into a man carrying a clay water jug. Trail right behind him,
**14** and whichever house he steps into, tell the homeowner, 'Our Teacher asks, "Where is my guest room where I can eat the Passover dinner with my disciples?"'
**15** He'll take you up to a spacious upstairs room, already set up with rugs and cushions. Get the meal ready for us right there."
**16** The two followers went into the city, found everything exactly the way Jesus said, and prepared the Passover dinner.
**17** That evening Jesus arrived with the Twelve.
**18** As they sat around the table eating, Jesus told them, "Listen closely: one of you sitting here sharing this meal with me is going to betray me."
**19** Shocked and heartbroken, they started asking him one by one, "It isn't me, is it?"
**20** Jesus replied, "It's one of the Twelve—someone dipping bread into the same dish with me right now.
**21** The Son of Man must go down the path Scripture laid out for him, yet how tragic it will be for the man who betrays the Son of Man! That man would've been far better off if he'd never been born."
**22** While they were still eating, Jesus picked up a loaf of bread, thanked God, broke it, handed it to them, and said, "Take this—it is my body."
**23** Then he picked up a cup, thanked God for it, and passed it to them, and every one of them drank from it.
**24** He told them, "This is my blood of the covenant, poured out for countless people.
**25** Believe me, I won't drink wine again until that day when I drink it brand-new in God's kingdom."
**26** After singing a closing hymn, they walked out to the Mount of Olives.
**27** On the way, Jesus told them, "Every single one of you is going to lose courage and scatter, just as Scripture says: 'I will strike down the shepherd, and the sheep will scatter everywhere.'
**28** Still, after I rise from death, I'll go ahead of you back to Galilee."
**29** Peter insisted, "Even if everybody else deserts you, I never will!"
**30** Jesus looked at him and said, "I'm telling you the truth: tonight, before a rooster crows twice, you'll deny knowing me three times."
**31** Peter pushed back even harder: "Even if I have to die right alongside you, I'll never deny you!" And every single one of the disciples promised the exact same thing.
**32** Soon they reached an olive grove called Gethsemane. Jesus told his disciples, "Sit down right here while I go pray."
**33** He brought Peter, James, and John a little farther with him, and waves of sheer dread and agony washed over him.
**34** He told them, "My heart feels crushed with sorrow to the point of dying. Stay right here and keep watch with me."
**35** Walking a few steps ahead, he collapsed onto the ground and prayed that, if it were at all possible, this terrible hour might pass him by.
**36** "Abba, Father," he prayed, "you can do anything! Please take this cup of suffering away from me. Yet don't do what I want—do what you want."
**37** When he walked back, he found them fast asleep. He spoke to Peter: "Simon, are you sleeping? Couldn't you stay awake for just one hour?
**38** Stay alert and keep praying so you don't cave in under testing! Your spirit wants to do right, yet your body is weak."
**39** Jesus walked off again and prayed the exact same prayer.
**40** When he came back a second time, he found them asleep again—their eyelids felt like lead, and they had no idea what excuse to give him.
**41** Coming back a third time, he told them, "Still sleeping and resting? That's enough! The moment has arrived—look, the Son of Man is being handed over into the grip of sinners.
**42** Get up, let's go! Here comes my betrayer right now."
**43** Instantly, before he even finished speaking, Judas—one of the Twelve—walked up leading a mob armed with swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests, legal scholars, and elders.
**44** Judas had set up a signal with them ahead of time: "The man I kiss is the one you want. Grab him and haul him off under tight guard."
**45** So Judas marched straight up to Jesus, greeted him, "Rabbi!" and gave him a warm kiss.
**46** At once the guards grabbed Jesus and arrested him.
**47** One of Jesus' companions pulled a sword, swung at the high priest's servant, and chopped off his ear.
**48** Jesus turned to the mob and asked, "Did you have to march out with swords and clubs to arrest me as if I were a dangerous bandit?
**49** Day after day I stood right among you teaching in the temple courts, and you never touched me! Yet all of this is happening so Scripture comes true."
**50** Just then, all his followers abandoned him and ran for their lives.
**51** Only one young man trailed behind wearing nothing but a linen sheet wrapped around his bare body. When the guards grabbed him too,
**52** he wiggled out of the sheet and sprinted away naked.
**53** The soldiers marched Jesus off to the high priest's residence, where all the chief priests, elders, and legal experts had assembled.
**54** Peter crept along far behind, slipping right inside the high priest's courtyard, where he sat down with the guards to warm himself by the fire.
**55** Inside, the chief priests and the whole high council hunted desperately for evidence to sentence Jesus to death, yet they came up empty.
**56** Lots of witnesses told lies about him, except none of their stories matched.
**57** Finally a few stood up and testified falsely:
**58** "We heard him brag, 'I'll tear down this temple built by human hands, and in three days I'll put up another one not built by hands!'"
**59** Even then, their stories didn't line up.
**60** So the high priest stood up in the center of the room and demanded of Jesus, "Aren't you going to defend yourself? What do you say about these charges against you?"
**61** Jesus kept quiet and didn't say a word. The high priest pressed him again: "Are you the Messiah, the Son of the Blessed God?"
**62** Jesus answered, "I am! And you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Almighty and coming on the clouds of heaven."
**63** The high priest ripped his robes in outrage and shouted, "What more witnesses do we need?
**64** You heard his blasphemy with your own ears! What's your verdict?" Every single one of them voted that he deserved to die.
**65** Then some of them started spitting in his face, blindfolding him, punching him, and mocking, "Prophesy who hit you!" Even the guards slapped him around as they hauled him away.
**66** Meanwhile, down in the courtyard below, one of the high priest's servant women walked by.
**67** Spotting Peter warming himself by the fire, she looked him over and said, "You were with that Jesus from Nazareth too!"
**68** Peter denied it flatly: "I don't know him, and I have no idea what you're talking about!" He walked out toward the front gate, and a rooster crowed.
**69** The servant woman spotted him again and announced to the people standing nearby, "This guy really is one of them!"
**70** Peter denied it again. A few minutes later, the bystanders challenged Peter themselves: "Of course you're one of them—your Galilean accent gives you away!"
**71** That time Peter started swearing curses and taking solemn oaths: "I don't even know this man you're talking about!"
**72** Right then a rooster crowed a second time. Suddenly Jesus' words flashed back into Peter's mind: "Before a rooster crows twice, you'll deny me three times." Crushed with grief, Peter broke down and cried.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `νάρδου πιστικῆς` (*nardou pistikēs*):** Expensive imported spikenard from northern India; *pistikos* likely means "pure / unadulterated / genuine" (or possibly "liquid").
- **v. 14 `τὸ κατάλυμά` (*to katalyma*):** "Guest room / lodging place" (contrasting with *anagaion* "upper room" in v. 15, prepared with dining couches *estrōmenon*).
- **v. 36 `Αββα ὁ πατήρ` (*Abba ho patēr*):** Aramaic *abbā* ("Father," an intimate familial address) paired with Greek *ho patēr*.
- **v. 41 `ἀπέχει` (*apechei*):** Commercial/idiomatic impersonal verb meaning "It is settled / paid in full / enough!"—signaling that the waiting period is over.
- **v. 68 `καὶ ἀλέκτωρ ἐφώνησεν` (*kai alektōr ephōnēsen*):** "And a rooster crowed" (`Treg`, `RP`, `NA28`), omitted by `WH` and a handful of Alexandrian witnesses (harmonizing with Matthew/Luke's single crow), though retained by SBLGNT to match the "twice" in vv. 30 and 72.
- **v. 72 `ἐπιβαλὼν ἔκλαιεν` (*epibalōn eklaien*):** A terse participle *epibalōn* ("having thrown [himself/thoughts/garment] upon it"), variously understood as "breaking down," "reflecting on it," or "covering his head" while weeping (*eklaien*, imperfect of sustained grief).
