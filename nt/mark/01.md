## Clear & Faithful

**1** The beginning of the good news of Jesus Christ.
**2** As it stands written in Isaiah the prophet: "Look, I am sending my messenger ahead of your face, who will prepare your way;
**3** a voice of one shouting in the wilderness: 'Make ready the way of the Lord; make his paths straight'"—
**4** John appeared, baptizing in the wilderness and heralding a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.
**5** The entire Judean countryside and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem kept going out to him, and they were baptized by him in the Jordan River while openly confessing their sins.
**6** John was dressed in camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist, and he lived on locusts and wild honey.
**7** He proclaimed this message: "One mightier than I is coming after me; I am not worthy even to stoop down and untie the strap of his sandals.
**8** While I baptized you in water, he will immerse you in the Holy Spirit."
**9** In those days it happened that Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee and was baptized into the Jordan by John.
**10** And at once, as he rose up out of the water, he saw the heavens torn open and the Spirit descending into him like a dove.
**11** A voice came out of the heavens: "You are my beloved Son; in you I take delight."
**12** At once the Spirit impelled him out into the desert wilderness.
**13** He remained in the wilderness forty days, put to the test by Satan; he was among the wild animals, and the angels waited on him.
**14** After John was handed over, Jesus entered Galilee proclaiming the good news of God
**15** and declaring, "The appointed time has reached fullness, and the kingdom of God has drawn near. Repent and put your trust in the good news."
**16** As he walked alongside the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, Simon's brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.
**17** Jesus said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of people."
**18** At once they abandoned their nets and followed him.
**19** Going a short distance farther, he saw James the son of Zebedee and his brother John; they too were in the boat repairing their nets.
**20** Immediately he called them, and leaving their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired laborers, they went off after him.
**21** They entered Capernaum, and as soon as the Sabbath arrived, he began teaching inside the synagogue.
**22** People were astounded at his teaching, because he taught them as one possessing true authority, unlike the scribes.
**23** Right there in their synagogue was a man controlled by an unclean spirit, and he shouted out,
**24** "What business do you have with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you arrived to ruin us? I recognize who you are—God's Holy One!"
**25** Jesus rebuked it, saying, "Be silenced, and come out of him!"
**26** The unclean spirit threw the man into convulsions, shrieked at the top of its voice, and came out of him.
**27** Everyone was stunned, so much so that they debated among themselves, "What is this? A fresh teaching backed by authority! He gives orders even to unclean spirits, and they obey him."
**28** News about him raced outward at once into every corner of the surrounding region of Galilee.
**29** Immediately upon leaving the synagogue, they went with James and John to the house of Simon and Andrew.
**30** Simon's mother-in-law was lying in bed with a high fever, and at once they told Jesus about her.
**31** He came over, took her by the hand, and lifted her up. The fever left her, and she began serving them.
**32** When evening came, after the sun had gone down, people kept bringing to him everyone who was sick and everyone oppressed by demons.
**33** The whole town had crowded together at the doorway.
**34** He cured many who suffered from various illnesses and expelled many demons; and he would not let the demons speak, because they recognized who he was.
**35** Very early in the morning, while it was still pitch-dark, he got up, slipped outside, and went off to a solitary place where he prayed.
**36** Simon and his companions tracked him down,
**37** found him, and said to him, "Everyone is searching for you!"
**38** He replied to them, "Let us move on to the neighboring market towns so I can proclaim the message there too—that is the very reason I came out."
**39** So he traveled throughout all Galilee, preaching in their synagogues and driving out demons.
**40** Then a man with leprosy approached him, pleading on his knees and saying, "If you are willing, you have the power to make me clean."
**41** Moved with deep indignation, Jesus reached out his hand, touched him, and said, "I am willing; be made clean."
**42** Instantly the leprosy vanished from him, and he was cleansed.
**43** Giving him a stern warning, Jesus sent him away at once
**44** with these words: "See that you tell no one a single thing; instead, go show yourself to the priest and present the offering Moses prescribed for your cleansing, as a testimony to them."
**45** Yet once the man went out, he began announcing the story everywhere and broadcasting the report, to the point that Jesus could no longer enter a town openly, but stayed outside in lonely areas; even so, people kept coming to him from all directions.

## Everyday English

**1** This is how the good news about Jesus the Messiah begins.
**2** Just like the prophet Isaiah wrote long ago: "Watch! I'm sending my messenger right ahead of you to clear your path;
**3** someone is shouting in the desert: 'Get the Lord's road ready! Straighten out his trails!'"—
**4** John showed up out in the desert, baptizing people and telling them to turn their lives around so God would forgive their sins.
**5** Crowds from all across Judea and everyone from Jerusalem streamed out to him. They admitted their sins openly, and he baptized them in the Jordan River.
**6** John wore rough camel-hair clothes with a leather belt around his waist, and he ate locusts and wild honey.
**7** Here is what he kept telling everyone: "Someone far greater than me is coming right behind me. I'm not even good enough to bend down and untie his sandal straps.
**8** I've washed you with water, yet he's going to immerse you in the Holy Spirit."
**9** Around that time, Jesus traveled from Nazareth in Galilee, and John baptized him in the Jordan River.
**10** The moment Jesus came up out of the water, he saw the sky ripped open and the Spirit swooping down into him like a dove.
**11** A voice spoke from heaven: "You are my Son, whom I deeply love. I am delighted with you."
**12** Right away, the Spirit pushed Jesus out into the desert.
**13** He stayed out there for forty days while Satan tested him. Wild animals were all around him, and angels took care of him.
**14** After John was arrested, Jesus went to Galilee and started sharing God's good news:
**15** "The waiting time is over! God's kingdom has arrived right here. Turn back to God and trust this good news!"
**16** As Jesus walked along the shore of Lake Galilee, he spotted Simon and his brother Andrew tossing a casting net into the water, since they made their living as fishermen.
**17** Jesus called to them, "Come follow me, and I'll teach you how to bring in people instead of fish."
**18** They dropped their nets on the spot and followed him.
**19** Walking a little farther down the beach, he noticed Zebedee's sons, James and John, sitting in their boat fixing their nets.
**20** Right away he called them too. They left their father Zebedee behind in the boat with the hired crew and went off with Jesus.
**21** They walked into Capernaum, and as soon as the Sabbath day arrived, Jesus went into the synagogue and started teaching.
**22** Everyone listening was blown away by how he taught, because he spoke with real authority—nothing like the religious scholars.
**23** Right then, a man in the synagogue who had an evil spirit shouted out,
**24** "Leave us alone, Jesus of Nazareth! Why are you interfering with us? Did you come to wipe us out? I know exactly who you are—God's Holy One!"
**25** Jesus shut the spirit down sharply: "Quiet! Get out of him!"
**26** The evil spirit shook the man violently, let out a loud shriek, and left him.
**27** Everyone there was stunned and started asking each other, "What just happened? This is brand-new teaching, and it has real power behind it! He even gives orders to evil spirits, and they do what he says!"
**28** Word about Jesus spread like wildfire all across the region of Galilee.
**29** As soon as they stepped out of the synagogue, Jesus went over to Simon and Andrew's house along with James and John.
**30** Simon's mother-in-law was sick in bed with a fever, so they told Jesus about her right away.
**31** He walked over, took hold of her hand, and helped her up. Her fever vanished on the spot, and she began getting a meal ready for them.
**32** That evening after sunset, people brought everyone who was sick or troubled by demons to Jesus.
**33** Practically the whole town packed around the front door.
**34** He healed large numbers of people suffering from all kinds of illnesses and drove out many demons. He refused to let the demons say a word, because they knew who he really was.
**35** Long before sunrise, while it was still dark outside, Jesus got up, slipped out of the house, and went to a quiet, lonely spot to pray.
**36** Simon and the others went out searching for him.
**37** When they finally tracked him down, they told him, "Everybody is looking for you!"
**38** Jesus answered, "Let's head to the nearby villages so I can share the message there as well. That's what I came here to do."
**39** So he traveled through the whole region of Galilee, speaking in their synagogues and driving out demons.
**40** One day a man with a skin disease came up to Jesus, dropped to his knees, and begged, "If you want to, you can make me clean."
**41** Deeply stirred, Jesus reached out, touched him, and said, "I do want to. Be clean!"
**42** Instantly the skin disease cleared up, and the man was completely healed.
**43** Jesus spoke sternly to him and sent him off right away,
**44** warning him, "Don't tell anyone about this. Go straight to the priest, let him examine you, and bring the sacrifice Moses required for your cleansing so the officials have proof."
**45** Instead, as soon as the man left, he started telling everybody and broadcasting what had happened. Soon Jesus couldn't even walk openly into any town, so he stayed out in deserted areas. Even there, crowds kept flocking to him from everywhere.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `εὐαγγελίου Ἰησοῦ χριστοῦ` (*euangeliou Iēsou Christou*):** *Euangelion* means "good announcement" or "glad tidings" (used in Greco-Roman inscriptions for royal accessions and victories). SBLGNT and Westcott-Hort end v. 1 at *Christou*, matching Codex Sinaiticus (`א*`) and early patristic witnesses; later critical editions (`Treg`, `NA28`) and Byzantine manuscripts (`RP`) add `υἱοῦ [τοῦ] θεοῦ` (*huiou [tou] theou*, "Son of God").
- **v. 4 `μετανοίας` (*metanoias*):** From *metanoia*, a thorough change of mind, direction, and allegiance—not merely feeling sorry, but turning back toward God's covenant rule.
- **v. 10 `σχιζομένους` (*schizomenous*):** Literally "being split/ripped apart" (contrasting with Matthew and Luke's gentler *anoigō*, "to open"), matching the tearing of the temple curtain in Mark 15:38.
- **v. 12 `εὐθὺς … ἐκβάλλει` (*euthys … ekballei*):** Mark uses *euthys* ("immediately/at once") repeatedly to drive the narrative pace, and *ekballei* ("drives out/casts out") is the same forceful verb used for expelling demons (v. 34).
- **v. 22 `ἐξουσίαν` (*exousian*):** Intrinsic authority or delegated right to act, contrasted with scribes who appealed to prior rabbinic precedent.
- **v. 41 `ὀργισθεὶς` (*orgistheis*):** SBLGNT adopts *orgistheis* ("moved with anger/indignation," attested in Codex Bezae `D` and Old Latin witnesses), whereas most manuscripts and `WH`/`NA28`/`RP` read `σπλαγχνισθεὶς` (*splangchnistheis*, "moved with deep compassion"). Anger here likely targets the destructive power of illness/impurity or the isolation it inflicts.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Malachi 3:1 & Exodus 23:20** (woven with Isaiah 40:3 in Mark 1:2): *"See, I am dispatching my messenger before your face, who will clear the path ahead of you."*
- **Isaiah 40:3** (quoted in Mark 1:3): *"A voice calls out in the desert: 'Clear a highway for the LORD; make straight in the wasteland a road for our God.'"*
- **Psalm 2:7 & Isaiah 42:1** (echoed in Mark 1:11): *"You are my own Son; this very day I have fathered you"* combined with *"Here stands my servant whom I sustain, my chosen one in whom my soul takes delight."*
- **Leviticus 14:2–32** (referenced in Mark 1:44): Prescribes priestly inspection and cleansing offerings for someone healed of *tsara'at* (ritual skin disease).
