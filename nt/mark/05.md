## Clear & Faithful

**1** They arrived on the opposite shore of the sea, in the region of the Gerasenes.
**2** As soon as Jesus stepped out of the boat, a man possessed by an unclean spirit met him coming out of the tombs.
**3** He made his home among the tombs, and no one could restrain him any longer, not even with a chain;
**4** for he had often been bound with shackles and chains, yet he snapped the chains apart and broke the shackles to pieces, and nobody had the strength to subdue him.
**5** Night and day, continuously among the tombs and in the hills, he would cry out and slash himself with stones.
**6** Catching sight of Jesus from a distance, he ran up and fell to his knees before him,
**7** shouting at the top of his voice, "What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you before God, do not torment me!"
**8** For Jesus had been commanding him, "Come out of the man, you unclean spirit!"
**9** Then Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" He answered him, "My name is Legion, because we are many."
**10** And he pleaded earnestly with him not to send them out of that territory.
**11** Now a large herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside,
**12** and the spirits begged him, "Send us into the pigs; let us enter them."
**13** So he gave them permission. Coming out, the unclean spirits entered the pigs, and the herd—numbering about two thousand—charged down the steep slope into the sea and drowned in the water.
**14** Their herders bolted and spread the news in the town and the countryside, and people came out to see what had happened.
**15** When they reached Jesus, they saw the man who had been demon-possessed sitting there clothed and in his right mind—the very man who had harbored the legion—and they were terrified.
**16** Eyewitnesses recounted to them what had happened to the demon-possessed man and to the pigs,
**17** and the townspeople began urging Jesus to leave their district.
**18** As he was stepping into the boat, the man who had been freed from the demons begged to stay with him.
**19** Yet Jesus refused him and told him instead, "Go home to your own people and report to them everything the Lord has done for you and how he has shown you mercy."
**20** So the man went off and began proclaiming throughout the Decapolis all that Jesus had done for him, and everyone was amazed.
**21** Once Jesus had crossed back over in the boat to the other side, a massive crowd gathered around him while he remained beside the sea.
**22** Then one of the synagogue leaders, named Jairus, arrived; seeing him, he fell at his feet
**23** and pleaded desperately with him, "My little daughter is at the point of death. Come and lay your hands on her so that she may be rescued and live."
**24** Jesus went with him, and a huge crowd followed along, pressing tightly against him.
**25** Among them was a woman who had suffered from hemorrhaging for twelve years;
**26** she had endured much under many doctors, spent everything she owned, and gained no relief at all—in fact, she had only grown worse.
**27** Having heard reports about Jesus, she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his cloak,
**28** for she kept telling herself, "If I can touch even his clothes, I will be healed."
**29** Instantly the flow of her blood dried up, and she sensed within her body that she had been cured of her affliction.
**30** At that very instant Jesus realized inside himself that power had gone forth from him; turning around in the crowd, he asked, "Who touched my clothes?"
**31** His disciples replied, "You see the crowd pressing in on every side, and yet you ask, 'Who touched me?'"
**32** Still he kept looking around to see who had done this.
**33** Trembling with fear, aware of what had happened to her, the woman stepped forward, fell down before him, and told him the whole truth.
**34** He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has made you whole. Go in peace, and remain freed from your affliction."
**35** While he was still speaking, messengers arrived from the synagogue leader's house saying, "Your daughter has died; why trouble the teacher any further?"
**36** Overhearing what was being said, Jesus told the synagogue leader, "Do not be afraid; simply keep trusting."
**37** Jesus permitted nobody else to accompany him aside from Peter, James, and James's brother John.
**38** When they reached the synagogue leader's home, he saw a noisy uproar—people weeping and wailing loudly.
**39** Stepping inside, he asked them, "Why all this commotion and weeping? The child has not died; she is asleep."
**40** They laughed him to scorn. Putting everyone outside, he took the child's father and mother along with his companions and entered the room where the child lay.
**41** Taking the child by the hand, he said to her, "Talitha koum"—which translates as, "Little girl, I tell you, get up!"
**42** At once the girl stood up and began walking around—she was twelve years old—and immediately they were overwhelmed with utter astonishment.
**43** He gave them strict orders that no one should learn of this, and told them to give her something to eat.

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus and his followers reached the far side of the lake, landing in the area of the Gerasenes.
**2** The moment Jesus stepped out of the boat, a man controlled by an evil spirit came out from the burial caves to meet him.
**3** This man lived among the tombs, and nobody could keep him tied up anymore—not even with iron chains.
**4** People had chained his hands and shackled his feet plenty of times, yet he always snapped the chains and smashed the shackles. No one was strong enough to control him.
**5** Day and night he wandered through the tombs and up in the hills, screaming and cutting himself with sharp stones.
**6** From far off he spotted Jesus, sprinted over, and dropped to his knees in front of him,
**7** yelling at the top of his lungs, "What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Swear to God you won't torture me!"
**8** He yelled that because Jesus was already ordering him, "Evil spirit, come out of this man!"
**9** Jesus asked him, "What's your name?" "My name is Legion," he answered, "because there are so many of us."
**10** Then he begged Jesus repeatedly not to kick them out of that area.
**11** Nearby on the hillside, a huge herd of pigs was rooting around for food.
**12** The spirits pleaded with Jesus, "Send us into those pigs! Let us go into them!"
**13** Jesus let them go. The evil spirits left the man and went into the pigs, and the entire herd—around two thousand pigs—stampeded down the steep cliff into the lake and drowned.
**14** The men tending the pigs ran off in panic and spread the story through the town and nearby farms, so people rushed out to see what had happened.
**15** When they got to Jesus, they saw the man who used to have the legion of demons sitting there, wearing clothes and thinking clearly—and they were scared stiff.
**16** People who had watched the whole thing explained what happened to the demon-troubled man and to the pigs.
**17** Then the locals started begging Jesus to leave their territory.
**18** As Jesus climbed back into the boat, the man who had been set free pleaded, "Please let me come with you!"
**19** Jesus wouldn't let him, telling him instead, "Go back home to your family and friends, and tell them everything the Lord has done for you and how merciful he's been."
**20** So the man went off and started broadcasting throughout the Ten Towns region all that Jesus had done for him, and everybody marveled.
**21** When Jesus sailed back across to the other side of the lake, a massive crowd gathered around him right on the beach.
**22** A synagogue official named Jairus pushed his way up, dropped at Jesus' feet,
**23** and begged him urgently, "My little girl is dying! Please come lay your hands on her so she'll recover and live!"
**24** Jesus started off with him, and a huge crowd trailed behind, squeezing him from every side.
**25** In the crowd was a woman who'd been bleeding nonstop for twelve years.
**26** She'd suffered horribly under doctor after doctor, spent every cent she had, and never got better—she only got worse.
**27** When she heard about Jesus, she slipped up behind him in the crowd and touched his coat,
**28** thinking to herself, "If I just brush against his clothes, I'll be healed."
**29** Right away the bleeding stopped, and she felt in her own body that her long illness was gone.
**30** Instantly Jesus realized power had flowed out of him. He spun around in the crowd and asked, "Who touched my clothes?"
**31** His followers said, "Look at this crowd pushing all around you—how can you ask, 'Who touched me?'"
**32** Yet Jesus kept scanning the crowd to find the person who'd done it.
**33** Terrified and shaking because she knew what had just happened to her, the woman stepped up, fell down in front of him, and confessed the whole story.
**34** Jesus gently told her, "Daughter, your trust has healed you. Go in peace, completely free from your illness."
**35** While he was still talking to her, people came from Jairus's house with terrible news: "Your daughter just died. Why bother the teacher anymore?"
**36** Jesus caught what they were saying, ignored their report, and told the synagogue leader, "Don't panic—just keep trusting."
**37** He didn't let anyone else come along with him except Peter, James, and James's brother John.
**38** When they reached Jairus's house, Jesus saw total chaos—people sobbing and wailing loudly.
**39** He walked inside and said to them, "Why all the uproar and crying? The girl isn't dead; she's only sleeping."
**40** Everyone burst out mocking him. So he cleared everybody out, brought only the girl's parents and his three disciples inside, and walked into the room where she lay.
**41** Taking the little girl by the hand, he spoke to her: "Talitha koum"—which means, "Little girl, I'm telling you, wake up!"
**42** Immediately the twelve-year-old girl stood up and began walking around! Her family was stunned beyond words.
**43** Jesus gave them strict instructions not to tell anybody what had happened, and added, "Get her something to eat."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `Γερασηνῶν` (*Gerasēnōn*):** SBLGNT (`WH Treg NA28`) reads "Gerasenes" (matching Gerasa / Kursi area on the eastern shore), whereas Byzantine manuscripts (`RP`) harmonize to `Γαδαρηνῶν` (*Gadarēnōn*, "Gadarenes," cf. Matt 8:28).
- **v. 9 `Λεγιών` (*Legiōn*):** A Roman military loanword (*legio*, normally ~5,000–6,000 soldiers), vividly framing the man's oppression in imperial occupation imagery.
- **v. 15 `σωφρονοῦντα` (*sōphronounta*):** "Being of sound mind / restored to self-control," contrasting starkly with the chaos of vv. 3–5.
- **v. 29 `μάστιγος` (*mastigos*):** Literally "whip" or "scourge" (also in v. 34 and Mark 3:10), portraying chronic illness as an unrelenting torment.
- **v. 36 `παρακούσας` (*parakousas*):** Literally "having heard alongside," carrying the dual sense of overhearing a report and deliberately brushing it aside / refusing to heed its discouragement (`RP` replaces it with *eutheōs akousas*, "immediately hearing").
- **v. 41 `Ταλιθα κουμ` (*Talitha koum*):** Mark preserves Jesus' Aramaic words (*ṭalyĕthā qûm*, "maiden/little girl, arise!") and translates them into Greek (`τὸ κοράσιον, σοὶ λέγω, ἔγειρε`) for his Gentile audience.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Psalm 65:7 & Psalm 107:10–14** (echoed in Mark 5:1–15): God subdues the roaring seas and breaks the iron shackles of those dwelling in darkness and the shadow of death.
- **Leviticus 15:25–27** (background to Mark 5:25–34): A twelve-year flow of blood rendered a woman ritually impure, making her bold touch in a crowd an act of courageous faith.
- **1 Kings 17:17–24 & 2 Kings 4:32–37** (echoed in Mark 5:35–43): Elijah and Elisha exclude onlookers and raise dead children behind closed doors.
