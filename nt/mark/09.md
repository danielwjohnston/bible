## Clear & Faithful

**1** And he told them, "Truly I say to you, there are some standing right here who will certainly not taste death until they see the kingdom of God having arrived in power."
**2** Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John with him and led them up a high mountain by themselves, completely alone. There he was transfigured before their eyes;
**3** his clothes became dazzlingly radiant, whiter than any launderer on earth could ever bleach them.
**4** Elijah appeared to them together with Moses, and they were conversing with Jesus.
**5** Peter spoke up and said to Jesus, "Rabbi, it is good for us to be here; let us put up three shelters—one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."
**6** In truth, he did not know what to say, for they were overcome with terror.
**7** Then a cloud appeared, casting its shadow over them, and a voice spoke out of the cloud: "This is my beloved Son; listen to him!"
**8** Suddenly, as they looked around, they saw no one with them any longer except Jesus alone.
**9** While they were coming down the mountain, he instructed them not to recount to anyone what they had seen until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.
**10** They kept the matter closely to themselves, debating among each other what "rising from the dead" could mean.
**11** Then they asked him, "Why do the scribes insist that Elijah must come first?"
**12** He answered them, "Elijah indeed comes first and restores all things. How then is it written concerning the Son of Man that he must suffer many things and be treated with utter contempt?
**13** Yet I tell you, Elijah has already come, and they did to him whatever they pleased, just as it stands written about him."
**14** When they returned to the other disciples, they saw a large crowd surrounding them and scribes arguing with them.
**15** The instant the whole crowd caught sight of Jesus, they were struck with awe and ran up to greet him.
**16** He asked them, "What are you arguing about with one another?"
**17** Someone from the crowd answered him, "Teacher, I brought my son to you because he is afflicted by a spirit that makes him unable to speak.
**18** Whenever it seizes him, it slams him down; he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and goes stiff. I asked your disciples to drive it out, yet they lacked the strength."
**19** Jesus responded to them, "O unbelieving generation, how much longer must I stay with you? How much longer must I put up with you? Bring him to me."
**20** So they brought the boy to him. As soon as the spirit saw Jesus, it immediately threw the boy into violent convulsions; he fell to the ground and rolled about, foaming at the mouth.
**21** Jesus asked his father, "How long has this been happening to him?" He replied, "Since early childhood;
**22** many times it has thrown him both into fire and into water to destroy him. Still, if you are able to do anything, take pity on us and help us!"
**23** Jesus said to him, "'If you are able'? All things are possible for the one who trusts!"
**24** Immediately the boy's father cried out, "I do believe—help my unbelief!"
**25** Seeing a crowd rapidly converging on the scene, Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, commanding it, "You deaf and mute spirit, I command you: come out of him and never enter him again!"
**26** With a loud shriek and violent convulsions, it came out. The boy lay looking like a corpse, so that most people were saying, "He is dead."
**27** Yet Jesus took him by the hand, lifted him up, and he stood on his feet.
**28** Once Jesus went indoors, his disciples questioned him privately, "Why were we unable to drive it out?"
**29** He replied to them, "This kind can come out by nothing except prayer."
**30** Leaving that region, they traveled onward through Galilee, and Jesus did not want anyone to know where he was,
**31** because he was teaching his disciples and telling them, "The Son of Man is being handed over into human hands, and they will kill him; yet after being killed, three days later he will rise."
**32** They did not understand what he meant, and they were afraid to ask him.
**33** They arrived in Capernaum. Once inside the house, he asked them, "What were you debating along the road?"
**34** They stayed silent, because on the road they had argued with one another about which of them was the greatest.
**35** Sitting down, he summoned the Twelve and told them, "Whoever wishes to be first must become last of all and servant of all."
**36** Taking a young child, he placed the child right in the middle of them, gathered the child into his arms, and said to them,
**37** "Whoever welcomes one such child in my name welcomes me; and whoever welcomes me welcomes not merely me, but the one who sent me."
**38** John said to him, "Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons in your name, and we tried to stop him because he was not following along with us."
**39** Jesus replied, "Do not stop him; for no one who performs a mighty work in my name will be able soon afterward to speak evil of me.
**40** Whoever is not against us is for us.
**41** Truly I tell you, anyone who gives you a cup of water to drink simply because you belong to Christ will never lose their reward.
**42** Yet whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble—it would be far better for them to have a heavy donkey-turned millstone hung around their neck and be cast into the sea.
**43** And if your hand leads you into sin, slice it off; it is far better for you to enter life disabled than to retain both hands and go away into Gehenna, into the fire that never dies.
**45** And if your foot leads you into sin, slice it off; it is far better for you to enter life crippled than to retain both feet and be cast into Gehenna.
**47** And if your eye causes you to stumble, tear it out; it is better for you to enter the kingdom of God with one eye than to keep both eyes and be thrown into Gehenna,
**48** where their worm never dies and the fire is never extinguished.
**49** For everyone will be salted with fire.
**50** Salt is good; yet if salt loses its saltiness, how will you restore its flavor? Have salt within yourselves, and live at peace with one another."

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus told them, "Listen closely: some of you standing right here won't die before you see God's kingdom arrive with power!"
**2** Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain completely by themselves. Right before their eyes, Jesus' appearance was transformed:
**3** his clothes turned brilliantly white—far whiter than any laundry bleacher on earth could ever make them.
**4** Suddenly Elijah and Moses appeared right there, talking with Jesus.
**5** Peter blurted out to Jesus, "Teacher, it's wonderful for us to be here! Let's build three shelters—one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."
**6** Honestly, he had no idea what to say because all three disciples were terrified.
**7** Then a cloud drifted over and shaded them, and a voice spoke from inside the cloud: "This is my dearly loved Son. Listen to him!"
**8** All of a sudden, when they looked around, nobody was there anymore except Jesus by himself.
**9** As they walked back down the mountain, Jesus ordered them not to tell a soul what they'd witnessed until the Son of Man had risen from death.
**10** They kept the secret to themselves, though they kept debating what "rising from death" actually meant.
**11** Then they asked him, "Why do the legal scholars keep saying that Elijah has to arrive first?"
**12** Jesus answered, "Elijah really does come first to put everything back in order. Yet why does Scripture also say that the Son of Man must suffer terribly and be despised?
**13** Let me tell you: Elijah has already come, and people treated him however they pleased, just as Scripture foretold about him."
**14** When they walked back down to the other disciples, they saw a massive crowd gathered around them and religious scholars arguing with them.
**15** The second the whole crowd spotted Jesus, they were stunned with amazement and sprinted over to welcome him.
**16** Jesus asked, "What are you all arguing about?"
**17** A man from the crowd spoke up: "Teacher, I brought my son to you because an evil spirit has taken away his speech.
**18** Whenever it grabs hold of him, it throws him to the ground; he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and turns rigid. I begged your disciples to drive it out, and they couldn't do it."
**19** Jesus sighed and said, "What an unbelieving generation! How long do I have to stay with you? How much longer must I put up with you? Bring the boy over here."
**20** So they brought him to Jesus. The moment the spirit saw Jesus, it threw the boy into violent spasms; he collapsed on the dirt and rolled around foaming at the mouth.
**21** Jesus asked the boy's father, "How long has he been going through this?" "Ever since he was a small child," the father replied.
**22** "Time after time it's flung him into fire and into water trying to kill him. Please—if you can do anything at all, have mercy on us and help us!"
**23** Jesus answered, "'If you can'? Everything is possible for someone who trusts!"
**24** Right away the boy's father cried out, "I do trust—please help me overcome my doubts!"
**25** Noticing a crowd rushing toward them, Jesus rebuked the evil spirit: "You deaf and mute spirit, I'm giving you an order: get out of this boy and never come back inside him!"
**26** The spirit screamed, shook the boy violently, and came out. The boy lay completely limp like a dead body, so most of the onlookers whispered, "He's dead."
**27** But Jesus reached down, took the boy's hand, and pulled him up, and he stood right up.
**28** Later, after Jesus went inside a house, his followers asked him privately, "Why couldn't we force that spirit out?"
**29** Jesus told them, "That kind of spirit can only be driven out through prayer."
**30** Leaving that area, they made their way quietly through Galilee. Jesus didn't want anyone to know where he was,
**31** because he was focused on teaching his disciples: "The Son of Man is going to be betrayed into human hands. They'll execute him, and three days after he's killed he'll rise again."
**32** They still couldn't make sense of what he meant, and they were too scared to ask him to explain.
**33** They walked into Capernaum. Once they were settled inside the house, Jesus asked them, "What were you guys arguing about on the road?"
**34** Nobody said a word, because along the way they'd been arguing over who among them was the most important.
**35** So Jesus sat down, called the Twelve around him, and said, "Anyone who wants to be number one has to take last place and become everyone's servant."
**36** Then he brought a small child over, stood the child right in the middle of them, wrapped his arms around the child, and told them,
**37** "Whoever welcomes one little child like this because of me welcomes me—and welcoming me means welcoming the One who sent me."
**38** John spoke up, "Teacher, we caught someone driving out demons using your name, so we tried to stop him because he isn't part of our group."
**39** Jesus said, "Don't stop him! Nobody who performs a miracle in my name is going to turn right around and badmouth me.
**40** Anyone who isn't working against us is on our side.
**41** Listen closely: anyone who hands you even a cup of cold water simply because you belong to the Messiah will never lose their reward.
**42** On the other hand, if anyone trips up one of these little ones who trust in me, they'd be better off having a huge millstone tied around their neck and being tossed straight into the sea.
**43** If your hand leads you into sin, chop it off! It's far better to enter eternal life missing a hand than to keep both hands and end up in Gehenna's unquenchable fire.
**45** If your foot leads you into sin, chop it off! It's better to enter eternal life crippled than to keep both feet and be thrown into Gehenna.
**47** And if your eye leads you into sin, gouge it out! It's better to enter God's kingdom with one eye than to keep both eyes and be thrown into Gehenna,
**48** where 'the worms never stop eating and the fire never goes out.'
**49** Everybody will be tested and purified with fire, like salt.
**50** Salt is great—yet if salt loses its flavor, how could you ever make it salty again? Keep that saltiness alive inside yourselves, and live in peace with each other."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `μετεμορφώθη` (*metemorphōthē*):** Literally "was transformed in form/appearance" (*metamorphoō*), revealing inner divine glory rather than an external disguise.
- **v. 3 `γναφεύς` (*gnapheus*):** A fuller or cloth-dresser who scoured and bleached woolens using alkaline earth and sulfur.
- **v. 29 `προσευχῇ` (*proseuchē*):** SBLGNT (`WH` / `NA28`) ends at "prayer"; `Treg` and Byzantine manuscripts (`RP`) add `καὶ νηστείᾳ` ("and fasting").
- **v. 42 `μύλος ὀνικός` (*mylos onikos*):** A massive upper millstone rotated by a donkey, contrasting with a small household hand-mill.
- **v. 44 `ὅπου ὁ σκώληξ αὐτῶν οὐ τελευτᾷ καὶ τὸ πῦρ οὐ σβέννυται` (*hopou ho skōlēx autōn ou teleuta kai to pyr ou sbennytai*):** Omitted in SBLGNT and Nestle 1904 (`WH` / `NA28` / earliest Alexandrian witnesses); `Treg` and `RP` repeat Isaiah 66:24 here after v. 43 (`ἄσβεστον`), whereas SBLGNT retains it solely at v. 48.
- **v. 46 `ὅπου ὁ σκώληξ αὐτῶν οὐ τελευτᾷ καὶ τὸ πῦρ οὐ σβέννυται` (*hopou ho skōlēx autōn ou teleuta kai to pyr ou sbennytai*):** Omitted in SBLGNT and Nestle 1904 (`WH` / `NA28`); `Treg` and `RP` also insert `εἰς τὸ πῦρ τὸ ἄσβεστον` ("into the unquenchable fire") and this refrain after v. 45, harmonizing all three sayings to v. 48.
- **v. 49 `πυρὶ ἁλισθήσεται` (*pyri halisthēsetai*):** "Will be salted with fire" (`WH` / `NA28`); `Treg` and `RP` expand with `καὶ πᾶσα θυσία ἁλὶ ἁλισθήσεται` ("and every sacrifice will be salted with salt," echoing Lev 2:13).
