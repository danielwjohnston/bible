## Clear & Faithful

**1** Once the Sabbath had passed, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome purchased aromatic spices so they might go and anoint Jesus' body.
**2** Very early on the first day of the week, right as the sun had risen, they arrived at the tomb.
**3** Along the way they were asking one another, "Who will roll away the stone for us from the entrance of the tomb?"
**4** Yet looking up, they saw that the stone—though exceedingly massive—had already been rolled aside.
**5** Entering inside the tomb, they saw a young man dressed in a white robe sitting on the right side, and they were gripped with amazement.
**6** He spoke to them: "Do not be alarmed! You are looking for Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has been raised; he is not here. Look at the spot where they laid him.
**7** Now go and tell his disciples—and Peter—'He is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see him, exactly as he told you.'"
**8** They hurried outside and fled from the tomb, because trembling and bewilderment had seized them; and they said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid. [Shorter ending in some manuscripts (Nestle 1904 v. 99): Yet they promptly reported everything they had been instructed to Peter's companions. Afterward Jesus himself sent out through them, from east to west, the sacred and imperishable proclamation of eternal salvation. Amen.]
**9** [[Longer ending in later manuscripts: After rising early on the first day of the week, Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had expelled seven demons.
**10** She went and told those who had been with him, who were mourning and weeping;
**11** Yet upon hearing that he was alive and had appeared to her, they refused to credit it.
**12** Afterward he appeared in a different form to two of them as they were walking along into the countryside;
**13** they went back and reported it to the rest, yet they did not believe them either.
**14** Later he appeared to the Eleven themselves as they reclined at table, and he rebuked their unbelief and stubbornness of heart because they had refused to trust those who had seen him after he had been raised.
**15** He told them, "Go out into the whole world and proclaim the good news to all creation.
**16** Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, whereas whoever disbelieves will be condemned.
**17** And these miraculous signs will attend those who trust: in my name they will drive out demons; they will speak in unfamiliar tongues;
**18** they will pick up snakes, and if they drink anything deadly, it will by no means harm them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover."
**19** So then the Lord Jesus, after speaking with them, was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God.
**20** And they went forth and proclaimed the message everywhere, while the Lord worked alongside them and confirmed the word through the signs that followed.]]

## Everyday English

**1** As soon as the Sabbath ended at sundown, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought fragrant burial spices so they could go anoint Jesus' body.
**2** At dawn on Sunday morning, just as the sun came up, they walked to the tomb.
**3** On the way they wondered aloud, "Who's going to roll that boulder away from the tomb's doorway for us?"
**4** When they glanced up, though, they noticed the stone had already been rolled back—even though it was enormous!
**5** Stepping inside the tomb, they spotted a young man sitting on the right side wearing a bright white robe, and they froze in shock.
**6** He reassured them: "Don't panic! You're searching for Jesus of Nazareth, the one who was crucified. He has risen—he isn't here! Look right here at the spot where they laid his body.
**7** Hurry back and tell his disciples—especially Peter—'He's heading up to Galilee ahead of you. You'll see him right there, just like he promised you.'"
**8** They ran straight out of the tomb and fled, shaken and bewildered. They didn't say a word to anybody, because they were terrified. [Shorter ending preserved in some manuscripts (Nestle 1904 v. 99): Then they quickly passed on every instruction to Peter and his friends. After that, Jesus himself sent out through them, from the rising sun to the setting sun, the holy and lasting good news of eternal rescue. Amen.]
**9** [[Longer ending found in later manuscripts: After rising early Sunday morning, Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene, the woman he'd freed from seven demons.
**10** She ran straight to tell his close companions, who were grieving and sobbing;
**11** yet even after hearing he was alive and she'd seen him with her own eyes, they didn't believe her.
**12** Next, Jesus showed up looking different to two followers while they were walking out into the countryside.
**13** Those two hurried back and told the others, yet nobody believed them either.
**14** Finally, Jesus appeared to the Eleven themselves while they were eating dinner, and he scolded them for their stubborn unbelief because they wouldn't trust the eyewitnesses who'd seen him alive.
**15** He commanded them, "Go out across the entire world and share the good news with every person alive.
**16** Whoever trusts this message and is baptized will be rescued, while anyone who refuses to trust will be condemned.
**17** And these miraculous signs will back up those who trust me: using my name they'll drive out demons and speak in brand-new languages;
**18** they'll handle poisonous snakes safely, and if they drink lethal poison it won't hurt them at all; they'll place hands on sick folks, and they'll heal."
**19** After finishing his instructions to them, the Lord Jesus was lifted up into heaven and took his seat at God's right hand.
**20** Then the disciples went out and preached everywhere, while the Lord worked right beside them and backed up their message with unmistakable miracles.]]

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 8 `ἐφοβοῦντο γάρ` (*ephobounto gar*):** Literally "for they were afraid"—ending the Gospel abruptly on the postpositive conjunction *gar* in the earliest Greek witnesses (Codex Sinaiticus `א` and Codex Vaticanus `B`, along with early Syriac and Armenian manuscripts, `WH`, and `NA28`). SBLGNT encloses the shorter ending (`Πάντα δὲ τὰ παρηγγελμένα…`) in double brackets `⟦...⟧` immediately inside v. 8, while Nestle 1904 sets it apart as `v. 99` (without final `ἀμήν`); it is attested in uncials `L`, `Ψ`, `099`, and `0112` (often paired with the longer ending).
- **v. 9–20 `Ἀναστὰς δὲ πρωῒ…` (*Anastas de prōi…*):** The longer ending is absent from `א` and `B`, marked with asterisks/obeli or double brackets in SBLGNT and `WH`, and attested in Alexandrinus `A`, Ephraemi Rescriptus `C`, Bezae `D`, `W`, `Treg`, and Byzantine manuscripts (`RP`). Note that `Nestle 1904` prints vv. 9–20 unbracketed with minor wording differences (e.g., `v. 14` lacks `ἐκ νεκρῶν`; `v. 18` reads `ὄφεις` with `RP` rather than `καὶ ἐν ταῖς χερσὶν ὄφεις` in `WH`/`NA28`; `v. 20` omits closing `Ἀμήν`).
- **v. 12 `ἐν ἑτέρᾳ μορφῇ` (*en hetera morphē*):** "In a different form/appearance," echoing the Emmaus road appearance in Luke 24:13–32.
- **v. 17 `γλώσσαις … καιναῖς` (*glōssais … kainais*):** "New tongues/languages"; `NA28` and `RP` retain `καιναῖς`, whereas `WH` and `Treg` omit `καιναῖς` ("speak in tongues").

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Mark 14:28** (recalled in Mark 16:7): *"Once I have been raised, I will walk ahead of you into Galilee."*
- **Luke 24:1–35 & John 20:1–18** (parallel to Mark 16:1–14): Early Easter morning discovery of the empty tomb, appearance to Mary Magdalene, and the two travelers on the road.
- **Matthew 28:18–20 & Acts 2:4, 28:3–6** (parallel to Mark 16:15–18): The universal commission to all creation, speaking in new tongues, and Paul surviving a viper bite unharmed on Malta.
- **Psalm 110:1** (echoed in Mark 16:19): *"Take your seat at my right side"*—fulfilled as the risen Lord takes his seat at God's right hand.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** When the Sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices, that they might come and anoint him.
**2** Very early on the first day of the week, they came to the tomb when the sun had risen.
**3** They were saying among themselves, “Who will roll away the stone from the door of the tomb for us?”
**4** for it was very big. Looking up, they saw that the stone was rolled back.
**5** Entering into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, dressed in a white robe; and they were amazed.
**6** He said to them, “Don’t be amazed. You seek Jesus, the Nazarene, who has been crucified. He has risen! He is not here. See the place where they laid him!
**7** But go, tell his disciples and Peter, ‘He goes before you into Galilee. There you will see him, as he said to you.’”
**8** They went out, and fled from the tomb, for trembling and astonishment had come on them. They said nothing to anyone; for they were afraid.
**9** Now when he had risen early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom he had cast out seven demons.
**10** She went and told those who had been with him, as they mourned and wept.
**11** When they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they disbelieved.
**12** After these things he was revealed in another form to two of them as they walked, on their way into the country.
**13** They went away and told it to the rest. They didn’t believe them, either.
**14** Afterward he was revealed to the eleven themselves as they sat at the table; and he rebuked them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they didn’t believe those who had seen him after he had risen.
**15** He said to them, “Go into all the world and preach the Good News to the whole creation.
**16** He who believes and is baptized will be saved; but he who disbelieves will be condemned.
**17** These signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak with new languages;
**18** they will take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it will in no way hurt them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover.”
**19** So then the Lord, after he had spoken to them, was received up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God.
**20** They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed. Amen.
