## Clear & Faithful

**1** Setting out from there, Jesus arrived in the region of Judea and across the Jordan. Once more crowds gathered around him, and according to his custom he taught them again.
**2** People asked him whether it was lawful for a husband to divorce his wife, testing him.
**3** He replied to them, "What instruction did Moses leave you?"
**4** They replied, "Moses allowed a man to write a certificate of divorce and send her away."
**5** Jesus said to them, "It was because of your stubborn hardness of heart that he wrote this command for you.
**6** Yet right from the start of creation, 'He created them male and female.'
**7** 'For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be united to his wife,
**8** and the pair will become a single flesh.' Consequently, they are no longer two separate people, but a single flesh.
**9** Therefore, what God has joined together, let no human being separate."
**10** Back inside the house, the disciples questioned him again about this matter.
**11** Jesus told them, "Any man who divorces his wife and weds another commits adultery against her;
**12** and if she divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery."
**13** People were bringing young children to him so that he might touch them, and the disciples rebuked them.
**14** Seeing this, Jesus grew indignant and told them, "Let the young children come to me; do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.
**15** Truly I say to you, whoever does not welcome the kingdom of God like a young child will certainly never enter it."
**16** Then he gathered them in his arms and warmly blessed them, laying his hands on them.
**17** As he was setting out onto the road, a man ran up, knelt before him, and asked, "Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?"
**18** Jesus answered him, "Why do you address me as good? Nobody is truly good except God alone.
**19** You already know the commandments: Do not murder, refrain from adultery, do not steal, give no false witness, never defraud anyone, and honor your father and mother."
**20** He replied to him, "Teacher, all these I have observed since my youth."
**21** Looking intently at him, Jesus loved him and said, "One thing you still lack: go, sell everything you own and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; then come, follow me."
**22** At that statement his face darkened, and he walked away grieving, for he held great wealth.
**23** Looking around at his disciples, Jesus said to them, "How hard it will be for those who possess riches to enter the kingdom of God!"
**24** The disciples were stunned by his words. Yet Jesus answered them again, "Children, how hard it is to enter the kingdom of God!
**25** It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a wealthy person to enter the kingdom of God."
**26** They were astounded beyond measure, saying to one another, "Then who can possibly be saved?"
**27** Looking straight at them, Jesus said, "With human beings it is impossible, yet not with God; for everything is possible with God."
**28** Peter began saying to him, "Look, we have left everything behind and followed you."
**29** Jesus replied, "Truly I tell you, there is no one who has left home or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for my sake and for the sake of the good news
**30** who will not receive a hundred times as much right now in this present age—homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields, along with persecutions—and in the coming age, eternal life.
**31** Yet many who stand first will become last, and the last will become first."
**32** Now they were on the road climbing up toward Jerusalem, with Jesus striding ahead of them; they were filled with awe, while those following behind were afraid. Taking the Twelve aside once more, he began telling them what was about to happen to him:
**33** "See, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and the scribes; they will condemn him to death and hand him over to the Gentiles,
**34** who will mock him, spit on him, flog him, and kill him; and after three days he will rise again."
**35** Then James and John, the sons of Zebedee, approached him and said, "Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we ask of you."
**36** He asked them, "What is it you want me to do on your behalf?"
**37** They answered him, "Allow one of us to sit on your right side and the other on your left side in your glory."
**38** Jesus answered them, "You do not realize what you are asking. Can you drink the cup that I drink, or be baptized with the baptism with which I am baptized?"
**39** "We can," they told him. Jesus said to them, "You will indeed drink the cup I drink and be baptized with the baptism I am baptized with;
**40** yet to sit at my right hand or at my left is not mine to grant, but belongs to those for whom it has been prepared."
**41** When the other ten heard about this, they began feeling indignant toward James and John.
**42** Calling them together, Jesus said to them, "You know that those recognized as rulers over the nations dominate them, and their great ones wield power over them.
**43** It is not to be like that among you. Instead, whoever wishes to become great among you must be your servant,
**44** and whoever wishes to be first among you must be slave of all.
**45** Indeed, even the Son of Man arrived not to be waited upon, but to serve others and offer his life as a ransom for many."
**46** They came into Jericho. As he was departing from Jericho with his disciples and a sizable crowd, Bartimaeus son of Timaeus, a blind beggar, was sitting beside the road.
**47** Hearing that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began shouting out, "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!"
**48** Many people scolded him to make him keep quiet, yet he shouted all the louder, "Son of David, have mercy on me!"
**49** Jesus stopped still and said, "Call him over." So they called the blind man, telling him, "Cheer up! Get on your feet; he is calling for you!"
**50** Throwing off his outer cloak, he sprang up and came to Jesus.
**51** Jesus replied to him, "What do you wish for me to do on your behalf?" The blind man answered him, "Rabbouni, I want to see again!"
**52** Jesus told him, "Go your way; your faith has healed you." Instantly he received his sight and began following Jesus along the road.

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus left that area and traveled into the territory of Judea and across the Jordan River. Crowds packed around him once again, and as usual he taught them.
**2** Some people came up to trap him with a question: "Does the law allow a man to divorce his wife?"
**3** Jesus turned it back on them: "What rule did Moses give you?"
**4** They answered, "Moses allowed a husband to write out divorce papers and send his wife away."
**5** Jesus told them, "Moses wrote that rule only because your hearts are so stubborn.
**6** Right from the start of creation, though, 'God made them male and female.'
**7** 'That's why a man leaves his father and mother and joins himself to his wife,
**8** and the two become one flesh.' So they're no longer two separate people—they're one flesh.
**9** Therefore, nobody has any right to split apart what God has bound together."
**10** Once they were back inside the house, the disciples asked Jesus more about this subject.
**11** He told them, "Any man who divorces his wife and marries somebody else commits adultery against her.
**12** And any woman who divorces her husband and marries somebody else also commits adultery."
**13** Parents were bringing little kids to Jesus so he could lay his hands on them, and the disciples kept shooing them away.
**14** When Jesus saw what they were doing, he got upset and told them, "Let the kids come to me! Don't block them, because God's kingdom belongs to people just like these children.
**15** Listen carefully: anyone who doesn't welcome God's kingdom the way a little child does will never step foot inside it."
**16** Then he scooped the children up in his arms, placed his hands on them, and blessed them warmly.
**17** As Jesus was heading back out onto the road, a man sprinted up, dropped to his knees in front of him, and asked, "Good Teacher, what do I need to do to receive eternal life?"
**18** Jesus answered, "Why are you calling me good? Nobody is good except God alone.
**19** You already know the commandments: don't murder, don't commit adultery, don't steal, don't lie in court, don't cheat anyone, and honor your father and mother."
**20** The man replied, "Teacher, I've kept every one of those since I was a kid."
**21** Jesus looked straight at him, felt deep love for him, and said, "There's just one thing missing: go sell everything you own, give the money to the poor, and you'll have real treasure in heaven. Then come follow me."
**22** At those words the man's face fell, and he walked away heartbroken, because he owned huge amounts of property.
**23** Jesus looked around at his followers and said, "How tough it is for wealthy people to walk into God's kingdom!"
**24** His followers were shocked to hear him say that. Jesus repeated it even more strongly: "My children, entering God's kingdom is extraordinarily hard!
**25** In fact, it's easier for a camel to squeeze through a sewing needle's eye than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom."
**26** The disciples were completely floored and whispered to each other, "Then how can anybody ever be saved?"
**27** Jesus looked right into their eyes and answered, "For humans it's impossible, though not for God—with God, anything is possible."
**28** Peter spoke up: "Look, we've walked away from everything to follow you!"
**29** Jesus assured them, "Believe me, nobody who has given up home, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children, or farmland for me and for the good news
**30** will miss out on receiving a hundred times more right here in this lifetime—homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and farmland, right alongside persecution—and eternal life in the world ahead.
**31** Still, lots of people who seem to be in first place now will end up last, and those in last place will end up first."
**32** They were climbing the road up toward Jerusalem with Jesus walking out front. The disciples were amazed, and the rest of the crowd following behind felt scared. Jesus pulled the Twelve aside again and started telling them what was going to happen to him:
**33** "Listen: we're heading up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man is going to be handed over to the chief priests and legal experts. They'll sentence him to death and hand him over to non-Jews,
**34** who will ridicule him, spit in his face, whip him, and execute him. Three days later, he'll come back to life."
**35** Just then Zebedee's sons, James and John, walked up to Jesus and said, "Teacher, we want you to promise you'll do whatever we ask."
**36** "What would you like me to do for you?" he asked.
**37** They said, "When you reign in glory, let one of us sit right beside you on your right and the other on your left."
**38** Jesus told them, "You have no idea what you're asking for! Can you drink the bitter cup I'm about to drink, or go through the overwhelming trial I'm about to face?"
**39** "Yes, we can," they answered. Jesus replied, "You will drink the cup I drink and go through the same baptism of suffering I face;
**40** yet it isn't up to me to hand out the seats on my right or left—those places belong to the people God has prepared them for."
**41** When the other ten disciples found out what James and John had asked, they got furious with them.
**42** So Jesus called them all together and said, "You know how the rulers of the nations throw their weight around and how top officials boss people around.
**43** Things can't work that way among you! Instead, whoever wants to become great among you has to become your servant,
**44** and whoever wants to be number one among you must become everyone's slave.
**45** Even the Son of Man didn't come to be waited on—he came to serve others and to give up his own life as the ransom price to free many."

**47** When he heard it was Jesus of Nazareth passing by, he started shouting, "Jesus, Son of David, have pity on me!"
**48** Plenty of people snapped at him to shut up, yet he only yelled louder: "Son of David, have pity on me!"

**50** Tossing his coat aside, Bartimaeus jumped to his feet and hurried over to Jesus.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 5 `σκληροκαρδίαν` (*sklērokardian*):** Literally "hardness of heart," explaining Mosaic divorce certification (Deut 24:1–4) as a concession restraining abuse rather than God's creation ideal.
- **v. 9 `συνέζευξεν` (*synezeuxen*):** From *syzeugnymi*, "yoked together" like a pair of oxen under a shared yoke—portraying marriage as an inseparable covenant partnership.
- **v. 19 `μὴ ἀποστερήσῃς` (*mē aposterēsēs*):** "Do not defraud / withhold what is owed" (echoing Exod 21:10 and Deut 24:14), placed alongside the Decalogue commands.
- **v. 45 `λύτρον ἀντὶ πολλῶν` (*lytron anti pollōn*):** *Lytron* denotes the ransom price paid to liberate a slave or prisoner of war, while *anti* means "in exchange for / in the place of," echoing the Suffering Servant of Isaiah 53.
- **v. 46 `Βαρτιμαῖος` (*Bartimaios*):** Aramaic patronymic *Bar-Timaī* ("son of Timaeus," which Mark translates as `ὁ υἱὸς Τιμαίου`); some witnesses shift the participle *prosaitēs* ("beggar") to a verb (*prosaitōn*, "begging").
- **v. 51 `Ραββουνι` (*Rabbouni*):** Aramaic honorific meaning "my Master" or "my great Teacher," retained untranslated by Mark for solemnity.
