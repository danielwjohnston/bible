## Clear & Faithful

**1** As Jesus was walking out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, "Teacher, look at these massive stones and magnificent buildings!"
**2** Jesus replied to him, "Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left standing upon another here that will not be torn down."
**3** Later, while he was sitting on the Mount of Olives directly opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,
**4** "Tell us, when will these things happen, and what will be the sign when all of them are about to be brought to completion?"
**5** Jesus began by telling them, "Watch out that no one leads you astray.
**6** Many will arrive using my name, claiming, 'I am he,' and they will mislead many.
**7** Whenever you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be terrified; these things must happen, yet the end is not yet.
**8** Nation will rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there will be earthquakes in various regions and famines—these are merely the first labor pains.
**9** Be on guard for yourselves: they will hand you over to local councils, you will be flogged in synagogues, and you will stand before governors and kings on account of me as a witness to them.
**10** And the good news must first be proclaimed to all the nations.
**11** Whenever they arrest you and hand you over, do not worry beforehand about what to say; instead, speak whatever is given to you in that very hour, for it is not you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.
**12** Brother will betray brother to death, and father his child; children will rebel against parents and have them put to death.
**13** You will be hated by everyone because of my name; yet whoever endures to the end will be saved.
**14** When you see the desolating sacrilege standing where he must not stand—let the reader understand—then let those in Judea flee to the mountains;
**15** let anyone on the housetop not climb down or go inside to salvage anything from their home,
**16** and let anyone out in the field not turn back to pick up their cloak.
**17** How terrible it will be for pregnant women and nursing mothers in those days!
**18** Pray that this may not happen during winter,
**19** for those days will bring distress such as has never occurred from the beginning of God's creation until now, and will never occur again.
**20** Indeed, unless the Lord had shortened those days, no living creature would survive; yet for the sake of the chosen ones whom he selected, he has shortened the days.
**21** At that time, if anyone tells you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'Look, over there!'—do not believe it.
**22** For false messiahs and false prophets will arise and perform signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, even the chosen ones.
**23** So stay alert! I have told you everything in advance.
**24** But in those days, following that distress, the sun will be darkened and the moon will withhold its light;
**25** the stars will fall from the sky, and the heavenly powers will be shaken.
**26** Then people will see the Son of Man arriving in the clouds with great power and glory.
**27** And he will dispatch his angels and gather his chosen ones from the four winds, from the farthest reaches of earth to the farthest reaches of heaven.
**28** Learn this lesson from the fig tree: as soon as its branch turns tender and puts out leaves, you recognize that summer is near.
**29** So also you, whenever you see these events unfolding, know that he is right at the doors.
**30** Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away before all these things take place.
**31** Both heaven and earth will pass away, yet my words will never fade away.
**32** Regarding that day or hour, however, no one knows—neither the angels in heaven nor the Son, only the Father.
**33** Stay alert and keep awake, because you do not know when the appointed time will arrive.
**34** It is like a man going abroad who leaves his household, delegates authority to his servants—each with an assigned task—and orders the gatekeeper to stay on watch.
**35** Keep watch therefore, since you do not know when the master of the house will return—whether at evening, midnight, rooster-crow, or dawn—
**36** so that when he arrives unexpectedly he does not find you asleep.
**37** And what I say to you, I say to everyone: stay awake!"

## Everyday English

**1** As Jesus was walking out of the temple complex, one of his followers remarked, "Teacher, look at the size of these stones and how incredible these buildings are!"
**2** Jesus answered him, "Take a good look at these huge buildings—not a single stone here will remain stacked on another. Every last one will be knocked down."
**3** Later, while Jesus was sitting on the Mount of Olives looking straight across at the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,
**4** "Tell us, when is all of this going to happen? What warning sign will show us when it's all about to wrap up?"
**5** Jesus began explaining to them: "Be careful that nobody tricks you!
**6** Lots of impostors will show up using my name, claiming, 'I'm the one!' and fooling plenty of people.
**7** When you hear news about wars breaking out and threats of war, don't panic. Those things have to happen first, though the final end isn't here yet.
**8** Country will attack country, and kingdom will clash with kingdom. Earthquakes will shake different regions, and food shortages will strike—yet these are only the opening contractions of labor pains.
**9** Watch out for yourselves! People will drag you before local courts, beat you in synagogues, and haul you in front of governors and kings because you belong to me, giving you the chance to testify to them.
**10** And the good news has to be announced to every nation first.
**11** When they arrest you and put you on trial, don't stress ahead of time about what you'll say. Just speak whatever God gives you in that moment, because it won't really be you talking—it'll be the Holy Spirit.
**12** Brothers will hand their own brothers over to be executed, fathers will turn in their children, and kids will rebel against their parents and have them killed.
**13** Everybody will hate you because you bear my name, yet anyone who holds out to the end will be rescued.
**14** When you spot the awful desecration standing where he has no business being—pay close attention as you read this!—everyone in Judea needs to run straight for the hills.
**15** Anyone relaxing on a flat rooftop shouldn't climb down or step inside to grab anything from the house.
**16** Anyone working out in the fields shouldn't head back home to pick up a jacket.
**17** How awful those days will be for women who are pregnant or nursing babies!
**18** Pray that none of this hits during winter,
**19** because those days will bring worse suffering than anything the world has ever seen since God created it until right now—and nothing like it will ever happen again.
**20** In fact, if the Lord hadn't cut that timeframe short, nobody would survive; yet for the sake of the chosen people he picked, he shortened those days.
**21** If someone tells you back then, 'Hey, the Messiah is right here!' or 'Look, he's over there!' don't buy it.
**22** Fake messiahs and phony prophets will pop up performing impressive signs and miracles to fool even God's chosen people, if they possibly could.
**23** So keep your eyes open! I've warned you about every bit of this ahead of time.
**24** Then, in those days right after that terrible suffering, the sun will go black and the moon will stop shining.
**25** Stars will drop out of the sky, and the powers holding the heavens together will shake violently.
**26** Then everyone will see the Son of Man coming in the clouds with tremendous power and glory.
**27** He'll send his angels out and gather his chosen people from every corner of the compass, from the edge of the earth to the edge of the sky.
**28** Take a hint from the fig tree: as soon as its twigs turn soft and green leaves sprout, you know summer is right around the corner.
**29** In the same way, when you see all these events happening, you'll know he's standing right outside the door!
**30** I promise you, this generation won't disappear before every single one of these things takes place.
**31** The sky and the earth will disappear someday, yet my words will never fade away.
**32** Nobody knows the exact day or hour when that will happen—not the angels in heaven, not even the Son, only the Father knows.
**33** Stay on your toes and keep awake, because you don't know when the moment will arrive.
**34** It's like a homeowner going away on a trip: he leaves his house behind, puts his servants in charge with specific jobs for each one, and tells the gatekeeper to stay wide awake.
**35** So stay wide awake! You don't know when the master of the house is walking back in—whether at dusk, midnight, before dawn, or sunrise.
**36** If he shows up suddenly, don't let him catch you fast asleep!
**37** What I'm telling you right now, I'm saying to everyone: stay alert!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 8 `ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων` (*archē ōdinōn*):** Literally "beginning of birth-pangs," Jewish apocalyptic imagery for the intense upheavals preceding messianic deliverance rather than the final end (*telos*) itself.
- **v. 14 `τὸ βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως ἑστηκότα` (*to bdelygma tēs erēmōseōs hestēkota*):** Mark pairs the neuter noun *bdelygma* ("detestable/desolating sacrilege") with the masculine participle *hestēkota* ("standing"), hinting at a personal agent/ruler; Byzantine manuscripts (`RP`) harmonize with Matt 24:15 by inserting *to rhēthen hypo Daniēl tou prophētou* ("spoken by Daniel the prophet") and shifting to neuter *hestōs*.
- **v. 20 `ἐκολόβωσεν` (*ekolobōsen*):** From *koloboō* ("cut short / amputate / curtail"), emphasizing sovereign divine restraint over the crisis duration.
- **v. 32 `οὐδὲ ὁ υἱός, εἰ μὴ ὁ πατήρ` (*oude ho huios, ei mē ho patēr*):** A striking christological statement affirming the Son's genuine human limitation regarding the timing of the consummation.
- **v. 35 `ὀψὲ … μεσονύκτιον … ἀλεκτοροφωνίας … πρωΐ` (*opse … mesonyktion … alektorophōnias … prōi*):** The four Roman three-hour night watches (6–9 p.m., 9 p.m.–midnight, midnight–3 a.m. "rooster-crow," and 3–6 a.m.).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Daniel 9:27, 11:31 & 12:11** (echoed in Mark 13:14): Speaks of "the detestable sacrilege bringing ruin" set up in the holy place.
- **Daniel 12:1 & Joel 2:2** (echoed in Mark 13:19): *"There shall be a time of distress such as has never occurred since nations first existed until that time."*
- **Isaiah 13:10 & 34:4** (echoed in Mark 13:24–25): *"The sun will be dark at its rising, and the moon will not shed its light... and all the host of heaven shall rot away."*
- **Daniel 7:13–14 & Zechariah 2:6** (echoed in Mark 13:26–27): The Son of Man arrives with the clouds of heaven, and God gathers his dispersed people from the four winds of the earth.
