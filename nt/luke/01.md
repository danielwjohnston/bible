## Clear & Faithful

**1** Inasmuch as many have undertaken to compile an orderly narrative about the events that have been brought to fulfillment among us,
**2** just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and ministers of the word handed them down to us,
**3** it seemed fitting to me as well, having investigated everything carefully from the start, to write it out for you in orderly sequence, most excellent Theophilus,
**4** so that you may know with certainty the reliability of the teachings in which you have been instructed.
**5** During the reign of Herod, king of Judea, there lived a priest named Zechariah from the priestly order of Abijah; his wife was descended from Aaron's daughters, and she was named Elizabeth.
**6** Both of them were upright before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and statutes of the Lord.
**7** They had no children, since Elizabeth was unable to conceive, and both of them were advanced in age.
**8** Now it happened that while he was serving as priest before God in the appointed turn of his division,
**9** according to the custom of the priesthood, he was chosen by lot to enter the sanctuary of the Lord and burn incense.
**10** Meanwhile, the entire assembly of people stood praying outside during the hour of the incense offering.
**11** Then an angel of the Lord appeared before him, standing on the right side of the altar where incense is burned.
**12** When Zechariah saw him, he was startled, and fear fell upon him.
**13** The angel told him, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your earnest prayer has been answered. Your wife Elizabeth will give birth to a son for you, and you are to name him John.
**14** He will bring you gladness and delight, and great numbers will celebrate when he is born,
**15** for he will be great before the Lord. He must never touch wine or fermented drink, and even while still in his mother's womb he will be filled with the Holy Spirit.
**16** Many of Israel's descendants he will turn back toward the Lord their God,
**17** and he himself will go ahead in his presence with Elijah's spirit and power, turning fathers' hearts toward their children and the rebellious to the insight of the upright, so as to prepare a ready people for the Lord."
**18** Zechariah asked the angel, "By what sign can I know this? I am already elderly, and my wife has reached advanced age."
**19** In reply the angel told him, "I am Gabriel, who stands in God's presence; I have been sent to speak with you and to announce these good tidings to you.
**20** Look—you will remain silent and unable to utter a word until the day these events occur, because you did not trust my message, which will come to fulfillment at its appointed time."
**21** Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah and wondering why he was lingering in the sanctuary.
**22** When he came out, he could not speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary; he kept gesturing to them and remained unable to speak.
**23** When the days of his priestly service were completed, he returned to his own home.
**24** After those days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she kept herself in seclusion for five months, saying,
**25** "Thus the Lord has acted for me in the days when he looked upon me to take away my reproach among the people."
**26** During the sixth month, God sent the angel Gabriel to a Galilean town called Nazareth,
**27** to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary.
**28** Having come to her, he said, "Greetings, favored one! The Lord is with you."
**29** She was deeply troubled by this greeting and kept pondering what kind of salutation this might be.
**30** The angel told her, "Have no fear, Mary, since you have gained favor in God's sight.
**31** Listen closely: you will become pregnant and bear a son, and you are to call his name Jesus.
**32** He will attain greatness and be known as the Son of the Most High; the Lord God will grant him the royal throne of his ancestor David,
**33** he will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and his kingdom will have no end."
**34** Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I do not know a man?"
**35** In response the angel told her, "The Holy Spirit will descend upon you, and the Most High's power will cast its shadow over you; for this reason the holy offspring to be born will be called Son of God.
**36** Furthermore, your kinswoman Elizabeth has herself conceived a son in her old age, and she who was known as barren is now in her sixth month,
**37** because no promise from God will ever fail of power."
**38** Mary responded, "Here I am, the Lord's servant; let it take place for me just as you have spoken." Then the angel left her.
**39** In those days Mary arose and traveled with haste into the hill country, to a town of Judah,
**40** where she stepped inside Zechariah's home and offered her greeting to Elizabeth.
**41** No sooner did Elizabeth hear Mary's greeting than the unborn baby leaped inside her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.
**42** Raising her voice in a mighty cry, she declared, "Most blessed are you among all women, and blessed is the offspring of your womb!
**43** And why is this granted to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?
**44** Indeed, the very moment the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the unborn child leaped with gladness inside my womb.
**45** Blessed is she who believed that there will be a fulfillment of what was spoken to her from the Lord."
**46** Mary then proclaimed: "My whole soul exalts the Lord,
**47** and my spirit has rejoiced exceedingly in God my deliverer,
**48** since he has looked graciously upon his servant's humble condition. Truly, from this moment on every generation will count me blessed,
**49** because the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is his name.
**50** His mercy extends from generation to generation toward those who fear him.
**51** He has acted mightily with his arm; he has dispersed the arrogant in the schemes of their hearts.
**52** He has brought down rulers from their thrones and exalted those of humble degree.
**53** Those who hungered he has satisfied with good gifts, while the wealthy he has dismissed empty-handed.
**54** He has come to the aid of his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy,
**55** just as he spoke to our ancestors, to Abraham and to his descendants forever."
**56** Mary remained with Elizabeth for roughly three months before returning home.
**57** Now the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, and she bore a son.
**58** When her neighbors and kin heard how abundantly the Lord had shown his mercy to her, they shared in her joy.
**59** On the eighth day, people arrived to circumcise the infant, and they intended to name him Zechariah after his father.
**60** But his mother answered, "No, he shall be called John."
**61** They protested to her, "Not a single person in your family line goes by that name."
**62** So they motioned to his father to find out what he wanted him to be named.
**63** Asking for a writing tablet, he wrote, "His name is John." And they were all amazed.
**64** Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue loosed, and he began speaking, blessing God.
**65** Awe came upon all their neighbors, and throughout the whole hill country of Judea all these events were widely discussed.
**66** Everyone who heard about it treasured these matters in their hearts, asking, "What will this child turn out to be?" For clearly the Lord's hand was upon him.
**67** His father Zechariah, filled by the Holy Spirit, uttered this prophecy, declaring:
**68** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, because he has visited and brought redemption to his people,
**69** He has raised up for us a mighty horn of deliverance within the household of his servant David,
**70** just as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets from of old—
**71** rescue from our foes and from the grip of everyone who despises us—
**72** to deal mercifully with our ancestors and recall his sacred covenant,
**73** the oath that he swore to our father Abraham, to grant us
**74** that we, being rescued from the hand of enemies, might serve him without fear
**75** in devotion and uprightness in his sight throughout our entire lives.
**76** And you, young child, will be titled prophet of the Most High; for you will march ahead in the Lord's presence to ready his paths,
**77** imparting to his people the knowledge of salvation through the pardon of their sins,
**78** owing to the heartfelt compassion of our God, through which the rising dawn from heaven's height will break upon us,
**79** to shed light on those dwelling in gloom and death's shadow, directing our steps along the path of peace."
**80** And the child grew and became strong in spirit, and he lived in the wilderness until the day of his public appearance to Israel.

## Everyday English

**1** Many people have already set out to write an organized history of the things that have taken place right here among us,
**2** passing along what the original eyewitnesses and messengers of the word shared with us.
**3** Since I've carefully traced everything from the very beginning, I thought it would be good to write out an accurate, step-by-step account for you too, honored Theophilus,
**4** so you can be completely sure about the truth of everything you were taught.
**5** Back when Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah from Abijah's priestly group. His wife Elizabeth was also from Aaron's priestly family.
**6** Both of them lived upright lives in God's sight, faithfully following all of the Lord's commands and laws without fault.
**7** Still, they had no children because Elizabeth couldn't conceive, and by now both of them were quite old.
**8** One day, while Zechariah's priestly group was on duty and he was serving before God,
**9** he was picked by lot—just as the priestly custom required—to go inside the Lord's temple and burn incense.
**10** Outside, a large crowd of people stood praying during the incense offering.
**11** Suddenly, an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing to the right of the incense altar.
**12** Zechariah was shocked at the sight and terrified.
**13** "Don't be afraid, Zechariah!" the angel told him. "God has heard your prayer. Your wife Elizabeth is going to give you a son, and you'll name him John.
**14** He'll bring you deep joy and celebration, and plenty of people will be thrilled when he's born,
**15** because he'll be great in the Lord's eyes. He must never touch wine or any strong drink, and he'll be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he leaves his mother's womb.
**16** He'll bring many Israelites back to the Lord their God,
**17** leading the way before him with Elijah's spirit and strength—reconciling fathers with their children and turning stubborn people toward godly wisdom, so a people is ready to welcome the Lord."
**18** "How can I know this will really happen?" Zechariah asked the angel. "I'm already an old man, and my wife is past childbearing age."
**19** The angel replied, "I am Gabriel, and I stand right in God's presence. He sent me to speak with you and bring you this wonderful news.
**20** Because you didn't believe what I told you—even though my words will come true right on schedule—you're going to lose your voice and stay silent until the day all this happens."
**21** Outside, the crowd was waiting for Zechariah and wondering why he was taking so long inside the temple.
**22** When he finally came out, he couldn't say a word to them. They realized he'd seen a vision inside, because he could only wave his hands to communicate and stayed completely speechless.
**23** Once his week of temple duty ended, he went back home.
**24** Soon afterward, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant and stayed out of public view for five months.
**25** "The Lord has stepped in for me," she said, "and finally removed the shame I carried in front of everyone."
**26** In the sixth month of her pregnancy, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a town in Galilee,
**27** to visit a young virgin named Mary who was engaged to Joseph, a descendant of King David.
**28** The angel came to her and said, "Hello, deeply favored woman! The Lord is with you."
**29** Mary was shaken by his greeting and tried to figure out what on earth it meant.
**30** "Don't be scared, Mary," the angel told her. "God has chosen to bless you.
**31** Listen—you're going to become pregnant and have a son, and you'll name him Jesus.
**32** He'll become truly great and carry the title Son of the Most High. The Lord God is going to give him King David's royal throne,
**33** and he'll rule over Jacob's family forever—his kingdom won't ever end."
**34** "How could that happen?" Mary asked the angel. "I'm still a virgin."
**35** The angel answered, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the Most High's power will wrap around you like a cloud. That's why this holy child will be called God's Son.
**36** Even your relative Elizabeth is pregnant with a son in her old age—people called her barren, yet she's already six months along!
**37** Nothing God promises is ever impossible for him."
**38** "I belong to the Lord as his servant," Mary said. "May everything happen to me just as you said." Then the angel left her.
**39** Right away, Mary packed up and hurried into the Judean hills to the town where Zechariah lived.
**40** She walked into his house and called out a greeting to Elizabeth.
**41** The instant Mary's voice reached Elizabeth's ears, the baby jumped inside her womb, and Elizabeth became filled with the Holy Spirit.
**42** She shouted out joyfully, "God has blessed you above all other women, and blessed is the child you're carrying!
**43** How did I ever deserve to have the mother of my Lord visit me?
**44** The instant your greeting reached my ears, my baby leaped for joy inside me!
**45** God blesses you for trusting that what the Lord promised you will actually happen."
**46** Then Mary sang out: "My whole soul praises the Lord's greatness,
**47** and my spirit celebrates God, my rescuer!
**48** He noticed his humble servant girl, and from now on, every generation will call me blessed,
**49** because the Mighty One has done extraordinary things for me—holy is his name!
**50** His mercy flows down through every generation to those who honor him.
**51** He has flexed his strong arm and scattered proud people along with their arrogant schemes.
**52** He has pulled rulers off their thrones and lifted up the overlooked.
**53** He has given hungry people plenty of good food and sent the wealthy away with nothing.
**54** He has stepped in to help his servant Israel, remembering his promise of mercy—

**57** When Elizabeth's due date arrived, she gave birth to a baby boy.
**58** Her neighbors and family heard how wonderfully kind the Lord had been to her, and they celebrated right along with her.

**62** So they used hand signs to ask the baby's father what he wanted to name him.

**64** Right then and there, Zechariah's mouth opened, his tongue worked again, and he began praising God aloud.
**65** Neighbors all around were filled with awe, and word about everything that happened spread across the whole Judean hill country.

**68** "Praise the Lord, the God of Israel! He has stepped in to rescue and free his people.
**69** He has raised up a mighty deliverer for us from the royal family of his servant David,
**70** just as he promised long ago through his holy prophets—
**71** salvation from our enemies and from everyone who hates us.
**72** He has kept faith with our ancestors and remembered his sacred covenant,
**73** the solemn oath he swore to our ancestor Abraham:
**74** to rescue us from our enemies' grip so we can worship him without fear,
**75** living pure and upright lives before him every single day.
**76** And as for you, little boy, you'll be called a prophet of the Most High, because you'll march ahead of the Lord to clear his path,

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `διήγησιν` (*diēgēsin*):** "Orderly account" or "narrative," paired in v. 3 with `καθεξῆς` (*kathexēs*, "in sequence/order"), signaling Luke's historiographical care.
- **v. 4 `ἀσφάλειαν` (*asphaleian*):** "Certainty," "firmness," or "reliability" (literally "un-slipping security"), explaining Luke's goal for Theophilus.
- **v. 14 `γενέσει` (*genesei*):** "Birth" or "origin" (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`), whereas `RP` reads `γεννήσει` (*gennēsei*, "begetting/birth").
- **v. 28 `κεχαριτωμένη` (*kecharitōmenē*):** Perfect passive participle of *charitoō* ("endowed with grace," "highly favored"). Later witnesses (`Treg`, `RP`) expand the verse with `ὁ ἄγγελος` ("the angel") and `εὐλογημένη σὺ ἐν γυναιξίν` ("blessed are you among women," harmonized from v. 42), absent from `WH` and `NA28`.
- **v. 35 `ἐπισκιάσει` (*episkiasei*):** "Will overshadow," recalling the glory-cloud (*shekinah* / LXX *episkiazō*) resting over the tabernacle in Exodus 40:35.
- **v. 38 `δούλη` (*doulē*):** "Female slave" or "bondservant" (cf. v. 48), expressing Mary's total availability to God's purpose.
- **v. 63 `πινακίδιον` (*pinakidion*):** Diminutive of *pinax*, a small wax-coated wooden writing tablet used with a stylus.
- **v. 78 `σπλάγχνα ἐλέους` (*splanchna eleous*):** Literally "inward parts/viscera of mercy," a Hebraic-Greek idiom (*rachamim*) for deep, visceral compassion, paired with `ἀνατολὴ ἐξ ὕψους` (*anatolē ex hypsous*, "rising sun/dawn" or "branch/shoot from on high," echoing LXX *anatolē* for Hebrew *tsemach*).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Malachi 3:1; 4:5–6 (3:22–23 LXX)** (echoed in vv. 17, 76): The messenger sent ahead to prepare the Lord's way in the spirit and power of Elijah, turning the hearts of parents toward their children.
- **1 Samuel 2:1–10 & Psalm 103:17; 111:9** (echoed in vv. 46–55): Hannah's song of reversal—exalting the lowly, filling the hungry, and humbling the mighty—shapes Mary's Magnificat alongside covenant remembrance of Abraham (Gen 12:3; 17:7).
- **Isaiah 9:2; 60:1–2; Zechariah 3:8; 6:12** (echoed in vv. 78–79): The *anatolē* (dawn / rising branch) brings light to those seated in darkness and death's shadow.

**1** Since many have undertaken to set in order a narrative concerning those matters which have been fulfilled among us,
**2** even as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word delivered them to us,
**3** it seemed good to me also, having traced the course of all things accurately from the first, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus;
**4** that you might know the certainty concerning the things in which you were instructed.
**5** There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the priestly division of Abijah. He had a wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.
**6** They were both righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord.
**7** But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they both were well advanced in years.
**8** Now while he executed the priest’s office before God in the order of his division
**9** according to the custom of the priest’s office, his lot was to enter into the temple of the Lord and burn incense.
**10** The whole multitude of the people were praying outside at the hour of incense.
**11** An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.
**12** Zacharias was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him.
**13** But the angel said to him, “Don’t be afraid, Zacharias, because your request has been heard. Your wife, Elizabeth, will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John.
**14** You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth.
**15** For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he will drink no wine nor strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit, even from his mother’s womb.
**16** He will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God.
**17** He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, ‘to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,’ and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to prepare a people prepared for the Lord.”
**18** Zacharias said to the angel, “How can I be sure of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is well advanced in years.”
**19** The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God. I was sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.
**20** Behold, you will be silent and not able to speak until the day that these things will happen, because you didn’t believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their proper time.”
**21** The people were waiting for Zacharias, and they marveled that he delayed in the temple.
**22** When he came out, he could not speak to them. They perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple. He continued making signs to them, and remained mute.
**23** When the days of his service were fulfilled, he departed to his house.
**24** After these days Elizabeth his wife conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying,
**25** “Thus has the Lord done to me in the days in which he looked at me, to take away my reproach among men.”
**26** Now in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,
**27** to a virgin pledged to be married to a man whose name was Joseph, of David’s house. The virgin’s name was Mary.
**28** Having come in, the angel said to her, “Rejoice, you highly favored one! The Lord is with you. Blessed are you among women!”
**29** But when she saw him, she was greatly troubled at the saying, and considered what kind of salutation this might be.
**30** The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

**32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David,
**33** and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. There will be no end to his Kingdom.”
**34** Mary said to the angel, “How can this be, seeing I am a virgin?”
**35** The angel answered her, “The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore also the holy one who is born from you will be called the Son of God.
**36** Behold, Elizabeth your relative also has conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month with her who was called barren.
**37** For nothing spoken by God is impossible.”
**38** Mary said, “Behold, the servant of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word.” Then the angel departed from her.
**39** Mary arose in those days and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah,
**40** and entered into the house of Zacharias and greeted Elizabeth.
**41** When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.
**42** She called out with a loud voice and said, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!
**43** Why am I so favored, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?
**44** For behold, when the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy!
**45** Blessed is she who believed, for there will be a fulfillment of the things which have been spoken to her from the Lord!”
