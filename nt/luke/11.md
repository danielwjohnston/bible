## Clear & Faithful

**1** One day Jesus was praying in a quiet place, and once he finished, one of his disciples requested, "Lord, teach us how to pray, just as John instructed his disciples."
**2** He said to them, "When you pray, say: 'Father, hallowed be your name; your kingdom come.
**3** Provide us each day with the bread we need for today,
**4** and forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone indebted to us; and do not lead us into temptation.'"
**5** Then he said to them, "Suppose one of you has a friend, and goes to him at midnight and says to him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves,
**6** since a friend of mine has just arrived from travel, and I lack anything to offer him';
**7** and that man from inside answers, 'Do not bother me; the door has already been locked, and my children are with me in bed; I cannot get up and give you anything.'
**8** I tell you, even though he will not get up and give him anything because he is his friend, yet because of his shameless persistence he will get up and give him whatever he needs.
**9** So I say to you: ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.
**10** Indeed, everyone who keeps asking receives, the seeker discovers, and to the person knocking, the door will be opened.
**11** Which father among you, if his son asks for a fish, will give him a snake instead of a fish?
**12** Or if he asks for an egg, will give him a scorpion?
**13** So if you, flawed as you are, know how to give good presents to your children, how much more readily will the Father from heaven grant the Holy Spirit to those who ask him!"
**14** On another occasion he was driving out a mute demon; once the demon departed, the mute man spoke, leaving the crowds astonished.
**15** Yet some among them claimed, "It is through Beelzebul, chief of the demons, that he expels demons."
**16** Still others, trying to test him, kept demanding a miraculous sign straight from heaven.
**17** Yet knowing their thoughts, he said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and a divided household falls upon another.
**18** Now if Satan too stands divided against himself, how can his kingdom endure? Yet you claim I expel demons through Beelzebul.
**19** If I expel demons through Beelzebul, through whom do your own followers expel them? For this reason they themselves will stand as your judges.
**20** But if it is by the finger of God that I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.
**21** When a strong man, fully armed, guards his own courtyard, his possessions are at peace;
**22** yet when someone stronger than he attacks and conquers him, he takes away the full armor in which he trusted and divides up his plunder.
**23** Anyone who does not stand with me works against me, and whoever does not gather alongside me scatters.
**24** When an unclean spirit goes out of a person, it wanders through waterless places seeking rest; and finding none, it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came.'
**25** When it comes, it finds it swept and put in order.
**26** Then it goes off and recruits seven other spirits more wicked than itself; entering in, they settle down there, leaving that person's final condition far worse than the first."
**27** While he was speaking these words, a woman out in the crowd lifted up her voice and called to him, "Blessed is the womb that carried you and the breasts that fed you!"
**28** He countered, "Rather, blessed are those who listen to God's word and guard it!"
**29** As the crowds were increasing, he began to say, "This generation is an evil generation; it seeks a sign, yet no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah.
**30** Just as Jonah served as a sign to the people of Nineveh, so too will the Son of Man serve as a sign to this generation.
**31** At the judgment, the Queen of the South will rise alongside the people of this generation and condemn them, because she journeyed from the farthest edges of the earth to listen to Solomon's wisdom—and look, something greater than Solomon stands here!
**32** The citizens of Nineveh will rise at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they turned back in repentance at Jonah's proclamation—and look, something greater than Jonah stands here!
**33** Nobody lights an oil lamp and tucks it away in a hidden cellar or beneath a grain basket; instead, one sets it on a lampstand so that everyone entering may see the radiance.
**34** The eye is the lamp of your body. When your eye is healthy, your whole body is full of light; yet when it is bad, your body is in darkness.
**35** See to it, therefore, that the light within you is not darkness.
**36** Therefore, if your entire body is luminous with no shadowy corner remaining, every part of it will shine brightly, just as when a lamp bathes you in its bright beam."
**37** While he was speaking, a Pharisee invited him to dine with him; so he went in and reclined at the table.
**38** When the Pharisee saw this, he was astonished that he did not first wash before the meal.
**39** Then the Lord answered him, "Right now you Pharisees scrub the exterior of the cup and the dish, while your interior overflows with greed and malice.
**40** Foolish people! Did not the one who made the outside make the inside as well?
**41** Instead, give what lies within as charity to the poor, and look—everything becomes pure for you.
**42** Yet woe to you Pharisees! You carefully tithe mint, rue, and every garden herb, while passing right over justice and God's love. Those greater matters you ought to have practiced, without neglecting these smaller ones.
**43** Woe to you Pharisees! You crave the seats of honor inside the synagogues and respectful bows in the public squares.
**44** Woe to you! For you are like unmarked graves, and people walk over them without realizing it."
**45** One of the legal scholars answered him, "Teacher, by saying these things you insult us too!"
**46** He said, "Woe to you legal scholars as well! For you load people with burdens hard to carry, yet you yourselves do not touch those burdens with a single finger.
**47** Woe to you! You construct monuments for the prophets, while it was your own ancestors who murdered them.
**48** So you are witnesses and approve of the deeds of your ancestors, because they killed them, and you build their tombs.
**49** That is why the wisdom of God declared, 'I will dispatch prophets and apostles among them, and some of them they will murder and hunt down,'
**50** so that accountability for the blood of every prophet poured out since the world's creation may fall upon this generation—
**51** stretching from Abel's blood to the blood of Zechariah, who died between the altar and the temple house. Yes, I tell you, it will be demanded from this generation!
**52** Woe to you legal scholars! For you have taken away the key of knowledge; you did not enter yourselves, and you hindered those who were entering."
**53** When he went out from there, the scribes and the Pharisees began to press him fiercely and to cross-examine him on many subjects,
**54** lying in wait to catch him in something from his own mouth.

## Everyday English

**1** One day Jesus was praying in a certain spot. When he finished, one of his disciples asked him, "Lord, teach us how to pray, just like John taught his disciples."
**2** Jesus told them, "Whenever you pray, say: 'Father, may your name be honored as holy; may your kingdom come.
**3** Give us the food we need for each day.
**4** Forgive our sins, just as we forgive everyone who owes us anything; and don't lead us into severe testing.'"
**5** Then he told them a story: "Suppose one of you has a friend and knocks on his door at midnight saying, 'Friend, lend me three loaves of bread!
**6** A friend of mine just showed up from a trip, and I have nothing in the house to feed him.'
**7** Suppose the man yells back from inside, 'Leave me alone! The door is already bolted, and my kids and I are in bed. I can't get up and give you anything!'
**8** I'm telling you, even if he won't get up just because you're his friend, your shameless persistence will force him out of bed to give you everything you need!
**9** So I'm telling you: keep asking, and you'll receive; keep searching, and you'll find; keep knocking, and the door will open for you.
**10** Everyone who asks receives, anyone who searches finds, and the door swings open for whoever knocks.
**11** Which father among you, if your son asks for a fish, would hand him a snake instead?
**12** Or if he asks for an egg, would hand him a scorpion?
**13** Even though you're flawed, you still know how to give good gifts to your kids—how much more surely will your Father in heaven pour out the Holy Spirit on anyone who asks him!"
**14** Another time, Jesus drove out a demon that kept a man from speaking. As soon as the demon left, the man started talking, and the crowds were amazed.
**15** Still, some people muttered, "He's driving out demons using the power of Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons!"
**16** Others wanted to test him and kept demanding a miraculous sign straight from heaven.
**17** Knowing what they were thinking, Jesus said to them, "Any kingdom torn by civil war collapses into ruin, and a divided household crumbles.
**18** If Satan is fighting against himself, how can his kingdom survive? Yet you're claiming I drive out demons by Beelzebul's power!
**19** If I drive out demons by Beelzebul, whose power do your own followers use to drive them out? They'll be the ones to prove you wrong!
**20** But if I'm driving out demons by God's own finger, then God's kingdom has already arrived right in front of you!
**21** When a strong, heavily armed warrior guards his own palace, his property stays safe.
**22** But when someone even stronger attacks and overpowers him, the victor strips away the armor he trusted in and hands out his loot.
**23** Anyone who isn't on my side is against me, and anyone who doesn't gather with me scatters the flock.
**24** When an evil spirit leaves someone, it wanders through dry, barren places looking for a place to rest. Finding none, it says, 'I'll go back to my old house that I left.'
**25** When it arrives, it finds the house swept clean and neatly decorated.
**26** So it goes and grabs seven other spirits even more wicked than itself, and they all move in and settle down—leaving that person in far worse shape than before."
**27** While Jesus was speaking, a woman in the crowd shouted out, "God bless the mother who carried you in her womb and nursed you!"
**28** Jesus answered, "Even more blessed are the people who listen to God's word and put it into practice!"
**29** As the crowds kept pressing in thicker, he began speaking: "This generation is wicked! It keeps begging for a miraculous sign, yet the only sign it's going to get is the sign of Jonah.
**30** Just as Jonah became a living sign to the people of Nineveh, so will the Son of Man be to this generation.
**31** On judgment day, the Queen of the South will stand up beside the people of this generation and condemn them, because she traveled from the ends of the earth to listen to Solomon's wisdom—and look, someone greater than Solomon is standing right here!
**32** On judgment day, the citizens of Nineveh will stand up beside this generation and condemn it, because they repented at Jonah's message—and look, someone greater than Jonah is standing right here!
**33** Nobody lights a lamp and hides it in a dark cellar or under a bucket! You put it up on a lampstand so everyone walking in can see the glow.
**34** Your eye is like a lamp for your body. When your eye is healthy and generous, your whole body is flooded with light; when your eye is stingy and bad, your whole body stays in the dark.
**35** Make sure the light inside you isn't actually darkness!
**36** If your entire body is filled with light and has no dark corners left, you'll shine all over like a lamp lighting you up with bright rays."
**37** When Jesus finished speaking, a Pharisee invited him over for lunch, so he went inside and sat down at the table.
**38** The Pharisee was shocked to notice that Jesus didn't perform the ceremonial handwashing before eating.
**39** Then the Lord told him, "You Pharisees scrub the outside of your cups and plates spotless, while your insides are stuffed with greed and corruption!
**40** How foolish! Didn't the same God who crafted the outside make the inside as well?
**41** Give to the poor from what's genuinely inside your hearts, and then everything becomes clean for you!
**42** How terrible for you Pharisees! You carefully measure out a tenth of your mint, rue, and every little garden herb, while ignoring justice and the love of God! You should have practiced justice and love without neglecting the smaller rules.
**43** How terrible for you Pharisees! You love grabbing the seats of honor in the synagogues and getting respectful bows in the public markets.
**44** How terrible for you! You're like hidden graves overgrown with grass—people walk right over them without ever knowing it."
**45** One of the legal scholars spoke up: "Teacher, when you talk like that, you're insulting us too!"
**46** Jesus replied, "How terrible for you legal experts as well! You pile crushing rules onto people's backs that nobody can carry, and you won't lift a single finger to help them!
**47** How terrible for you! You build fancy monuments for the prophets—the very prophets your ancestors murdered!
**48** By doing that, you prove you agree with what your ancestors did: they killed the prophets, and you build their tombs!
**49** That's why God's wisdom said, 'I will send them prophets and apostles, and they will murder some and chase down others,'
**50** so that this generation will be held responsible for the blood of every prophet spilled since the world began—
**51** from the blood of Abel all the way to the blood of Zechariah, who was murdered between the altar and the temple sanctuary. Yes, I promise you, this generation will answer for all of it!
**52** How terrible for you legal scholars! You snatched away the key to true knowledge—you didn't walk through the door yourselves, and you blocked everyone who tried to go in!"
**53** As soon as Jesus stepped outside, the legal experts and Pharisees turned fiercely hostile and began peppering him with rapid-fire questions,
**54** setting traps to catch him saying something they could use against him.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2–4 `Πάτερ` (*Pater*):** Luke's shorter form of the Lord's Prayer (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`) omits the liturgical expansions found in `RP` (harmonized from Matt 6:9–13: `ἡμῶν ὁ ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς`, `Γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου...`, and `ἀλλὰ ῥῦσαι ἡμᾶς ἀπὸ τοῦ πονηροῦ`).
- **v. 3 `ἐπιούσιον` (*epiousion*):** A rare adjective occurring in the NT only here and in Matt 6:11; variously understood as "for today's subsistence," "needful for existence," or "for the coming day" (*epiousa [hēmera]*).
- **v. 8 `ἀναίδειαν` (*anaideian*):** Literally "shamelessness" or "impudence" (only NT occurrence), describing persistence that refuses to be turned away out of embarrassment.
- **v. 20 `ἐν δακτύλῳ θεοῦ` (*en daktylō theou*):** "By the finger of God" (compare Matt 12:28 "Spirit of God"), echoing Exod 8:19 (8:15 MT) where Pharaoh's magicians confess, "This is the finger of God."
- **v. 22 `αὐτοῦ` (*autou*):** `WH`, `Treg`, `RP`, and `NA28` read `ἰσχυρότερος αὐτοῦ` ("one stronger than he"); a minor textual variant omits `αὐτοῦ`.
- **v. 41 `τὰ ἐνόντα` (*ta enonta*):** Literally "the things within" (either the contents inside the cup/dish, i.e., sharing one's food/wealth with the poor, or inner attitudes of the heart).
- **v. 51 `ἀπὸ αἵματος Ἅβελ ἕως αἵματος Ζαχαρίου` (*apo haimatos Habel heōs haimatos Zachariou*):** `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` omit the article `τοῦ` before both occurrences of `αἵματος` ("blood"), whereas `RP` inserts `τοῦ` in both places. Abel (Gen 4:8) and Zechariah son of Jehoiada (2 Chr 24:20–22) span the first and last books of the Hebrew canonical order.
- **v. 53 `ἀποστοματίζειν` (*apostomatizein*):** Literally "to make someone speak from memory / catechize," hence to badger with rapid-fire questions hoping to trap him.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 8:19 (8:15 MT)** (echoed in v. 20): The Egyptian magicians acknowledge God's own finger (*daktylos theou*) at work in Moses's miracles.
- **Jonah 3:1–10 & 1 Kings 10:1–10** (cited in vv. 29–32): Nineveh's repentance at Jonah's warning and the Queen of Sheba's journey to hear Solomon's wisdom testify against unbelief.
- **Genesis 4:8 & 2 Chronicles 24:20–22** (recalled in v. 51): The murder of Abel in Genesis and the stoning of Zechariah in the temple court in Chronicles bracket Israel's scriptural witness to martyred prophets.

**28** But he said, “On the contrary, blessed are those who hear the word of God, and keep it.”
**29** When the multitudes were gathering together to him, he began to say, “This is an evil generation. It seeks after a sign. No sign will be given to it but the sign of Jonah the prophet.
**30** For even as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so the Son of Man will also be to this generation.
**31** The Queen of the South will rise up in the judgment with the men of this generation and will condemn them, for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, one greater than Solomon is here.
**32** The men of Nineveh will stand up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, for they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and behold, one greater than Jonah is here.
**33** “No one, when he has lit a lamp, puts it in a cellar or under a basket, but on a stand, that those who come in may see the light.
**34** The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is good, your whole body is also full of light; but when it is evil, your body also is full of darkness.
**35** Therefore see whether the light that is in you isn’t darkness.
**36** If therefore your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it will be wholly full of light, as when the lamp with its bright shining gives you light.”
**37** Now as he spoke, a certain Pharisee asked him to dine with him. He went in and sat at the table.
**38** When the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that he had not first washed himself before dinner.
**39** The Lord said to him, “Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but your inward part is full of extortion and wickedness.
**40** You foolish ones, didn’t he who made the outside make the inside also?
**41** But give for gifts to the needy those things which are within, and behold, all things will be clean to you.
**42** But woe to you Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every herb, but you bypass justice and God’s love. You ought to have done these, and not to have left the other undone.
**43** Woe to you Pharisees! For you love the best seats in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces.
**44** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like hidden graves, and the men who walk over them don’t know it.”
**45** One of the lawyers answered him, “Teacher, in saying this you insult us also.”
**46** He said, “Woe to you lawyers also! For you load men with burdens that are difficult to carry, and you yourselves won’t even lift one finger to help carry those burdens.
**47** Woe to you! For you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.
**48** So you testify and consent to the works of your fathers. For they killed them, and you build their tombs.
**49** Therefore also the wisdom of God said, ‘I will send to them prophets and apostles; and some of them they will kill and persecute,
**50** that the blood of all the prophets, which was shed from the foundation of the world, may be required of this generation,
**51** from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zachariah, who perished between the altar and the sanctuary.’ Yes, I tell you, it will be required of this generation.
**52** Woe to you lawyers! For you took away the key of knowledge. You didn’t enter in yourselves, and those who were entering in, you hindered.”
**53** As he said these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to be terribly angry, and to draw many things out of him,
**54** lying in wait for him, and seeking to catch him in something he might say, that they might accuse him.
