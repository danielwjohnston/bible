## Clear & Faithful

**1** On the first day of the week, at very early dawn, the women went to the tomb carrying the spices they had prepared.
**2** To their surprise, the stone had been rolled away from the tomb entrance,
**3** yet when they entered, they did not find the body.
**4** While they stood bewildered by this, all at once two men appeared beside them in gleaming garments.
**5** As the women grew terrified and bowed their faces toward the ground, the men said to them, "Why are you searching for the living one among the dead?
**6** He is not here, but has been raised! Remember how he spoke to you while he was still in Galilee,
**7** explaining that the Son of Man had to be betrayed into the hands of sinful people, be crucified, and rise to life on the third day."
**8** Immediately they recalled his sayings,
**9** and heading back from the tomb, they announced all these events to the eleven and to everyone else.
**10** It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women accompanying them who kept recounting these events to the apostles.
**11** Yet these words struck them as idle nonsense, and they did not believe the women.
**12** Peter, however, got up and ran to the tomb; stooping to look in, he saw the linen burial cloths lying by themselves, and he went home marveling at what had happened.
**13** Now that very day, two of them were traveling to a village named Emmaus, about sixty stadia from Jerusalem,
**14** and they were talking with each other about all these things that had happened.
**15** As they were conversing and discussing together, Jesus himself approached and walked along with them,
**16** yet their eyes were restrained from recognizing him.
**17** He asked them, "What is this conversation you are trading back and forth as you walk?" They came to a stop, their faces downcast.
**18** One of them, called Cleopas, replied to him, "Are you the sole visitor staying in Jerusalem unaware of the events that took place there over these past days?"
**19** He asked them, "Which events?" They answered him, "Everything regarding Jesus of Nazareth, who proved to be a prophet powerful in both deed and speech before God and the whole people—
**20** how our chief priests and rulers handed him over to be condemned to death and crucified him.
**21** Yet we were hoping that he was the one about to redeem Israel! Moreover, besides all this, today is already the third day since these things took place.
**22** Still, some women from our group amazed us: they were at the tomb early at dawn,
**23** unable to locate his body, they returned claiming even to have witnessed a vision of angels declaring that he is alive.
**24** Some of those with us went to the tomb and found things just as the women had said, yet him they did not see."
**25** Jesus replied to them, "How foolish you are, and how slow of heart to trust everything the prophets declared!
**26** Did the Messiah not have to endure these sufferings before entering into his glory?"
**27** Starting with Moses and continuing through every prophet, he explained to them in all the Scriptures the passages referring to himself.
**28** They approached the village where they were going, and he acted as though he were going farther on.
**29** But they urged him strongly, saying, "Stay with us, because evening is near and the day is already spent." So he went in to stay with them.
**30** When he reclined at table with them, he took the bread, blessed it, broke it, and began handing it to them.
**31** Immediately their eyes were opened wide, and they recognized him—and he vanished from their sight.
**32** They exclaimed to each other, "Were our hearts not burning inside us as he talked with us along the road and unlocked the Scriptures for us?"
**33** Getting up that very hour, they returned to Jerusalem and found the eleven gathered together along with those with them,
**34** who were saying, "The Lord has truly been raised and has appeared to Simon!"
**35** Then the two recounted what had happened on the road, and how he had become known to them in the breaking of the bread.
**36** While they were still talking about these things, Jesus himself stood right among them.
**37** Startled and terrified, they supposed they were seeing a spirit.
**38** He asked them, "Why are you so shaken, and why do doubts keep rising inside your hearts?
**39** Examine my hands and feet—see that it is truly I myself! Touch me and verify, because a spirit lacks flesh and bones such as you observe me possessing."
**40** With those words, he displayed his hands and feet to them.
**41** While they still could not believe it for sheer joy and amazement, he asked them, "Do you have anything here to eat?"
**42** They offered him a portion of broiled fish,
**43** and taking it, he ate it right before their eyes.
**44** Then he said to them, "These are my words that I spoke to you while I was still with you: that everything written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled."
**45** At that point he opened up their understanding so they could grasp the Scriptures,
**46** and he said to them, "Thus it is written: that the Christ should suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,
**47** and that repentance leading to the pardon of sins would be heralded in his name among all nations, starting at Jerusalem.
**48** You yourselves stand as witnesses to these events.
**49** Look, I am sending out the promise of my Father upon you; stay here in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."
**50** Afterward he escorted them out as far as Bethany, raised his hands, and pronounced a blessing over them.
**51** Even as he blessed them, he separated from them and was lifted up into heaven.
**52** After bowing in worship before him, they went back to Jerusalem filled with tremendous joy,
**53** and they remained continually inside the temple courts, offering praise and blessing to God.

## Everyday English

**1** Very early Sunday morning at dawn, the women walked out to the tomb carrying the burial spices they'd mixed.
**2** To their shock, they found the heavy stone already rolled away from the entrance,
**3** and when they stepped inside, the body wasn't there!
**4** While they stood baffled over what had happened, two men suddenly appeared beside them wearing clothes that gleamed like lightning.
**5** Terrified, the women dropped their faces toward the ground, and the men asked them, "Why are you hunting among the dead for someone who is alive?
**6** He isn't in here—God raised him! Recall what he spoke to you back when he was still in Galilee:
**7** that the Son of Man had to be betrayed into the hands of sinful people, crucified, and rise again on the third day!"
**8** All at once, his words came flooding back to them.
**9** They hurried back from the tomb and reported everything to the eleven apostles and everyone else.
**10** It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the rest of the women with them who kept telling the apostles what they'd seen.
**11** But the whole story sounded like pure nonsense to the men, and they refused to believe them.
**12** Peter, however, jumped up and sprinted to the tomb. Peeking inside, he saw only the linen burial strips lying there by themselves, and he walked home amazed at what had occurred.
**13** That exact same day, two of Jesus's followers were walking to a village named Emmaus, about seven miles outside Jerusalem.
**14** As they walked, they kept talking over everything that had happened.
**15** Right while they were debating and discussing it all, Jesus himself caught up and started walking alongside them—
**16** yet something kept their eyes from recognizing who he was.
**17** "What's this heavy conversation you two are going back and forth about on your walk?" he asked. Both men stopped in their tracks, looking heartbroken.
**18** One of them, named Cleopas, answered, "Are you the only visitor staying in Jerusalem who hasn't heard everything that went down there over the past few days?"
**19** "What happened?" Jesus asked. "All the events surrounding Jesus of Nazareth," they replied. "He was a prophet—powerful in action and speech before God and all the people!
**20** Yet our chief priests and rulers handed him over to be sentenced to death, and they crucified him.
**21** We had honestly hoped he was the one who was going to rescue Israel! On top of all that, today is already the third day since it happened.
**22** Wait—some women from our group completely stunned us! They went out to the tomb at daybreak,
**23** couldn't find his body, and came back claiming they saw a vision of angels who told them he's alive!
**24** A few of our friends ran out to the tomb and found everything just as the women reported, though they didn't see Jesus himself."
**25** Then Jesus told them, "How foolish and slow-hearted you are to trust everything the prophets promised!
**26** Didn't the Messiah have to suffer all these things before stepping into his glory?"
**27** Starting with Moses and moving right through all the prophets, he walked them through every Scripture that pointed to himself.
**28** Soon they reached the village they were heading toward, and Jesus acted like he was going to keep walking farther down the road.
**29** But they begged him, "Please stay with us! It's getting late, and the sun's already going down." So he stepped inside to spend the evening with them.
**30** Once they sat down for dinner, he picked up the bread, blessed it, broke it apart, and started handing pieces to them.
**31** Instantly their eyes flew wide open and they knew it was him—and just like that, he disappeared from their sight!
**32** They looked at each other and said, "Weren't our hearts burning inside us while he talked with us along the road and unlocked the Scriptures for us?"
**33** Without waiting another minute, they got right up and hurried all the way back to Jerusalem. There they found the eleven apostles and the others gathered together,
**34** already celebrating: "The Lord really has risen! He even appeared to Simon!"
**35** Then the two travelers told the whole story of what happened on the road and how they recognized him the moment he broke the bread.
**36** While they were still swapping stories, Jesus himself suddenly stood right in the middle of the room!
**37** Startled and scared half to death, they thought they were staring at a ghost.
**38** "Why are you so shaken up?" Jesus asked them. "Why are doubts racing through your minds?
**39** Look right here at my hands and my feet—it's really me! Touch me and see for yourselves: a ghost doesn't have real flesh and bones like you see I have."
**40** Saying that, he held out his hands and feet for them to see.
**41** While they still could barely believe it because they were overwhelmed with joy and wonder, he asked, "Do you have anything to eat around here?"
**42** So they brought over a cooked piece of fish,
**43** and he took it and ate it right in front of all of them.
**44** Then he told them, "This is what I meant when I was still with you: everything written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms had to come true."
**45** Right then he opened their minds so they could finally understand the Scriptures,
**46** telling them, "Scripture promised that the Messiah would suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,
**47** and that turning back to God for the forgiveness of sins would be announced in his name to every nation—starting right here in Jerusalem.
**48** You are my eyewitnesses to all of this!
**49** Watch—I'm sending the gift my Father promised right onto you. Stay put in the city until you're wrapped in power from heaven above."
**50** Then Jesus led them out toward Bethany, raised his hands, and blessed them.
**51** Right while he was blessing them, he left them and was lifted straight up into heaven.
**52** After worshiping him, they went back to Jerusalem bursting with joy,
**53** and spent all their time in the temple praising God.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `τὸ σῶμα` (*to sōma*):** SBLGNT (`WH`) ends the verse with "the body," whereas `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and **Nestle 1904** read *to sōma tou kyriou Iēsou* (`τοῦ Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ`, "the body of the Lord Jesus"), a well-known Western Non-Interpolation variant.
- **v. 6 `οὐκ ἔστιν ὧδε, ἀλλὰ ἠγέρθη` (*ouk estin hōde, alla ēgerthē*):** Present in `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and Nestle 1904 ("He is not here, but has been raised"), whereas `⟦WH⟧` double-brackets this sentence because it is omitted by Codex Bezae (D) and several Old Latin witnesses.
- **v. 12 `Ὁ δὲ Πέτρος ἀναστὰς ἔδραμεν ἐπὶ τὸ μνημεῖον` (*Ho de Petros anastas edramen epi to mnēmeion*):** Peter's run to the tomb and sight of the *othonia* ("linen strips") is printed by `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and Nestle 1904, while `⟦WH⟧` double-brackets the entire verse as absent from Codex Bezae and Old Latin witnesses.
- **v. 18 `Κλεοπᾶς` (*Kleopas*):** Abbreviated Greek name (*Kleopatros*), distinct from *Klōpas* in John 19:25; note `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read *heis onomati* ("one named"), whereas `RP` reads *ho heis hō onoma* ("the one whose name").
- **v. 36 `ἐν μέσῳ αὐτῶν` (*en mesō autōn*):** SBLGNT (`WH`) ends the verse at "in their midst," whereas `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and **Nestle 1904** (which encloses the clause in double brackets `[[καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς Εἰρήνη ὑμῖν]]`) add `καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς Εἰρήνη ὑμῖν` (*kai legei autois Eirēnē hymin*, "and says to them, 'Peace to you'").
- **v. 40 `καὶ τοῦτο εἰπὼν ἔδειξεν αὐτοῖς τὰς χεῖρας καὶ τοὺς πόδας` (*kai touto eipōn edeixen autois tas cheiras kai tous podas*):** Enclosed in single editorial brackets `[...]` in SBLGNT (and `⟦WH⟧` in apparatus) as a Western Non-Interpolation omitted by Codex Bezae and Old Latin/Syriac witnesses, though retained by `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and Nestle 1904.
- **v. 51 `καὶ ἀνεφέρετο εἰς τὸν οὐρανόν` (*kai anephereto eis ton ouranon*):** Enclosed in single editorial brackets `[...]` in SBLGNT (and `⟦WH⟧` in apparatus) as a Western Non-Interpolation absent from Sinaiticus (first hand), Codex Bezae, and some Old Latin manuscripts, while attested by P75, Vaticanus, Alexandrinus, `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and Nestle 1904.
- **v. 52 `προσκυνήσαντες αὐτὸν` (*proskynēsantes auton*):** Enclosed in single editorial brackets `[...]` in SBLGNT (and `⟦WH⟧` in apparatus) as a Western Non-Interpolation omitted in Codex Bezae and Old Latin witnesses, though strongly attested in P75, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus, `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`, and Nestle 1904.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Hosea 6:2 & Jonah 1:17** (background to vv. 7, 46): *"On the third day he will lift us up to life"*—Scriptural pattern behind the Messiah's third-day resurrection.
- **Genesis 3:7 & 19:2–3** (echoed in vv. 29–31): *the opening of their eyes* and urgent hospitality at dusk frame the Emmaus recognition meal.
- **Genesis 45:24–28 & Psalm 39:3** (echoed in vv. 11, 32): Disbelief turning into a burning heart when hidden truth is unveiled.
- **Isaiah 49:6 & Joel 2:28–32** (alluded to in vv. 47, 49): Repentance and forgiveness proclaimed to all nations, empowered by the Spirit poured out from on high.
