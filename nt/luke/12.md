## Clear & Faithful

**1** Meanwhile, when many thousands of the crowd had gathered together so that they were trampling on one another, he began to speak first to his disciples: "Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.
**2** Nothing is covered up that will not be uncovered, and nothing is hidden that will not become known.
**3** Consequently, everything you have uttered in the dark will be overheard in daylight, and whatever you whispered into someone's ear inside private rooms will be announced from the rooftops.
**4** I tell you, my friends, do not be afraid of those who kill the body and after that can do nothing more.
**5** Yet I will show you whom to fear: fear the one who, after killing, has authority to cast into Gehenna. Yes, I tell you, fear him!
**6** Are five sparrows not sold for a couple of copper coins? Even so, not a single one of them goes forgotten in God's sight.
**7** Indeed, even the hairs of your head are all numbered. Do not be afraid; you are worth far more than many sparrows.
**8** I assure you, whoever openly acknowledges me before people, the Son of Man will likewise acknowledge before God's angels;
**9** yet whoever denies me before others will be disowned before the angels of God.
**10** Anyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man can be pardoned, whereas the one who slanders the Holy Spirit will never be pardoned.
**11** When they bring you before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, do not worry about how or what you will speak in your defense, or what you will say;
**12** because the Holy Spirit will instruct you in that very moment on what needs to be said."
**13** Someone out of the crowd called to him, "Teacher, order my brother to share the inheritance with me."
**14** Jesus replied to him, "Sir, who appointed me as judge or arbitrator between you?"
**15** Then he said to them, "Watch out and guard against every kind of greed, because a person's life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions."
**16** And he told them a parable, saying, "The land of a certain rich man yielded a bountiful harvest.
**17** He reasoned within himself, saying, 'What shall I do, since I have nowhere to store my crops?'
**18** Then he decided, 'Here is my plan: I will pull down my storehouses and construct bigger ones, where I can stockpile all my wheat and my possessions.
**19** And I will say to my soul: Soul, you have plenty of goods stored away for many years ahead; rest, eat, drink, and enjoy yourself.'
**20** Yet God said to him, 'Fool! This very night your soul is being demanded back from you; and the things you prepared—whose will they be?'
**21** That is what happens to anyone who hoards wealth for himself while failing to grow rich toward God."
**22** Turning to his disciples, he said, "For this reason I urge you: stop worrying over your life—what you are going to eat—or over your body—what you are going to wear.
**23** Life surely counts for more than food, and the body for more than clothing.
**24** Look closely at the ravens: they neither plant nor harvest, they possess no pantry or barn, yet God keeps them fed. How much greater value do you hold than birds!
**25** And which of you by being anxious can add a single cubit to his lifespan?
**26** So if you are powerless to manage even the tiniest thing, why worry about everything else?
**27** Observe the lilies and how they bloom: they neither labor nor spin thread; yet I assure you, not even Solomon dressed in all his splendor matched the beauty of one of these.
**28** If God clothes even wild field grass—alive today and tossed into a clay oven tomorrow—in such splendor, how much more surely will he clothe you, people of little faith!
**29** And do not keep seeking what you will eat and what you will drink, and do not be tossed about by worry;
**30** for all the nations of the world eagerly seek these things, yet your Father knows that you need them.
**31** Rather, pursue his kingdom first, and all these provisions will be supplied to you as well.
**32** Have no fear, my little flock, because it has delighted your Father to grant you the kingdom.
**33** Sell your possessions and give to the needy; make for yourselves purses that do not wear out, an unfailing treasure in heaven, where no thief comes near and no moth destroys.
**34** For wherever your true treasure lies, there your heart will reside as well.
**35** Keep your waist dressed for action and your lamps burning,
**36** and be like people waiting for their master to return from the wedding banquet, so that when he comes and knocks they may open to him immediately.
**37** Blessed are those servants whom the master finds awake when he comes. Truly I tell you, he will dress himself to serve, have them recline at table, and come up and wait on them.
**38** Whether he comes in the second watch or in the third and finds them so, blessed are those servants.
**39** Understand this: if the homeowner had known at what hour the thief was coming, he would not have allowed his house to be broken into.
**40** You also must be ready, because the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect."
**41** Peter said, "Lord, are you telling this parable for us, or for everyone as well?"
**42** And the Lord said, "Who then is the faithful and wise manager whom his master will put in charge of his household staff, to give them their portion of grain at the proper time?
**43** Blessed is that servant whom his master finds doing so when he returns.
**44** Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions.
**45** Yet if that servant says in his heart, 'My master is taking a long time to come,' and begins to beat the male and female servants and to eat and drink and get drunk,
**46** the master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour he does not know, and will cut him in pieces and assign his place with the unfaithful.
**47** That servant who knew his master's will, yet did not prepare or act according to his will, will receive a severe beating;
**48** whereas the one who did not know it, yet did things deserving punishment, will receive a light beating. From everyone to whom much has been given, much will be required; and from the one to whom much has been entrusted, even more will be demanded.
**49** I came to ignite fire upon the earth, and how I long for it to be blazing already!
**50** I also have a baptism to pass through, and how deeply pressed I am until it is finished!
**51** Do you suppose that I came to grant peace on the earth? No, I tell you, rather division!
**52** From this point onward, five members inside a single household will stand divided, three against two and two against three.
**53** They will split apart—father opposing son and son opposing father, mother opposing daughter and daughter opposing mother, mother-in-law opposing her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law opposing mother-in-law."
**54** He also said to the crowds, "When you see a cloud rising in the west, immediately you say, 'A rainstorm is coming,' and so it happens.
**55** And whenever a south wind blows, you predict, 'Scorching heat is on the way,' and so it turns out.
**56** Hypocrites! You know how to read the face of earth and sky; why, then, do you fail to discern this decisive hour?
**57** Why do you not also decide for yourselves what justice requires?
**58** For as you are going with your opponent before a magistrate, make every effort along the way to settle with him, lest he drag you before the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.
**59** I tell you, you will certainly not get out from there until you have paid back the very last copper coin."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `ζύμης` (*zymēs*):** "Leaven" or "yeast," a metaphor for pervasive, hidden influence—here explicitly defined as *hypokrisis* ("play-acting / hypocrisy").
- **v. 11 `πῶς ἢ τί` (*pōs ē ti*):** "How or what" (`WH`, `Treg`, `RP`, `NA28`), covering both manner and substance of legal defense (*apologeomai*); a minor textual variant omits `ἢ τί`.
- **v. 20 `ἀπαιτοῦσιν` (*apaitousin*):** "They demand back" (`NA28`, `RP`; impersonal plural equivalent to passive "is demanded back"), treating life as a loan recalled by its Creator; `WH` and `Treg` read `αἰτοῦσιν` ("they ask/require").
- **v. 25 `πῆχυν` (*pēchyn*):** "A cubit" (roughly 18 inches / 45 cm), used metaphorically with `ἡλικίαν` (*hēlikian*, either physical "stature" or temporal "lifespan," where a single cubit on a track of life represents a tiny increment). `WH` and `NA28` omit `ἕνα` ("one") before `πῆχυν`, whereas `Treg` and `RP` include it.
- **v. 29 `μετεωρίζεσθε` (*meteōrizesthe*):** From *meteōros* ("raised in midair"), meaning to be buoyed up with anxious suspense or tossed about by shifting hopes and fears.
- **v. 39 `οὐκ ἂν ἀφῆκεν` (*ouk an aphēken*):** `NA28` omits `ἐγρηγόρησεν ἂν καί` ("would have kept watch and"), which `WH`, `Treg`, and `RP` include (harmonized from Matt 24:43).
- **v. 42 `σιτομέτριον` (*sitometrion*):** A measured grain/food ration distributed by an estate steward (*oikonomos*) to household staff (*therapeia*).
- **v. 59 `λεπτόν` (*lepton*):** The smallest Judean bronze coin in circulation (valued at half a Roman *quadrans*, cf. Mark 12:42).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Ecclesiastes 2:18–21; 5:18–19; Psalm 49:10–20** (background to vv. 16–21): The futility of hoarding wealth when death abruptly transfers one's store to another.
- **Psalm 147:9 & Job 38:41** (echoed in v. 24): God's providence feeding the ravens and their young when they cry out.
- **Micah 7:6** (quoted in v. 53): Prophetic picture of family loyalty fracturing in a time of crisis ("a son scorns his father, a daughter rebels against her mother, and a daughter-in-law opposes her mother-in-law").

**17** He reasoned within himself, saying, ‘What will I do, because I don’t have room to store my crops?’
**18** He said, ‘This is what I will do. I will pull down my barns, build bigger ones, and there I will store all my grain and my goods.
**19** I will tell my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years. Take your ease, eat, drink, and be merry.”’
**20** “But God said to him, ‘You foolish one, tonight your soul is required of you. The things which you have prepared—whose will they be?’
**21** So is he who lays up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.”
**22** He said to his disciples, “Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life, what you will eat, nor yet for your body, what you will wear.
**23** Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothing.
**24** Consider the ravens: they don’t sow, they don’t reap, they have no warehouse or barn, and God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds!
**25** Which of you by being anxious can add a cubit to his height?
**26** If then you aren’t able to do even the least things, why are you anxious about the rest?
**27** Consider the lilies, how they grow. They don’t toil, neither do they spin; yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.
**28** But if this is how God clothes the grass in the field, which today exists and tomorrow is cast into the oven, how much more will he clothe you, O you of little faith?
**29** “Don’t seek what you will eat or what you will drink; neither be anxious.
**30** For the nations of the world seek after all of these things, but your Father knows that you need these things.
**31** But seek God’s Kingdom, and all these things will be added to you.
**32** “Don’t be afraid, little flock, for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the Kingdom.
**33** Sell what you have and give gifts to the needy. Make for yourselves purses which don’t grow old, a treasure in the heavens that doesn’t fail, where no thief approaches and no moth destroys.
**34** For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.
**35** “Let your waist be dressed and your lamps burning.
**36** Be like men watching for their lord when he returns from the wedding feast, that when he comes and knocks, they may immediately open to him.
**37** Blessed are those servants whom the lord will find watching when he comes. Most certainly I tell you that he will dress himself, make them recline, and will come and serve them.
**38** They will be blessed if he comes in the second or third watch and finds them so.
**39** But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have watched and not allowed his house to be broken into.
**40** Therefore be ready also, for the Son of Man is coming in an hour that you don’t expect him.”
**41** Peter said to him, “Lord, are you telling this parable to us, or to everybody?”
**42** The Lord said, “Who then is the faithful and wise steward, whom his lord will set over his household, to give them their portion of food at the right times?
**43** Blessed is that servant whom his lord will find doing so when he comes.
**44** Truly I tell you that he will set him over all that he has.
**45** But if that servant says in his heart, ‘My lord delays his coming,’ and begins to beat the menservants and the maidservants, and to eat and drink and to be drunken,
**46** then the lord of that servant will come in a day when he isn’t expecting him and in an hour that he doesn’t know, and will cut him in two, and place his portion with the unfaithful.
**47** That servant who knew his lord’s will, and didn’t prepare nor do what he wanted, will be beaten with many stripes,
**48** but he who didn’t know, and did things worthy of stripes, will be beaten with few stripes. To whomever much is given, of him will much be required; and to whom much was entrusted, of him more will be asked.
**49** “I came to throw fire on the earth. I wish it were already kindled.
**50** But I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how distressed I am until it is accomplished!
**51** Do you think that I have come to give peace on the earth? I tell you, no, but rather division.
**52** For from now on, there will be five in one house divided, three against two, and two against three.
**53** They will be divided, father against son, and son against father; mother against daughter, and daughter against her mother; mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law, and daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.”
**54** He said to the multitudes also, “When you see a cloud rising from the west, immediately you say, ‘A shower is coming,’ and so it happens.
**55** When a south wind blows, you say, ‘There will be a scorching heat,’ and it happens.
**56** You hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky, but how is it that you don’t interpret this time?
**57** “Why don’t you judge for yourselves what is right?
**58** For when you are going with your adversary before the magistrate, try diligently on the way to be released from him, lest perhaps he drag you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.
**59** I tell you, you will by no means get out of there until you have paid the very last penny.”
