## Clear & Faithful

**1** On one occasion, as the crowd pressed close around him to listen to God's word, Jesus was standing beside Lake Gennesaret,
**2** and he saw two boats moored by the edge of the lake; the fishermen had stepped out of them and were washing their nets.
**3** Getting into one of the boats, which belonged to Simon, he asked him to push out a little way from the shore. Then he sat down and began teaching the crowds from the boat.
**4** Once he stopped speaking, he told Simon, "Row out into the deep water and drop your nets for a haul."
**5** Simon replied, "Master, we labored all through the night and caught nothing; nevertheless, on your word I will lower the nets."
**6** When they had done this, they enclosed such a huge number of fish that their nets began to tear.
**7** So they waved to their companions in the second boat to come and assist them. They rowed over and loaded both boats until they started sinking.
**8** When Simon Peter saw this, he fell down at Jesus's knees, saying, "Depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinful man!"
**9** For astonishment had seized him and all who were with him at the catch of fish they had taken,
**10** along with James and John, Zebedee's sons, who were partners with Simon. Jesus told Simon, "Have no fear; from this time forward, it is people you will be catching alive."
**11** After bringing their boats onto shore, they abandoned everything and became his followers.
**12** While he was in one of the towns, a man covered in leprosy appeared there. Upon seeing Jesus, he fell facedown and pleaded with him, "Lord, if you are willing, you have power to cleanse me."
**13** Reaching out his hand, Jesus touched the man and declared, "I am willing—be cleansed!" At once the leprosy vanished from him.
**14** And he ordered him to tell no one, "Instead, go and show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing just as Moses commanded, as a testimony to them."
**15** Yet the report about him spread all the more, and great crowds gathered together to hear him and to be healed of their infirmities.
**16** But he himself would regularly withdraw to solitary places and pray.
**17** On one of those days as he was teaching, Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there, who had come from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem; and the power of the Lord was with him to heal.
**18** Just then some men came carrying on a mat a man who was paralyzed, trying to bring him inside and lay him down before Jesus.
**19** Finding no way to bring him in because of the crowd, they went up onto the roof and lowered him on his cot through the tiles into the middle of the room right in front of Jesus.
**20** Recognizing their faith, Jesus told him, "Friend, your sins have been forgiven."
**21** The scribes and Pharisees started debating among themselves, "Who is this man uttering blasphemies? Who holds the power to forgive sins except God alone?"
**22** Knowing straightaway what they were thinking, Jesus replied to them, "Why are you arguing like this within your hearts?
**23** Which is simpler to declare: 'Your sins have been forgiven,' or 'Stand up and walk around'?
**24** So that you may know beyond doubt that the Son of Man possesses authority on earth to pardon sins"—he turned to the paralyzed man—"I tell you: stand up, lift your mat, and go home."
**25** Immediately he stood up before them, picked up what he had been lying on, and went home glorifying God.
**26** Amazement seized everyone, and they glorified God; filled with awe, they kept saying, "We have seen extraordinary things today!"
**27** Afterward Jesus went out and spotted a tax collector named Levi seated at his toll booth, and he told him, "Follow me."
**28** Leaving everything behind, he stood up and followed him.
**29** Then Levi gave a great banquet for him in his own house, and a large crowd of tax collectors and others were reclining at table with them.
**30** The Pharisees and their scribes grumbled to his disciples, asking, "Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?"
**31** Jesus replied to them, "Healthy people do not need a doctor, only those who are ill.
**32** I have come to summon not the upright, but sinners to repentance."
**33** Then they said to him, "John's disciples fast often and offer prayers, and likewise those of the Pharisees, but yours eat and drink."
**34** Jesus said to them, "You cannot make the wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them, can you?
**35** Days are coming, however, when the bridegroom will be snatched away from them; on those days they will fast."
**36** He also told them a parable: "No one tears a patch from a new garment and sews it onto an old garment; otherwise he will both tear the new one, and the patch from the new will not match the old.
**37** And no one pours new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the new wine will burst the skins, the wine will spill out, and the wineskins will be ruined.
**38** Instead, new wine must be poured into fresh wineskins.
**39** And no one after drinking old wine wants new, for he says, 'The old is good.'"

## Everyday English

**1** One day, as a huge crowd pressed in around Jesus to hear God's message, he was standing by the shore of Lake Gennesaret.
**2** He spotted two boats pulled up on the beach while the fishermen had climbed out to rinse their nets.
**3** Stepping into one of the boats—the one belonging to Simon—Jesus asked him to push off a little way from shore. Then he sat down and taught the crowds right from the boat.
**4** When he finished teaching, he told Simon, "Row out into deep water and drop your nets for a catch."
**5** "Master," Simon replied, "we've slaved away all night long without catching a single fish! Still, since you say so, I'll let down the nets."
**6** They did, and they scooped up so many fish that their nets started ripping apart.
**7** They waved frantically for their partners in the other boat to come help them. They rowed over and packed both boats so full of fish that they began to sink.
**8** When Simon Peter saw what happened, he dropped to his knees in front of Jesus and cried, "Get away from me, Lord! I'm a sinful man!"
**9** He and everyone with him were stunned at the massive catch of fish they'd hauled in—
**10** and so were Zebedee's sons, James and John, who were Simon's fishing partners. "Don't be afraid," Jesus told Simon. "From today on, you're going to be catching people instead of fish."
**11** As soon as they pulled their boats up onto the beach, they walked away from everything and followed him.
**12** Later, while Jesus was visiting one of the towns, a man covered from head to toe with a severe skin disease came up. Seeing Jesus, he threw himself face down on the ground and pleaded, "Lord, if you want to, you can make me clean!"
**13** Jesus reached out and touched him. "I do want to," he said. "Be clean!" Instantly, the skin disease vanished.
**14** Jesus warned him not to tell anyone, adding, "Go straight to the priest for an inspection and bring the offering Moses prescribed for your cleansing, as proof to them."
**15** Even so, word about Jesus kept spreading faster and wider, and huge crowds gathered to listen to him and be healed of their illnesses.
**16** Meanwhile, Jesus often slipped away to lonely places to pray.
**17** One day while he was teaching, Pharisees and experts in the law were sitting nearby—men who had traveled from every village across Galilee and Judea, and even from Jerusalem. And the Lord's healing power was flowing through Jesus.
**18** Just then, some men carried up a paralyzed man on a stretcher, trying to get him inside and lay him down right in front of Jesus.
**19** Since they couldn't squeeze through the packed crowd, they climbed up onto the flat roof, pulled back some roof tiles, and lowered the man on his mat straight down into the middle of the room in front of Jesus.
**20** When Jesus saw their faith, he told him, "My friend, your sins are forgiven."
**21** Right away, the legal scholars and Pharisees started muttering to themselves, "Who does this man think he is, talking blasphemy? Nobody can forgive sins except God!"
**22** Knowing immediately what they were thinking, Jesus asked them, "Why are you questioning this in your minds?
**23** Which is easier to say: 'Your sins are forgiven,' or 'Stand up and walk'?
**24** Let me show you that the Son of Man actually has authority here on earth to forgive sins." Turning to the paralyzed man, he said, "I'm telling you: stand up, pick up your mat, and head home!"
**25** On the spot, the man stood up in front of everybody, grabbed the mat he'd been lying on, and walked home praising God.
**26** Everyone was gripped with astonishment and praised God. Filled with awe, they said, "We've witnessed unbelievable things today!"
**27** After that, Jesus went outside and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at his collection booth. "Follow me," Jesus told him.
**28** Levi walked away from everything, stood up, and began following him.
**29** Later, Levi threw a big dinner party for Jesus at his own home, and a large crowd of tax collectors and other guests sat down to eat with them.
**30** The Pharisees and their legal experts complained to Jesus's disciples, "How can you eat and drink with tax collectors and outcasts?"
**31** Jesus answered them, "Healthy people don't need a doctor—sick people do.
**32** I didn't come to invite self-righteous people, but sinners, to turn their lives around."
**33** Next they challenged him, "John's followers fast regularly and say set prayers, and the Pharisees' followers do too—why do yours keep eating and drinking?"
**34** Jesus replied, "You wouldn't force wedding guests to fast while the groom is still celebrating with them, would you?
**35** A day is coming when the groom will be taken away from them—that's when they'll fast."
**36** He also gave them an illustration: "Nobody rips a piece off a brand-new coat to patch up an old one. If you did, you'd ruin the new coat, and the new patch wouldn't even match the old fabric.
**37** And nobody pours fresh new wine into old, brittle wineskins. If you do, the fermenting wine will split the skins open, spill all over the ground, and destroy the skins.
**38** No, fresh wine has to go into brand-new wineskins!
**39** Besides, nobody who's just tasted aged wine wants the new stuff—they'll tell you, 'The old wine is wonderful.'"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 5 `Ἐπιστάτα` (*Epistata*):** "Master," "Commander," or "Chief"—a title unique to Luke in the New Testament (5:5; 8:24, 45; 9:33, 49; 17:13), used by disciples instead of *Rabbi*.
- **v. 10 `ζωγρῶν` (*zōgrōn*):** Present active participle of *zōgreō* ("to capture alive," from *zōos* + *agreō*, contrasting with catching fish for death).
- **v. 19 `διὰ τῶν κεράμων` (*dia tōn keramōn*):** "Through the roof tiles," adapting the Palestinian flat mud-and-branch roof (Mark 2:4) to Greco-Roman terracotta tiled roofing familiar to Luke's wider audience.
- **v. 26 `παράδοξα` (*paradoxa*):** Literally "things contrary to expectation/opinion," i.e., extraordinary wonders (the only NT occurrence of *paradoxos*).
- **v. 38 `βλητέον` (*blēteon*):** Verbal adjective of necessity ("must be put/poured"). `WH` and `NA28` end the sentence here; `Treg` and `RP` add `καὶ ἀμφότεροι συντηροῦνται` ("and both are preserved together") from Matt 9:17.
- **v. 39 `χρηστός` (*chrēstos*):** "Good," "mellow," or "palatable" (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`); `RP` reads the comparative `χρηστότερός` ("better"). `WH` omits the opening `καὶ` ("and") of v. 39.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Leviticus 14:2–32** (referenced in v. 14): Priestly inspection and cleansing offerings prescribed by Moses for someone healed of *tsara'at* (skin disease).
- **Jeremiah 16:16 & Ezekiel 47:9–10** (background to vv. 4–10): Prophetic imagery of fishers gathering people and abundant fish swarming in renewed waters.
- **Isaiah 54:5; 62:5 & Hosea 2:19–20** (echoed in vv. 34–35): God as Israel's bridegroom, framing Jesus's table fellowship as messianic wedding joy.
