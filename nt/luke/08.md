## Clear & Faithful

**1** Soon afterward, Jesus traveled through cities and villages, preaching and proclaiming the good news of the kingdom of God. The twelve were with him,
**2** accompanied by several women who had been healed of evil spirits and illnesses: Mary, known as Magdalene, from whom seven demons had come out;
**3** Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's household manager; Susanna; and many others, who provided for them out of their own resources.
**4** When a large crowd had gathered and people from every town were coming to him, he spoke through a parable:
**5** "A farmer went out to scatter his seed. As he sowed, part fell beside the road and was trampled down, and wild birds ate it up.
**6** Other seed dropped onto rocky ground, and once it sprouted, it dried up for lack of moisture.
**7** Other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up with it and choked it.
**8** Still other seed fell into good soil, and when it sprouted, it produced a crop a hundredfold." As he said these things, he called out, "Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear!"
**9** His disciples inquired of him what the meaning of this parable was.
**10** He replied, "To you has been entrusted the knowledge of the secrets of God's kingdom, whereas to the rest they are spoken in parables, so that 'though looking they may fail to see, and though listening they may fail to grasp.'
**11** Here is what the parable signifies: the seed is God's message.
**12** Those beside the road represent people who hear, and afterward the devil arrives and snatches the message out of their hearts so they will not believe and be saved.
**13** Those on the rocky ground are the ones who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; yet these have no root—they believe for a while, and in a time of testing they fall away.
**14** As for what fell among the thorns, these are the ones who hear, yet as they go on their way they are choked by the worries, wealth, and pleasures of life, and their fruit does not reach maturity.
**15** And as for what fell in the good soil, these are the ones who, having heard the word with a noble and good heart, hold it fast and bear fruit with endurance.
**16** No one lights a lamp and covers it with a jar or puts it under a bed; instead, he puts it on a lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.
**17** For nothing is hidden that will not become visible, nor is anything concealed that will not be known and come into the open.
**18** Take care then how you listen; for to the one who has, more will be given, and from the one who does not have, even what he thinks he has will be taken away."
**19** Then his mother and his brothers came to him, yet they could not reach him because of the crowd.
**20** It was reported to him, "Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see you."
**21** He answered them, "My true mother and brothers are these people—those who listen to God's message and put it into practice."
**22** One day he got into a boat with his disciples and said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side of the lake." So they set sail.
**23** As they were sailing along, he fell asleep. A windstorm swept down onto the lake, and the boat began taking on water, and they were in danger.
**24** They went and woke him up, saying, "Master, Master, we are perishing!" He awoke and rebuked the wind and the raging waves; they subsided, and there was calm.
**25** He asked them, "Where is your trust?" Filled with awe and wonder, they asked one another, "Who can this man be, that he gives orders even to winds and water, and they obey him?"
**26** Next they sailed across to the territory of the Gerasenes, lying opposite Galilee.
**27** As he stepped onto the shore, a man from the town met him who was possessed by demons. For a long time he had worn no clothing, and he did not live in a house, but among the tombs.
**28** Seeing Jesus, he let out a shriek, collapsed before him, and cried in a loud voice, "What have you to do with me, Jesus, Son of God Most High? I plead with you, do not torture me!"
**29** For Jesus had been commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. Many times it had seized him, and though he was kept under guard, bound with chains and shackles, he would break the bonds and be driven by the demon into the wilderness.
**30** Jesus questioned him, "What name do you bear?" He replied, "Legion," since a multitude of demons had entered into him.
**31** And they pleaded with him not to command them to depart into the abyss.
**32** Now a large herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside, and they begged him to permit them to enter those pigs. So he gave them permission.
**33** Coming out of the man, the demons went into the swine; and the herd plunged down the steep bank into the lake and choked in the water.
**34** When the herdsmen saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the town and throughout the countryside.
**35** Townspeople came out to inspect what had occurred; arriving at Jesus, they discovered the man from whom the demons had departed seated at Jesus's feet, fully clothed and restored to his right mind, and fear gripped them.
**36** Those who had seen it told them how the demon-possessed man had been healed.
**37** Then the whole multitude from the surrounding region of the Gerasenes asked Jesus to depart from them, for they were gripped by great fear. So he got into the boat and returned.
**38** The man freed from the demons kept pleading to remain with Jesus, yet he sent him off with these instructions:
**39** "Return to your home and tell how much God has done for you." So he went away, proclaiming throughout the whole town how much Jesus had done for him.
**40** When Jesus came back across, the crowd welcomed him warmly, since everyone had been expecting him.
**41** Just then a man named Jairus arrived; he was a leader of the synagogue. Falling at Jesus's feet, he pleaded with him to come to his house,
**42** because he had an only daughter, about twelve years old, and she was dying. As Jesus went, the crowds pressed tightly around him.
**43** Now a woman afflicted with chronic bleeding for twelve years, who had spent all her living on doctors yet could not be cured by any of them,
**44** approached from behind and touched the tassel of his outer garment, and at that very moment her bleeding ceased.
**45** Jesus asked, "Who touched me?" When everyone denied it, Peter said, "Master, the crowds are pressing and crowding all around you!"
**46** Jesus insisted, "Someone did touch me, because I felt power go forth from me."
**47** When the woman saw that she could not remain hidden, she came trembling and fell down before him. In the presence of all the people she declared why she had touched him and how she had been healed immediately.
**48** Jesus comforted her, "Daughter, your trust has healed you; depart in peace."
**49** While he was still speaking, someone came from the synagogue leader's house, saying, "Your daughter has died; do not trouble the teacher any longer."
**50** Hearing this, Jesus answered him, "Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be saved."
**51** When he arrived at the house, he permitted no one to enter with him except Peter, John, James, and the child's father and mother.
**52** Everyone was weeping and mourning for her, but he said, "Do not weep, for she has not died, she is sleeping."
**53** And they laughed at him, knowing that she was dead.
**54** Yet he took her by the hand and called out, "Child, arise!"
**55** Her spirit returned, and she got up at once; and he directed that something be given her to eat.
**56** Her parents were astounded, but he ordered them to tell no one what had happened.

## Everyday English

**1** Afterward, Jesus traveled from town to town and village to village, announcing the good news about God's kingdom. The twelve apostles went with him,
**2** along with several women he had freed from evil spirits and cured of sicknesses—Mary Magdalene (out of whom seven demons had been driven),
**3** Joanna (the wife of Chuza, who managed Herod's estate), Susanna, and many other women who used their own money to support Jesus and his group.
**4** A huge crowd kept growing as people streamed out from every town to meet Jesus, so he told them this story:
**5** "A farmer went out to plant his seed. As he scattered it, some seed fell on the footpath, where people stepped on it and birds swooped down and ate it up.
**6** Other seed fell on shallow soil over rock; it sprouted quickly, then dried up because there wasn't enough moisture.
**7** Other seed landed among thorny weeds, and as the weeds grew up beside it, they choked the young plants out.
**8** Still other seed dropped into rich, fertile dirt, and it grew up to yield a harvest a hundred times larger than what was planted." Wrapping up the story, he called out, "If you've got ears to listen, pay attention!"
**9** His disciples asked him what the story meant.
**10** He told them, "You've been given the privilege of understanding the hidden truths of God's kingdom, while everyone else hears them only in stories—so that 'even when they look, they won't really see, and even when they listen, they won't grasp it.'
**11** Here is what the story means: the seed represents God's message.
**12** The footpath stands for people who hear the message, then the devil comes right in and snatches it out of their hearts so they won't believe and be saved.
**13** The rocky soil stands for people who welcome the message with excitement the moment they hear it—yet they have no deep roots. They believe for a little while, then bail out the minute testing hits.
**14** The seed among thorny weeds stands for people who hear the message, yet as they go through life they get strangled by daily worries, money, and chasing pleasure, so their crop never ripens.
**15** But the seed in fertile soil stands for people who hear God's message with an honest, good heart, cling to it tightly, and stick with it until it produces a real harvest.
**16** Nobody lights an oil lamp only to hide it under a bucket or shove it under a bed! You put it up on a lampstand so anyone walking into the house can see the light.
**17** Everything hidden away will eventually be uncovered, and every secret will come out into broad daylight.
**18** So pay close attention to how you listen! Anyone who truly has something will be given even more, while anyone who has nothing will lose even what they think they hold onto."
**19** Around that time, Jesus's mother and brothers showed up to see him, though they couldn't get anywhere near him because of the packed crowd.
**20** Word reached him: "Your mother and brothers are standing outside waiting to see you."
**21** Jesus responded to the crowd, "My real mother and brothers are the people who listen to God's word and actually put it into practice."
**22** One day Jesus climbed into a boat with his disciples and said, "Let's cross over to the other side of the lake." So they pushed off.
**23** While they were sailing across, Jesus fell fast asleep. Suddenly a violent windstorm swept down over the lake; waves started swamping the boat, and they were in serious danger.
**24** They rushed over and shook him awake: "Master! Master! We're going to drown!" Jesus woke up and scolded the wind and the crashing waves. Instantly the storm stopped, and the water went dead calm.
**25** Then he asked them, "Where did your faith go?" Terrified and amazed, they whispered to each other, "Who can this man be? He gives orders even to the wind and the water, and they obey him!"
**26** They pulled ashore in the Gerasene territory, directly across the lake from Galilee.
**27** As Jesus stepped onto the beach, a man from the town ran up to him—a man tormented by demons. For a long time he hadn't worn any clothes, and instead of living in a house, he lived out in the graveyard caves.
**28** The instant he saw Jesus, he shrieked, dropped down in front of him, and yelled at the top of his lungs, "What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Please, I beg you, don't torture me!"
**29** Jesus had already started ordering the evil spirit to leave the man. Over and over, the spirit had overpowered him; even when people chained his hands and feet and kept him under guard, he'd snap the iron chains and the demon would drive him out into lonely desert places.
**30** Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" "Legion," he answered, because a whole army of demons had moved inside him.
**31** The demons kept begging Jesus not to banish them down into the bottomless pit.
**32** Nearby on the hillside, a huge herd of pigs was rooting around for food, so the demons begged Jesus to let them go into the pigs instead. Jesus let them.
**33** Leaving the man, the demons entered the pigs, and the entire herd stampeded down the steep cliff into the lake and drowned.
**34** When the pig herders saw what happened, they bolted and spread the news through the town and the surrounding farms.
**35** People poured out to see it for themselves. When they reached Jesus, they found the man who'd been freed from the demons sitting calmly at Jesus's feet, wearing clothes and thinking clearly—and that scared them.
**36** The eyewitnesses explained how the demon-possessed man had been rescued.
**37** Then everyone from the whole Gerasene district begged Jesus to leave their area, because intense fear had gripped them. So Jesus climbed back into the boat and headed back.
**38** The man who had been set free from the demons pleaded to come along with him, but Jesus sent him home instead:
**39** "Go back to your family and tell them everything God has done for you." So the man went off and announced throughout the entire town everything Jesus had done for him.
**40** When Jesus sailed back across the lake, the crowd cheered to welcome him—everyone had been waiting for him.
**41** Just then a synagogue leader named Jairus arrived, threw himself at Jesus's feet, and begged him to come to his house,
**42** because his only daughter—just twelve years old—was dying. As Jesus walked along, the crowd crushed in around him from every side.
**43** Among them was a woman who had suffered from nonstop bleeding for twelve years. She had spent every last coin she owned on doctors, yet nobody could cure her.
**44** Slipping up behind Jesus, she touched the tassel of his outer cloak, and on the spot her bleeding stopped.

**56** Her parents were completely stunned, and Jesus instructed them not to tell anyone what had happened.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `ἐπιτρόπου` (*epitropou*):** "Steward," "manager," or "fiscal agent" of Herod Antipas's household/estates, indicating that Joanna held high social standing and financial means.
- **v. 14 `τελεσφοροῦσιν` (*telesphorousin*):** "Bring fruit to maturity/ripeness" (only NT occurrence), contrasting with seedlings choked before harvest.
- **v. 26 `Γερασηνῶν` (*Gerasēnōn*):** `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read "Gerasenes" (also in v. 37), whereas `RP` harmonizes to `Γαδαρηνῶν` ("Gadarenes") matching Matt 8:28.
- **v. 31 `ἄβυσσον` (*abysson*):** "The abyss" or bottomless pit, the cosmic prison of rebellious spirits (also Rev 9:1–2; 20:1–3; cf. Rom 10:7).
- **v. 35 `σωφρονοῦντα` (*sōphronounta*):** Present participle of *sōphroneō* ("being of sound mind," "rational and self-controlled"), dramatically reversing his former violent bondage.
- **v. 43 `ἰατροῖς προσαναλώσασα ὅλον τὸν βίον` (*iatrois prosanalōsasa holon ton bion*):** "Having spent all her livelihood on physicians." SBLGNT, `Treg`, `RP`, and `NA28` include this clause (echoing Mark 5:26), whereas `WH` and Nestle 1904 omit these five words (likely from early Alexandrian witnesses such as `P75` and `B`, or homoioteleuton/scribal condensation). `bios` here means "means of subsistence / living," as in Luke 15:12 and 21:4.
- **v. 44 `κρασπέδου` (*kraspedou*):** The corner tassel (*tzitzit*) worn on a Jewish outer cloak in obedience to Num 15:38–39 and Deut 22:12.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Isaiah 6:9–10** (quoted in v. 10): Prophetic warning about seeing without perceiving and hearing without understanding, applied to responses to the kingdom parables.
- **Psalm 107:23–30** (echoed in vv. 22–25): Sailors endangered by a sudden storm cry out to the Lord, who stills the tempest and waves into calm.
- **Numbers 15:38–39 & Malachi 4:2 (3:20 MT)** (background to v. 44): The covenantal tassel (*kraspedon*) of the cloak and healing in the "wings" (*kanaph* / cloak-corners) of God's righteousness.

**1** Soon afterwards, he went about through cities and villages, preaching and bringing the good news of God’s Kingdom. With him were the twelve,

**20** Some people told him, “Your mother and your brothers stand outside, desiring to see you.”
**21** But he answered them, “My mother and my brothers are these who hear the word of God and do it.”
**22** Now on one of those days, he entered into a boat, himself and his disciples, and he said to them, “Let’s go over to the other side of the lake.” So they launched out.
**23** But as they sailed, he fell asleep. A wind storm came down on the lake, and they were taking on dangerous amounts of water.
**24** They came to him and awoke him, saying, “Master, Master, we are dying!” He awoke and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water; then they ceased, and it was calm.
**25** He said to them, “Where is your faith?” Being afraid, they marveled, saying to one another, “Who is this then, that he commands even the winds and the water, and they obey him?”
**26** Then they arrived at the country of the Gadarenes, which is opposite Galilee.
**27** When Jesus stepped ashore, a certain man out of the city who had demons for a long time met him. He wore no clothes, and didn’t live in a house, but in the tombs.
**28** When he saw Jesus, he cried out and fell down before him, and with a loud voice said, “What do I have to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I beg you, don’t torment me!”
**29** For Jesus was commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For the unclean spirit had often seized the man. He was kept under guard and bound with chains and fetters. Breaking the bonds apart, he was driven by the demon into the desert.
**30** Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” He said, “Legion,” for many demons had entered into him.
**31** They begged him that he would not command them to go into the abyss.
**32** Now there was there a herd of many pigs feeding on the mountain, and they begged him that he would allow them to enter into those. Then he allowed them.
**33** The demons came out of the man and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and were drowned.
**34** When those who fed them saw what had happened, they fled and told it in the city and in the country.
**35** People went out to see what had happened. They came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting at Jesus’ feet, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.
**36** Those who saw it told them how he who had been possessed by demons was healed.
**37** All the people of the surrounding country of the Gadarenes asked him to depart from them, for they were very much afraid. Then he entered into the boat and returned.
**38** But the man from whom the demons had gone out begged him that he might go with him, but Jesus sent him away, saying,
**39** “Return to your house, and declare what great things God has done for you.” He went his way, proclaiming throughout the whole city what great things Jesus had done for him.
**40** When Jesus returned, the multitude welcomed him, for they were all waiting for him.
**41** Behold, a man named Jairus came. He was a ruler of the synagogue. He fell down at Jesus’ feet and begged him to come into his house,
**42** for he had an only born daughter, about twelve years of age, and she was dying. But as he went, the multitudes pressed against him.
**43** A woman who had a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her living on physicians and could not be healed by any,
**44** came behind him and touched the fringe of his cloak. Immediately the flow of her blood stopped.
**45** Jesus said, “Who touched me?” When all denied it, Peter and those with him said, “Master, the multitudes press and jostle you, and you say, ‘Who touched me?’”
**46** But Jesus said, “Someone did touch me, for I perceived that power has gone out of me.”
**47** When the woman saw that she was not hidden, she came trembling; and falling down before him declared to him in the presence of all the people the reason why she had touched him, and how she was healed immediately.
**48** He said to her, “Daughter, cheer up. Your faith has made you well. Go in peace.”
**49** While he still spoke, one from the ruler of the synagogue’s house came, saying to him, “Your daughter is dead. Don’t trouble the Teacher.”
**50** But Jesus hearing it, answered him, “Don’t be afraid. Only believe, and she will be healed.”
**51** When he came to the house, he didn’t allow anyone to enter in, except Peter, John, James, the father of the child, and her mother.
**52** All were weeping and mourning her, but he said, “Don’t weep. She isn’t dead, but sleeping.”
**53** They were ridiculing him, knowing that she was dead.
**54** But he put them all outside, and taking her by the hand, he called, saying, “Child, arise!”
**55** Her spirit returned, and she rose up immediately. He commanded that something be given to her to eat.
**56** Her parents were amazed, but he commanded them to tell no one what had been done.
