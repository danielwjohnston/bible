## Clear & Faithful

**1** Jesus said to his disciples, "Temptations to stumble are sure to come, yet woe to the one through whom they arrive!
**2** It would serve him far better to have a stone millwheel hung around his neck and be cast into the sea than to trip up even one of these little ones.
**3** Watch yourselves carefully. If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him.
**4** Even if he sins against you seven times in a single day and seven times turns back to you saying, 'I repent,' you must forgive him."
**5** The apostles implored the Lord, "Add to our faith!"
**6** The Lord answered, "If you had faith the size of a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.
**7** Which of you who has a servant plowing or tending sheep will say to him when he comes in from the field, 'Come straight over and sit down to eat'?
**8** Will he not instead say to him, 'Prepare something for my supper, tie up your tunic, and serve me until I have finished eating and drinking; after that you may eat and drink'?
**9** Does he feel indebted to the servant because he carried out what was ordered?
**10** So it is with you as well: when you have done everything you were commanded, say, 'We are unworthy servants; we have only done what we ought to have done.'"
**11** Now on his way toward Jerusalem, Jesus was traveling along the border between Samaria and Galilee.
**12** As he entered a certain village, ten men afflicted with leprosy met him. Standing at a distance,
**13** they raised their voices and cried out, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"
**14** Seeing them, he instructed them, "Go and present yourselves to the priests." And as they went along, they became cleansed.
**15** One of them, seeing that he had been healed, turned back, praising God with a loud voice,
**16** and threw himself facedown at Jesus's feet, thanking him—and he was a Samaritan.
**17** Jesus responded, "Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine?
**18** Was no one found to return and give glory to God except this foreigner?"
**19** Then Jesus told him, "Stand up and continue on your journey; your trust has restored you."
**20** Asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would arrive, Jesus answered them, "The kingdom of God does not come with signs you can track by observation,
**21** nor will people announce, 'Here it is!' or 'Over there!'—because look, God's kingdom is right in your midst."
**22** He spoke to the disciples: "Days lie ahead when you will yearn to witness even a single day of the Son of Man, yet you will not see it.
**23** People will tell you, 'Look, over there!' or 'Look, right here!' Do not run off or chase after them.
**24** For just as lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one horizon to the other, so will the Son of Man be in his day.
**25** Prior to that, however, he must endure great suffering and be repudiated by this generation.
**26** Just as it happened in the days of Noah, so too will it be in the days of the Son of Man:
**27** people were eating and drinking, marrying and being given in marriage, right up to the day Noah entered the ark; then the flood came and destroyed them all.
**28** Likewise, just as it happened in the days of Lot: people were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building;
**29** yet on the very day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained down from heaven and destroyed them all.
**30** That is precisely how it will occur on the day the Son of Man is unveiled.
**31** On that day, whoever is on the rooftop with his belongings inside the house must not go down to fetch them, and whoever is out in the field must likewise not turn back.
**32** Call to mind Lot's wife!
**33** Whoever tries to preserve his life will lose it, and whoever loses his life will preserve it alive.
**34** I tell you, on that night two people will be in one bed; the one will be taken and the other left behind.
**35** Two women will be grinding grain together; the one will be taken and the other left behind."
**37** In response they asked him, "Where, Lord?" He told them, "Wherever the corpse lies, there the vultures will gather."

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus warned his disciples, "Things that trip people up are bound to happen, but watch out if you're the one causing them!
**2** You'd be better off having a giant millstone tied around your neck and getting dumped in the ocean than causing one of these little ones to stumble.
**3** So stay alert! If a brother or sister sins against you, confront them; and if they're sorry and turn around, forgive them.
**4** Even if they wrong you seven times in one day and come back seven times saying, 'I'm sorry,' you still forgive them."
**5** The apostles pleaded with the Lord, "Give us more faith!"
**6** The Lord answered, "If your faith is even as tiny as a mustard seed, you could tell this mulberry tree, 'Pull up your roots and plant yourself in the sea!'—and it would obey you.
**7** Think about it: if one of you has a servant out plowing fields or herding sheep, do you greet him when he gets back from work by saying, 'Come sit down right away and eat dinner'?
**8** No! You tell him, 'Cook my dinner, put on your apron, and wait on me while I eat and drink. You can eat and drink after I'm done.'
**9** Do you owe the servant special thanks just for doing his assigned job?
**10** It's the same with you: once you've done everything you were told to do, simply say, 'We're ordinary servants—we've only done our duty.'"
**11** As Jesus kept heading toward Jerusalem, he walked along the borderland between Samaria and Galilee.
**12** Just as he walked into a village, ten men with leprosy came toward him. Keeping their distance,
**13** they shouted out loud, "Jesus, Master, take pity on us!"
**14** Seeing them, Jesus said, "Go show yourselves to the priests." And as they were walking along, their skin was completely healed.
**15** One of them, realizing he'd been cured, turned around and came back shouting praise to God at the top of his lungs.
**16** He dropped facedown right at Jesus's feet and thanked him—and that man was a Samaritan.
**17** Jesus asked, "Didn't all ten get healed? Where are the other nine?
**18** Nobody came back to thank God except this outsider?"
**19** Then Jesus told him, "Stand up and go on your way; your trust has made you whole."
**20** When the Pharisees asked Jesus when God's kingdom was going to arrive, he replied, "God's kingdom doesn't arrive with visible signs you can track on a calendar.
**21** People won't be able to point and say, 'Look, over here!' or 'There it is!' Because look—God's kingdom is already right here among you!"
**22** Then he told his disciples, "Hard days are coming when you'll ache to see even a single day of the Son of Man, and you won't see it.
**23** People will tell you, 'Look over there!' or 'Look right here!' Don't run after them or chase those rumors.
**24** When the Son of Man appears in his day, it'll be like lightning flashing from one end of the sky to the other.
**25** Still, first he has to suffer a great deal and be rejected by this generation.
**26** Things will unfold just like they did back in Noah's time when the Son of Man arrives:
**27** people kept eating, drinking, and getting married right up to the day Noah stepped inside the ark—then the flood swept in and wiped them all out.
**28** It'll be just like Lot's time too: people were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting crops, and building houses,
**29** until the very day Lot walked out of Sodom—then fire and burning sulfur poured from the sky and destroyed everybody.
**30** That's exactly how it'll be on the day the Son of Man is unveiled.
**31** On that day, if you're up on the flat roof, don't climb down into your house to pack up your stuff; and if you're out working in the field, don't turn around to go back home.
**32** Remember what happened to Lot's wife!
**33** Anyone who tries to cling to their own life will lose it, while anyone willing to let go of it will keep it truly alive.
**34** Listen to me: on that night, two people will be sleeping in the same bed—one will be taken away, and the other left behind.
**35** Two women will be grinding flour at the same millstone—one will be taken away, and the other left behind."
**37** "Where will all this happen, Lord?" they asked. He answered, "Wherever there's a dead body, that's where the vultures circle overhead."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `σκάνδαλα` (*skandala*):** Plural of *skandalon* ("bait-stick of a trap, snare, stumbling block"), describing anything that trips up another person's faith or obedience.
- **v. 2 `λίθος μυλικός` (*lithos mylikos*):** "A millstone" (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`), whereas `RP` reads *mylos onikos* ("a donkey-turned millstone"), echoing Mark 9:42 and Matt 18:6.
- **v. 10 `δοῦλοι ἀχρεῖοι` (*douloi achreioi*):** Literally "unprofitable/unworthy slaves" (often used in self-effacing humility to mean "ordinary servants who have earned no extra merit or debt from their master").
- **v. 11 `διὰ μέσον Σαμαρείας καὶ Γαλιλαίας` (*dia meson Samareias kai Galilaias*):** Literally "through the midst of Samaria and Galilee," referring to the east–west border corridor between the two regions leading toward the Jordan valley route to Jerusalem.
- **v. 18 `ἀλλογενής` (*allogenēs*):** "Foreigner" or "one of another race/lineage" (the exact Greek term used on the Jerusalem temple warning balustrade barring non-Jews from the inner courts), highlighting the Samaritan's gratitude over the nine Israelite lepers.
- **v. 21 `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` (*entos hymōn*):** Prepositional phrase meaning either "within you" or "among you / in your midst / within your grasp"; addressed here to the Pharisees (v. 20), it points to the kingly presence already standing among them in Jesus.
- **v. 24 `ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ αὐτοῦ` (*en tē hēmera autou*):** Printed by SBLGNT, `Treg`, `NA28`, and `RP` ("in his day"); omitted by `WH`.
- **v. 36 `δύο ἐν ἀγρῷ· εἷς παραλημφθήσεται καὶ ὁ ἕτερος ἀφεθήσεται` (*dyo en agrō; heis paralēmphthēsetai kai ho heteros aphethēsetai*):** Omitted in SBLGNT (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`) and Nestle 1904; `TR` adds this verse ("Two will be in a field; the one will be taken and the other left"), likely assimilated from Matt 24:40.
- **v. 37 `ἀετοί` (*aetoi*):** Denotes either "eagles" or "vultures/carrion birds" (such as the griffon vulture), proverbial for unmistakable, visible judgment converging wherever spiritual death lies.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Leviticus 13:45–46; 14:1–32** (background to vv. 12–14): Mosaic Torah regulations requiring people with skin disease (*leprosy*) to stand apart and present themselves to priests for inspection and cleansing rites.
- **Genesis 7:11–23** (referenced in vv. 26–27): Noah's entry into the ark and the sudden onset of the global flood (*kataklysmos* in LXX Gen 6:17; 7:6).
- **Genesis 19:15–26** (referenced in vv. 28–32): Lot's flight from Sodom, the rain of fire and sulfur, and Lot's wife turning back to become a pillar of salt.
- **Job 39:30 & Habakkuk 1:8** (proverbial background to v. 37): *"Wherever the carcass lies, there the vultures gather,"* an image of swift, unmistakable judgment.
