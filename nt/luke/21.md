## Clear & Faithful

**1** Looking up, Jesus saw the wealthy people putting their gifts into the temple treasury.
**2** He also noticed a certain destitute widow dropping in two tiny copper coins,
**3** and remarked, "In truth I assure you, this destitute widow dropped in more than everyone else.
**4** For all of those people contributed to the gifts out of their surplus, whereas she, out of her poverty, put in all the livelihood she possessed."
**5** When some were remarking about the temple—how it was adorned with handsome stones and votive gifts—he said,
**6** "As for these things you are gazing at, the days will come when not one stone will be left upon another that will not be torn down."
**7** So they asked him, "Teacher, when will these things happen, and what will be the sign when they are about to take place?"
**8** He replied, "Watch out that you are not deceived; for many will come in my name, claiming, 'I am he,' and 'The time has drawn near.' Do not follow after them.
**9** And when you hear of wars and uprisings, do not panic; these things must happen first, yet the end does not follow at once."
**10** He went on to tell them, "People will rise in conflict against people, and kingdom against kingdom.
**11** There will be great earthquakes, and in various places famines and plagues, along with terrifying sights and mighty signs from heaven.
**12** Yet before all these events, they will seize you and persecute you, handing you over to synagogues and prisons, bringing you before kings and governors because of my name.
**13** This will turn out for you as an opportunity for testimony.
**14** Settle it firmly in your hearts, then, not to rehearse beforehand how to defend yourselves;
**15** for I will give you speech and wisdom that none of your adversaries will be able to withstand or contradict.
**16** You will even be betrayed by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and some of you they will put to death;
**17** and you will be hated by everyone on account of my name.
**18** Even so, not a single hair upon your head will ever be lost.
**19** Through your steadfast perseverance you will preserve your souls.
**20** When you see Jerusalem surrounded by encamped armies, know then that her desolation has come near.
**21** Then those in Judea must flee to the mountains, those inside the city must get out, and those in the countryside must not enter her;
**22** for these are the days of vengeance, to fulfill everything that has been written.
**23** Alas for pregnant women and nursing mothers during those days! Terrible distress will settle over the land, and fierce wrath against this people.
**24** Multitudes will fall by the sword's edge and be marched off as prisoners into all nations, while Jerusalem lies trampled underfoot by Gentiles until the appointed eras of the Gentiles run their course.
**25** There will be signs in sun, moon, and stars, and on earth distress among the nations in bewilderment at the roaring of the sea and its surging waves,
**26** with people fainting from dread and apprehension of what is coming upon the inhabited world; for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.
**27** Then people will witness the Son of Man arriving in a cloud with mighty power and splendor.
**28** As soon as these events start occurring, stand erect and raise your heads, since your deliverance is approaching."
**29** He shared a parable with them: "Observe the fig tree and every other tree.
**30** As soon as they sprout leaves, you can see for yourselves and recognize that summer is already near.
**31** So too, when you see these things taking place, know that the kingdom of God is near.
**32** Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away until everything has taken place.
**33** Sky and earth will pass away, yet my words will never vanish.
**34** Guard yourselves carefully, lest your hearts grow sluggish with dissipation, drunkenness, and the anxieties of daily life, and that day spring upon you suddenly
**35** like a trap; for it will come upon everyone living across the face of the whole earth.
**36** Stay alert at every season, praying that you may have strength to escape all these things that are about to take place and to stand before the Son of Man."
**37** Each day he was teaching in the temple, and each night he would go out and stay on the hill called the Mount of Olives;
**38** and early every morning all the people came to him in the temple to listen to him.

## Everyday English

**1** As Jesus looked up, he watched rich people dropping their donations into the temple offering boxes.
**2** Then he spotted a poor widow walking up and dropping in just two tiny copper coins worth next to nothing.
**3** Jesus said, "I'm telling you the honest truth: this poor widow gave more than every single one of them!
**4** All those wealthy donors gave out of spare change they wouldn't even miss, while out of her poverty she gave every penny she had to live on."
**5** When some of his followers started admiring the temple—how magnificent the stonework and donated treasures looked—Jesus told them,
**6** "Take a good look at all this: the time is coming when not a single stone here will remain stacked on another—every last one will be knocked down!"
**7** They asked him, "Teacher, when will this happen? And what warning sign will show us it's about to begin?"
**8** "Be careful you aren't fooled," Jesus warned. "Plenty of impostors will show up using my name, claiming, 'I'm the one!' and 'The time has come!' Don't follow them.
**9** When you hear news of wars and violent revolts, don't be terrified. Those things have to happen first, yet the end won't arrive immediately."
**10** He went on to tell them, "One nation will attack another, and kingdoms will clash with kingdoms.
**11** Massive earthquakes will strike, along with famines and deadly plagues in place after place, plus terrifying sights and huge signs in the sky.
**12** But even before any of that happens, people will arrest you and persecute you. They'll drag you into synagogues, throw you in jail, and haul you before kings and governors because of my name.
**13** That will become your stage to testify about me!
**14** So make up your minds ahead of time not to stress over preparing your defense speech,
**15** because I myself will give you words and wisdom that none of your opponents will be able to refute or shout down.
**16** Even your own parents, brothers, relatives, and close friends will turn you in, and some of you will be executed.
**17** Everybody will hate you because you bear my name—
**18** yet not a single hair on your head will truly be lost!
**19** By holding steady under pressure, you'll keep your lives.
**20** Whenever you see Jerusalem ringed by enemy armies, recognize that its destruction is right around the corner.
**21** At that moment, anyone in Judea needs to run for the hills; anyone inside Jerusalem needs to evacuate; and anyone out in the countryside must stay out of the city!
**22** Because those are the days of judgment when everything Scripture predicted comes true.
**23** How terrible it will be for pregnant women and nursing mothers in those days! Severe hardship will fall on the land and fierce anger on this people.
**24** Many will be cut down by the sword or marched away as prisoners to every nation on earth, and Jerusalem will be trampled under foreign boots until the era of the nations runs its course.
**25** Then strange signs will appear in the sun, moon, and stars. Here on earth, nations will panic in helplessness as the oceans roar and heave,
**26** and people will collapse from sheer terror over what's hitting the world—because the very powers of the skies will be shaken loose!
**27** Right then, everyone will see the Son of Man arriving on a cloud with awesome power and dazzling glory.
**28** So when you see these things starting to unfold, stand tall and lift your heads high—your rescue is right at the door!"
**29** Then Jesus gave them a picture: "Watch the fig tree and every other tree.
**30** As soon as they sprout fresh buds, you don't need anyone to tell you—you know summer is almost here.
**31** In the exact same way, when you see all this happening, you can be sure God's kingdom is right at hand.
**32** Listen carefully: this generation won't vanish before every bit of this takes place.
**33** Sky and earth will pass away, but my words will never fade or fail.
**34** Watch yourselves closely! Don't let your hearts get dulled by partying, heavy drinking, and everyday worries—otherwise that day will snap shut on you without warning
**35** like a hidden trap. For it's going to hit everyone living across the entire world.
**36** Stay wide awake at all times, praying for strength to make it safely through everything about to happen and to stand tall in front of the Son of Man."
**37** Every day Jesus taught inside the temple, and each evening he walked out to spend the night on the Mount of Olives;
**38** and at first light each morning, the whole crowd flocked back to the temple to listen to him.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `λεπτὰ δύο` (*lepta dyo*):** Two *lepta* (plural of *lepton*, "thin/peeled"), the smallest bronze Judean coin in circulation (worth roughly 1/64th of a daily laborer's denarius), yet representing "all the living (*bion*) she had" (v. 4).
- **v. 5 `ἀναθήμασιν` (*anathēmasin*):** Dedicatory votive offerings (such as Herod's golden vine and royal spoils) hung in the temple precincts, distinct from the related noun *anathema* ("cursed thing").
- **v. 11 `λιμοὶ καὶ λοιμοὶ` (*limoi kai loimoi*):** Classical Greek paronomasia (sound-play) pairing "famines" (*limoi*) with "pestilences/plagues" (*loimoi*); `NA28` and `RP` put *limoi kai loimoi*, while `WH` and `Treg` reverse to *loimoi kai limoi*.
- **v. 19 `κτήσασθε` (*ktēsasthe*):** Aorist middle imperative (`NA28`, `RP`: "gain/win your lives by endurance"), whereas `WH` and `Treg` read future indicative *ktēsesthe* ("you will gain").
- **v. 24 `καιροὶ ἐθνῶν` (*kairoi ethnōn*):** "Appointed seasons/times of the nations," framing the post-70 CE trampling of Jerusalem within a bounded divine timetable (`WH` appends *kai esontai*, linking smoothly into v. 25).
- **v. 28 `ἀπολύτρωσις` (*apolytrōsis*):** "Redemption / release upon ransom," used in Luke only here to describe the cosmic liberation accompanying the Son of Man's arrival.
- **v. 34 `κραιπάλῃ` (*kraipalē*):** Medical/symposium term (unique in the NT) for the headache, stupor, and hangover resulting from overindulgence.
- **v. 36 `κατισχύσητε` (*katischysēte*):** "Prevail / have sufficient strength" (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`), whereas `RP` reads *kataxiōthēte* ("be counted worthy," cf. Luke 20:35).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Jeremiah 26:6–18 & Micah 3:12** (background to v. 6): Prophetic warnings that the Jerusalem temple itself would be torn down like Shiloh if Israel persisted in injustice.
- **Isaiah 19:2 & 2 Chronicles 15:6** (echoed in v. 10): *"People will clash against people, and realm against realm"* as upheaval precedes divine intervention.
- **Daniel 7:13–14 & 12:1** (alluded to in vv. 22, 27): The Son of Man coming on the clouds with power and glory alongside a time of unprecedented distress.
- **Isaiah 13:10; 34:4 & Joel 2:30–31** (echoed in vv. 25–26): Cosmic portents in sun, moon, stars, and roaring seas signaling the Day of the Lord.
- **Isaiah 24:17–18** (echoed in vv. 34–35): *"Dread, pitfall, and trap await those dwelling on the earth"*—the sudden global net from which vigilance and prayer seek deliverance.
