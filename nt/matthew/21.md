## Clear & Faithful

**1** As they drew near to Jerusalem and came to Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples ahead,
**2** telling them, "Head into the village across from you; right away you will spot a tethered donkey with her colt beside her. Untie them and bring both to me.
**3** If anyone says anything to you, tell them, 'The Lord needs them,' and he will send them right away."
**4** This happened so that what was spoken through the prophet would be fulfilled:
**5** "Tell the daughter of Zion, 'Look, your king is coming to you, humble and mounted on a donkey, and on a colt, the foal of a pack animal.'"
**6** The disciples went and did as Jesus had directed them.
**7** They led back the donkey and the colt, placed their outer garments upon them, and Jesus sat on top.
**8** A very large crowd spread their cloaks along the road, while others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.
**9** Both the crowds walking in front of him and those trailing behind shouted aloud, "Hosanna to David's Son! Blessed is the one arriving in the Lord's name! Hosanna in the highest heavens!"
**10** When he entered Jerusalem, the whole city was stirred up, asking, "Who is this?"
**11** And the crowds kept answering, "This is Jesus the prophet, from Nazareth in Galilee."
**12** Jesus entered the temple courts and drove out all who were buying and selling in the temple area; he overturned the tables of the money changers and the benches of those selling doves.
**13** He told them, "It is written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer,' yet you are turning it into a den of bandits."
**14** Blind and lame people approached him in the temple courts, and he healed them.
**15** When the chief priests and the scribes saw the marvelous things he did, and the children shouting in the temple courts, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" they grew indignant
**16** and asked him, "Do you hear what these children are saying?" Jesus replied to them, "Yes. Have you never read, 'Out of the mouth of infants and nursing babies you have prepared praise'?"
**17** Leaving them behind, he went outside the city to Bethany and spent the night there.
**18** Early in the morning, as he was returning to the city, he grew hungry.
**19** Seeing a solitary fig tree beside the road, he walked up to it and found nothing on it except leaves. He said to it, "May fruit never come from you again!" And instantly the fig tree withered.
**20** When the disciples saw it, they marveled and asked, "How did the fig tree wither so instantaneously?"
**21** Jesus replied to them, "Truly I assure you, if you possess faith without wavering, you will accomplish not merely what happened to the fig tree—even if you command this mountain, 'Be lifted up and hurled into the sea,' it will take place.
**22** Whatever you ask for in prayer, believing, you will receive."
**23** After he entered the temple courts, while he was teaching, the chief priests and elders of the people approached him and asked, "By what authority are you doing these things, and who gave you this authority?"
**24** Jesus answered them, "I will also ask you one question; if you answer me, I too will tell you by what authority I do these things.
**25** Where did John's baptism come from—from heaven, or from human beings?" They discussed this among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will ask us, 'Why then did you not believe him?'
**26** Yet if we say, 'From human beings,' we fear the crowd, because everyone regards John as a prophet."
**27** So they answered Jesus, "We do not know." He said to them in turn, "Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.
**28** Now what do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first and said, 'Son, go work in the vineyard today.'
**29** He answered, 'I do not want to,' yet afterward he changed his mind and went.
**30** Then he went to the second son and said the same thing. He answered, 'I am going, sir,' yet did not go.
**31** Which of the two did his father's will?" They said, "The first." Jesus told them, "Truly I tell you, the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering the kingdom of God ahead of you.
**32** For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, whereas the tax collectors and prostitutes believed him. Even when you saw this, you did not afterward change your minds and believe him.
**33** Listen to another parable: There was a landowner who planted a vineyard, put a fence around it, dug a winepress in it, built a watchtower, leased it to tenant farmers, and went away on a journey.
**34** When the harvest season drew near, he sent his servants to the tenants to collect his share of the fruit.
**35** The tenants seized his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned a third.
**36** Once more he dispatched additional servants—a larger group than before—and the tenants treated them identically.
**37** Finally he sent his own son to them, thinking, 'They will respect my son.'
**38** Yet when the tenants saw the son, they said among themselves, 'This is the heir! Come, let us kill him and seize his inheritance.'
**39** So they seized him, threw him out of the vineyard, and killed him.
**40** When the owner of the vineyard arrives, what will he do to those tenants?"
**41** They replied to him, "He will bring those wretches to a wretched end, and lease the vineyard to other tenants who will deliver his fruit to him at the proper seasons."
**42** Jesus asked them, "Have you never read in the Scriptures: 'The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone; this came from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes'?
**43** For this reason I tell you, the kingdom of God will be taken away from you and given to a nation that produces its fruit.
**44** And whoever falls on this stone will be shattered; and anyone on whom it falls will be crushed to dust."
**45** Hearing his parables, the chief priests and Pharisees recognized that he was talking directly about them.
**46** Although they sought to arrest him, they were afraid of the crowds, because the people regarded him as a prophet.

## Everyday English

**1** As Jesus and his followers approached Jerusalem and reached Bethphage near the Mount of Olives, he sent two disciples on ahead
**2** with these instructions: "Walk into the village right across from you. As soon as you enter, you'll spot a donkey tied up with her colt beside her. Untie them and bring them to me.
**3** If anyone asks what you're doing, simply say, 'The Lord needs them,' and he'll let them go immediately."
**4** Everything happened this way to fulfill the prophet's words:
**5** "Announce to Daughter Zion, 'Look! Your king is arriving—gentle, riding on a donkey, on a young colt, the foal of a pack animal.'"
**6** The two disciples went off and followed Jesus' instructions to the letter.
**7** They brought back the donkey and the colt, draped their coats across them, and Jesus mounted and rode on.
**8** A massive crowd laid their cloaks down like a carpet on the road, while others chopped down tree branches and scattered them across his path.
**9** Both the crowds marching in front and those trailing behind cheered loudly, "Hosanna to the Son of David! God bless the one coming in the Lord's name! Hosanna in the highest heavens!"
**10** When Jesus rode into Jerusalem, the entire city was buzzing with excitement. "Who is this man?" people asked.
**11** And the crowds answered, "This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth up in Galilee!"
**12** Jesus walked straight into the temple courtyard and cleared out everyone buying and selling there. He flipped over the tables of the money exchangers and knocked over the stalls of the dove merchants.
**13** He called out to them, "Scripture says, 'My house will be known as a house of prayer,' yet you've turned it into a hideout for thieves!"
**14** People who were blind and lame came to him right there in the temple courtyard, and he healed them.
**15** When the chief priests and legal scholars witnessed the astonishing miracles he performed—and heard children shouting through the temple courts, "Hosanna to the Son of David!"—they were furious.
**16** "Do you hear what these kids are shouting?" they demanded. "Yes," Jesus answered. "Haven't you ever read the verse, 'Out of the mouths of little children and nursing babies you have brought forth praise'?"
**17** Then he left them, walked out of the city to Bethany, and stayed there for the night.
**18** Early the next morning, as Jesus walked back toward Jerusalem, he felt hungry.
**19** Spotting a single fig tree along the roadside, he walked over to check it, only to find nothing on it but leaves. He said to the tree, "May you never produce fruit again!" Instantly, the fig tree dried up.
**20** When the disciples saw it, their jaws dropped: "How did that fig tree shrivel up so fast?"
**21** Jesus told them, "Believe me, if you genuinely trust God without doubting, you won't only do what happened to this fig tree—you could even tell this mountain, 'Lift up and drop into the sea,' and it would happen!
**22** Anything you pray for with genuine trust, you'll receive."
**23** Back in the temple courtyard, while Jesus was teaching, the chief priests and Jewish elders walked up to challenge him: "What gives you the right to act like this? Who gave you that authority?"
**24** Jesus replied, "Let me ask you one question first. Answer me, and I'll tell you what authority backs up what I'm doing.
**25** Where did John get his authority to baptize—from heaven, or just from people?" They huddled together debating: "If we answer, 'From heaven,' he'll ask, 'Then why didn't you believe him?'
**26** But if we say, 'Just from people,' the crowd might turn on us, since everyone around here considers John a prophet."
**27** So they answered Jesus, "We don't know." "In that case," Jesus said, "I won't tell you what authority backs up what I do either."
**28** "Tell me what you think about this story," he continued. "A man had two sons. He went to the older one and said, 'Son, go work in the vineyard today.'
**29** 'No, I don't want to,' the son answered. Later on, though, he felt sorry about it and went anyway.
**30** Then the father went to the second son and gave the same order. 'Sure thing, sir!' he answered—yet he never went.
**31** Which of the two boys actually did what his father wanted?" "The first one," they replied. Jesus told them, "Listen closely: tax collectors and prostitutes are walking into God's kingdom ahead of you!
**32** When John came showing you the right way to live, you refused to believe him—yet the tax collectors and prostitutes did believe him. And even after seeing that, you still refused to change your minds and trust him.
**33** Listen to another story: A landowner planted a vineyard, built a wall around it, carved out a winepress, and put up a watchtower. Then he rented it out to tenant farmers and left on a long trip.
**34** When harvest time came around, he sent his servants to the farmers to collect his share of the grapes.
**35** Instead, the farmers grabbed his servants—they beat one, murdered another, and pelted a third with stones.
**36** So the owner sent another group of servants, even larger than the first, and the farmers treated them the exact same way.
**37** Finally, he sent his own son, thinking, 'At least they'll respect my son.'
**38** But when the farmers saw the son coming, they plotted together, 'Here comes the heir! Let's murder him and take the property for ourselves!'
**39** So they grabbed him, dragged him outside the vineyard, and killed him.
**40** Now, when the vineyard owner comes back, what's he going to do to those farmers?"
**41** They answered, "He'll wipe out those wicked men without mercy and lease the vineyard to new tenants who'll hand over the harvest right on time."
**42** Jesus asked them, "Have you never read this verse in Scripture? 'The stone that the builders tossed aside has become the most important stone of all; the Lord himself did this, and it's astonishing to see.'
**43** That's why I'm telling you: God's kingdom will be taken away from you and handed over to a people who actually produce its harvest.
**44** Anyone who stumbles over this stone will be broken to pieces—and anyone it lands on will be ground to powder."
**45** When the chief priests and Pharisees heard his parables, it hit home that he was talking directly about them.
**46** They wanted to arrest him on the spot, yet they were too afraid of the crowds, who looked up to Jesus as a prophet.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 9 `Ὡσαννά` (*Hōsanna*):** Greek transliteration of Hebrew/Aramaic *hosha na* ("Save, we pray!" from Ps 118:25), which had become a liturgical acclamation of praise and messianic welcome.
- **v. 13 `σπήλαιον λῃστῶν` (*spēlaion lēstōn*):** *Lēstēs* means "robber, bandit, or insurrectionist" (not a petty pickpocket), echoing Jeremiah 7:11's indictment of treating the temple as a refuge for lawbreakers.
- **v. 29–31 `Ὁ πρῶτος` (*Ho prōtos*):** SBLGNT, NA28, and RP place the son who initially refuses at v. 29 and the son who verbally agrees at v. 30, with the answer `Ὁ πρῶτος` ("The first") in v. 31; WH (and Treg at v. 31) reverses the sequence so the verbally compliant son comes first and the answer is `Ὁ ὕστερος` ("The latter").
- **v. 44 `Καὶ ὁ πεσὼν ἐπὶ τὸν λίθον τοῦτον συνθλασθήσεται· ἐφʼ ὃν δʼ ἂν πέσῃ λικμήσει αὐτόν` (*Kai ho pesōn epi ton lithon touton synthlasthēsetai; eph' hon d' an pesē likmēsei auton*):** Present in SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28, and RP, while noted as omitted in `WHmarg` (absent from Western witnesses such as `D` and `33`, and absent from the parallel in Mark 12 / Luke 20:18 retains it). `λικμήσει` (*likmēsei*) means literally "winnow/scatter like chaff," hence "crush to dust."

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11** (combined in v. 5): *"Proclaim to Daughter Zion..."* (Isa 62:11) joined with *"Look, your royal king is approaching you—humble, seated upon a donkey, upon a young foal of a pack animal"* (Zech 9:9).
- **Psalm 118:25–26** (quoted in v. 9): *"Save us now, O Lord!... Blessed be the one arriving in the Lord's name!"*
- **Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11** (combined in v. 13): *"My temple will be known as a house of prayer for every nation"* contrasted with *"Has this temple... become a hideout for robbers in your sight?"*
- **Psalm 8:2** (quoted from LXX in v. 16): *"From the lips of small children and nursing infants you have established praise."*
- **Isaiah 5:1–7** (background to v. 33): The Song of the Vineyard—fence, winepress, watchtower—and the owner's expectation of fruit.
- **Psalm 118:22–23; Isaiah 8:14–15; Daniel 2:34–35, 44** (quoted/alluded to in vv. 42, 44): The rejected cornerstone and the stone that shatters and crushes to dust.
