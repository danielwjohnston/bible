## Clear & Faithful

**1** Now after Jesus had been born in Bethlehem of Judea during King Herod's reign, magi from eastern lands arrived in Jerusalem,
**2** asking, "Where is the newborn king of the Jews? For we saw his star at its rising and have come to pay him homage."
**3** When King Herod heard this, he was deeply disturbed, and all Jerusalem with him.
**4** Gathering all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he inquired of them where the Messiah was to be born.
**5** They told him, "In Bethlehem of Judea; for so it stands written by the prophet:
**6** 'And you, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd my people Israel.'"
**7** Then Herod secretly summoned the wise men and determined from them the exact time when the star had appeared.
**8** Sending them to Bethlehem, he said, "Go and search diligently for the child; and when you find him, report back to me so that I too may come and pay him homage."
**9** Having heard the king out, they went on their way; and suddenly the star they had observed at its rising moved ahead of them until it arrived and stood right above where the child was.
**10** When they saw the star, they were filled with overwhelming joy.
**11** Entering the house, they saw the child with Mary his mother, and they fell down and paid him homage. Opening their treasure boxes, they presented him with gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
**12** Being warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they departed for their own country by another route.
**13** Once they had gone, an angel of the Lord suddenly appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, "Rise, take the child together with his mother, and escape into Egypt; stay there until I give you word, for Herod is intending to hunt down the child and destroy him."
**14** So he got up, took the child and his mother by night, and departed for Egypt,
**15** remaining there until Herod's death—so that what the Lord had spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled: "Out of Egypt I called my son."
**16** Then Herod, realizing that he had been outwitted by the wise men, flew into a rage and sent soldiers to kill all the male children in Bethlehem and throughout its surrounding region who were two years old and under, matching the time he had carefully learned from the wise men.
**17** Then what had been spoken through the prophet Jeremiah was fulfilled:
**18** "A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and bitter lamentation—Rachel weeping for her children, and refusing to be comforted, because they are no more."
**19** After Herod's death, an angel of the Lord suddenly appeared in a dream to Joseph there in Egypt,
**20** saying, "Get up, take the child and his mother, and go to the land of Israel, for those who sought the child's life are dead."
**21** So he got up, took the child and his mother, and entered the land of Israel.
**22** However, learning that Archelaus was reigning over Judea as successor to his father Herod, he was afraid to travel there; and after receiving a warning in a dream, he turned aside into the region of Galilee.
**23** There he went and settled in a town called Nazareth, so that what had been spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled: "He shall be called a Nazarene."

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus was born in the Judean town of Bethlehem during the reign of King Herod. Soon afterward, scholarly stargazers from the east showed up in Jerusalem,
**2** asking around, "Where is the baby born to be king of the Jews? We spotted his star rising in the east and traveled here to bow before him."
**3** When King Herod got word of this, he panicked—and the whole city of Jerusalem panicked right along with him.
**4** He called a meeting of all the chief priests and experts in the law to ask them where the Messiah was supposed to be born.
**5** "In Bethlehem in Judea," they answered him, "because the prophet wrote:
**6** 'You, Bethlehem in Judah's territory, aren't the smallest of Judah's leading towns at all! A leader will step out from you to guide and care for my people Israel.'"
**7** Herod quietly invited those eastern scholars in to pin down the exact date the star had first become visible.
**8** Then he sent them off to Bethlehem with these instructions: "Go track down every detail about the child. As soon as you locate him, let me know so I can go honor him myself."
**9** Once they heard the king out, they left. Sure enough, the same star they'd noticed earlier moved out in front of them until it hovered right above the spot where the boy was.
**10** Seeing the star again filled them with pure delight.
**11** They stepped inside the house, saw the young child together with his mother Mary, dropped to their knees, and bowed in reverence. Then they opened up their travel chests and offered him gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
**12** Warned in a dream not to report back to Herod, they took a different road home to their own land.
**13** Right after they left, an angel of the Lord came to Joseph in a dream and urged him, "Wake up! Take the child and his mother and run away to Egypt. Stay put until I let you know it's safe, because Herod is going to hunt this child down to kill him."
**14** Joseph got straight up in the middle of the night, gathered the boy and his mother, and escaped to Egypt.
**15** They stayed there until Herod died. That fulfilled what the Lord had declared through his prophet: "I called my son out of Egypt."
**16** Once Herod figured out that the eastern visitors had tricked him, he went ballistic. He ordered his men to slaughter every boy two years old and younger in Bethlehem and its nearby villages, based on the timeline he'd gotten from the scholars.
**17** That terrible day fulfilled what the prophet Jeremiah had written:
**18** "A cry echoed in Ramah—loud sobbing and grief. Rachel is crying over her children and won't let anyone comfort her, because they're gone."
**19** After Herod's death, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream while he was still in Egypt.
**20** "Get up," the angel said, "take the child and his mother back to the land of Israel, because the people trying to murder him are dead."
**21** So Joseph got up, took the boy and his mother, and traveled back into Israel.
**22** But when he learned that Archelaus had taken over Judea from his father Herod, Joseph was scared to settle there. Warned again in a dream, he detoured north into the region of Galilee
**23** and moved into a village named Nazareth. That way, what the prophets had spoken came true: "He will be known as a Nazarene."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `μάγοι` (*magoi*):** Originally Persian/Median priestly astrologers and court scholars skilled in interpreting celestial phenomena and dreams; rendered traditionally as "wise men" or "magi."
- **v. 2 `ἐν τῇ ἀνατολῇ` (*en tē anatolē*):** Singular *anatolē* means "the rising" (of a star/sun) or "the east," contrasting with the plural *apo anatolōn* ("from eastern lands") in v. 1.
- **v. 2 `προσκυνῆσαι` (*proskynēsai*):** From *proskyneō* ("to prostrate oneself," "do homage," or "worship"), used both for royal reverence before a king and religious worship before God.
- **v. 12 `χρηματισθέντες` (*chrēmatisthentes*):** Passive participle of *chrēmatizō*, meaning "having received a divine oracle/warning," used again in v. 22.
- **v. 18 `ἠκούσθη` (*ēkousthē*):** SBLGNT (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`) reads "a voice was heard, weeping and great lamentation"; `RP` adds *thrēnos kai* ("dirge/wailing and") in alignment with the LXX of Jer 31:15 (38:15 LXX).
- **v. 23 `Ναζωραῖος` (*Nazōraios*):** Greek gentilic title ("Nazarene"), echoing both the humble town of Nazareth and prophetic wordplay on Hebrew *netzer* ("shoot/branch," Isa 11:1) or *nazir* ("consecrated one").

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, behold, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, saying,
**2** “Where is he who is born King of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east, and have come to worship him.”
**3** When King Herod heard it, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.
**4** Gathering together all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he asked them where the Christ would be born.
**5** They said to him, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is written through the prophet,
**6** ‘You Bethlehem, land of Judah, are in no way least among the princes of Judah; for out of you shall come a governor who shall shepherd my people, Israel.’”
**7** Then Herod secretly called the wise men, and learned from them exactly what time the star appeared.
**8** He sent them to Bethlehem, and said, “Go and search diligently for the young child. When you have found him, bring me word, so that I also may come and worship him.”
**9** They, having heard the king, went their way; and behold, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them until it came and stood over where the young child was.
**10** When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.
**11** They came into the house and saw the young child with Mary, his mother, and they fell down and worshiped him. Opening their treasures, they offered to him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
**12** Being warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went back to their own country another way.
**13** Now when they had departed, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, “Arise and take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and stay there until I tell you, for Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.”
**14** He arose and took the young child and his mother by night and departed into Egypt,
**15** and was there until the death of Herod, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying, “Out of Egypt I called my son.”
**16** Then Herod, when he saw that he was mocked by the wise men, was exceedingly angry, and sent out and killed all the male children who were in Bethlehem and in all the surrounding countryside, from two years old and under, according to the exact time which he had learned from the wise men.
**17** Then that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying,
**18** “A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, weeping and great mourning, Rachel weeping for her children; she wouldn’t be comforted, because they are no more.”
**19** But when Herod was dead, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, saying,
**20** “Arise and take the young child and his mother, and go into the land of Israel, for those who sought the young child’s life are dead.”
**21** He arose and took the young child and his mother, and came into the land of Israel.
**22** But when he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in the place of his father, Herod, he was afraid to go there. Being warned in a dream, he withdrew into the region of Galilee,
**23** and came and lived in a city called Nazareth; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophets that he will be called a Nazarene.
