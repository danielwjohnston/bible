## Clear & Faithful

**1** At that moment Jesus addressed the crowds and his disciples,
**2** saying: "The scribes and the Pharisees have seated themselves on Moses' chair.
**3** Therefore do and observe everything they tell you, yet do not act according to their deeds—for they speak, and do not act.
**4** They tie up heavy burdens and pile them onto people's shoulders, yet they themselves are unwilling to lift a finger to move them.
**5** Everything they do is done to be noticed by people: they widen their phylacteries and lengthen their tassels;
**6** they love the place of honor at banquets and the front seats in the synagogues,
**7** and respectful greetings in the marketplaces, and being addressed by people as 'Rabbi.'
**8** Yet you are not to be called 'Rabbi,' for you have one Teacher, and you are all brothers.
**9** Nor are you to call anyone on earth your 'father,' for you have one Father, who is in heaven.
**10** Neither are you to be called 'instructors,' because you have one Instructor, the Messiah.
**11** Rather, the greatest among you shall be your servant.
**12** Anyone who elevates himself will be brought low, and anyone who lowers himself will be raised high.
**13** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you slam shut the kingdom of heaven in people's faces; for neither do you enter yourselves, nor do you allow those who are trying to enter to go in.
**15** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you travel across sea and dry land to make a single convert, and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a child of Gehenna as yourselves.
**16** Woe to you, blind guides, who say, 'If anyone swears by the sanctuary, it is nothing; yet if anyone swears by the gold of the sanctuary, he is bound by his oath.'
**17** Foolish and blind! Which is greater—the gold, or the sanctuary that made the gold sacred?
**18** And you say, 'If anyone swears by the altar, it is nothing; yet if anyone swears by the offering on it, he is bound by his oath.'
**19** Blind fools! Which is greater—the offering, or the altar that makes the offering sacred?
**20** So anyone swearing by the altar swears by the altar itself and everything resting upon it;
**21** and whoever swears by the sanctuary swears by it and by the one who dwells in it;
**22** and anyone swearing by heaven swears by God's throne and by the One seated upon it.
**23** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you tithe mint, dill, and cumin, yet have neglected the weightier provisions of the law—justice, mercy, and faithfulness. These you ought to have practiced, without neglecting the others.
**24** Blind guides, who strain out the gnat yet swallow the camel whole!
**25** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you scrub the outside of the cup and the dish, while inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence.
**26** Blind Pharisee! Cleanse the inside of the cup and the dish first, so that its outside may become clean as well.
**27** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you resemble whitewashed tombs, which look attractive on the outside, yet inside are packed with dead bones and every kind of impurity.
**28** In the same way, on the outside you appear righteous to people, yet inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.
**29** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you construct tombs for the prophets and adorn the memorials of righteous people,
**30** and say, 'If we had lived in the days of our ancestors, we would not have shared with them in shedding the blood of the prophets.'
**31** Thus you testify against yourselves that you are descendants of those who murdered the prophets.
**32** Fill up, then, the measure of your ancestors' guilt!
**33** Serpents! Brood of vipers! How can you escape the judgment of Gehenna?
**34** Therefore look: I am sending to you prophets, sages, and scribes; some of them you will kill and crucify, and others you will flog in your synagogues and pursue from town to town,
**35** so that all righteous blood shed upon the earth will fall on you, beginning with the blood of righteous Abel all the way to the blood of Zechariah, Barachiah's son, whom you slew between the temple sanctuary and the altar.
**36** Truly I assure you, every bit of this will fall upon this very generation.
**37** Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your children together, just as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you refused!
**38** See, your temple-house is abandoned to you, empty and desolate.
**39** For I tell you, you will never see me again until you say, 'Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.'"

## Everyday English

**1** Next, Jesus turned to the crowds and his disciples
**2** and said: "The legal scholars and Pharisees sit in Moses' seat of authority.
**3** So practice and obey everything they instruct you to do—just don't imitate how they live, because they preach one thing and do another.
**4** They bundle up crushing rules and load them onto people's backs, yet they won't lift even a fingertip to help carry them.
**5** Everything they do is a show to impress onlookers—they make their Scripture boxes extra wide and the tassels on their robes extra long.
**6** They crave the head seats at dinner parties and the front benches in the synagogues,
**7** along with respectful bows in the public squares and hearing people call them 'Rabbi.'
**8** Don't ever let people call you 'Rabbi,' because you have only one Teacher, and all of you are equals as brothers and sisters.
**9** Don't call anyone on earth your spiritual 'Father,' because you have only one Father, who is in heaven.
**10** And don't let anyone call you 'Master Instructor,' because you have only one Instructor—the Messiah.
**11** Whoever is greatest among you must act as your servant.
**12** Anyone who promotes themselves will be brought down low, while anyone who humbles themselves will be lifted up.
**13** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You slam the door to heaven's kingdom right in people's faces. You won't step inside yourselves, and you block anyone else who tries to enter!
**15** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You cross oceans and continents just to win a single convert, and once you've won them, you turn them into someone twice as fit for Gehenna as yourselves!
**16** How terrible it will be for you blind guides! You argue, 'If someone swears an oath by the temple building, it doesn't count; but if they swear by the gold inside the temple, the oath is binding.'
**17** How foolish and blind can you get? Which matters more—the gold, or the temple that makes that gold holy?
**18** Then you argue, 'If someone swears by the altar, it doesn't count; but if they swear by the sacrifice sitting on it, the oath is binding.'
**19** You're completely blind! Which matters more—the offering, or the altar that makes that offering sacred?
**20** Whenever someone swears by the altar, they're swearing by the altar and everything resting on it;
**21** whenever someone swears by the temple, they're swearing by the temple and by God who lives inside it;
**22** and whenever someone swears by heaven, they're swearing by God's throne and by the One seated on it!
**23** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You carefully measure out a tenth of your garden spices—mint, dill, and cumin—while ignoring the things in God's Law that actually carry weight: justice, mercy, and faithfulness. You should have practiced these first, without neglecting the smaller duties.
**24** You blind guides! You carefully strain a tiny gnat out of your drink, yet gulp down a whole camel!
**25** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You polish the outside of the cup and the plate, while the inside overflows with greed and self-indulgence.
**26** You blind Pharisee! Wash the inside of the cup and plate first—then the outside will be genuinely clean too.
**27** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You're just like whitewashed graves—spotless and pretty on the outside, yet packed full of rotting bones and filth on the inside.
**28** That's exactly how you are: on the surface you look upright to everyone, while inside you're bursting with hypocrisy and lawbreaking.
**29** How terrible it will be for you, legal scholars and Pharisees—you frauds! You build fancy tombs for the prophets and decorate memorials for godly people,
**30** claiming, 'If we'd lived back in our ancestors' day, we never would have joined them in murdering the prophets!'
**31** By saying that, you're admitting straight out that you're the children of those who killed the prophets.
**32** Go ahead, then—finish off the dirty work your ancestors started!
**33** You snakes! You nest of vipers! How do you think you'll escape the sentence of Gehenna?
**34** Watch what happens: I'm sending you prophets, wise teachers, and scholars—some you'll execute and crucify, others you'll whip inside your synagogues and chase from town to town.
**35** As a result, all the innocent blood ever spilled on earth will fall on your heads, from righteous Abel all the way down to Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you murdered right between the sanctuary and the altar.
**36** Mark my words: every bit of this will fall on this very generation.
**37** O Jerusalem, Jerusalem—city that murders the prophets and stones the messengers sent to her! How many times I longed to gather your children close to me, like a mother hen tucking her chicks safely under her wings—and you wouldn't let me!
**38** Look around you: your temple is left abandoned and empty.
**39** I promise you, you won't see me again until the day you cry out, 'God bless the one coming in the Lord's name!'"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `τῆς Μωϋσέως καθέδρας` (*tēs Mōyseōs kathedras*):** "The seat/chair of Moses," referring to the authoritative teaching seat in the synagogue from which Torah was expounded.
- **v. 5 `φυλακτήρια … κράσπεδα` (*phylaktēria … kraspeda*):** *Phylaktēria* (lit. "safeguards," corresponding to *tefillin*, small leather boxes with Torah scrolls worn on forehead and arm, Deut 6:8); *kraspeda* are the blue-threaded ritual fringes/tassels (*tzitzit*, Num 15:38; Deut 22:12).
- **v. 13 `κλείετε τὴν βασιλείαν τῶν οὐρανῶν` (*kleiete tēn basileian tōn ouranōn*):** In SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28, and TR, this "shutting the kingdom of heaven" woe stands at **v. 13**, whereas `RP` moves it to v. 14 and places the "devouring widows' houses" woe at v. 13.
- **v. 14 `Οὐαὶ ὑμῖν, γραμματεῖς καὶ Φαρισαῖοι ὑποκριταί, ὅτι κατεσθίετε τὰς οἰκίας τῶν χηρῶν…` (*Ouai hymin, grammateis kai Pharisaioi hypokritai, hoti katesthiete tas oikias tōn chērōn…*):** Omitted in SBLGNT and Nestle 1904 (following WH, Treg, NA28; absent from `א`, `B`, `D`, `L`, `Θ`). `TR` inserts this woe ("Woe to you... who devour widows' houses...") at **v. 14** (assimilated from Mark 12:40 / Luke 20:47), while `RP` inserts it at v. 13 and renumbers SBLGNT v. 13 as v. 14—accounting for the transposed numbering of vv. 13–14 in editions based on `RP` vs SBLGNT/Nestle 1904.
- **v. 24 `διϋλίζοντες τὸν κώνωπα` (*diylizontes ton kōnōpa*):** Filtering wine through cloth to avoid swallowing a ceremonially unclean insect (Lev 11:20–23), juxtaposed with swallowing the largest unclean beast in Palestine (`κάμηλον`, camel)—a vivid Aramaic/Greek hyperbolic contrast.
- **v. 27 `τάφοις κεκονιαμένοις` (*taphois kekoniamenois*):** Tombs coated with lime/whitewash before Passover so pilgrims would not inadvertently step on them and contract corpse-impurity (Num 19:16).
- **v. 35 `Ζαχαρίου υἱοῦ Βαραχίου` (*Zachariou huiou Barachiou*):** Echoes Zechariah son of Jehoiada stoned in the temple court in 2 Chr 24:20–22 (the final book of the Hebrew canon, creating an Abel-to-Zechariah canonical span), combined in name with Zechariah son of Berechiah (Zech 1:1).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Deuteronomy 6:8; Numbers 15:38–40** (alluded to in v. 5): Commands regarding frontlets/binding words and tassels on garments as reminders of God's commandments.
- **Micah 6:8; Zechariah 7:9** (echoed in v. 23): Prioritizing justice, steadfast mercy, and faithfulness above ritual punctiliousness.
- **Genesis 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20–22** (alluded to in v. 35): The murder of Abel (the first martyr in Genesis) through Zechariah (stoned in the temple court in Chronicles, the last book of the Hebrew Scriptures).
- **Jeremiah 12:7; 22:5** (echoed in v. 38): *"I have abandoned my house"* and *"this palace will turn into a ruin."*
- **Psalm 118:26** (quoted in v. 39): *"May the one arriving in the Lord's name be blessed!"*

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** Then Jesus spoke to the multitudes and to his disciples,
**2** saying, “The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses’ seat.
**3** All things therefore whatever they tell you to observe, observe and do, but don’t do their works; for they say, and don’t do.
**4** For they bind heavy burdens that are grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not lift a finger to help them.
**5** But they do all their works to be seen by men. They make their phylacteries broad and enlarge the fringes of their garments,
**6** and love the place of honor at feasts, the best seats in the synagogues,
**7** the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called ‘Rabbi, Rabbi’ by men.
**8** But you are not to be called ‘Rabbi’, for one is your teacher, the Christ, and all of you are brothers.
**9** Call no man on the earth your father, for one is your Father, he who is in heaven.
**10** Neither be called masters, for one is your master, the Christ.
**11** But he who is greatest among you will be your servant.
**12** Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.
**13** “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour widows’ houses, and as a pretense you make long prayers. Therefore you will receive greater condemnation.
**14** “But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you shut up the Kingdom of Heaven against men; for you don’t enter in yourselves, neither do you allow those who are entering in to enter.
**15** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you travel around by sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of Gehenna as yourselves.
**16** “Woe to you, you blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple, he is obligated.’
**17** You blind fools! For which is greater, the gold or the temple that sanctifies the gold?
**18** And, ‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is on it, he is obligated.’
**19** You blind fools! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift?
**20** He therefore who swears by the altar, swears by it and by everything on it.
**21** He who swears by the temple, swears by it and by him who has been living in it.
**22** He who swears by heaven, swears by the throne of God and by him who sits on it.
**23** “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint, dill, and cumin, and have left undone the weightier matters of the law: justice, mercy, and faith. But you ought to have done these, and not to have left the other undone.
**24** You blind guides, who strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel!
**25** “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and unrighteousness.
**26** You blind Pharisee, first clean the inside of the cup and of the platter, that its outside may become clean also.
**27** “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitened tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones and of all uncleanness.
**28** Even so you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.
**29** “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you build the tombs of the prophets and decorate the tombs of the righteous,
**30** and say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we wouldn’t have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.’
**31** Therefore you testify to yourselves that you are children of those who killed the prophets.
**32** Fill up, then, the measure of your fathers.
**33** You serpents, you offspring of vipers, how will you escape the judgment of Gehenna?
**34** Therefore, behold, I send to you prophets, wise men, and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify; and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues and persecute from city to city,
**35** that on you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zachariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the sanctuary and the altar.
**36** Most certainly I tell you, all these things will come upon this generation.
**37** “Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the city that kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to her! How often I would have gathered your children together, even as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you would not!
