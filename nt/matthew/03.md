## Clear & Faithful

**1** During that time John the Baptist arrived, preaching in the Judean wilderness,
**2** saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near."
**3** For this is the one spoken of through the prophet Isaiah: "A voice of one calling out in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight.'"
**4** John himself dressed in a garment of camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist, while his diet consisted of locusts and wild honey.
**5** Then Jerusalem and all Judea and the whole region around the Jordan were going out to him,
**6** and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River as they openly confessed their sins.
**7** But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, "Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?
**8** Therefore bear fruit worthy of repentance,
**9** and do not suppose that you can say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I tell you that out of these stones God is able to raise up children for Abraham.
**10** Even now the axe lies at the root of the trees; every tree, therefore, that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.
**11** I baptize you in water for repentance, yet the one coming behind me is stronger than I am—I am not even fit to carry his sandals. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.
**12** His winnowing shovel is ready in his hand; he will clean out his threshing floor completely and gather his wheat into the storehouse, while burning up the chaff with an inextinguishable fire."
**13** At that time Jesus arrived from Galilee at the Jordan, coming to John so that he might be baptized by him.
**14** Yet John tried to deter him, saying, "I need to be baptized by you, and do you come to me?"
**15** But Jesus answered him, "Let it be so for now, for in this way it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness." Then John consented.
**16** As soon as Jesus had been baptized, he stepped up out of the water; and at once the heavens opened, and he saw God's Spirit coming down like a dove and resting upon him.
**17** At the same time a voice from the heavens spoke: "This is my beloved Son, in whom I take great delight."

## Everyday English

**1** Around that time, John the Baptist showed up in the Judean desert, announcing his message:
**2** "Turn away from your sins and change your hearts, because heaven's kingdom is right around the corner!"
**3** John was the very person the prophet Isaiah had described: "Someone is shouting in the desert: 'Get the Lord's road ready! Clear a straight path for him.'"
**4** John dressed in rough camel-hair clothes with a leather belt strapped around his waist, and he lived on locusts and wild honey.
**5** Crowds poured out to meet him from Jerusalem, from every corner of Judea, and from the entire Jordan Valley.
**6** As they admitted their sins openly, John baptized them in the Jordan River.
**7** When he noticed a large group of Pharisees and Sadducees showing up to be baptized, he confronted them: "You nest of snakes! Who tipped you off to run from the judgment that's coming?
**8** Show by the way you live that you've truly turned your lives around!
**9** Don't comfort yourselves by saying, 'Abraham is our ancestor.' I'm telling you, God can turn these stones lying on the ground into Abraham's children!
**10** The axe is already resting against the roots of the trees. Any tree that doesn't produce good fruit gets chopped down and tossed into the fire.
**11** I'm only baptizing you with water to mark a fresh start, but someone far greater than me is on his way—I'm not even good enough to carry his sandals. He will immerse you in the Holy Spirit and in fire.
**12** He already has his winnowing shovel in hand. He'll clean off his threshing floor, store his wheat safely in the barn, and burn the husks in a fire that never goes out."
**13** Then Jesus traveled from Galilee down to the Jordan River to be baptized by John.
**14** John tried to talk him out of it: "I'm the one who needs you to baptize me—why are you coming to me?"
**15** Jesus replied, "Go ahead with it for now. This is the right way for us to complete everything God requires." So John agreed.
**16** As soon as Jesus was baptized and stepped up out of the water, the sky split open, and he saw God's Spirit swoop down like a dove and settle on him.
**17** At the same moment, a voice rang out from heaven: "This is my dearly loved Son—I take great joy in him!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `μετανοεῖτε` (*metanoeite*):** Present imperative of *metanoeō* ("repent," literally "change one's mind/orientation"), calling for an ongoing turning of heart and conduct toward God's approaching reign.
- **v. 6 `ἐξομολογούμενοι` (*exomologoumenoi*):** Middle participle of *exomologeō* ("to confess openly," "acknowledge without reserve"), indicating public acknowledgment of sin accompanying immersion.
- **v. 11 `καὶ πυρί` (*kai pyri*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` retain "and fire," whereas `RP` omits *kai pyri* in some manuscripts; in context "fire" recalls both purifying refinement (Mal 3:2–3) and the unquenchable judgment fire of vv. 10, 12.
- **v. 14 `διεκώλυεν` (*diekōlyen*):** Conative imperfect of *diakōlyō*, indicating John was actively trying to hold Jesus back or dissuade him before yielding in v. 15.
- **v. 15 `δικαιοσύνην` (*dikaiosynēn*):** "Righteousness" or "God's saving/covenant order," signifying faithful alignment with God's redemptive purpose.
- **v. 16 `ἠνεῴχθησαν` (*ēneōchthēsan*):** SBLGNT and `WH` read "the heavens were opened" without a pronoun, while `Treg`, `NA28`, and `RP` add *autō* ("to him"); similarly, `WH` reads *pneuma theou* ("Spirit of God") without articles, whereas `Treg`, `NA28`, and `RP` read *to pneuma tou theou*.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Isaiah 40:3** (quoted in v. 3): *"A voice calls out: 'In the wilderness clear the way of the LORD; make level in the desert a highway for our God.'"*
- **2 Kings 1:8** (echoed in v. 4): Elijah wore a garment of hair with a leather belt around his waist, identifying John with the prophetic Elijah tradition.
- **Malachi 3:1–3; 4:1** (echoed in vv. 10–12): Portrays the coming Lord refining his people like fire and burning up the wicked like stubble.
- **Psalm 2:7 & Isaiah 42:1** (echoed in v. 17): *"You are my son"* combined with *"the chosen one in whom my soul takes delight,"* uniting royal Messiah and Servant motifs.

## Reference Text (WEB Updated, Public Domain)

**1** In those days, John the Baptizer came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea, saying,
**2** “Repent, for the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand!”
**3** For this is he who was spoken of by Isaiah the prophet, saying, “The voice of one crying in the wilderness, make the way of the Lord ready! Make his paths straight!”
**4** Now John himself wore clothing made of camel’s hair with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey.
**5** Then people from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him.
**6** They were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins.
**7** But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming for his baptism, he said to them, “You offspring of vipers, who warned you to flee from the wrath to come?
**8** Therefore produce fruit worthy of repentance!
**9** Don’t think to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham for our father,’ for I tell you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones.
**10** Even now the ax lies at the root of the trees. Therefore every tree that doesn’t produce good fruit is cut down, and cast into the fire.
**11** “I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, but he who comes after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit.
**12** His winnowing fork is in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his threshing floor. He will gather his wheat into the barn, but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire.”
**13** Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to John, to be baptized by him.
**14** But John would have hindered him, saying, “I need to be baptized by you, and you come to me?”
**15** But Jesus, answering, said to him, “Allow it now, for this is the fitting way for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then he allowed him.
**16** Jesus, when he was baptized, went up directly from the water: and behold, the heavens were opened to him. He saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming on him.
**17** Behold, a voice out of the heavens said, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.”
