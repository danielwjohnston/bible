## Clear & Faithful

**1** Summoning his twelve disciples, he granted them authority over unclean spirits, so that they could expel them and cure every disease and every kind of weakness.
**2** These are the names of the twelve apostles: first, Simon, called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James, Zebedee's son, and his brother John;
**3** Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the toll collector; James, Alphaeus's son, and Thaddaeus;
**4** Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot—the one who betrayed him.
**5** These twelve Jesus sent out after instructing them: "Do not take any road leading to the Gentiles, and do not enter any town of the Samaritans;
**6** rather, travel to the lost sheep belonging to the house of Israel.
**7** As you go, proclaim this message: 'The kingdom of heaven has come near.'
**8** Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, drive out demons; freely you have received, freely give.
**9** Do not acquire gold, silver, or copper coins for your money belts,
**10** nor a traveler's pack for the journey, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker deserves his food.
**11** Whatever town or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and stay there until you depart.
**12** As you enter the household, give it your greeting;
**13** If that household proves worthy, let your peace rest upon it; yet if it proves unworthy, let your peace return back to you.
**14** And if anyone does not welcome you or listen to your words, as you leave that house or town, shake the dust off your feet.
**15** Truly I say to you, it will go easier for the territory of Sodom and Gomorrah on judgment day than for that town.
**16** Listen, I am sending you forth like sheep surrounded by wolves; be therefore as discerning as serpents and as guileless as doves.
**17** Beware of people, for they will hand you over to local councils and flog you in their synagogues;
**18** and because of me you will even be dragged before governors and kings as a witness to them and to the nations.
**19** Yet when they hand you over, do not worry about how or what you will speak, for in that hour what you are to say will be given to you;
**20** because you are not the ones doing the speaking—rather, it is your Father's Spirit speaking within you.
**21** Brother will betray brother to death, and a father his child; children will rebel against parents and have them put to death.
**22** You will be hated by everyone because of my name; yet the one who endures to the end will be saved.
**23** When they persecute you in one town, flee to the next; for truly I tell you, you will not finish going through the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes.
**24** A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a slave above his master.
**25** It is enough for the disciple to become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have called the head of the house Beelzebul, how much more the members of his household!
**26** Therefore do not fear them, for nothing is veiled that will not be revealed, and nothing hidden that will not become known.
**27** Whatever I speak to you in darkness, proclaim in broad light; and whatever you hear whispered in your ear, herald from the housetops.
**28** Have no fear of those who kill the body yet lack the power to kill the soul; rather, revere the one who holds power to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.
**29** Are not two sparrows sold for a copper coin? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground apart from your Father.
**30** As for you, even the hairs of your head are all numbered.
**31** So do not be afraid; you are worth far more than many sparrows.
**32** Therefore, everyone who acknowledges me before people, I also will acknowledge before my Father who is in heaven;
**33** yet whoever denies me before people, I also will deny before my Father who is in heaven.
**34** Do not suppose that I came to bring peace upon the earth; I did not come to bring peace, but a sword.
**35** For I have come to turn a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a bride against her mother-in-law;
**36** and a person's enemies will be the members of his own household.
**37** Whoever cherishes father or mother above me is unworthy of me, and whoever cherishes son or daughter above me is unworthy of me;
**38** and whoever does not take up his cross and follow after me is unworthy of me.
**39** Whoever finds his life will lose it, and whoever loses his life for my sake will find it.
**40** Whoever receives you receives me, and whoever receives me receives the one who sent me forth.
**41** Whoever welcomes a prophet because he is a prophet will receive a prophet's reward, and whoever welcomes a righteous person because he is righteous will receive a righteous person's reward.
**42** And whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because he is a disciple—truly I say to you, he will by no means lose his reward."

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus gathered his twelve disciples and gave them authority to drive out evil spirits and cure every kind of illness and ailment.
**2** Here are the names of the twelve apostles: first, Simon (nicknamed Peter) and his brother Andrew; James and John, the sons of Zebedee;
**3** Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas and Matthew (who had worked as a tax collector), James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus,
**4** Simon the Zealot, and Judas Iscariot, who later turned Jesus in.
**5** Jesus sent these twelve out with clear orders: "Stay off the roads heading into Gentile territory, and don't visit any Samaritan towns.
**6** Go instead to the lost sheep of Israel's household.
**7** As you travel, announce this message: 'Heaven's kingdom is right here!'
**8** Cure the sick, bring the dead back to life, heal skin diseases, and force demons out. You got this gift free of charge—so give it out free of charge!
**9** Don't pack gold, silver, or copper coins in your money belts.
**10** Don't take a supply bag for the trip, an extra shirt, spare sandals, or a walking stick—a worker has earned his keep.
**11** Whenever you arrive in a town or village, ask around for a dependable host, and stay at that house until you move on.
**12** When you step inside the home, offer it your blessing of peace.
**13** If that household deserves it, let your peace rest on them; if not, take your peace back with you.
**14** Whenever someone refuses to welcome you or listen to what you say, brush the dust off your sandals as you walk out of that house or town.
**15** Mark my words: on judgment day, the towns of Sodom and Gomorrah will have it easier than that town!
**16** Listen carefully: I'm sending you out like sheep straight into a pack of wolves. So be street-smart like snakes and harmless like doves.
**17** Watch out for people—they'll drag you before local courts and whip you inside their synagogues.
**18** You'll even be hauled in front of governors and kings on my account so you can testify before them and the nations.
**19** When they arrest you, don't stress over how to defend yourselves or what words to use. Right when the moment arrives, you'll be given what to say—
**20** because you won't be the ones doing the talking; it'll be the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.
**21** Brothers will hand over their own brothers to be executed, and fathers will turn in their own children. Kids will turn against their parents and have them killed.
**22** Everybody will hate you because you belong to me. Still, whoever holds out to the end will be saved.
**23** When they persecute you in one town, run to the next one. Trust me, you won't even make it through all the towns of Israel before the Son of Man arrives.
**24** A student doesn't outrank his teacher, and a servant doesn't outrank his master.
**25** It's plenty for a student to end up like his teacher, and a servant like his master. If they called the head of the house 'Beelzebul,' imagine what insults they'll throw at his family!
**26** Don't let them scare you! Nothing is covered up that won't be uncovered, and nothing is hidden that won't come to light.
**27** Whatever I whisper to you in the dark, announce in broad daylight; whatever you hear privately in your ear, shout from the rooftops!
**28** Don't be terrified of people who can only kill your body but can't touch your soul. Fear the One who can destroy both soul and body in hell.
**29** Aren't two tiny sparrows sold for a penny? Yet not even one falls dead to the ground without your Father knowing.
**30** Why, even every single hair on your head has been counted!
**31** So quit being afraid—you matter infinitely more to God than flocks of sparrows.
**32** Everyone who stands up for me publicly before others, I'll stand up for before my Father in heaven.
**33** But anyone who disowns me publicly before others, I'll disown before my Father in heaven.
**34** Don't assume I came to bring cozy peace to the earth—I didn't come to bring peace, I came to bring a sword!
**35** I came to create division between a son and his father, a daughter and her mother, and a daughter-in-law and her mother-in-law;
**36** a person's worst enemies will be inside their own family.
**37** Whoever loves father or mother more than me doesn't deserve me, and whoever loves son or daughter more than me doesn't deserve me.
**38** Anyone who refuses to shoulder his cross and walk behind me doesn't deserve me.
**39** Whoever clings to his life will lose it, and whoever gives up his life for my sake will truly find it.
**40** Anyone who welcomes you is welcoming me, and anyone who welcomes me is welcoming the Father who sent me.
**41** Anyone who welcomes a prophet because they're a prophet will share a prophet's reward, and anyone who welcomes a godly person because they're godly will share a godly person's reward.
**42** And anyone who hands even a cup of cold water to one of these humble followers just because they belong to me—I promise you, that person won't miss out on their reward!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `Θαδδαῖος` (*Thaddaios*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read "Thaddaeus"; `RP` reads *Lebbaios ho epiklētheis Thaddaios* ("Lebbaeus who was surnamed Thaddaeus"), harmonizing early lists of the Twelve.
- **v. 4 `Καναναῖος` (*Kananaios*):** Transliterates Aramaic *qan'ana* ("zealous/Zealot"), not "Canaanite" or "from Cana"; `RP` uses the variant spelling *Kananitēs*.
- **v. 8 `νεκροὺς ἐγείρετε` (*nekrous egeirete*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` include "raise the dead," whereas `RP` omits this phrase.
- **v. 16 `φρόνιμοι` (*phronimoi*) & `ἀκέραιοι` (*akeraioi*):** *Phronimos* means "shrewd/practically discerning"; *akeraios* literally means "unmixed/unadulterated," hence "pure, guileless, innocent."
- **v. 25 `Βεελζεβούλ` (*Beelzeboul*):** Name for the ruler of demons, echoing the Philistine deity Baal-zebub ("lord of flies," 2 Kgs 1:2) or Aramaic *beel-zebul* ("lord of the dwelling/house"), creating a sharp pun on *oikodespotēs* ("master of the house").
- **v. 29 `ἀσσαρίου` (*assariou*):** Roman bronze *as* (worth one-sixteenth of a denarius, a day's wage), underscoring negligible market value contrasted with the Father's watchful care.
- **v. 32 `ὁμολογήσει ἐν ἐμοί` (*homologēsei en emoi*):** Semitic-colored construction ("confess/acknowledge in me") expressing loyal public allegiance to Jesus.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Micah 7:6** (quoted in vv. 35–36): *"A son despises his father, a daughter rebels against her mother, a daughter-in-law opposes her mother-in-law; a person's own household members are his foes."*
- **Genesis 19:24–28** (alluded to in v. 15): The devastating judgment of Sodom and Gomorrah becomes the benchmark for rejecting the apostolic peace-greeting.
- **1 Kings 17:10–16** (background to v. 41): The widow of Zarephath welcomes Elijah as a prophet and receives a prophet's sustaining reward.
