## Clear & Faithful

**1** After the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the tomb.
**2** Suddenly a great earthquake occurred, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, approached, rolled back the stone, and sat upon it.
**3** His appearance shone like lightning, and his garment gleamed white as snow.
**4** The guards shook with fear of him and became like dead men.
**5** Yet the angel answered the women, "Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus who was crucified.
**6** He is not here—he has been raised, exactly as he promised! Come, inspect the spot where he lay.
**7** And go quickly and tell his disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead, and look, he is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see him.' Look, I have told you."
**8** So they hurried away from the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to bring the news to his disciples.
**9** Suddenly Jesus met them and said, "Greetings!" They came up, took hold of his feet, and worshiped him.
**10** Then Jesus told them, "Do not be afraid; go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me."
**11** While they were going, some of the guard entered the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had taken place.
**12** After assembling with the elders and consulting together, they gave a large sum of silver to the soldiers
**13** and instructed them, "Say, 'His disciples came at night and stole him while we were asleep.'
**14** And if this reaches the governor's ears, we will win him over and keep you out of trouble."
**15** So they took the money and did as they had been instructed; and this story has been spread widely among the Jews to this very day.
**16** Meanwhile, the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had directed them.
**17** When they saw him, they worshiped him, though some hesitated in doubt.
**18** Jesus approached and spoke to them, saying: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.
**19** Therefore go and make disciples of every nation, baptizing them into the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit,
**20** teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And look, I am with you always, to the very end of the age."

## Everyday English

**1** Once the Sabbath ended, right as dawn broke on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary walked out to visit the grave.
**2** All at once, a massive earthquake shook the ground—an angel of the Lord came down from heaven, walked up to the tomb, rolled the heavy stone aside, and sat down on top of it!
**3** He blazed like a flash of lightning, and his clothes gleamed as white as fresh snow.
**4** The guards trembled so hard in terror that they collapsed like dead men.
**5** The angel spoke directly to the women: "Don't be frightened! I know you're looking for Jesus, the one who was crucified.
**6** He isn't here—he has risen, just like he promised! Step over and look at the spot where his body lay.
**7** Now hurry back and tell his disciples, 'He has risen from the dead, and he's already heading ahead of you to Galilee—you'll see him there!' Remember, I've given you the message."
**8** Half terrified and bursting with joy, the women hurried away from the tomb and ran straight to tell his disciples.
**9** Right along the way, Jesus himself met them and said, "Hello!" They rushed forward, wrapped their arms around his feet, and worshiped him.
**10** Jesus told them, "Don't be afraid. Go tell my brothers to head up to Galilee—they'll see me there."
**11** While the women were on their way, some of the guards hurried back into the city and reported everything that had happened to the chief priests.
**12** The chief priests called an urgent meeting with the elders, hatched a plan, and handed the soldiers a hefty bribe of silver,
**13** telling them, "Spread this story: 'His disciples snuck in during the night and stole the body while we were fast asleep.'
**14** And if word of this reaches the governor, we'll smooth things over with him and make sure you don't face any punishment."
**15** The soldiers took the cash and followed their script—and that rumor has circulated among Jewish people right down to today.
**16** Meanwhile, the eleven disciples traveled up to Galilee to the exact mountain Jesus had told them to meet at.
**17** When they saw him there, they bowed down in worship, though a few of them still hesitated in doubt.
**18** Jesus walked up to them and declared: "Every bit of authority in heaven and on earth has been handed over to me!
**19** So go out and make disciples from every nation on earth—baptizing them into the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit,
**20** and teaching them to live out everything I've commanded you. And remember: I am right here with you every single day, all the way to the finish of this age!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `Ὀψὲ δὲ σαββάτων, τῇ ἐπιφωσκούσῃ εἰς μίαν σαββάτων` (*Opse de sabbatōn, tē epiphōskousē eis mian sabbatōn*):** Literally "late of the Sabbath, at the dawning toward day one of the week"—Jewish Aramaic/Greek idiom (*mia sabbatōn*, "first day after the Sabbath," i.e., Sunday morning).
- **v. 2–4 `σεισμός … ἐσείσθησαν` (*seismos … eseisthēsan*):** Echoing wordplay between the earthquake (*seismos*) and the guards being "shaken" (*eseisthēsan*) with terror.
- **v. 9 `Χαίρετε` (*Chairete*):** Plural imperative of *chairō* ("Rejoice!"), the standard everyday Koine greeting ("Greetings! / Hello!") that carries an undertone of resurrection joy.
- **v. 17 `οἱ δὲ ἐδίστασαν` (*hoi de edistasan*):** From *distazō* ("to hesitate, waver between two minds, doubt"—used only here and at Matt 14:31 in the NT), indicating either some among the eleven or a mixed response of worship and hesitation before Jesus draws near in v. 18.
- **v. 19 `μαθητεύσατε … βαπτίζοντες … διδάσκοντες` (*mathēteusate … baptizontes … didaskontes*):** One main imperative ("make disciples of all nations") flanked by two modal/instrumental participles ("baptizing" and "teaching"), preceded by the participle `πορευθέντες` ("having gone / as you go"). Note `τὸ ὄνομα` ("the name," singular) governs all three Persons.
- **v. 20 `αἰῶνος` (*aiōnos*):** SBLGNT, WH, Treg, and NA28 conclude the Gospel at `αἰῶνος` ("end of the age"); RP appends the liturgical `Ἀμήν` ("Amen").

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Daniel 7:13–14** (echoed in v. 18): *"To him was granted sovereignty, splendor, and royal power, so that every people, nation, and tongue would serve him; his rule is an eternal rule."*
- **Daniel 10:5–9** (echoed in vv. 3–4): Radiance like lightning, dazzling appearance, and witnesses falling powerless in dread at an angelic vision.
- **Psalm 2:7–8; Isaiah 49:6** (background to v. 19): Extending God's reign and salvation to all the nations (*panta ta ethnē*).
- **Genesis 28:15; Isaiah 41:10; Matthew 1:23** (echoed in v. 20): *"Look, I am right here with you"*—closing Matthew's Gospel with the Emmanuel promise announced in Matt 1:23 (*"God with us"*).

**1** Now after the Sabbath, as it began to dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb.
**2** Behold, there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from the sky and came and rolled away the stone from the door and sat on it.
**3** His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow.
**4** For fear of him, the guards shook, and became like dead men.
**5** The angel answered the women, “Don’t be afraid, for I know that you seek Jesus, who has been crucified.
**6** He is not here, for he has risen, just like he said. Come, see the place where the Lord was lying.
**7** Go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has risen from the dead, and behold, he goes before you into Galilee; there you will see him.’ Behold, I have told you.”
**8** They departed quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to bring his disciples word.
**9** As they went to tell his disciples, behold, Jesus met them, saying, “Rejoice!” They came and took hold of his feet, and worshiped him.
**10** Then Jesus said to them, “Don’t be afraid. Go tell my brothers that they should go into Galilee, and there they will see me.”
**11** Now while they were going, behold, some of the guards came into the city and told the chief priests all the things that had happened.
**12** When they were assembled with the elders and had taken counsel, they gave a large amount of silver to the soldiers,
**13** saying, “Say that his disciples came by night and stole him away while we slept.
**14** If this comes to the governor’s ears, we will persuade him and make you free of worry.”
**15** So they took the money and did as they were told. This saying was spread abroad among the Jews, and continues until today.
**16** But the eleven disciples went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had sent them.
**17** When they saw him, they bowed down to him; but some doubted.
**18** Jesus came to them and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to me in heaven and on earth.
**19** Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
**20** teaching them to observe all things that I commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.” Amen.
