## Clear & Faithful

**1** The Pharisees and Sadducees approached and, to test Jesus, asked him to display a sign from heaven.
**2** He replied to them, "When evening arrives, you say, 'Clear weather ahead, for the sky is glowing red,'
**3** and in the morning, 'Stormy weather today, for the sky is red and overcast.' You know how to read the face of the sky, yet you cannot interpret the signs of the times!
**4** A wicked and unfaithful generation demands a sign, yet no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah." Then he left them and went away.
**5** When the disciples crossed to the opposite shore, they had forgotten to bring bread.
**6** Jesus told them, "Watch out and guard against the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."
**7** They began discussing this among themselves, saying, "It is because we did not bring any bread."
**8** Aware of this, Jesus said, "You of little faith, why are you debating among yourselves about not having brought bread?
**9** Do you still not understand? Do you not remember the five loaves for the five thousand and how many baskets you gathered?
**10** Or the seven loaves for the four thousand and how many large hampers you gathered?
**11** How do you fail to grasp that I was not speaking to you about bread? Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."
**12** Then they realized he had not told them to guard against yeast used in bread, but against the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.
**13** When Jesus entered the district of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, "Who do people say the Son of Man is?"
**14** They answered, "Some say John the Baptist, others say Elijah, and still others Jeremiah or one of the prophets."
**15** He asked them, "But who do you say that I am?"
**16** Simon Peter spoke up and answered, "You are the Messiah—the living God's own Son."
**17** Jesus responded to him, "Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah! For flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father who is in heaven.
**18** And I tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not overpower it.
**19** I will entrust to you the keys of heaven's kingdom: anything you bind on earth will stand bound in heaven, and anything you loose on earth will stand loosed in heaven."
**20** Then he strictly ordered the disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah.
**21** From that point on, Jesus began explaining to his disciples that he had to go to Jerusalem, endure great suffering from the elders, chief priests, and scribes, be put to death, and rise again on the third day.
**22** Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him: "God forbid it, Lord! This shall never happen to you!"
**23** Turning around, Jesus said to Peter, "Get behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to me, because you are not thinking according to God's perspective, but according to human values."
**24** Then Jesus addressed his disciples: "Whoever wishes to follow behind me must renounce himself, shoulder his cross, and come after me.
**25** Anyone determined to preserve his own life will forfeit it, while anyone who forfeits his life on my account will discover it.
**26** What profit is there for a person to gain the whole world yet forfeit his own soul? Or what can anyone give in exchange for his soul?
**27** For the Son of Man is going to come in his Father's glory with his angels, and then he will repay each person according to what they have done.
**28** Truly I tell you, some who are standing right here will not taste death before they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."

## Everyday English

**1** Some Pharisees and Sadducees came up to trap Jesus, asking him to perform a miraculous sign straight from heaven.
**2** Jesus answered them, "In the evening you say, 'We're in for good weather because the sky is red.'
**3** And at sunrise you say, 'A storm is coming today because the sky looks dark and red.' You're experts at reading the weather in the sky, yet you're clueless when it comes to reading the signs of what God is doing right now!
**4** Only an evil, unfaithful generation keeps demanding proof like that—and the only sign it's going to get is the sign of Jonah." With that, he walked away and left them behind.
**5** Once they reached the other side of the lake, the disciples realized they'd forgotten to pack any bread.
**6** Jesus warned them, "Keep your eyes open and stay away from the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."
**7** They started murmuring to each other, "He's saying that because we didn't bring bread."
**8** Knowing what they were thinking, Jesus said, "Why are you worrying about forgetting bread? Where's your trust?
**9** Don't you get it yet? Have you already forgotten the five loaves for five thousand people and all those baskets of leftovers?
**10** What about the seven loaves for four thousand people and all those huge hampers you filled?
**11** How could you possibly think I was talking about literal bread? Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees!"
**12** That's when it clicked—he wasn't warning them about baking yeast at all, but about the toxic teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.
**13** When Jesus arrived near Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, "Who are people saying the Son of Man is?"
**14** "Some think you're John the Baptist," they answered. "Others say Elijah, or Jeremiah, or one of the other prophets."
**15** "What about you?" he asked. "Who do you say I am?"
**16** Simon Peter spoke right up: "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God!"
**17** Jesus told him, "You're truly blessed, Simon son of Jonah! No human being revealed that to you—my Father in heaven showed it to you.
**18** Now listen to what I'm telling you: your name is Peter—'Rock'—and on this rock I'm going to build my community, and even the powers of death won't be able to defeat it.
**19** I'll hand you the keys to heaven's kingdom. Whatever you forbid on earth will stand confirmed in heaven, and whatever you permit on earth will stand confirmed in heaven."
**20** Then he gave the disciples strict orders not to tell anyone he was the Messiah.
**21** From that point on, Jesus started laying out plainly to his disciples that he had to go to Jerusalem, endure terrible suffering at the hands of the elders, chief priests, and legal experts, be executed, and rise back to life on the third day.
**22** Peter pulled him aside and scolded him: "Never, Lord! God wouldn't let that happen to you!"
**23** Jesus spun around and told Peter, "Step back behind me, Satan! You're getting right in my way, because you're looking at things from a human point of view instead of God's."
**24** Then Jesus spoke to all his disciples: "If anyone wants to follow me, they have to give up control of their own lives, shoulder their cross, and walk my path.
**25** Anyone who clings to their life will lose it, while anyone who gives up their life for me will actually discover true life.
**26** Think about it: what good does it do to own the entire world if you lose your own soul? Is there anything you could trade to buy your soul back?
**27** The Son of Man is coming in his Father's splendor alongside his angels, and then he'll pay everyone back based on how they actually lived.
**28** I promise you this: some people standing right here won't die before they see the Son of Man arriving in his royal power."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2–3 `Ὀψίας γενομένης … οὐ δύνασθε` (*Opsias genomenēs … ou dynasthe*):** Present in SBLGNT, Treg, NA28, and RP, whereas WH encloses these weather-sign sayings in double brackets (`⟦WH⟧`), reflecting their absence from major early witnesses such as Codex Sinaiticus (`א`) and Codex Vaticanus (`B`).
- **v. 18 `Πέτρος … πέτρᾳ` (*Petros … petra*):** Wordplay between the masculine personal name *Petros* ("stone/rock") and the feminine noun *petra* ("bedrock/cliff"). Also `ἐκκλησίαν` (*ekklēsian*, "assembly/congregation/church") echoes the Septuagint's rendering of *qahal* ("assembly of Israel").
- **v. 19 `ἔσται δεδεμένον … ἔσται λελυμένον` (*estai dedemenon … estai lelymenon*):** Future periphrastic perfect passive participles ("will be having-been-bound / having-been-loosed"), often understood as expressing either heaven's ratification or alignment with prior heavenly decree.
- **v. 22 `Ἵλεώς σοι` (*Hileōs soi*):** Literally "[May God be] merciful/gracious to you," an idiom of shocked protest equivalent to "Heaven forbid!"
- **v. 25 `ψυχήν` (*psychēn*):** Spans "physical life" and "inner self/soul," creating a deliberate double-entendre across vv. 25–26: preserving earthly life at the cost of discipleship forfeits true, enduring life.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Jonah 1:17; 2:10** (alluded to in v. 4): Jonah's three days in the great fish and deliverance prefigure the Son of Man's death and resurrection (cf. Matt 12:39–40).
- **Isaiah 22:22** (echoed in v. 19): *"I will place the key of David's house upon his shoulder; when he opens, none can shut, and when he shuts, none can open."*
- **Job 38:17; Isaiah 38:10** (echoed in v. 18): Old Testament imagery of the "doors of Sheol" (*pylai hadou* in LXX) representing the realm of mortality.
- **Psalm 62:12 / Proverbs 24:12** (echoed in v. 27): *"For you repay each person according to their deeds."*

**7** They reasoned among themselves, saying, “We brought no bread.”
**8** Jesus, perceiving it, said, “Why do you reason among yourselves, you of little faith, because you have brought no bread?

**11** How is it that you don’t perceive that I didn’t speak to you concerning bread? But beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”
**12** Then they understood that he didn’t tell them to beware of the yeast of bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

**14** They said, “Some say John the Baptizer, some, Elijah, and others, Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”
**15** He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?”
**16** Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”

**18** I also tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my assembly, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.
**19** I will give to you the keys of the Kingdom of Heaven, and whatever you bind on earth will have been bound in heaven; and whatever you release on earth will have been released in heaven.”
