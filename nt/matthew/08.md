## Clear & Faithful

**1** When Jesus came down from the mountain, large crowds followed him.
**2** Just then, a man with leprosy came up and bowed low before him, saying, "Lord, if you choose, you have the power to cleanse me."
**3** Stretching out his hand, Jesus touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.
**4** Then Jesus said to him, "See that you tell no one; instead, go show yourself to the priest and offer the gift that Moses prescribed, as a testimony to them."
**5** When he entered Capernaum, a Roman centurion approached him, pleading with him
**6** and saying, "Lord, my servant lies paralyzed at home, suffering terrible pain."
**7** Jesus told him, "I myself will come and heal him."
**8** The centurion replied, "Lord, I am unworthy to have you step beneath my roof; simply give the command by a word, and my servant will be restored.
**9** For I too am a man under authority, having soldiers under my command; I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."
**10** Hearing this, Jesus marveled and said to those following him, "Truly I tell you, I have not found anyone in Israel with faith so great as this.
**11** I tell you that many will come from east and west and recline at the banquet table with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven,
**12** while the heirs of the kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth."
**13** Then Jesus said to the centurion, "Go; let it be done for you just as you have believed." And his servant was healed in that very hour.
**14** Entering Peter's house, Jesus saw his mother-in-law lying sick with a fever.
**15** He touched her hand, and the fever departed from her; she stood up and began waiting on him.
**16** When evening came, they brought him many who were demon-possessed; he drove out the spirits with a word and healed all who were sick,
**17** in order to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah: "He himself took our weaknesses and bore away our diseases."
**18** Seeing large crowds around him, Jesus gave orders to cross over to the other side.
**19** Then a scribe approached and said to him, "Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go."
**20** Jesus replied to him, "Foxes have dens and birds of the sky have roosts, yet the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head."
**21** Another of the disciples asked him, "Lord, permit me first to depart and bury my father."
**22** But Jesus told him, "Follow me, and let the dead bury their own dead."
**23** As he stepped aboard a boat, his disciples went along behind him.
**24** Suddenly a violent storm arose on the sea, so that waves were swamping the boat; yet Jesus himself was fast asleep.
**25** They went and woke him up, saying, "Lord, save us! We are perishing!"
**26** He said to them, "Why are you so cowardly, O people of little faith?" Then he rose, rebuked the winds and the sea, and a deep calm settled over them.
**27** The men were amazed, asking, "What sort of person is this, that even the winds and the sea obey his command?"
**28** When he arrived on the other side, in the region of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men met him, emerging from the tombs—so extremely violent that no one could pass along that road.
**29** Suddenly they screamed, "What have we to do with you, Son of God? Have you come here to torment us before the appointed time?"
**30** Now at some distance from them a large herd of pigs was feeding.
**31** The demons begged him, "If you drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs."
**32** He told them, "Go." So they came out and entered the pigs, and at once the entire herd charged down the steep embankment into the sea and perished in the waters.
**33** The herdsmen fled, ran into the town, and reported everything—including what had happened to the demon-possessed men.
**34** Then the whole town came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw him, they urged him to leave their territory.

## Everyday English

**1** As Jesus walked down the mountainside, huge crowds followed right behind him.
**2** Just then, a man suffering from a skin disease came up, knelt before him, and pleaded, "Sir, if you want to, you have the power to make me clean."
**3** Jesus reached out and touched the man. "I do want to," he said. "Be clean!" Instantly his skin disease vanished.
**4** Then Jesus told him, "Make sure you don't broadcast this to anyone. Instead, go straight to the priest, let him examine you, and present the offering Moses required as proof to everyone."
**5** When Jesus walked into Capernaum, a Roman army officer approached him and begged for help:
**6** "Sir, my young servant is lying paralyzed at home in terrible agony."
**7** "I'll come and heal him," Jesus offered.
**8** The officer replied, "Sir, I don't deserve to have you step under my roof. Just give the order from right here, and my servant will get well.
**9** I understand how authority works—I take orders from superiors, and I have soldiers under me. When I tell one soldier, 'Go,' he goes. I tell another, 'Come here,' and he comes. I tell my servant, 'Do this,' and he gets it done."
**10** Jesus was amazed when he heard that, and turned to the people trailing him: "Listen to me—I haven't seen faith this strong in anyone across all of Israel!
**11** Mark my words: crowds of people will travel from east and west to sit down at the feast with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in heaven's kingdom,
**12** while those who were born heirs to the kingdom will be thrown outside into the dark, where people weep and grind their teeth in regret."
**13** Then Jesus told the officer, "Go on home. What you believed for will happen." And his servant recovered at that exact moment.
**14** Later, Jesus went into Peter's home and saw Peter's mother-in-law lying in bed with a high fever.
**15** He touched her hand, the fever broke immediately, and she got right up and began preparing food for him.
**16** That evening, people brought him many who were controlled by evil spirits. With a single command he drove the spirits out, and he cured everyone who was ill.
**17** All this fulfilled what the prophet Isaiah had announced: "He took our weaknesses on himself and carried away our sicknesses."
**18** Seeing how large the crowd around him had grown, Jesus gave orders to sail across to the opposite shore.
**19** Just then, a legal scholar walked up and told him, "Teacher, I'll follow you wherever you go!"
**20** Jesus replied, "Foxes have burrows and wild birds have nests, yet the Son of Man doesn't even own a place to rest his head."
**21** Another disciple asked him, "Lord, let me go home first and bury my father."
**22** Jesus answered him, "Follow me right now, and let those who are spiritually dead take care of burying their dead."
**23** Then Jesus climbed into a boat, and his disciples boarded with him.
**24** Out of nowhere, a ferocious storm churned up the lake so violently that waves crashed over the boat—while Jesus slept right through it.
**25** The disciples scrambled over and woke him up, shouting, "Lord, save us! We're going to drown!"
**26** "Why are you so terrified, you people with such tiny faith?" Jesus asked. Then he stood up, scolded the wind and the waves, and everything went dead calm.
**27** The men stared in awe and whispered to each other, "Who on earth is this? Even the wind and the lake obey him!"
**28** When they landed on the far shore in Gadarene territory, two men possessed by demons rushed out from the graveyard caves to confront him. They were so dangerous that nobody dared travel down that road.
**29** They shrieked at him, "What business do you have with us, Son of God? Did you come here to torture us before the final day?"
**30** Off in the distance, a massive herd of pigs was rooting around for food.
**31** The demons pleaded with Jesus, "If you're going to force us out, send us into that herd of pigs."
**32** "Go ahead," he told them. They left the men and swept into the pigs, and instantly the entire herd stampeded off the steep bank into the lake and drowned.
**33** The pig herders bolted into town and told everyone what had happened—including the story of the two demon-possessed men.
**34** Before long, the entire town marched out to meet Jesus. As soon as they saw him, they begged him to leave their region.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 6 `παῖς` (*pais*):** Greek *pais* can mean either "boy/child" or "servant/attendant"; the parallel in Luke 7:2 uses *doulos* ("slave"), indicating a valued household servant.
- **v. 17 `ἀσθενείας` (*astheneias*):** Literally "weaknesses" or "infirmities," matching Matthew's own translation of Isa 53:4 directly from Hebrew rather than the LXX's spiritualized "sins."
- **v. 24 `σεισμός` (*seismos*):** Normally "earthquake" or violent "shaking," here describing a sudden, violent squall churning the Sea of Galilee.
- **v. 25 `σῶσον` (*sōson*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read simply *Kyrie, sōson, apollymetha* ("Lord, save! We are perishing"); `RP` adds *hēmas* ("us").
- **v. 26 `γαλήνη` (*galēnē*):** "Deep calm" or "stillness" on open water, heightening the sharp contrast with the *seismos megas* ("great upheaval") of v. 24.
- **v. 28 `Γαδαρηνῶν` (*Gadarēnōn*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read "Gadarenes" (referring to Gadara, whose territory extended to the southeast shore of Galilee), whereas `RP` reads *Gergesēnōn* ("Gergesenes," harmonized with Origen's local identification).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Leviticus 14:2–32** (referenced in v. 4): Mosaic ritual inspection and thanksgiving offerings required of a healed leper to verify restoration to the community.
- **Isaiah 53:4** (quoted in v. 17): *"Surely it was our infirmities he lifted up, and our pains that he carried."*
- **Psalm 107:23–30** (echoed in vv. 24–27): Sailors caught in a storm cry out to the LORD, who stills the raging sea into a calm.
