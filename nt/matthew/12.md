## Clear & Faithful

**1** At that time Jesus walked through the grainfields on the Sabbath. His disciples became hungry and began to pluck heads of grain and eat them.
**2** When the Pharisees saw this, they said to him, "Look, your disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath."
**3** But he replied to them, "Have you not read what David did when he and his companions grew hungry—
**4** how he entered the house of God, and they ate the consecrated bread, which was not lawful for him or his companions to eat, but only for the priests?
**5** Or have you not read in the Law that every Sabbath the priests inside the temple break the Sabbath rest and remain blameless?
**6** Yet I tell you that something far greater than the temple stands here.
**7** If you had grasped what this means—'It is mercy I desire, rather than sacrifice'—you would never have condemned the blameless.
**8** Because the Son of Man holds lordship over the Sabbath."
**9** Moving on from there, he entered their synagogue.
**10** There happened to be a man with a shriveled hand; and hoping to bring an accusation against him, they asked Jesus, "Is healing permitted on the Sabbath?"
**11** He said to them, "Which one of you, having a single sheep, if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not take hold of it and lift it out?
**12** How much more valuable is a human being than a sheep! Therefore it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath."
**13** Then he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and it was restored as healthy as the other.
**14** Meanwhile, the Pharisees stepped outside and plotted together against him on how to do away with him.
**15** Aware of this, Jesus withdrew from there. Many followed him, and he healed them all,
**16** strictly warning them not to make him publicly known,
**17** so that what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah might be fulfilled:
**18** "Here is my chosen servant, my beloved one, in whom my soul takes delight; I will place my Spirit upon him, and he will announce justice to the nations.
**19** He will not argue or cry aloud, nor will anyone hear him shouting in the public squares.
**20** A bruised reed he will not break, and a smoldering wick he will not extinguish, until he brings justice through to victory;
**21** and in his name the Gentiles will place their hope."
**22** Then a demon-possessed man who was blind and mute was brought to him; and Jesus healed him, so that the mute man both spoke and saw.
**23** All the crowds were astonished and asked, "Could this possibly be the Son of David?"
**24** But when the Pharisees heard it, they said, "This fellow casts out demons only by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."
**25** Knowing their thoughts, he said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and no city or household divided against itself will stand.
**26** If Satan drives out Satan, he has turned against himself; how then can his kingdom survive?
**27** And if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your own sons cast them out? Because of this, they will be your judges.
**28** Yet if it is by the Spirit of God that I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has already arrived upon you.
**29** Or how can anyone enter a strong man's house and seize his possessions unless he first binds the strong man? Only then can he plunder his house.
**30** Whoever is not with me is against me, and whoever does not gather with me scatters.
**31** For this reason I tell you, every sin and slander will be pardoned for humanity, yet slander against the Spirit will never be pardoned.
**32** Anyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man will receive pardon, yet anyone who speaks against the Holy Spirit will receive no pardon, neither in the present age nor in the age to come.
**33** Either make the tree healthy and its fruit good, or make the tree rotten and its fruit bad; for a tree is recognized by its fruit.
**34** You brood of vipers, how can you speak good things when you are evil? For out of the overflow of the heart the mouth speaks.
**35** The good person brings out good things from a good storehouse, and the evil person brings out evil things from an evil storehouse.
**36** I tell you that on the day of judgment people will render an account for every careless word they utter;
**37** because by your own words you will be declared righteous, and by your own words you will be found guilty."
**38** Then a group of scribes and Pharisees spoke up to him, "Teacher, we want to witness a miraculous sign from you."
**39** He answered them, "An evil and adulterous generation demands a sign, yet no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.
**40** For just as Jonah was in the belly of the great sea creature for three days and three nights, so the Son of Man will be in the heart of the earth for three days and three nights.
**41** The people of Nineveh will stand up at the judgment alongside this generation and condemn it, because they repented when Jonah preached—and look, someone greater than Jonah stands right here!
**42** The queen of the South will rise up at the judgment alongside this generation and condemn it, because she journeyed from the farthest corners of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom—and look, someone greater than Solomon stands right here!
**43** Whenever an unclean spirit goes out of a person, it wanders through waterless places searching for rest and finds none.
**44** Then it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came out.' When it arrives, it finds the house unoccupied, swept clean, and put in order.
**45** Then it goes and brings along seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and they enter and settle there; and the final condition of that person becomes worse than the first. So will it be also with this wicked generation."
**46** While he was still addressing the crowds, just then his mother and brothers stood outside, hoping to speak with him.
**47** Someone said to him, "Look, your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to speak with you."
**48** But he answered the one who told him, "Who is my mother, and who are my brothers?"
**49** And gesturing with his hand toward his disciples, he declared, "Here are my mother and my brothers!
**50** Because anyone who carries out the will of my Father in heaven is my brother, my sister, and my mother."

## Everyday English

**1** One Sabbath around that time, Jesus walked with his disciples along a path through some grainfields. Because his disciples were hungry, they started picking heads of grain and eating the kernels.
**2** When the Pharisees spotted this, they challenged Jesus: "Look! Your disciples are breaking the law by working on the Sabbath!"
**3** Jesus shot back, "Haven't you ever read what David did when he and his men were starving?
**4** He walked right into God's sanctuary and they ate the sacred bread offered to God—even though neither he nor his men were allowed to eat it, only the priests!
**5** Or haven't you read in Moses's law that priests actually break Sabbath rest inside the temple every Sabbath, yet remain completely innocent?
**6** Well, I'm telling you that something far greater than the temple is standing right here!
**7** If you truly understood what God meant when he said, 'I want mercy, not animal sacrifices,' you wouldn't be condemning innocent people.
**8** Besides, the Son of Man rules over the Sabbath."
**9** Jesus left that spot and walked into their local synagogue.
**10** Inside was a man with a shriveled hand. Looking for a way to press charges against Jesus, they asked him, "Does the law allow healing on the Sabbath?"
**11** Jesus answered, "Suppose one of you owns a single sheep and it tumbles into a ditch on the Sabbath. Wouldn't you grab hold of it and pull it right back out?
**12** And how much more does a human being matter than a sheep! So yes, doing good on the Sabbath is completely lawful."
**13** Then he told the man, "Hold out your hand!" The man held it out, and it instantly became just as healthy as his other hand.
**14** Furious, the Pharisees marched out and started plotting how to get rid of Jesus.
**15** Realizing what they were up to, Jesus slipped away from that town. Huge crowds trailed after him, and he cured every single sick person among them,
**16** though he warned them strictly not to spread word about him.
**17** This fulfilled what God had spoken through the prophet Isaiah:
**18** "Look at my chosen servant, the one I love, the one who brings joy to my soul! I will place my Spirit upon him, and he will announce justice to the nations.
**19** He won't pick fights or yell in the streets; nobody will hear him shouting in public squares.
**20** He won't snap a bent reed or snuff out a barely smoking candlewick, until he leads justice to total victory;
**21** and the nations will put their hope in his name."
**22** Later, people brought him a man who was blind and unable to speak because of a demon. Jesus healed him on the spot, so the man could both talk and see.
**23** The entire crowd gasped in amazement: "Could this man actually be the Son of David?"
**24** Overhearing this, the Pharisees sneered, "The only reason he can cast out demons is that Beelzebul, the prince of demons, is backing him!"
**25** Reading their minds, Jesus replied, "Any kingdom torn by civil war collapses into ruins, and any city or family split against itself can't stay standing.
**26** If Satan is driving out Satan, he's fighting against himself—how could his kingdom possibly survive?
**27** And if I'm using Beelzebul to cast out demons, who are your own followers using when they cast them out? Your own people will prove you wrong!
**28** But if I'm driving out demons by the Spirit of God, then God's kingdom has already broken in upon you.
**29** Think about it: how could someone walk into a strong warrior's house and carry off his valuables unless he ties up that warrior first? Once he's tied up, the house can be stripped clean.
**30** Anyone who isn't on my side is working against me, and anyone who doesn't help me gather is scattering the flock.
**31** That's why I'm warning you: people can be forgiven for every sin and every insult against God, except slandering the Holy Spirit—that won't be forgiven.
**32** Speak a word against the Son of Man and you can be forgiven; speak against the Holy Spirit and you won't be forgiven, not in this life or in the age to come.
**33** Either cultivate a healthy tree with healthy fruit, or accept a rotten tree with rotten fruit—a tree is always judged by its fruit!
**34** You nest of snakes, how can evil people say anything genuinely good? Whatever overflows inside the heart is what spills out of the mouth.
**35** A good person pulls good things out of a heart stocked with goodness, while an evil person pulls evil things out of a heart stocked with evil.
**36** Listen closely: on judgment day people will have to answer for every careless, empty word they've ever spoken.
**37** Your own words will clear you, or your own words will convict you."
**38** Then some legal scholars and Pharisees spoke up: "Teacher, we want you to perform a miraculous sign for us to see."
**39** Jesus answered them, "Only a corrupt and unfaithful generation keeps demanding a sign! No sign will be handed to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.
**40** Just as Jonah spent three days and three nights inside the belly of the giant sea monster, the Son of Man will spend three days and three nights deep inside the earth.
**41** At the final judgment, the people of Nineveh will stand right next to this generation and condemn it—because they repented when Jonah preached, and look, someone far greater than Jonah is standing here!
**42** The Queen of the South will also rise up at the judgment alongside this generation and condemn it—because she traveled from the ends of the earth to listen to Solomon's wisdom, and look, someone far greater than Solomon is standing here!
**43** When an evil spirit leaves a person, it wanders across dry, desert places looking for somewhere to settle, and comes up empty.
**44** So it says, 'I'll go back to the house I left.' When it returns, it finds the place vacant, freshly swept, and neatly arranged.
**45** Off it goes to recruit seven more spirits even nastier than itself; they all move in and take over, leaving that person worse off than before. That's exactly what's going to happen to this wicked generation."
**46** While Jesus was still addressing the crowds, his mother and brothers arrived and stood outside, trying to talk with him.
**47** Someone alerted him, "Look, your mother and brothers are standing outside hoping to speak with you."
**48** Jesus replied to the messenger, "Who is my mother, and who are my brothers?"
**49** Then he gestured toward his disciples and declared, "Right here are my mother and my brothers!
**50** Anyone who does what my Father in heaven wants—that person is my brother, my sister, and my mother."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `ἄρτους τῆς προθέσεως` (*artous tēs protheseōs*):** Literally "loaves of the setting-forth" (the twelve loaves of consecrated showbread placed weekly on the golden table in the sanctuary, Lev 24:5–9). Notice `WH` and `NA28` read plural *ephagon* ("they ate," referring to David and his men), whereas `Treg` and `RP` read singular *ephagen* ("he ate").
- **v. 18 `παῖς` (*pais*):** Rendering Hebrew *ebed* ("servant" of the LORD in Isa 42:1), applied to Jesus as the Spirit-anointed Servant who brings *krisis* ("justice/judgment") to the nations.
- **v. 28 `ἔφθασεν` (*ephthasen*):** Aorist of *phthanō* ("to arrive unexpectedly/come upon"), signaling that the promised reign of God has already broken into the present through Jesus's Spirit-empowered deliverance.
- **v. 36 `ῥῆμα ἀργόν` (*rhēma argon*):** *Argos* (*a-* + *ergon*) means "idle," "unproductive," "careless," or "barren of good."
- **v. 40 `κήτους` (*kētous*):** Genitive of *kētos* ("great sea creature / sea monster"), echoing the Septuagint's *kētei megalō* in Jonah 2:1 (1:17 ET).
- **v. 47 `εἶπεν δέ τις αὐτῷ … ζητοῦντές σοι λαλῆσαι` (*eipen de tis autō ... zētountes soi lalēsai*):** Included in SBLGNT (`Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`) and Nestle 1904; **omitted by `WH`** (following Codex Sinaiticus's first hand, Vaticanus, and several early witnesses where scribal eyes may have skipped from the identical ending of v. 46 *autō lalēsai* to v. 47 *soi lalēsai*).

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **1 Samuel 21:1–6 & Hosea 6:6** (referenced in vv. 3–4, 7): David and his hungry men receive the holy bread from the priest Ahimelech, illustrating mercy outranking ritual restriction.
- **Isaiah 42:1–4** (quoted in vv. 18–21): The gentle, Spirit-endowed Servant brings covenant justice to the nations without breaking bruised reeds or quenching smoldering wicks.
- **Jonah 1:17 (2:1 MT) & 3:5; 1 Kings 10:1–10** (referenced in vv. 40–42): Jonah's three-day deliverance and Nineveh's repentance, alongside the Queen of Sheba's pilgrimage to Solomon.
