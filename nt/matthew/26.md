## Clear & Faithful

**1** Once Jesus had concluded all these teachings, he told his disciples,
**2** "You know that after two days the Passover takes place, and the Son of Man is handed over to be crucified."
**3** Then the chief priests and the elders of the people assembled in the courtyard of the high priest, who was named Caiaphas,
**4** and they plotted together to arrest Jesus by stealth and put him to death.
**5** Yet they kept saying, "Not during the festival, lest a riot break out among the people."
**6** Now during Jesus' stay in Bethany at Simon the leper's house,
**7** a woman approached him holding an alabaster jar of very costly perfume, and she poured it onto his head as he reclined at table.
**8** When the disciples saw this, they became indignant and asked, "Why this waste?
**9** This could have been sold for a large sum and given to the poor."
**10** Aware of this, Jesus said to them, "Why are you troubling the woman? She has performed a noble act for me.
**11** After all, the poor are always with you, whereas you will not always have me.
**12** By pouring this perfume onto my body, she did it to prepare me for burial.
**13** Truly I tell you, wherever this good news is proclaimed in the whole world, what she has done will also be spoken of in memory of her."
**14** At that point one of the twelve, named Judas Iscariot, went over to the chief priests
**15** and asked, "What are you willing to give me if I hand him over to you?" And they weighed out thirty pieces of silver for him.
**16** From that moment onward, he kept looking for a good opportunity to betray him.
**17** On the first day of Unleavened Bread, the disciples approached Jesus and asked, "Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover?"
**18** He answered, "Go into the city to a certain man and tell him, 'The Teacher says: "My appointed time is near; I am going to celebrate the Passover at your house with my disciples."'"
**19** So the disciples carried out what Jesus had directed them, making ready the Passover meal.
**20** When evening arrived, he was reclining at table with the twelve.
**21** While they were eating, he said, "Truly I tell you, one of you will betray me."
**22** Deeply distressed, each of them began asking him one after another, "Surely it is not I, Lord?"
**23** He answered, "The one who dipped his hand with me in the bowl—this man will betray me.
**24** The Son of Man goes just as it is written concerning him, yet woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would have been better for that man if he had never been born."
**25** Then Judas, his betrayer, answered, "Surely it is not I, Rabbi?" Jesus told him, "You yourself have said it."
**26** As they were eating, Jesus took bread, blessed it, broke it, and giving it to the disciples, said, "Take, eat; this is my body."
**27** Then he took a cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them, saying, "Drink from it, all of you;
**28** because this is my covenant blood, poured out on behalf of many for the remission of sins.
**29** Yet I assure you, from this moment on I will never again drink of this fruit of the vine until the day I drink it anew alongside you inside my Father's kingdom."
**30** After singing a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.
**31** At that point Jesus told them, "Tonight every one of you will fall away because of me, as Scripture says: 'I will strike down the shepherd, and the flock's sheep will be scattered.'
**32** Yet after I have been raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee."
**33** Peter answered him, "Even if everyone else falls away because of you, I will never fall away."
**34** Jesus said to him, "Truly I tell you, this very night, before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times."
**35** Peter insisted to him, "Even if I have to die with you, I will never deny you!" And all the disciples said the same thing.
**36** Then Jesus arrived with them at a garden plot named Gethsemane, telling his disciples, "Remain seated here while I walk over there and pray."
**37** Taking Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him, he began to feel deep sorrow and anguish.
**38** Then he said to them, "My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch with me."
**39** Going a little farther, he fell on his face and prayed: "My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me; yet not as I will, but as you will."
**40** Then he returned to the disciples and found them sleeping, and he said to Peter, "So, were you unable to stay awake with me for even one hour?
**41** Stay awake and keep praying, lest you fall into testing; the spirit is eager indeed, yet the flesh is frail."
**42** Again, a second time, he went away and prayed, "My Father, if this cannot pass unless I drink it, let your will be done."
**43** Returning again, he found them sleeping, for their eyes had grown heavy.
**44** Leaving them again, he went away and prayed a third time, saying the very same words once more.
**45** Then he returned to the disciples and told them, "Are you still sleeping and resting? Look, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of sinners.
**46** Get up, let us go! Look, my betrayer has drawn near."
**47** While he was still speaking, Judas—one of the twelve—arrived, accompanied by a large crowd armed with swords and clubs from the chief priests and elders of the people.
**48** Now his betrayer had arranged a signal with them, saying, "The one I kiss is the man; arrest him."
**49** Immediately he walked straight up to Jesus, said, "Greetings, Rabbi!" and kissed him warmly.
**50** Jesus said to him, "Friend, do what you have come for." Then they came forward, laid hands on Jesus, and arrested him.
**51** Suddenly one of those with Jesus reached out his hand, drew his sword, struck the high priest's servant, and cut off his ear.
**52** Then Jesus said to him, "Return your sword to its sheath, because everyone who draws a sword will die by a sword.
**53** Or do you suppose that I cannot appeal to my Father, and he would immediately station at my side more than twelve legions of angels?
**54** How then would the Scriptures be fulfilled, which say that it must happen this way?"
**55** At that hour Jesus said to the crowds, "Have you come out with swords and clubs as against a bandit to seize me? Day after day I sat in the temple courts teaching, yet you did not arrest me.
**56** All of this has taken place so that the prophetic Scriptures might be fulfilled." At that moment every single disciple deserted him and ran away.
**57** The men who had seized Jesus marched him off to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and elders had assembled.
**58** Meanwhile Peter kept following him at a distance as far as the high priest's courtyard, and going inside, he sat down with the guards to see how it would turn out.
**59** The chief priests and the entire Sanhedrin were searching for false testimony against Jesus so that they could put him to death,
**60** yet they found none, even though many false witnesses came forward. Finally two came forward
**61** and stated, "This fellow declared, 'I am able to tear down the sanctuary of God and rebuild it in three days.'"
**62** Standing up, the high priest said to him, "Have you no answer to make? What is this they are testifying against you?"
**63** Yet Jesus remained silent. And the high priest said to him, "I place you under oath by the living God: tell us whether you are the Messiah, the Son of God."
**64** Jesus replied to him, "You have said so. Furthermore I tell you, from this time on you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and coming on the clouds of heaven."
**65** Then the high priest tore his garments and exclaimed, "He has blasphemed! Why do we still need witnesses? Look, you have just heard the blasphemy!
**66** What is your verdict?" They answered, "He deserves death!"
**67** At that they spat into his face and struck him with fists; others slapped him,
**68** mocking, "Prophesy to us, Messiah! Who hit you?"
**69** Meanwhile Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard, and a servant girl approached him, saying, "You also were with Jesus the Galilean."
**70** Yet he denied it in front of everyone, saying, "I do not know what you are talking about."
**71** As he went out to the gateway, another servant girl saw him and told those standing there, "This fellow was with Jesus of Nazareth."
**72** Once more he denied it, swearing under oath: "I do not know that man!"
**73** A little while later, those standing nearby approached Peter and said, "Surely you are one of them too, for even your accent gives you away."
**74** Then he began to call down curses on himself and swear, "I do not know the man!" Immediately a rooster crowed.
**75** And Peter remembered the word Jesus had spoken: "Before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times." Going outside, he wept bitterly.

## Everyday English

**1** Once Jesus finished teaching all these things, he told his disciples,
**2** "You know Passover starts in two days—and that's when the Son of Man will be handed over to be nailed to a cross."
**3** Around that time, the chief priests and Jewish elders met together inside the courtyard of the high priest, Caiaphas.
**4** They hatched a secret plot to capture Jesus by trickery and have him executed.
**5** "Just don't do it during the festival," they cautioned each other, "or the crowds might riot."
**6** While Jesus was staying in Bethany at the home of Simon the leper,
**7** a woman walked up carrying an alabaster jar of expensive perfume and poured it straight over his head as he reclined at dinner.
**8** The disciples were outraged when they saw it. "Why throw away something so valuable?" they complained.
**9** "That perfume could've been sold for a small fortune and the cash handed out to the poor!"
**10** Knowing what they were muttering, Jesus said, "Why are you giving this woman a hard time? She's done something truly beautiful for me!
**11** You'll always have poor people around you, yet you won't always have me.
**12** By pouring this perfume on my body, she's preparing me ahead of time for my burial.
**13** I promise you, wherever this good news is preached across the whole world, people will also tell the story of what she did in honor of her."
**14** Then Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples, went straight to the chief priests
**15** and asked, "How much will you pay me if I hand him over to you?" They counted out thirty silver coins for him.
**16** From that moment on, Judas watched for the right chance to betray him.
**17** On the first day of the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the disciples asked Jesus, "Where would you like us to get the Passover meal ready for you?"
**18** He told them, "Head into the city to a certain man's house and tell him, 'The Teacher says, "My time has almost arrived, and I'm celebrating Passover at your place with my disciples."'"
**19** The disciples followed his instructions and got the Passover meal ready.
**20** When evening fell, Jesus sat down at the table with the twelve.
**21** While they were eating, he told them, "I'm telling you the truth: one of you right here is going to betray me."
**22** Heartbroken, they started asking him one by one, "Lord, it couldn't be me, could it?"
**23** Jesus replied, "The one who just dipped his bread in the bowl with me—he's the one who'll betray me.
**24** The Son of Man is going to his death just as Scripture foretold about him, yet how horrible it will be for the man who betrays him! He'd be better off if he'd never been born."
**25** Then Judas, the very one betraying him, spoke up: "Rabbi, it isn't me, is it?" "You've said it yourself," Jesus replied.
**26** As they kept eating, Jesus picked up a loaf of bread, blessed God, broke it into pieces, and handed it to his disciples, saying, "Take this and eat it—this is my body."
**27** Next he picked up a cup, gave thanks to God, and passed it to them: "Drink from this, every one of you!
**28** This is my blood that seals God's covenant, poured out for countless people so their sins can be forgiven.
**29** And mark my words: I won't drink from this fruit of the vine again until the day I drink it fresh with you in my Father's kingdom."
**30** After singing a hymn together, they walked out toward the Mount of Olives.
**31** On the way, Jesus told them, "Tonight every single one of you will stumble and scatter because of me. Scripture says, 'I'll strike down the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will scatter.'
**32** Yet after I rise from the dead, I'll go ahead of you back to Galilee."
**33** Peter spoke up quickly: "Even if everyone else loses their nerve because of you, I'll never back down!"
**34** Jesus looked at him and said, "I'm telling you plainly: before a rooster crows tonight, you'll deny knowing me three times."
**35** "Even if I have to die alongside you," Peter insisted, "I'll never disown you!" Every single one of the disciples chimed in with the same promise.
**36** Then Jesus brought them to a grove called Gethsemane and told his disciples, "Wait right here while I walk over there and pray."
**37** Bringing Peter and Zebedee's two sons along with him, Jesus felt crushed by deep grief and agony.
**38** He told them, "My soul is aching so deeply it feels like dying. Stay right here and keep watch with me."
**39** Walking just a few steps ahead, he dropped face down to the ground and prayed, "My Father, if there's any way possible, take this bitter cup away from me! Still, not what I want—whatever you want."
**40** He walked back to his disciples and found them fast asleep. "Couldn't you stay awake with me for even one hour?" he asked Peter.
**41** "Stay alert and pray so you don't cave in to temptation! Your spirit is eager, yet your human body is weak."
**42** He stepped away a second time and prayed, "My Father, if this cup can't pass without my drinking it, then let your will be done."
**43** When he came back, he found them asleep again because they couldn't keep their eyes open.
**44** Leaving them once more, he went back and prayed a third time using the exact same words.
**45** Finally he returned to his disciples and said, "Still sleeping and resting? Look, the moment has arrived! The Son of Man is being handed over into the grip of sinners.
**46** Get up and let's go—my betrayer is already here!"
**47** Before Jesus finished speaking, Judas—one of the twelve—walked up leading a huge mob armed with swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests and Jewish elders.
**48** Judas had set up a signal with them beforehand: "Whichever man I kiss, that's him—grab him!"
**49** He walked straight over to Jesus, said, "Hello, Rabbi!" and gave him a warm kiss.
**50** Jesus replied, "My friend, go ahead with what you came here to do." At that, the men surged forward, grabbed Jesus, and arrested him.
**51** Instantly, one of Jesus' companions reached out, pulled his sword, swung at the high priest's servant, and sliced off his ear.
**52** Jesus told him, "Put your sword back where it belongs! Everyone who lives by the sword will die by the sword.
**53** Do you honestly think I couldn't call on my Father right now, and he'd send more than twelve legions of angels to back me up?
**54** Yet if I did that, how could Scripture come true when it says things have to happen this way?"
**55** Turning to the mob, Jesus asked, "Am I some kind of armed rebel that you came out to capture me with swords and clubs? Day after day I sat teaching openly in the temple courtyard, and you never laid a finger on me!
**56** Yet all of this is happening to fulfill what the prophets wrote." Right then, all of his disciples abandoned him and ran away.
**57** The men holding Jesus marched him straight to Caiaphas the high priest, where the legal scholars and elders had assembled.
**58** Peter trailed far behind them all the way into the high priest's courtyard, slipped inside, and sat down by the guards to see how everything would end.
**59** Inside, the chief priests and the whole council hunted for false evidence against Jesus so they could have him executed,
**60** yet even though plenty of liars stepped up to testify, they couldn't build a case. At last, two men came forward
**61** and claimed, "This man boasted, 'I can tear down God's temple and rebuild it in three days!'"
**62** The high priest jumped to his feet and demanded, "Aren't you going to defend yourself? What do you say to these charges?"
**63** Jesus stayed completely silent. Then the high priest said, "I put you under solemn oath before the living God: tell us straight out—are you the Messiah, the Son of God?"
**64** "You've said it yourself," Jesus answered. "And let me tell all of you: from now on you'll see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Almighty and coming on the clouds of heaven!"
**65** Hearing that, the high priest ripped his own robes and shouted, "Blasphemy! Why do we need any more witnesses? You just heard the blasphemy with your own ears!
**66** What's your verdict?" "He's guilty and deserves death!" they shouted back.
**67** Then they spat right in his face and beat him with their fists, while others slapped him
**68** and jeered, "Come on, Messiah—prophesy for us! Who just hit you?"
**69** All this time, Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. A servant girl walked over to him and said, "You were with Jesus from Galilee too!"
**70** Right in front of everyone, Peter denied it: "I have no idea what you're talking about!"
**71** As he slipped out toward the front gate, another girl spotted him and told the bystanders, "That guy was with Jesus of Nazareth!"
**72** Once again Peter denied it, swearing an oath: "I don't even know the man!"
**73** A few minutes later, some bystanders walked up to Peter and said, "You're definitely one of his group—your northern accent gives you away!"

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 15 `ἔστησαν … τριάκοντα ἀργύρια` (*estēsan … triakonta argyria*):** Literally "weighed out thirty silver pieces" (shekels of silver, echoing Zech 11:12 and the Exod 21:32 indemnity price for a slave).
- **v. 28 `τὸ αἷμά μου τῆς διαθήκης` (*to haima mou tēs diathēkēs*):** "My blood of the covenant" (WH, NA28, SBLGNT), echoing Exod 24:8; Treg and RP add `καινῆς` ("new," echoing Jer 31:31 and Luke 22:20 / 1 Cor 11:25).
- **v. 37 `ἀδημονεῖν` (*adēmonein*):** Strong verb denoting acute distress, bewilderment, and emotional anguish.
- **v. 45 `Καθεύδετε τὸ λοιπὸν` (*Katheudete to loipon*):** Ambiguous Greek imperative/interrogative—either gentle irony ("Still sleeping and resting?") or tragic permission ("Sleep on for what little time remains" before the arrest interrupts).
- **v. 50 `Ἑταῖρε, ἐφʼ ὃ πάρει` (*Hetaire, eph' ho parei*):** Elliptical Greek expression, literally "Companion, upon what you are present"—either an ironic statement ("Friend, [do] what you came for") or a poignant question ("Friend, why are you here?").
- **v. 53 `δώδεκα λεγιῶνας` (*dōdeka legiōnas*):** Roman military loanword (*legio*), a full legion numbering around 6,000 soldiers—so more than 72,000 angels.
- **v. 64 `ἐκ δεξιῶν τῆς δυνάμεως` (*ek dexiōn tēs dynameōs*):** "At the right hand of Power"—a reverential Jewish circumlocution ("the Power") for God, combined with Dan 7:13's cloud-riding Son of Man.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Zechariah 11:12–13; Exodus 21:32** (alluded to in v. 15): *"Then they weighed out thirty silver pieces as my pay"* (Zech 11:12), matching the valuation of a gored slave (Exod 21:32).
- **Exodus 24:8; Jeremiah 31:31–34** (echoed in v. 28): Moses sprinkles *"the covenant's blood"* at Sinai (Exod 24:8), paired with Jeremiah's promise of a covenant bringing forgiveness of sins (Jer 31:34).
- **Zechariah 13:7** (quoted in v. 31): *"Strike down the shepherd, so the sheep scatter away."*
- **Psalm 42:5, 11** (echoed in v. 38): *"Why are you deeply grieved, O my soul?"* (*perilypos* in LXX Ps 41:6, 12).
- **Psalm 110:1; Daniel 7:13** (combined in v. 64): Seated at the right hand of God and coming upon the clouds of heaven.
