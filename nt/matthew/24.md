## Clear & Faithful

**1** As Jesus walked out of the temple courts and was leaving, his disciples came up to point out to him the temple buildings.
**2** He answered them, "Do you not see all these things? Truly I tell you, not one stone here will be left on top of another—every single one will be torn down."
**3** Later, as he sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples approached him privately and asked, "Tell us, when will these things happen, and what will be the sign of your coming and of the end of the age?"
**4** Jesus answered them, "Watch out that no one leads you astray.
**5** For many will come in my name, claiming, 'I am the Messiah,' and they will deceive many.
**6** You will hear of wars and rumors of wars; see that you are not alarmed, for this must take place, yet the end is not here yet.
**7** Indeed, nation will rise up against nation and kingdom against kingdom, and famines and earthquakes will strike in place after place.
**8** All of these are only the initial throes of labor pains.
**9** Then they will hand you over to persecution and kill you, and you will be hated by all nations because of my name.
**10** At that time many will fall away, betray one another, and hate one another;
**11** numerous false prophets will appear and deceive many people;
**12** and because lawlessness will multiply, the love of most people will grow cold.
**13** Yet whoever stands firm all the way to the end will be delivered.
**14** And this good news of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole inhabited world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come.
**15** Therefore, whenever you witness the horrifying sacrilege—foretold through the prophet Daniel—standing inside the holy precinct (let the reader pay close heed),
**16** at that moment let everyone in Judea escape into the hill country.
**17** Whoever is on the housetop must not go down to take anything out of his house,
**18** and whoever is in the field must not turn back to pick up his cloak.
**19** Woe to women who are pregnant and to those nursing infants in those days!
**20** Pray that your flight may not happen in winter or on a Sabbath.
**21** For at that time there will be great distress, unlike anything that has happened from the beginning of the world until now, nor will anything like it ever happen again.
**22** If those days had not been cut short, no living being would survive; yet for the sake of the chosen ones, those days will be cut short.
**23** Then if anyone tells you, 'Look, here is the Messiah!' or 'There he is!' do not believe it.
**24** For false messiahs and false prophets will appear and display great signs and wonders so as to deceive, if possible, even the chosen ones.
**25** Listen—I have told you in advance.
**26** Therefore if they tell you, 'Look, he is out in the desert,' do not go out; or 'Look, he is in the inner rooms,' do not believe it.
**27** For just as lightning flashes from the east and shines across to the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man.
**28** Wherever the carcass lies, there the vultures will gather.
**29** Right after the anguish of those days, the sun will grow dark, the moon will cease giving its brightness, stars will drop from the sky, and the heavenly powers will be shaken.
**30** At that moment the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky, and every tribe on earth will beat their chests in mourning; they will watch the Son of Man arriving upon heaven's clouds with tremendous power and splendor.
**31** He will dispatch his angels with a resounding trumpet call, and they will assemble his chosen ones from the four winds, stretching from one horizon of the heavens to the other.
**32** Now learn this lesson from the fig tree: as soon as its branch becomes tender and puts out leaves, you know that summer is near.
**33** In the same way, when you see all these things, know that he is right at the door.
**34** Truly I assure you, this generation will certainly not pass away until all these things have taken place.
**35** Both heaven and earth will vanish, yet my words will never pass away.
**36** However, regarding that day and hour no one knows—not even the angels of heaven, nor the Son—but only the Father alone.
**37** Just as it was in the days of Noah, so it will be at the coming of the Son of Man.
**38** For as in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, right up to the day Noah entered the ark,
**39** and they knew nothing until the flood arrived and swept them all away—so also will be the coming of the Son of Man.
**40** At that time two men will be out in the field: one is taken along, and the other is left behind.
**41** Two women will be grinding grain at the mill: one is taken and one is left.
**42** Stay alert, therefore, because you do not know on which day your Lord is coming.
**43** Understand this: if the homeowner had known in which watch of the night the thief was coming, he would have stayed awake and not allowed his house to be broken into.
**44** For this reason you too must be ready, because the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect.
**45** Who then is the faithful and wise servant whom the master has put in charge of his household staff to give them their food at the proper time?
**46** Blessed is that servant whom his master finds doing so when he returns!
**47** Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions.
**48** Yet if that wicked servant says in his heart, 'My master is taking a long time,'
**49** and starts beating his fellow servants while feasting and drinking alongside drunkards,
**50** that servant's master will arrive on a day he does not anticipate and at an hour he does not know,
**51** and will cut him to pieces and assign his share with the hypocrites, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

## Everyday English

**1** As Jesus walked out of the temple complex and started heading away, his disciples came over to admire the temple's impressive buildings with him.
**2** Jesus turned and said, "Take a good look at all this. I'm telling you the truth: not a single stone here will remain standing on top of another—every piece will be demolished."

**4** Jesus answered them, "Be on your guard so nobody fools you.

**7** Nation will clash with nation, and kingdom with kingdom, and famines and earthquakes will hit place after place.
**8** Yet all of that is just the first wave of labor pains.
**9** Then people will arrest you, torture you, and execute you, and every nation will hate you because you belong to me.
**10** At that point, many will lose their faith, turn on each other, and hate one another.
**11** Plenty of fake prophets will appear and fool a lot of people.
**12** And as lawlessness spins out of control, most people's love will turn ice cold.
**13** Still, anyone who holds out to the end will be rescued.
**14** Meanwhile, this good news about God's kingdom will be announced across the entire inhabited world so every nation hears the testimony—and only then will the end arrive.
**15** So when you see the horrifying sacrilege that the prophet Daniel warned about standing inside the holy place—pay close attention as you read this—
**16** everyone in Judea needs to run straight for the mountains!
**17** Anyone up on a flat roof shouldn't waste a second climbing down to grab things from inside the house,
**18** and anyone out working in the field shouldn't run back to fetch their coat.
**19** How hard it will be in those days for pregnant mothers and women nursing babies!

**21** Because the suffering then will be worse than anything the world has ever seen since creation—and nothing like it will ever happen again.
**22** In fact, if God didn't cut those days short, nobody would survive; yet for the sake of his chosen people, he will shorten them.
**23** During that time, if someone tells you, 'Look, here's the Messiah!' or 'Over here!' don't buy it.
**24** Phony messiahs and fake prophets will pop up performing dazzling signs and miracles—trying to trick even God's chosen people, if that were possible.
**25** Listen: I've warned you ahead of time!
**26** So if people tell you, 'He's out in the desert!' don't head out there. If they whisper, 'He's hiding in the back rooms!' don't believe them.
**27** Just like a bolt of lightning flashes across the sky from east to west, that's how unmistakable the Son of Man's arrival will be.
**28** Wherever a dead body lies, vultures are bound to circle overhead.
**29** Right after those days of agony end, the sun will go dark, the moon won't shine, stars will drop from the sky, and the cosmic powers in the heavens will be shaken.
**30** Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky, and every nation on earth will weep in grief as they watch the Son of Man coming on heaven's clouds with tremendous power and radiant glory.
**31** At the blast of a mighty trumpet, he'll send his angels out to gather his chosen people from all four corners of the wind, from one end of the sky to the other.
**32** Learn this simple lesson from a fig tree: the moment its twigs soften and sprout fresh leaves, you know summer is right around the corner.
**33** In the same way, when you spot all these things happening, you know he's right at the doorstep.
**34** I assure you, this generation won't vanish before every bit of this happens.
**35** Sky and earth will pass away, yet my words will never pass away.
**36** As for the exact day and hour, nobody knows—not the angels in heaven, not even the Son—only the Father himself.
**37** The Son of Man's arrival will be just like what happened in Noah's day.
**38** Right up until the day Noah stepped inside the ark, people kept eating, drinking, getting married, and throwing weddings,
**39** completely oblivious until the flood hit and swept them all away. That's how it will be when the Son of Man arrives.
**40** Two men will be working out in the field: one gets taken and the other gets left behind.
**41** Two women will be grinding flour at the millstone: one gets taken and the other gets left behind.
**42** So keep watch! You have no idea what day your Lord is coming back.
**43** Just think: if a homeowner knew which hour of the night a burglar was breaking in, he'd stay wide awake and stop his house from being ransacked.
**44** That's why you need to stay ready too—the Son of Man will arrive at an hour you'd never expect.
**45** Picture a faithful, sensible manager whom the master leaves in charge of the household staff to hand out meals on schedule.

**47** Believe me, the master will put him in charge of everything he owns.
**48** On the other hand, suppose a wicked manager tells himself, 'My master's taking forever to come back,'
**49** and starts beating the other workers while eating and getting drunk with partygoers.
**50** His master will show up on a day he never saw coming, at an hour he has no clue about,

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 3 `παρουσίας` (*parousias*):** Literally "presence" or "arrival," standard Hellenistic term for the state visit of a king or emperor, adopted in the NT for Christ's royal return (vv. 27, 37, 39).
- **v. 7 `λιμοὶ` (*limoi*):** "Famines"; SBLGNT, WH, Treg, and NA28 read `λιμοὶ καὶ σεισμοί` ("famines and earthquakes"), whereas RP adds `καὶ λοιμοί` ("and plagues/pestilences," assimilated from Luke 21:11).
- **v. 15 `τὸ βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως` (*to bdelygma tēs erēmōseōs*):** "The abomination of desolation," reproducing the Septuagint phrasing of Dan 9:27; 11:31; 12:11 for an idolatrous desecration of the sanctuary.
- **v. 28 `ἀετοί` (*aetoi*):** Greek *aetos* covers both "eagles" and "vultures" (carrion-eating raptors, cf. Job 39:27–30; Hab 1:8), here paired with `πτῶμα` (*ptōma*, "corpse/carcass").
- **v. 36 `οὐδὲ ὁ υἱός` (*oude ho huios*):** "Nor the Son," present in SBLGNT, WH, and NA28 (`א*`, `B`, `D`, `Θ`), while omitted in Treg and RP (likely due to theological discomfort or harmonization with v. 42).
- **v. 43 `διορυχθῆναι` (*diorychthēnai*):** Literally "to be dug through," referring to thieves tunneling through sun-dried mudbrick house walls (cf. Matt 6:19–20; Job 24:16).
- **v. 51 `διχοτομήσει` (*dichotomēsei*):** Literally "will cut in two" (from *dichotomeō*), an image of severe judicial execution or complete expulsion/dismemberment.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Daniel 9:27; 11:31; 12:11** (cited explicitly in v. 15): Speaks of the *"detestable sacrilege causing ruin"* set up in the holy sanctuary.
- **Daniel 12:1** (echoed in v. 21): *"A season of distress will arrive unlike any ever seen from the origin of nations until that moment."*
- **Isaiah 13:10; 34:4; Joel 2:10, 31** (woven into v. 29): Cosmic darkening of sun, moon, and stars heralding the Day of the Lord.
- **Daniel 7:13–14; Zechariah 12:10–14** (combined in v. 30): The tribes mourning and *"one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven"* with dominion and glory.
- **Genesis 6:5–13; 7:7–23** (alluded to in vv. 37–39): Noah's entry into the ark and the sudden arrival of the flood.

**1** Jesus went out from the temple, and was going on his way. His disciples came to him to show him the buildings of the temple.
**2** But he answered them, “You see all of these things, don’t you? Most certainly I tell you, there will not be left here one stone on another, that will not be thrown down.”
**3** As he sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to him privately, saying, “Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign of your coming, and of the end of the age?”
**4** Jesus answered them, “Be careful that no one leads you astray.
**5** For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will lead many astray.
**6** You will hear of wars and rumors of wars. See that you aren’t troubled, for all this must happen, but the end is not yet.
**7** For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be famines, plagues, and earthquakes in various places.
**8** But all these things are the beginning of birth pains.
**9** “Then they will deliver you up to oppression and will kill you. You will be hated by all of the nations for my name’s sake.
**10** Then many will stumble, and will deliver up one another, and will hate one another.
**11** Many false prophets will arise and will lead many astray.
**12** Because iniquity will be multiplied, the love of many will grow cold.
**13** But he who endures to the end will be saved.
**14** This Good News of the Kingdom will be preached in the whole world for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.
**15** “When, therefore, you see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand),
**16** then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.
**17** Let him who is on the housetop not go down to take out the things that are in his house.
**18** Let him who is in the field not return back to get his clothes.
**19** But woe to those who are with child and to nursing mothers in those days!
**20** Pray that your flight will not be in the winter nor on a Sabbath,
**21** for then there will be great suffering, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now, no, nor ever will be.

**23** “Then if any man tells you, ‘Behold, here is the Christ!’ or, ‘There!’ don’t believe it.

**28** For wherever the carcass is, that is where the vultures gather together.

**34** Most certainly I tell you, this generation will not pass away until all these things are accomplished.
**35** Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.
**36** “But no one knows of that day and hour, not even the angels of heaven, but my Father only.
**37** As the days of Noah were, so will the coming of the Son of Man be.
**38** For as in those days which were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ship,

**41** Two women will be grinding at the mill: one will be taken and one will be left.

**44** Therefore also be ready, for in an hour that you don’t expect, the Son of Man will come.
