## Clear & Faithful

**1** Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and his brother John, and led them up a high mountain by themselves.
**2** And he was transfigured before their eyes; his face shone like the sun, and his clothes became as radiant as the light.
**3** Suddenly Moses and Elijah appeared to them, conversing with him.
**4** Peter spoke up to Jesus, "Lord, it is wonderful that we are here! If you wish, I can construct three shelters right here—one for you, another for Moses, and another for Elijah."
**5** While he was still speaking, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and a voice from the cloud declared, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I delight; listen to him."
**6** When the disciples heard this, they fell face down and were terrified.
**7** Jesus walked over, laid a hand on them, and said, "Rise up; have no fear."
**8** When they lifted up their eyes, they saw no one except Jesus alone.
**9** As they were coming down the mountain, Jesus instructed them, "Do not tell anyone about the vision until the Son of Man has been raised from the dead."
**10** The disciples questioned him, "Why then do the scribes teach that Elijah must arrive first?"
**11** He answered, "Elijah indeed is coming and will restore all things;
**12** yet I tell you that Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did to him whatever they pleased. In the same way, the Son of Man is also going to suffer at their hands."
**13** Then the disciples realized that he was speaking to them about John the Baptist.
**14** When they came to the crowd, a man approached Jesus, knelt before him,
**15** and pleaded, "Lord, have mercy on my son, for he suffers from seizures and is in terrible distress; he often falls into the fire and often into the water.
**16** I brought him to your disciples, yet they were unable to heal him."
**17** Jesus answered, "You unbelieving and perverse generation! How long must I stay with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring him here to me."
**18** Jesus sternly rebuked the demon, and it departed from him; from that very hour the boy was restored.
**19** Later the disciples came to Jesus privately and asked, "Why could we not drive it out?"
**20** He told them, "Because of your meager faith. Truly I assure you, if you have faith like a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move, and nothing will be impossible for you."
**22** While they were gathering together in Galilee, Jesus told them, "The Son of Man is about to be handed over into human hands;
**23** they will kill him, and on the third day he will be raised." And they were deeply distressed.
**24** When they arrived in Capernaum, the collectors of the two-drachma temple tax approached Peter and asked, "Does your teacher not pay the two-drachma tax?"
**25** "Yes," he replied. When Peter entered the house, Jesus spoke to him first: "What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth collect customs or taxes—from their own sons, or from outsiders?"
**26** When he answered, "From outsiders," Jesus said to him, "Then the sons are exempt.
**27** Still, so that we do not give offense to them, go to the lake, cast a hook, and take the first fish that comes up. When you open its mouth, you will find a four-drachma coin; take that and give it to them for both me and you."

## Everyday English

**1** Six days after that, Jesus brought Peter, James, and James's brother John up onto a high mountain where they could be alone.
**2** Right in front of them, Jesus' entire appearance changed—his face blazed like the sun, and his clothes turned dazzlingly bright like pure light.
**3** All of a sudden, Moses and Elijah appeared right there talking with him.
**4** Peter blurted out to Jesus, "Lord, it's wonderful that we're here! If you'd like, I can build three shelters here—one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."
**5** Before Peter even finished talking, a luminous cloud enveloped them, and a voice spoke out of the cloud: "This is my dearly loved Son, who brings me great joy. Listen to him!"
**6** Hearing that voice, the disciples dropped to the ground face first, completely terrified.
**7** Jesus walked over, touched them, and said, "Stand up. Don't be afraid."
**8** When they looked back up, nobody else was there—only Jesus.
**9** On their way back down the mountain, Jesus warned them, "Don't breathe a word about what you saw to anyone until the Son of Man rises from the dead."
**10** The disciples asked him, "Then why do the religious scholars insist that Elijah has to arrive first?"
**11** Jesus answered, "Elijah is indeed supposed to come and set everything right.
**12** But I'm telling you, Elijah has already arrived, and people didn't recognize him—instead, they treated him however they felt like. The Son of Man is going to suffer the exact same way at their hands."
**13** It dawned on the disciples then that he was talking about John the Baptist.
**14** Once they rejoined the crowd, a man came up, dropped to his knees in front of Jesus,
**15** and begged, "Lord, please take pity on my son! He gets violent seizures and is suffering horribly—he keeps collapsing into the fire or into the water.
**16** I brought him to your disciples, but they couldn't heal him."
**17** Jesus replied, "What a faithless, mixed-up generation! How much longer do I have to stay with you? How much longer must I put up with you? Bring the boy over here to me."
**18** Jesus sternly commanded the demon, and it left the boy, who was completely healed right on the spot.
**19** Afterward, the disciples pulled Jesus aside in private and asked, "Why couldn't we force that demon out?"
**20** "Because you have so little trust," Jesus told them. "Let me be clear: if your faith is even as tiny as a mustard seed, you can tell this mountain, 'Shift from here over to there,' and it will move! Nothing will be out of reach for you."
**22** While they were meeting together back in Galilee, Jesus told them, "The Son of Man is soon going to be betrayed into human hands.
**23** They will execute him, and on the third day he'll be raised back to life." Hearing this filled the disciples with deep sorrow.
**24** When they got to Capernaum, the men collecting the annual temple tax approached Peter and asked, "Doesn't your teacher pay the temple tax?"
**25** "Of course he does," Peter said. As soon as Peter walked into the house, Jesus brought it up before Peter could say a word: "What's your take on this, Simon? Who do earthly kings collect tolls and taxes from—their own children, or other people?"
**26** "From other people," Peter answered. "Exactly," Jesus replied. "That means the children go free.
**27** Still, we don't want to stir up unnecessary trouble with them. Go down to the lake, throw in a line, and pull in the very first fish you catch. Open its mouth and you'll find a coin worth enough for both of us—take it and pay the tax for me and yourself."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 2 `μετεμορφώθη` (*metemorphōthē*):** Aorist passive of *metamorphoō* ("to be transformed in appearance/form"), denoting a visible revelation of divine glory rather than a mere optical illusion.
- **v. 15 `σεληνιάζεται` (*selēniazetai*):** Literally "is moon-struck" (from *selēnē*, "moon"), ancient medical/popular terminology for epilepsy or periodic seizures once associated with lunar cycles.
- **v. 20 `ὀλιγοπιστίαν` (*oligopistian*):** "Little-faith" or "scanty trust" (WH, Treg, NA28), contrasting with RP's `ἀπιστίαν` ("unbelief").
- **v. 21 `Τοῦτο δὲ τὸ γένος οὐκ ἐκπορεύεται εἰ μὴ ἐν προσευχῇ καὶ νηστείᾳ` (*Touto de to genos ouk ekporeuetai ei mē en proseuchē kai nēsteia*):** Omitted in SBLGNT and Nestle 1904 (following WH and NA28; absent in earliest Alexandrian witnesses such as `א*` and `B`), while added as v. 21 in Treg and RP ("But this kind does not go out except by prayer and fasting"), likely harmonized from Mark 9:29.
- **v. 24 `δίδραχμα` (*didrachma*):** Double-drachma silver coins equivalent to the half-shekel annual temple tax (Exod 30:13); in v. 27, a `στατήρ` (*statēr*, four drachmas) covers the tax for two persons.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Exodus 24:15–18; 34:29–35** (echoed in vv. 1–6): Six days, a high mountain, a radiant face, and a cloud from which God's voice speaks at Sinai.
- **Psalm 2:7; Isaiah 42:1; Deuteronomy 18:15** (woven together in v. 5): *"My beloved Son"* (Ps 2:7), *"in whom I delight"* (Isa 42:1), and *"listen to him"* (Deut 18:15, the prophet like Moses).
- **Malachi 4:5–6** (alluded to in vv. 10–12): Promise that Elijah will come before the great day of the Lord to restore and turn hearts.
- **Exodus 30:13–14** (background to vv. 24–27): The half-shekel ransom offering required of Israelite men for the sanctuary.
