## Clear & Faithful

**1** Once Jesus had concluded these sayings, he left Galilee and traveled into the Judean territory across the Jordan.
**2** Large crowds followed him, and he healed them there.
**3** Then Pharisees approached him to test him and asked, "Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any and every reason?"
**4** He answered, "Have you not read that the Creator from the beginning made them male and female,
**5** and said, 'For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh'?
**6** So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let no human being separate."
**7** They asked him, "Why then did Moses command a man to give a certificate of divorce and send her away?"
**8** He told them, "Because of your hardness of heart Moses permitted you to divorce your wives, yet from the beginning it was not so.
**9** I tell you plainly that any man who divorces his wife—unless on account of sexual unfaithfulness—and marries another woman commits adultery; and anyone who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."
**10** His disciples said to him, "If that is a man's situation with his wife, it is better not to marry!"
**11** He replied to them, "Not everyone can accept this teaching, but only those to whom it has been given.
**12** For there are eunuchs who were born that way from their mother's womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by people, and there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. Let the one who is able to accept this accept it."
**13** Then young children were brought to him so that he might lay his hands on them and pray, yet the disciples rebuked those who brought them.
**14** But Jesus said, "Let the young children come to me and do not stop them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these."
**15** After laying his hands on them, he went on from there.
**16** Just then a man came up to him and asked, "Teacher, what good deed must I do to obtain eternal life?"
**17** He said to him, "Why do you ask me about what is good? There is only one who is good. Yet if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments."
**18** "Which ones?" he inquired. Jesus answered, "'Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not bear false witness,
**19** show honor to your father and mother,' and 'Love your neighbor just as yourself.'"
**20** The young man said to him, "I have kept all of these. What do I still lack?"
**21** Jesus told him, "If you want to be complete, go, sell your possessions and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; then come, follow me."
**22** When the young man heard that word, he went away grieving, because he owned great wealth.
**23** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "Truly I tell you, it will be hard for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven.
**24** Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God."
**25** When the disciples heard this, they were utterly amazed and asked, "Who then can be saved?"
**26** Jesus looked straight at them and said, "With human beings this is impossible, yet with God all things are possible."
**27** Then Peter answered him, "Look, we have left everything and followed you. What then will there be for us?"
**28** Jesus said to them, "Truly I assure you that at the renewal of all things, when the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, you who have followed me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
**29** And everyone who has left houses, or brothers or sisters, or father or mother, or children, or fields for the sake of my name will receive a hundred times as much and will inherit eternal life.
**30** Yet many who are first will be last, and the last will be first."

## Everyday English

**1** Once Jesus finished teaching those things, he left Galilee and traveled into Judea on the far side of the Jordan River.
**2** Huge crowds followed him along the way, and he healed the sick there.
**3** Some Pharisees came up to trap him with a question: "Does our Law allow a husband to divorce his wife for just any reason at all?"
**4** Jesus answered, "Haven't you read in Scripture that the Creator made humanity male and female right from the start,
**5** and declared, 'That's why a man leaves his father and mother and clings to his wife, and the two become one single flesh'?
**6** So they aren't two separate people anymore—they're one flesh. Nobody has the right to split apart what God has joined together."
**7** They pressed back, "Then why did Moses say a man can hand his wife a divorce paper and send her off?"
**8** Jesus told them, "Moses only gave you permission to divorce your wives because your hearts are stubborn—that's never how it was meant to be from the beginning.
**9** Here's what I tell you: anyone who divorces his wife—unless she has been sexually unfaithful—and marries someone else is committing adultery, and anyone who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."
**10** Hearing that, his disciples remarked, "If a husband is locked into marriage like that, maybe it's better not to get married at all!"
**11** Jesus told them, "Not everybody can live with this standard—only those whom God equips for it.
**12** Some men are unable to marry from birth, others were made eunuchs by human hands, and still others choose celibacy for the sake of heaven's kingdom. Anyone who can embrace that calling should embrace it."
**13** About then, people brought little children to Jesus so he could place his hands on them and pray for them, but the disciples scolded them for bothering him.
**14** Jesus stepped in: "Let the little children come to me! Don't hold them back, because heaven's kingdom belongs to people just like them."
**15** He placed his hands on them in blessing, and then moved on from there.
**16** Soon a man hurried up to Jesus and asked, "Teacher, what good thing do I have to do to gain eternal life?"
**17** Jesus replied, "Why are you asking me about what's good? Only God is truly good. If you want to enter eternal life, obey the commandments."
**18** "Which ones?" the man asked. Jesus said, "'Don't murder, don't commit adultery, don't steal, don't lie in testimony,
**19** honor your father and mother,' and 'Love your neighbor the same way you love yourself.'"
**20** "I've obeyed every single one of those," the young man said. "What am I still missing?"
**21** Jesus told him, "If you want to go all the way and lack nothing, go sell everything you own, give the money to the poor—you'll gain treasure in heaven—and then come follow me."
**22** When the young man heard that, he walked away heartbroken, because he was extremely wealthy.
**23** Jesus turned to his disciples and said, "Listen to me: it's terribly difficult for a wealthy person to enter heaven's kingdom.
**24** In fact, I'll say it again: it's easier for a camel to squeeze through a sewing needle's eye than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom!"
**25** That stunned the disciples. "Then who on earth can be saved?" they asked.
**26** Jesus looked them in the eye and said, "For human beings, it's impossible—but with God, nothing is impossible."
**27** Peter spoke up next: "Wait—we gave up everything to follow you! What's waiting for us?"
**28** Jesus promised them, "Believe me, when the whole world is made new and the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, all of you who followed me will also sit on twelve thrones governing Israel's twelve tribes.
**29** And anyone who has given up homes, brothers, sisters, father, mother, children, or land because of me will get back a hundred times more and inherit eternal life.
**30** Yet many who seem first right now will end up last, and many who seem last will end up first."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 6 `συνέζευξεν` (*synezeuxen*):** From *syzeugnymi*, "to yoke together" (as oxen under a single yoke), depicting marriage as a divine joining rather than a disposable contract.
- **v. 9 `καὶ ὁ ἀπολελυμένην γαμήσας μοιχᾶται` (*kai ho apolelymenēn gamēsas moichatai*):** Included in SBLGNT, Treg, and RP ("and the one who marries a divorced woman commits adultery"), whereas WH and NA28 omit this final clause (matching `א` and `D` in part, though `B` retains a similar clause).
- **v. 11 `χωροῦσι` (*chōrousi*):** From *chōreō*, "to have space/capacity for, make room for, accept."
- **v. 17 `Τί με ἐρωτᾷς περὶ τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ; εἷς ἐστιν ὁ ἀγαθός` (*Ti me erōtas peri tou agathou? Heis estin ho agathos*):** Read by WH, Treg, and NA28 ("Why do you ask me concerning what is good? One is the Good [One]"); RP harmonizes with Mark 10:18 / Luke 18:19 (`Τί με λέγεις ἀγαθόν; Οὐδεὶς ἀγαθός εἰ μὴ εἷς ὁ θεός`, "Why do you call me good?...").
- **v. 28 `παλιγγενεσίᾳ` (*palingenesia*):** Compound of *palin* ("again") and *genesis* ("birth/origin"), signifying cosmic renewal or the new age of the coming kingdom.
- **v. 29 `ἑκατονταπλασίονα` (*hekatontaplasiona*):** "A hundredfold" (NA28, RP), whereas WH and Treg read `πολλαπλασίονα` ("manifold / many times as much"). Notice also that RP includes `ἢ γυναῖκα` ("or wife"), omitted by WH, Treg, and NA28.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 1:27; 2:24** (quoted in vv. 4–5): *"As male and female God formed them"* and *"For this reason a man leaves father and mother behind, clings to his wife, and the pair become a single flesh."*
- **Deuteronomy 24:1–4** (alluded to in v. 7): The Mosaic provision regulating a written certificate of divorce.
- **Exodus 20:12–16 / Deuteronomy 5:16–20; Leviticus 19:18** (quoted in vv. 18–19): The ethical commandments of the Decalogue paired with *"Show love to your neighbor as you do to yourself."*
- **Isaiah 65:17; 66:22** (conceptual background to v. 28): Promise of new heavens and a new earth in God's coming renewal.
