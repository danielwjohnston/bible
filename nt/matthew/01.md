## Clear & Faithful

**1** An account of the genealogy of Jesus the Messiah, the son of David, the son of Abraham.
**2** Abraham was the father of Isaac, Isaac the father of Jacob, and Jacob the father of Judah and his brothers;
**3** Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar, Perez the father of Hezron, and Hezron the father of Aram;
**4** Aram was the father of Amminadab, Amminadab the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon the father of Salmon;
**5** Salmon was the father of Boaz by Rahab, Boaz the father of Obed by Ruth, and Obed the father of Jesse;
**6** and Jesse was the father of David the king. David was the father of Solomon by the wife of Uriah;
**7** Solomon was the father of Rehoboam, Rehoboam the father of Abijah, and Abijah the father of Asaph;
**8** Asaph was the father of Jehoshaphat, Jehoshaphat the father of Joram, and Joram the father of Uzziah;
**9** Uzziah was the father of Jotham, Jotham the father of Ahaz, and Ahaz the father of Hezekiah;
**10** Hezekiah was the father of Manasseh, Manasseh the father of Amos, and Amos the father of Josiah;
**11** and Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers around the deportation to Babylon.
**12** After the Babylonian deportation, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel, and Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel;
**13** Zerubbabel was the father of Abiud, Abiud the father of Eliakim, and Eliakim the father of Azor;
**14** Azor was the father of Zadok, Zadok the father of Achim, and Achim the father of Eliud;
**15** Eliud was the father of Eleazar, Eleazar the father of Matthan, and Matthan the father of Jacob;
**16** and Jacob fathered Joseph, Mary's husband, from whom Jesus, called the Messiah, was born.
**17** Altogether, then, there were fourteen generations from Abraham down to David, fourteen from David to the Babylonian deportation, and fourteen from the Babylonian deportation to the Messiah.
**18** Now the birth of Jesus the Messiah took place in this way: when his mother Mary had been pledged in marriage to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child through the Holy Spirit.
**19** Because Joseph her husband was a righteous man and did not want to disgrace her publicly, he resolved to divorce her quietly.
**20** Yet as he was mulling these matters over, suddenly an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, "Joseph, descendant of David, have no fear about taking Mary as your wife, since what has been conceived inside her comes from the Holy Spirit.
**21** She is going to bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, because he is the one who will rescue his people from their sins."
**22** All this occurred so that what the Lord had spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled:
**23** "Look, the virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will give him the name Emmanuel"—which means, when translated, "God with us."
**24** When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did just as the angel of the Lord had commanded him and took his wife into his home,
**25** and he did not have marital relations with her until she gave birth to a son; and he named him Jesus.

## Everyday English

**1** This is the family history of Jesus the Chosen King, a descendant of David and Abraham.
**2** Abraham's son was Isaac. Isaac's son was Jacob. Jacob's sons were Judah and his brothers.
**3** Judah had twin sons, Perez and Zerah, with Tamar. Perez had a son named Hezron, and Hezron had Aram.
**4** Aram's son was Amminadab, Amminadab's was Nahshon, and Nahshon's was Salmon.
**5** Salmon and Rahab were the parents of Boaz. Boaz and Ruth were the parents of Obed. Obed's son was Jesse,
**6** and Jesse was the father of King David. David became the father of Solomon through Uriah's widow.
**7** Solomon's son was Rehoboam, Rehoboam's was Abijah, and Abijah's was Asaph.
**8** Asaph's son was Jehoshaphat, Jehoshaphat's was Joram, and Joram's was Uzziah.
**9** Uzziah's son was Jotham, Jotham's was Ahaz, and Ahaz's was Hezekiah.
**10** Hezekiah's son was Manasseh, Manasseh's was Amos, and Amos's was Josiah.
**11** Josiah was the father of Jeconiah and his brothers around the time Judah was carried off to Babylon.
**12** Once the Babylonian exile happened, Jeconiah became the father of Shealtiel, and Shealtiel had Zerubbabel.
**13** Zerubbabel's son was Abiud, Abiud's was Eliakim, and Eliakim's was Azor.
**14** Azor's son was Zadok, Zadok's was Achim, and Achim's was Eliud.
**15** Eliud's son was Eleazar, Eleazar's was Matthan, and Matthan's was Jacob.
**16** Jacob became the father of Joseph, who married Mary. Mary was the mother of Jesus, who is called the Messiah.
**17** Putting it all together, there are fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen from David to the exile in Babylon, and fourteen from that exile to the Messiah.
**18** Here is how Jesus the Messiah came to be born: his mother Mary was engaged to Joseph, but before they began living together as husband and wife, she was discovered to be pregnant by the Holy Spirit.
**19** Joseph was an honorable man and didn't want to humiliate her in public, so he planned to end the engagement quietly.
**20** While he was mulling this over, an angel of the Lord suddenly showed up to him in a dream and said, "Joseph, descendant of David, don't hesitate to marry Mary. The baby growing inside her comes from the Holy Spirit.
**21** She's going to have a boy, and you must name him Jesus, because he's the one who will rescue his people from their sins."
**22** Everything unfolded this way to fulfill what the Lord had announced through his prophet:
**23** "Look, the virgin will get pregnant and have a son, and people will name him Emmanuel"—meaning "God is here with us."
**24** Once Joseph woke up, he followed the angel's instructions and brought Mary home as his wife.
**25** Still, he didn't sleep with her until after her baby boy was born, and Joseph gave him the name Jesus.

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 1 `βίβλος γενέσεως` (*biblos geneseōs*):** Literally "book/scroll of origin" or "account of lineage," directly echoing the Greek Septuagint phrasing of Gen 2:4 and 5:1 (*biblos geneseōs*) to signal a new creation and covenantal beginning.
- **v. 1 `χριστοῦ` (*christou*):** From *christos* ("anointed one"), the Greek rendering of Hebrew *Mashiach* ("Messiah"), denoting the promised royal deliverer from David's line.
- **v. 5 `Βόες` (*Boes*) & `Ἰωβήδ` (*Iōbēd*):** Earliest Alexandrian witnesses (`WH`, `NA28`) spell these names *Boes* and *Iōbēd*, whereas `Treg` prints *Boos* and `RP` reads *Booz* and *Ōbēd* (corresponding to Boaz and Obed in Ruth 4:21–22).
- **v. 7–8 `Ἀσάφ` (*Asaph*):** Early Greek manuscripts (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`) spell King Asa's name *Asaph* (identical to the psalmist Asaph), whereas `RP` harmonizes to *Asa* matching 1 Kgs 15.
- **v. 10 `Ἀμώς` (*Amōs*):** Early Greek manuscripts (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`) spell King Amon's name *Amōs* (identical to the prophet Amos), whereas `RP` harmonizes to *Amōn* matching 2 Kgs 21.
- **v. 18 `γένεσις` (*genesis*):** Meaning "origin," "birth," or "beginning" (`WH`, `Treg`, `NA28`), linking back to v. 1; `RP` reads *gennēsis* ("begetting/birth").
- **v. 19 `δειγματίσαι` (*deigmatisai*):** "To expose to public shame" or "make an example of"; `RP` uses the compounded form *paradeigmatisai*.
- **v. 21 `Ἰησοῦν` (*Iēsoun*):** Greek form of Hebrew *Yeshua*/*Yehoshua* ("YHWH saves"), explicitly unpacked in the causal clause "for he will save (*sōsei*) his people."
- **v. 25 `υἱόν` (*huion*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` read simply "a son," whereas `RP` expands to *ton huion autēs ton prōtotokon* ("her firstborn son"), likely harmonized from Luke 2:7.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Genesis 2:4; 5:1 (LXX)** (echoed in v. 1): *Biblos geneseōs* links Jesus's genealogy with the primal record of heaven, earth, and humanity's generations.
- **Ruth 4:18–22 & 1 Chronicles 2:1–15; 3:10–19** (source lineage in vv. 2–16): Traces the royal Judahite line through Tamar, Rahab, Ruth, and Bathsheba down to the Babylonian deportation and post-exilic house of Zerubbabel.
- **2 Samuel 7:12–16** (background to vv. 1, 6, 17): God's covenant promise to establish David's royal offspring forever.
- **Isaiah 7:14** (quoted in v. 23): *"For this reason the Lord himself will give you a sign: the young woman/virgin is pregnant and will bear a son, and she will name him Immanuel."*
