## Clear & Faithful

**1** Stepping into a boat, Jesus crossed back over and came to his own town.
**2** Just then, people brought to him a paralyzed man lying on a cot. Observing their faith, Jesus told the paralytic, "Have courage, child; your sins are forgiven."
**3** At this, some of the scribes said to themselves, "This man is blaspheming."
**4** Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said, "Why are you harboring evil thoughts in your hearts?
**5** After all, which is simpler to declare: 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Rise and walk'?
**6** Yet so that you may know that the Son of Man holds authority on earth to pardon sins"—at that he said to the paralytic—"Rise, lift up your cot, and return to your home."
**7** So the man got up and went home.
**8** When the crowds saw this, they were filled with awe and glorified God, who had granted such authority to human beings.
**9** As Jesus passed on from there, he saw a man named Matthew sitting at the tax booth, and said to him, "Follow me." And he got up and followed him.
**10** Later, as Jesus was reclining at table inside the house, many tax collectors and sinners arrived and were dining alongside Jesus and his disciples.
**11** Seeing this, the Pharisees kept asking his disciples, "Why does your teacher dine alongside tax collectors and sinners?"
**12** Hearing this, Jesus replied, "Those who are healthy do not need a physician, but those who are sick do.
**13** Go and learn what this means: 'I desire mercy, and not sacrifice.' For I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners."
**14** Then John's disciples came to him and asked, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast frequently, while your disciples do not fast?"
**15** Jesus answered them, "Can the wedding guests mourn while the bridegroom is still with them? The days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast.
**16** No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth onto an old cloak, for the patch pulls away from the cloak and leaves a worse tear.
**17** Nor do people pour new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the skins burst, the wine spills out, and the wineskins are ruined. Instead, they pour new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved together."
**18** While he was speaking these words to them, suddenly a certain leader approached, bowed before him, and said, "My daughter just died; yet come and place your hand upon her, and she will live."
**19** Rising up, Jesus accompanied him, together with his disciples.
**20** Just then, a woman who had suffered a flow of blood for twelve years approached from behind and touched the fringe of his garment,
**21** for she kept telling herself, "If only I touch his cloak, I will be made well."
**22** Turning around and seeing her, Jesus said, "Take heart, daughter; your faith has healed you." And the woman was made well from that very hour.
**23** When Jesus entered the leader's house and saw the flute players and the noisy crowd of mourners,
**24** he said, "Step outside, for the little girl has not died, but is asleep." And they laughed at him in scorn.
**25** Once the crowd had been put outside, he went in, took her by the hand, and the little girl stood up.
**26** And the report of this spread throughout that entire region.
**27** As Jesus went on from there, two blind men followed him, shouting, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!"
**28** When he entered the house, the blind men approached him, and Jesus asked them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this?" They answered him, "Yes, Lord."
**29** Then he touched their eyes and said, "According to your faith let it be done for you."
**30** And their eyes were opened. Jesus sternly warned them, "See that no one finds out."
**31** Yet they went out and spread the news about him through that whole region.
**32** While they were departing, people suddenly brought to him a mute man afflicted by a demon.
**33** Once the demon had been driven out, the mute man spoke. The crowds marveled, saying, "Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!"
**34** But the Pharisees kept saying, "He drives out demons by the ruler of the demons."
**35** Jesus continued going through all the towns and villages, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness.
**36** Seeing the crowds, he was deeply moved with compassion for them, because they were troubled and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.
**37** Then he told his disciples, "The harvest truly is abundant, yet the workers are scarce;
**38** plead therefore with the Lord of the harvest so that he may send workers out into his harvest."

## Everyday English

**1** Jesus got into a boat, sailed back across the lake, and returned to his hometown.
**2** Just then, some friends carried over a paralyzed man lying on a mat. When Jesus saw how much faith they had, he told the paralyzed man, "Cheer up, son! Your sins are forgiven."
**3** Immediately some of the legal scholars muttered to themselves, "This guy is insulting God!"
**4** Sensing what they were thinking, Jesus asked, "Why are you nursing dark thoughts in your hearts?
**5** Which is easier to say: 'Your sins are forgiven,' or 'Stand up and walk'?
**6** I'll prove to you that the Son of Man has real authority here on earth to forgive sins." Then he turned to the paralyzed man: "Stand up, grab your mat, and head home!"
**7** The man stood right up and walked off to his house.
**8** Watching this, the crowds were filled with reverence and praised God for trusting human beings with authority like that.
**9** As Jesus walked on from there, he spotted a man named Matthew sitting at the tax-collection booth. "Come follow me," Jesus told him. Matthew got up and walked away with him.
**10** Later, while Jesus was eating dinner at the house, lots of tax collectors and outcasts showed up and joined him and his disciples at the table.
**11** When the Pharisees noticed this, they confronted his disciples: "Why is your teacher sharing meals with tax collectors and known sinners?"
**12** Jesus overheard them and answered, "Healthy people don't need a doctor—sick people do!
**13** Go study what Scripture means by, 'I want mercy, not animal sacrifices.' I didn't come to invite people who think they're already good—I came for sinners."
**14** Then John's disciples came over and asked Jesus, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast all the time, while your followers never fast?"
**15** Jesus answered, "Can wedding guests be sad while the groom is still celebrating with them? Someday the groom will be taken away from them—that's when they'll fast.
**16** Besides, nobody sews a brand-new patch of unwashed cloth onto an old coat; the new patch shrinks, yanks away from the old fabric, and makes the rip even worse.
**17** And nobody pours fresh fermenting wine into old, brittle wineskins—if they do, the skins split open, the wine pours out, and the skins are destroyed. No, people pour new wine into brand-new wineskins so both last."
**18** While Jesus was still talking, a local synagogue official walked in, bowed before him, and pleaded, "My little girl just died. Please come lay your hand on her, and she'll come back to life!"
**19** Jesus stood up and walked over with him, his disciples trailing along.
**20** Along the way, a woman who had been bleeding nonstop for twelve years slipped up behind him and touched the fringe of his robe.
**21** She kept thinking to herself, "If I can just touch his coat, I'll be healed."
**22** Jesus turned around, looked at her, and said, "Take heart, daughter! Your faith has made you well." At that exact instant, she was healed.
**23** When Jesus arrived at the official's home and saw the hired flute players and the noisy crowd mourning,
**24** he told them, "Clear out! The girl isn't dead—she's only sleeping." They laughed in his face.
**25** Once the crowd was ushered outside, Jesus stepped into the room, took her by the hand, and the little girl got right up.
**26** Word of what happened raced through that entire region.
**27** As Jesus walked away from there, two blind men trailed behind him, shouting, "Have pity on us, Son of David!"
**28** Once he went indoors, the two blind men came right up to him. Jesus asked them, "Do you trust that I have the power to do this?" "Yes, Lord," they answered.
**29** Then he touched their eyes and said, "Let it happen for you just as you believed."
**30** Instantly their sight returned! Jesus gave them a strict warning: "Make sure nobody hears about this."
**31** Yet as soon as they stepped outside, they broadcast the story everywhere in that district.
**32** Just as they were heading out, people brought Jesus a man who couldn't speak because a demon controlled him.
**33** Once Jesus drove the demon out, the man started talking! The crowds were stunned and said, "We've never seen anything like this happen in Israel!"
**34** Meanwhile, the Pharisees grumbled, "He uses the prince of demons to drive out demons."
**35** Jesus kept visiting every town and village, teaching inside their synagogues, sharing the good news of God's kingdom, and curing every illness and ailment.
**36** Looking out over the crowds, his heart ached with compassion for them, because they were worn down and lost—like sheep with nobody to shepherd them.
**37** So he told his disciples, "The grain field is huge and ready for harvest, yet there are hardly any workers!
**38** Beg the Lord who owns the harvest to send more workers out into his fields."

## Crossings & Lexical Notes

- **v. 4 `εἰδώς` (*eidōs*):** "Knowing/perceiving" (`WH`, `Treg`), whereas `NA28` and `RP` read *idōn* ("seeing").
- **v. 13 `ἁμαρτωλούς` (*hamartōlous*):** SBLGNT, `WH`, `Treg`, and `NA28` conclude with "but sinners"; `RP` adds *eis metanoian* ("to repentance"), harmonizing with Luke 5:32.
- **v. 14 `πολλά` (*polla*):** "Often/much" (`Treg`, `NA28`, `RP`), omitted in `WH` and some Alexandrian witnesses.
- **v. 16 `ἀγνάφου` (*agnaphou*):** Literally "unfulled/unwashed/new" cloth that has not yet been shrunk or softened, so when wet it shrinks and tears away the older woven garment.
- **v. 20 `κρασπέδου` (*kraspedou*):** The twisted corner tassel (*tzitzit*) worn on the outer cloak in obedience to Num 15:38–39 and Deut 22:12.
- **v. 30 `ἐνεβριμήθη` (*enebrimēthē*):** From *embrimaomai*, conveying intense emotional urgency—"sternly/strictly charged."
- **v. 36 `ἐσκυλμένοι καὶ ἐρριμμένοι` (*eskylmenoi kai errimmenoi*):** *Skyllō* ("harassed/flayed/exhausted") and *rhiptō* ("cast down/abandoned/prostrate"), painting the crowds as battered, shepherdless sheep.

## Supplemental Texts & Allusions

- **Hosea 6:6** (quoted in v. 13): *"For I delight in steadfast love/mercy rather than sacrifice, and in the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings."*
- **Numbers 15:38–39** (background to v. 20): Command to attach blue-corded tassels to garment corners as reminders of God's commandments.
- **Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 22:17; Ezekiel 34:5–6** (echoed in v. 36): Israel's scattered people likened to shepherdless sheep preyed upon and neglected by their leaders.
