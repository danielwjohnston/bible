# Product documentation

This documentation expands the existing translation and offline-reader project into its long-term Scripture study vision. Requirements describe intended behavior, not features already implemented.

1. [Vision](VISION.md): purpose, principles, scope, and composable study dimensions.
2. [Study taxonomy](STUDY_TAXONOMY.md): all 82 supplied categories and 4,588 entries, supporting components, and the reconstruction provenance caveat.
3. [Requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md): durable capabilities, acceptance criteria, and source traceability.
4. [Prototype capability record](PROTOTYPES.md): three privately supplied HTML examples, inspected without publishing their content or assets.
5. [Corpus and licensing](CORPUS_AND_LICENSING.md): copyleft foundation, source provenance, separate rights layers, and release gates.
6. [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md): incremental delivery without narrowing the full vision.
7. [Decisions and open questions](DECISIONS.md): existing commitments and unresolved choices.
8. [Documentation verification](VERIFICATION.md): baseline, preservation checks, and limits of this review.

The full preceding **82-category taxonomy and all its individual entries remain a long-term product requirement**. All 82 categories and 4,588 category-list entries are now documented from the user-supplied reconstruction, with a [category and entry coverage register](TAXONOMY_COVERAGE.md) and [machine-readable registry](taxonomy/registry.json). All 743 complete entries in the earlier original-conversation excerpt agree exactly. The supplied files explicitly warn that they were manually reconstructed rather than independently verified against a direct transcript; that remaining fidelity question is recorded under D01 and TAX-01. No category-list entries were invented or omitted by this documentation update.

The repository's existing [README](../README.md), [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md), chapter format, and [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](../LICENSE) remain authoritative for existing work. These documents do not relicense any files, import the prototypes, or claim that the planned platform already exists.
