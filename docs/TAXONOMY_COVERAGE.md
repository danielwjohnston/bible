## Extension and review rules

Preserve labels and original IDs when adding synonyms, dimensions, alternate classifications, or later methods. New entries require namespaced IDs, provenance, activities, prerequisites, representation choices, accessible alternatives, evidence expectations, and explicit coverage mapping. Do not turn a category-level demonstration into a whole-category completion claim. A deferred entry remains visible with its reason, owner when assigned, and revisit condition.

Generate bounded combinations from compatible dimensions rather than precomputing an unbounded inventory. Map a recipe to its corpus revision and sources, distinguish its subject/method/lens/output/objective, and keep interpretation, inference, and application separate. Prototype traceability remains in [PROTOTYPES.md](PROTOTYPES.md); requirements and milestone definitions are in [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
